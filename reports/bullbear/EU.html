一、階段走勢預測：關鍵節點

（一）12 月關鍵節點：市場預期ECB於12/17升息1碼、存款利率由2.50%升到2.75%的機率約91%，前一週Fed 12/10也可能升息；指數從11/13高點664回落，635附近和年線625.8是主要支撐，雙央行升息落地前偏向區間整理。

December key points: The market estimates a 91% chance that the ECB will raise rates by 25 bp on 12/17, moving the deposit rate from 2.50% to 2.75%; the Fed may also hike on 12/10. The index fell from the 11/13 peak of 664 to around 635, with the yearly line at 625.8 providing main support. Prior to the dual-central-bank hikes, the market is likely to stay in a range.

（二）2027 年 Q1 轉折：Lagarde 將於2027/2/7提前卸任，新總裁上任後會否暫停升息是關鍵；德國2027年預算國防支出約1,090億歐元、基建投資1,175億歐元開始執行；UBS與德意志銀行看2027年底STOXX 600約750點，DZ Bank看DAX 28,500點。

2027 Q1 turning point: Lagarde is scheduled to step down early on 2027/2/7; whether the new ECB president will pause rate hikes is critical. Germany’s 2027 budget allocates roughly €109 billion to defence and €117.5 billion to infrastructure, both set to start spending. UBS and Deutsche Bank project the STOXX 600 at about 750 points by year-end 2027, while DZ Bank targets the DAX at 28,500 points.

二、多空核心邏輯分析

（一）支撐多頭的三大動能

Three drivers supporting the bulls

1. 德國財政擴張：2027年預算草案總支出5,554億歐元、舉債2,036億歐元，國防支出由822億升到約1,090億歐元，並有5,000億歐元基建基金，有利國防、工業與營建類股。

German fiscal expansion: The 2027 budget draft totals €555.4 billion in spending and €203.6 billion in borrowing, with defence rising from €82.2 billion to about €109 billion and a €500 billion infrastructure fund—beneficial for defence, industrial and construction stocks.

2. 獲利與估值支撐：UBS預估2027年EPS成長13%（市場共識約10%），目標750點；德意志銀行也看750點，認為預估本益比位在近年區間低檔，下檔空間有限。

Earnings and valuation support: UBS forecasts 2027 EPS growth of 13% (consensus ~10%) and a target of 750 points; Deutsche Bank also sees 750 points, noting that the projected P/E sits at the low end of recent ranges, limiting downside.

3. 科技股風險緩解：11/23黃仁勳回任輝達執行長、輝達單日大漲逾30%，DAX當天上漲2.15%、結束六連跌，STOXX 600單日漲幅是5月以來最大，半導體賣壓暫時緩和。

Tech-stock risk easing: On 11/23 Nvidia’s CEO Jensen Huang returned, sending Nvidia up more than 30% in a day; the DAX rose 2.15% that day, ending a six-day decline, and the STOXX 600 posted its biggest single-day gain since May, temporarily easing semiconductor selling pressure.

（二）壓抑走勢的三大風險

Three risks holding the market back

1. 歐美雙雙升息：歐元區10月整體通膨4.0%（三年新高）、核心2.6%，ECB今年已升息兩次；Fed與ECB可能在12月同步升息，加上美10年債殖利率約4.9%，壓抑估值。

Euro-US rate hikes: Euro-area inflation hit 4.0% in October (three-year high) with core at 2.6%; the ECB has already raised rates twice this year. A simultaneous Fed-ECB hike in December, combined with a U.S. 10-year yield near 4.9%, could suppress valuations.

2. 美國新關稅不確定：美國最高法院2月判對等關稅無效後，美方準備對歐盟等經濟體推出替代關稅（最快11/24公布，稅率未明），歐盟擔心97%對美出口可能被課稅，衝擊汽車、藥品與晶片。

Uncertainty over new US tariffs: After the U.S. Supreme Court ruled in February that equivalent tariffs are invalid, the U.S. is preparing alternative tariffs on the EU and other economies (announcement expected by 11/24, rate not disclosed). The EU fears 97% of its exports to the U.S. could be taxed, hitting autos, pharmaceuticals and chips.

3. 科技反彈未確認：輝達股價仍比10/6高點低約47%，董事會調查還在進行，有分析師稱這波是「死貓跳」；11/24半導體與原物料股回吐，STOXX 600收在632.7點。

Tech rebound unconfirmed: Nvidia’s price remains about 47% below its 10/6 high; the board investigation is ongoing, and some analysts label the move a “dead-cat bounce.” On 11/24 semiconductor and commodity stocks retreated, leaving the STOXX 600 at 632.7 points.

三、投資觀察指標

若要追蹤12月至2027年Q1的多空風向轉換，建議密切留意以下三個訊號：

To track the shift between bull and bear from December to Q1 2027, watch these three signals:

（一）央行會議結果：12/10 FOMC與12/17 ECB是否升息、ECB新經濟預測，以及2027/2 Lagarde卸任後新總裁的政策立場，會決定歐股估值上限。

Central-bank meeting outcomes: Whether the FOMC on 12/10 and the ECB on 12/17 raise rates, the ECB’s new economic forecasts, and the policy stance of the incoming ECB president after Lagarde’s 2027/2 departure will set the valuation ceiling for European equities.

（二）歐元與能源價格：歐元11/16跌破1.10、創19個月新低，11/24約在1.0965；伊朗戰爭影響下，油價與天然氣價格回落與否，直接影響ECB升息壓力與能源股表現。

Euro and energy prices: The euro fell below 1.10 on 11/16, hitting a 19-month low and was around 1.0965 on 11/24. Oil and natural-gas price movements amid the Iran conflict will directly affect ECB rate-hike pressure and energy-stock performance.

（三）指數技術位置：STOXX 600能否守住年線625.8（DAX年線24,954），重新站上650並挑戰11/13高點664.14（DAX 26,772），會決定是否轉回多頭格局。

Index technical levels: Whether the STOXX 600 can hold the yearly line at 625.8 (DAX yearly line 24,954), climb back above 650 and challenge the 11/13 high of 664.14 (DAX 26,772) will determine if the market reverts to a bullish stance.
