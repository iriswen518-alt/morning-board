一、階段走勢預測：關鍵節點

（一）12 月關鍵節點：11/26韓央行利率決策（34位經濟學家中32位預期升息1碼至3.25%）與12/10 FOMC是否升息；指數11/20–23兩日重挫8.36%，連三日觸發sidecar，11/24因黃仁勳回任大漲6.32%重回5,006點，11/25又跌回4,768點，短線仍以震盪探底看待。

December key points: BOK rate decision on 11/26 (32 of 34 economists expect a 0.25 ppt hike to 3.25%) and whether the 12/10 FOMC raises rates; the index fell 8.36% on 11/20-23, triggered side-car for three consecutive days, rebounded 6.32% on 11/24 after Jensen Huang’s return to lead, reaching 5,006 points, then slipped back to 4,768 points on 11/25 – short-term view remains a bottom-finding oscillation.

（二）2027 年 Q1 轉折：韓國投資證券預期Q4 2026見底後分段回升，NH證券看上半年強、下半年弱，大信證券則認為Q2較有利；16家券商2027年目標上緣介於7,500–10,500點，多落在8,000點區間，但需先收復60日線（6,115）與240日線（6,169）才算趨勢翻多。

2027 Q1 turning point: Korean investment houses expect a staged rise after a Q4 2026 bottom; NH Securities sees a strong first half and weaker second half, while Daishin Securities views Q2 as more favorable. Sixteen brokerages set 2027 targets between 7,500–10,500 points, most clustering around the 8,000-point range, but a trend reversal requires retaking the 60-day line (6,115) and the 240-day line (6,169).

二、多空核心邏輯分析

（一）支撐多頭的三大動能

Three drivers supporting the bulls

1. 評價跌至歷史低檔：KB證券指出10月底KOSPI預估本益比僅4.6倍，遠低於10年平均10.1倍；韓華引用預估ROE 25.5%、股價淨值比1.08倍，基本面與股價明顯背離。

Valuation at historic lows: KB Securities notes the KOSPI P/E forecast at the end of October is only 4.6×, far below the 10-year average of 10.1×; Hanwha cites an estimated ROE of 25.5% and a price-to-book of 1.08×, indicating a clear disconnect between fundamentals and price.

2. HBM需求與報價續強：TrendForce預估2027年HBM4價格每Gb約3–5.5美元，高於2026年約2.05美元；HBM佔三大記憶體廠DRAM晶圓投片比重將由23%升至約30%，SK海力士已鎖定逾半2027–28年產能合約。

HBM demand and pricing stay strong: TrendForce projects 2027 HBM4 prices at $3-5.5 per GB, up from about $2.05 in 2026; HBM’s share of total DRAM wafer starts will rise from 23% to roughly 30%, with SK Hynix already locking in more than half of its 2027-28 capacity contracts.

3. 政策護盤與券商目標：韓國11/22召開緊急F4會議強化證券市場穩定基金啟動機制；券商2027年目標多在8,000點區間，最保守的iM證券上緣7,500點，仍隱含逾50%上漲空間。

Policy support and broker targets: Korea held an emergency F4 meeting on 11/22 to strengthen the market-stabilization fund mechanism; most brokers set 2027 targets in the 8,000-point band, with the most conservative iM Securities capping at 7,500 points, still implying over 50% upside.

（二）壓抑走勢的三大風險

Three risks holding the market back

1. 外資撤出規模龐大：外資已連續七個月淨賣超KOSPI，累計約1,152億美元；11/2–11/20淨賣超約16.29兆韓元，其中三星電子與SK海力士佔大宗，指數高度集中兩檔權值股放大跌幅。

Foreign outflows are massive: Foreign investors have net-sold KOSPI for seven consecutive months, totaling about $115.2 billion; net sales from 11/2-11/20 were roughly 16.29 trillion KRW, dominated by Samsung Electronics and SK Hynix, amplifying the index’s exposure to these two heavyweight stocks.

2. AI投資獲利疑慮：輝達財報亮眼仍難穩住股價，加上美方可能加強對中晶片出口管制，AI供應鏈資金撤離；高盛將三星目標價由50萬下修至35萬韓元、SK海力士由350萬下修至200萬韓元。

AI investment profit doubts: Nvidia’s strong earnings have not steadied its share price, and potential tighter U.S. export controls on Chinese chips are prompting AI-supply-chain capital withdrawals; Goldman Sachs cut Samsung’s target price from 500,000 to 350,000 KRW and SK Hynix’s from 3.5 million to 2 million KRW.

3. 槓桿與利率雙壓：2026年融資餘額6/24創38.63兆韓元新高，斷頭賣壓與單一個股槓桿ETF放大波動；韓央行偏鷹、可能升息至3.25%甚至2027年3.75%，加上美國10年債殖利率約4.9%，壓抑股市評價。

Leverage and rate pressure: Margin balances hit a new high of 38.63 trillion KRW on 6/24 2026, fueling forced selling and magnifying volatility in single-stock leveraged ETFs; the BOK remains hawkish, possibly raising rates to 3.25% now and 3.75% in 2027, while U.S. 10-year yields sit around 4.9%, suppressing equity valuations.

三、投資觀察指標

若要追蹤12月至2027年Q1的多空風向轉換，建議密切留意以下三個訊號：

To track the shift between bull and bear from December to Q1 2027, watch these three signals:

（一）央行利率路徑：11/26韓央行決議與點陣圖、12/10 FOMC是否升息；若聯準會未升息且美債殖利率回落，有利外資回補韓股。

Central bank rate path: BOK decision and dot-plot on 11/26, and whether the 12/10 FOMC raises rates; if the Fed holds and U.S. yields retreat, foreign investors may start rebuilding Korean equity positions.

（二）外資買賣與韓元：觀察外資是否由連續賣超轉為買超（11/24單日買超7,392億韓元為正面訊號），以及美元兌韓元能否守穩1,340附近。

Foreign flow and KRW: Watch for a shift from net-selling to net-buying by foreigners (a single-day net buy of 7,392 billion KRW on 11/24 was a positive sign) and whether the USD/KRW rate can hold near 1,340.

（三）記憶體與AI訊號：三星、SK海力士HBM4出貨與合約進度、記憶體報價走勢，以及輝達與美國AI巨頭資本支出指引是否穩定。

Memory and AI signals: Track Samsung and SK Hynix HBM4 shipment and contract progress, memory price trends, and whether Nvidia and other U.S. AI giants keep their capex guidance stable.
