一、階段走勢預測：關鍵節點

（一）12 月關鍵節點：市場預期 12 月 FOMC 升息 1 碼的機率約 95%，焦點轉向主席華許是否暗示續升。標普 500 從 10/6 高點 7,819 回檔，11/20 收 6,910 低點後反彈，12 月先看能否站穩 240 日線 7,276。

December key points: The market estimates about a 95% chance of a 25 bp rate hike at the December FOMC, shifting focus to whether Chair Warsh hints at another increase. The S&P 500 fell from the October 6 high of 7,819, rebounded after the November 20 low of 6,910, and in December the focus is on holding above the 240-day line at 7,276.

（二）2027 年 Q1 轉折：路透 42 位策略師中位數預估 2027 年底標普 8,150、道瓊 53,800，顯示中期仍偏多；但 Q1 要通過升息效果、通膨降溫與 AI 獲利檢驗，若核心 PCE 回落，指數才有機會挑戰 7,800 前高。

2027 Q1 turning point: Reuters surveyed 42 strategists; the median forecast for year-end 2027 is an S&P 500 at 8,150 and a Dow at 53,800, indicating a medium-term bullish bias. However, Q1 must confirm the impact of rate hikes, cooling inflation, and AI earnings. Only if core PCE declines will the index have a chance to retest the prior high of 7,800.

二、多空核心邏輯分析

（一）支撐多頭的三大動能

Three drivers supporting the bulls

1. 獲利仍在成長：LSEG 估標普 500 獲利 2026 年成長 37.1%、2027 年再增 16.4%，經濟穩定加上獲利動能，是策略師看好 2027 年底 8,150 點的主要理由。

Earnings still growing: LSEG projects S&P 500 earnings to rise 37.1% in 2026 and another 16.4% in 2027. Stable economics and earnings momentum are the main reasons strategists are bullish on an 8,150-point target by year-end 2027.

2. AI 核心信心回補：黃仁勳回任輝達執行長，輝達單日大漲 31.5%、市值單日增加近 1 兆美元，那斯達克一天漲 6.51%，顯示 AI 龍頭基本面信心仍在，拉回時有買盤承接。

AI core confidence restored: Jensen Huang returns as NVIDIA CEO, NVIDIA jumps 31.5% in a day adding nearly $1 trillion in market cap, and Nasdaq climbs 6.51% in the same session, showing continued confidence in AI leaders and buying support on pullbacks.

3. 回檔已大幅修正：黃仁勳離職消息後標普 500 累計跌逾 9%，一度比高點低約 12%，三大指數進入技術性修正，部分過熱評價已被消化，中長線資金找到進場點。

Pullback already largely corrected: After Huang’s departure news, the S&P 500 fell more than 9%, briefly sitting about 12% below its peak. The three major indices entered a technical correction, digesting some over-heated valuations and giving medium-term capital entry points.

（二）壓抑走勢的三大風險

Three risks holding the market back

1. 聯準會轉向升息：通膨居高不下，市場押注 12 月升息 1 碼機率約 95%，甚至有小幅升 2 碼的預期；美債 10 年殖利率約 4.9%、2 年約 4.8%，資金成本高壓抑科技股評價。

Fed turning to rate hikes: Persistent inflation keeps the market betting on a ~95% chance of a 25 bp hike in December, with some speculation of a 50 bp move; 10-year Treasury yields around 4.9% and 2-year around 4.8% raise funding costs and suppress tech valuations.

2. 反彈主要是機械買盤：野村指出 11 月大反彈主因是 VIX 重挫、空頭回補與槓桿 ETF 再平衡（估被動買進約 410 億美元），不是基本面改善，隔天標普即跌 0.9%、那指跌 1.43%。

Rebound driven mainly by mechanical buying: Nomura notes the November rally was mainly due to a VIX plunge, short covering, and leveraged-ETF rebalancing (estimated passive buying of about $41 billion), not fundamental improvement. The S&P fell 0.9% and the Nasdaq 1.43% the next day.

3. 集中度與關稅風險：AI 巨頭佔標普 500 權重近一半，指數基金暗藏集中風險；美國擬對中國及數十國提高關稅的消息，也再度壓抑風險偏好。

Concentration and tariff risk: AI giants account for nearly half of the S&P 500 weight, embedding concentration risk in index funds; U.S. plans to raise tariffs on China and dozens of other countries also dampen risk appetite.

三、投資觀察指標

若要追蹤12月至2027年Q1的多空風向轉換，建議密切留意以下三個訊號：

To track the shift between bull and bear from December to Q1 2027, watch these three signals:

（一）通膨數據走向：10 月核心 PCE 市場預估年增約 3.2%（9 月 3.1%），之後 11 月 CPI、PCE 若續升，將推高 2027 年續升息預期，對成長股壓力最大。

Inflation data trajectory: Market expects October core PCE to rise about 3.2% year-over-year (vs. 3.1% in September). If November CPI and PCE continue to rise, expectations for further 2027 rate hikes will increase, pressuring growth stocks the most.

（二）FOMC 與點陣圖：12 月會議除了升息幅度，更要看點陣圖與華許記者會是否暗示 2027 年還要升，以及 10 年債殖利率能否守在 5% 以下。

FOMC and dot-plot: In December the focus will be not only on the size of any hike but also on the dot-plot and Warsh’s press conference for clues on whether 2027 will see additional hikes, and whether the 10-year yield can stay below 5%.

（三）技術關卡與 AI 股：標普 500 看 240 日線 7,276 點能否站穩、下檔 6,910 低點是否守住；那斯達克看 240 日線 24,868 點，以及輝達等 AI 股能否止穩不再大起大落。

Technical levels and AI stocks: Watch whether the S&P 500 can hold the 240-day line at 7,276 and defend the low of 6,910; monitor Nasdaq’s 240-day line at 24,868 and see if AI stocks like NVIDIA can stabilize without large swings.
