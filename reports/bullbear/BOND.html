一、階段走勢預測：關鍵節點

（一）12 月關鍵節點：12/10 FOMC 市場已完全計價至少升息1碼，華勒曾提2碼。10月CPI公布後10年期一度站上5%、11/13達5.15%；11/24回落至約4.9%。重點看升息幅度與點陣圖是否暗示升息延續至2027年。

December key points: The 12/10 FOMC market has fully priced in at least a 25 bp hike, and Waller once floated 50 bp. After the October CPI release, the 10-year briefly rose above 5% and hit 5.15% on 11/13, then fell to about 4.9% on 11/24. Focus on whether the hike size and the dot-plot suggest rate hikes continuing into 2027

（二）2027 年 Q1 轉折：彭博調查經濟學家預期12月與3月各升息一次。若核心PCE從3%以上回落，殖利率有機會向4.6%靠近；若通膨續黏、AI與財政發債再擴大，10年期恐重回5%以上。機構2027年底預測介於4.6%（高盛）至5.0%（大摩）。

2027 Q1 turning point: Bloomberg surveys show economists expect one hike in December and another in March. If core PCE falls back below 3%, yields could move toward 4.6%; if inflation stays sticky, AI-driven spending and fiscal borrowing expand, the 10-year could climb back above 5%. Institutions forecast end-2027 yields between 4.6% (Goldman) and 5.0% (Morgan Stanley)

二、多空核心邏輯分析

（一）支撐債市多頭（殖利率下行）的三大動能

Three drivers for a bond rally (lower yields)

1. 利率已在高檔：10年期約4.9%，10月初創24年新高後回落，且遠高於240日均線4.54%，已大幅反映升息預期，高殖利率提供的票息收益可抵禦價格波動。

Rates already high: The 10-year is around 4.9%, after hitting a 24-year high in early October and then retreating, well above its 240-day average of 4.54%. The high yield already reflects rate-hike expectations, and the coupon income from high yields can offset price volatility

2. 避險買盤回流：科技股因輝達執行長異動劇烈震盪，股市漲勢一轉弱，債市即走揚；11/24油價下跌也緩解通膨疑慮，10年期一度下跌7個基點至4.89%。

Safe-haven buying returns: Tech stocks wobble after Nvidia’s CEO change, weakening equity momentum and lifting bonds; the 11/24 oil-price drop eased inflation concerns, briefly pulling the 10-year down 7 bps to 4.89%

3. 升息終點漸近：9月啟動三年來首次升息，12月再升後市場預期3月接近終點；財長貝森特警告過度緊縮會傷經濟，一旦經濟轉弱，長天期債券價格反彈空間大。

Rate-hike end approaching: September started the first hike in three years; a December hike leads the market to expect the cycle to near its end by March. Treasury Secretary Bessent warned that overtightening could hurt the economy, and a weakening economy would give long-term bonds ample room to rebound

（二）壓抑債市（殖利率上行）的三大風險

Three risks for bonds (higher yields)

1. 通膨黏著不退：核心CPI連三個月月增0.3%，10月核心PCE預期由3.1%升至3.2%，高盛資產管理認為升息週期可能延續至2027年，短債殖利率持續承壓。

Inflation remains sticky: Core CPI has risen 0.3% month-over-month for three consecutive months; October core PCE is expected to rise from 3.1% to 3.2%. Goldman Sachs Asset Management sees the rate-hike cycle extending into 2027, keeping short-term yields under pressure

2. 財政供給壓力：FY2026赤字1.992兆美元，FY2027預估約1.96至2.06兆美元；TD證券估11月至明年1月淨票券供給達6,780億美元，2027年標售規模調整也受關注。

Fiscal supply pressure: FY2026 deficit is $1.992 trillion, with FY2027 projected at $1.96-$2.06 trillion. TD Securities estimates net bond supply of $678 billion from November through January, and the 2027 auction size is also under scrutiny

3. AI債務擴張：Fed報告指AI相關借款佔非金融投資級發債31%，高盛估2027年超大型科技業發債達4,400億美元，推升期限溢價；超大型科技業信用利差今年擴大約30個基點。

AI-driven debt expansion: The Fed reports AI-related borrowing accounts for 31% of non-financial investment-grade issuance. Goldman projects 2027 ultra-large tech issuances at $440 billion, pushing term premium higher; credit spreads for ultra-large tech have widened about 30 bps this year

三、投資觀察指標

若要追蹤12月至2027年Q1的多空風向轉換，建議密切留意以下三個訊號：

To track the shift between bull and bear from December to Q1 2027, watch these three signals:

（一）FOMC 升息幅度：12/10 會議升1碼或2碼、點陣圖與鮑爾談話是否暗示2027年持續升息，將直接決定2年期與10年期殖利率方向及曲線形狀。

FOMC hike magnitude: Whether the 12/10 meeting raises rates by 25 bp or 50 bp, and whether the dot-plot and Powell’s remarks signal continued hikes into 2027, will directly shape the direction of 2-year and 10-year yields and the curve shape

（二）PCE 與 CPI：每月核心PCE與核心CPI月增率能否降至0.3%以下，是殖利率由空轉多的最關鍵訊號；11/25公布的10月PCE為首要觀察。

PCE vs. CPI: The ability of monthly core PCE and core CPI growth to fall below 0.3% is the most critical signal for a shift from bearish to bullish yields; the October PCE released on 11/25 is the primary data point

（三）信用利差變化：CoreWeave 2032年債殖利率已逾16%，觀察AI高收益債壓力是否外溢至投資級；PIMCO認為資料中心建設放緩後AI債利差可能收斂，攸關債券基金配置。

Credit spread changes: CoreWeave’s 2032 bond yield has already exceeded 16%; watch whether AI high-yield stress spills into investment-grade. PIMCO believes AI spreads may compress after data-center construction slows, a key factor for bond-fund allocations
