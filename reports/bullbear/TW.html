一、階段走勢預測：關鍵節點

（一）12 月關鍵節點：指數 10/6 收 49,823 點創高後，11 月受黃仁勳請辭風波與 Fed 升息疑慮連殺，11/20 收 36,033 點、距高點回檔約 28%；11/24 黃仁勳回任激勵單日大漲 3,310 點（史上最大漲點）收 40,122 點，但外資仍連 7 日賣超近 2,980 億元，預期 12 月在年線（約 39,500 點）上下反覆震盪。

December key points: After the index closed at a high of 49,823 on Oct 6, November was hit by the Jensen Huang resignation saga and Fed-rate-hike concerns, dropping to 36,033 on Nov 20 – about a 28% pullback from the peak; on Nov 24 Huang’s return sparked a single-day surge of 3,310 points (the largest daily jump on record) to close at 40,122, yet foreign investors continued a 7-day net sell of roughly NT$298 billion. Expect December to oscillate around the 240-day moving average (≈ 39,500 points).

（二）2027 年 Q1 轉折：群益投顧估 2027 年區間 35,000–50,000 點、第一金投顧看 U 型走勢（低點 36,000、高點 55,000），法人普遍認為修正已消化部分評價，Q1 隨財報與外資回補有望回升。

2027 Q1 turning point: Capital Investment Advisory estimates a 2027 range of 35,000–50,000 points, First Capital Investment Advisory sees a U-shape (low 36,000, high 55,000). Institutions generally believe valuation adjustments have already absorbed part of the premium, so Q1 could rebound with earnings releases and foreign-institution net buying.

二、多空核心邏輯分析

（一）支撐多頭的三大動能

Three drivers supporting the bulls

1. 估值修正提供安全邊際：指數自 10 月高點回檔一度近 28%，權值股本益比明顯回落；凱基投顧以 18–19 倍本益比推算 2027 年合理區間約 47,455–50,092 點，現價具折價空間。

Valuation correction provides a safety margin: The index has retraced nearly 28% from its October high, and the P/E of weighted-stock components has fallen noticeably; KGI projects a 2027 fair range of about 47,455–50,092 points using an 18–19 × earnings multiple, indicating discount potential at current levels.

2. AI 供應鏈獲利動能延續：黃仁勳回任輝達執行長，化解領導層不確定性；台新投顧預估台灣 50 成分股 2027 年獲利成長上看 31.6%，AI 伺服器、先進製程與封裝仍是主要成長引擎。

AI supply-chain profit momentum continues: Jensen Huang’s return as NVIDIA CEO removes leadership uncertainty; Taishin forecasts 31.6% profit growth for Taiwan’s 50-stock index constituents in 2027, with AI servers, advanced process and packaging remaining primary growth engines.

3. 內資承接與年底行情：外資賣超期間投信持續買超（11/24 買超 84 億元）；12 月外資放長假、投信作帳，歷史統計台股 12 月上漲機率約七成。

Domestic buying and year-end rally: During foreign-institution net selling, domestic investment trusts kept buying (NT$84 billion net buy on Nov 24); with foreign investors on a long holiday in December and window dressing by domestic investment trusts, historical data show about a 70% chance of a December rise in the Taiwan market.

（二）壓抑走勢的三大風險

Three risks holding the market back

1. 外資撤出與期貨空單：11 月外資累計賣超逾 8,000 億元，台指期淨空單逾 10 萬口，新台幣走貶，若空單未回補，反彈易遇賣壓。

Foreign-institution outflow and futures shorts: In November foreign investors sold over NT$800 billion net, Taiwan-index futures held more than 100,000 short contracts, and the NT$ weakened; if shorts are not covered, rebounds could face selling pressure.

2. 美國利率與 Fed 轉鷹：美 10 年債殖利率約 4.9%、2 年債約 4.8%，市場擔憂 Fed 12 月升息，壓縮科技股評價；輝達財報優於預期股價仍走跌，顯示利多鈍化。

US rates and a hawkish Fed: 10-year US Treasury yield around 4.9%, 2-year about 4.8%; markets fear a December Fed hike, compressing tech valuations; NVIDIA’s earnings beat did not lift the stock, showing diminishing upside.

3. 籌碼與情緒劇烈波動：單日漲跌皆逾 2,000 點，融資多殺多後量能萎縮（11/24 成交值 4,194 億元創今年新低），加上 AI 資本支出回本疑慮，市場易受單一消息左右。

Choppy volume and sentiment swings: Daily moves exceed 2,000 points, margin-trading volume shrank after a sell-off (Nov 24 turnover NT$419.4 billion, a yearly low), and doubts over AI cap-ex payback make the market vulnerable to single-news shocks.

三、投資觀察指標

若要追蹤12月至2027年Q1的多空風向轉換，建議密切留意以下三個訊號：

To track the shift between bull and bear from December to Q1 2027, watch these three signals:

（一）外資現貨買賣超與台指期淨空單：外資何時止賣、期貨淨空單能否明顯回補，搭配指數能否站穩年線（約 39,500 點），為底部確立的關鍵訊號。

Foreign-institution spot net flow and futures net shorts: Timing of the end to foreign selling and whether futures shorts are materially covered, together with the index holding above the 240-day moving average (≈ 39,500 points), will signal bottom formation.

（二）12 月 10 日 FOMC 決議與新台幣匯率：若 Fed 釋出暫停升息訊號、新台幣止貶回升，有利外資回流電子權值股，啟動年底至元月行情。

Dec 10 FOMC decision and NT$ exchange rate: If the Fed signals a pause in hikes and the NT$ stops depreciating, foreign capital may flow back into electronic heavyweight stocks, kick-starting a rally from year-end into January.

（三）台積電 11 月營收與 2027 年 1 月法說會：檢視 AI 需求延續性與 2027 年資本支出指引，是決定 Q1 行情方向與權值股評價的核心依據。

TSMC November revenue and Jan 2027 earnings call: Monitoring AI demand persistence and the 2027 cap-ex guidance will be central to shaping Q1 market direction and heavyweight stock valuations.
