一、階段走勢預測：關鍵節點

（一）12 月關鍵節點：12/9 FOMC 若升 1 碼屬預期內，Waller 提 2 碼才是上檔風險；12/17 歐央、12/17–18 台灣央行理監事會（重貼現率 2%、10 月 CPI 2.33%）是否升息，加上央行守 33 元，決定新台幣貶幅。

December key points: If the 12/9 FOMC raises rates by 1 bp as expected, a 50 bp hike (floated by Waller) is the upside risk; the ECB decision on 12/17 and the Taiwan Central Bank board meeting on 12/17-18 (discount rate 2%, Oct CPI 2.33%) will determine whether rates rise and, together with the central bank defending 33 TWD, set the size of TWD depreciation.

（二）2027 年 Q1 轉折：摩根大通預估 Fed 1、3 月續升至 4.50%–4.75%，若成真美元撐在高檔；MUFG 則看 12 月後不再升息、日銀升至 2.00%，美元年內走弱。外資是否回補台股決定新台幣能否回到 32 元以下。

2027 Q1 turning point: JPM forecasts the Fed will raise rates again in Jan and Mar to 4.50%-4.75%; if that happens the USD stays high. MUFG expects no more hikes after Dec and a BOJ rate of 2.00%, leading to USD weakness later this year. Foreign-capital re-entry into Taiwanese equities will decide if TWD can fall back below 32.

二、多空核心邏輯分析

（一）支撐美元走強的三大動能

Three drivers for a stronger US dollar

1. Fed 轉向升息：美國通膨黏著、10 月會議紀錄顯示幾乎所有官員預期續升息，市場押注 12 月升息機率約九成五，美 2 年債 4.8%、10 年債 4.9% 撐起利差優勢。

Fed shifts to hikes: Inflation remains sticky, the Oct meeting minutes show almost all officials expect further hikes; the market assigns about 95% probability to a Dec hike. US 2-yr yields at 4.8% and 10-yr at 4.9% support the yield advantage.

2. 技術面突破：DXY 11/24 收 103.87，創 2025 年 4 月以來新高，站穩 240 日線 99.75 之上，今年以來 +5.7%，多頭旗形突破後動能延續。

Technical breakout: DXY closed at 103.87 on 11/24, a new high since Apr 2025, staying above the 240-day line at 99.75. YTD up 5.7%, momentum continues after a bullish flag breakout.

3. 外資匯出新台幣：11 月外資賣超台股逾 8,000 億元、近一月淨匯出約百億美元，加上股利匯出與壽險避險比率降至約 45%，美元兌新台幣逼近 33 元。

Foreign capital pulling out of NT dollars: In Nov foreign investors sold over TWD 8 trillion of equities, net outflow of roughly USD 100 billion in the past month, combined with dividend repatriation and life-insurance hedging ratio falling to about 45%, pushing USD/TWD toward 33.

（二）壓抑美元的三大風險

Three risks for the US dollar

1. 升息循環近尾聲：MUFG 預期 12 月後 Fed 不再升息、ING 看 DXY 2027 年底回落至約 97，一旦升息定價見頂，美元易出現獲利回吐。

Rate-hike cycle near end: MUFG expects the Fed to stop hiking after Dec, ING projects DXY falling to around 97 by end-2027; once rate-hike pricing peaks, the USD may see profit-taking.

2. 日圓歐元回升：MUFG 預估日銀 12 月升息並於 2027 年再升兩次至 2.00%，美元兌日圓由約 157 回落至 149，歐元兌美元由 1.12 升至 1.18，壓抑美元指數。

Yen and euro rebound: MUFG forecasts a BOJ hike in Dec and two more hikes to 2.00% by 2027, moving USD/JPY from ~157 back to 149 and EUR/USD from 1.12 up to 1.18, dampening the Dollar Index.

3. 央行守住 33 元：台灣央行 10 月起每週至少進場調節，尾盤屢守 33 元；外匯局指外資賣超已縮小、出口商結售美元增加，若 12 月升息將緩和新台幣貶勢。

Central bank defends NT$33: Taiwan’s central bank has been intervening weekly since Oct, repeatedly holding the rate at 33 in late trading; the foreign exchange bureau notes that foreign-sell pressure has eased and exporters are buying more USD. A Dec rate hike would ease TWD depreciation.

三、投資觀察指標

若要追蹤12月至2027年Q1的多空風向轉換，建議密切留意以下三個訊號：

To track the shift between bull and bear from December to Q1 2027, watch these three signals:

（一）FOMC 升息幅度：12/9 會議升 1 碼或 2 碼、點陣圖是否暗示 2027 年 1、3 月續升，直接影響美元高點是否延續至 Q1。

FOMC hike magnitude: Whether the 12/9 meeting raises rates by 1 bp or 2 bp, and whether the dot-plot hints at further hikes in Jan and Mar 2027, will directly affect whether the USD peak carries into Q1.

（二）日銀與歐央動向：12 月日銀是否升息、12/17 歐央是否維持 2.50%，以及法國政治風險，決定日圓與歐元能否反攻美元。

BOJ and ECB moves: Whether the BOJ hikes in Dec, whether the ECB keeps policy at 2.50% on 12/17, and French political risk, will determine if the yen and euro can retake ground against the USD.

（三）外資與台灣央行：外資賣超是否收斂轉買、12/17–18 理監事會是否升息、央行 33 元防線與壽險避險比率變化，決定美元兌新台幣走向。

Foreign-capital and central bank: Whether foreign-sell pressure turns into buying, the outcome of the 12/17-18 board meeting on rate hikes, the 33 TWD defense line, and changes in life-insurance hedging ratios will shape the USD/TWD trajectory.
