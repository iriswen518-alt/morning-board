一、階段走勢預測：關鍵節點

（一）12 月關鍵節點：Fed 12/8–9 若升息，日銀 12/17–18 升至 1.5%（路透調查 86% 經濟學家預期）後美日利差不變，日圓仍貼近 160；指數 11/20 曾單日重挫 5.06% 至 56,437 點，56,000 點附近為短線支撐，240 日線 60,455 點為上檔壓力。

December key points: If the Fed raises rates on 12/8–9, and the BoJ lifts to 1.5% on 12/17–18 (86% of economists in Reuters poll expect this), the US-Japan rate spread stays unchanged and the yen remains near 160; the index fell 5.06% in a single day on 11/20 to 56,437 points, with the 56,000 level acting as short-term support and the 240-day line at 60,455 points serving as upper resistance.

（二）2027 年 Q1 轉折：近六成經濟學家預期日銀 2027 年 3 月底前升至 1.75%，終端利率看法分歧於 1.75%–2%；野村 11/9 將 2027 年末目標由 73,000 下修至 66,000 點，大和預估 2027 年前半刷新 72,366 點高點，關鍵在 AI 投資疑慮是否消退。

2027 Q1 turning point: About 60% of economists expect the BoJ to raise rates to 1.75% by the end of March 2027, with terminal rate views ranging 1.75%–2%; Nomura cut its 2027 year-end target on 11/9 from 73,000 to 66,000 points, while Daiwa forecasts a new high of 72,366 points in the first half of 2027, hinging on whether AI investment concerns dissipate.

二、多空核心邏輯分析

（一）支撐多頭的三大動能

Three drivers supporting the bulls

1. 黃仁勳回任激勵：Nvidia 董事會 11/23 決議讓黃仁勳回任執行長，11/24 日經大漲 3.91% 至 58,644 點，愛德萬測試漲 14.1%、東京威力科創漲 10.5%，顯示 AI 鏈仍有強勁買盤。

Jensen Huang reinstated: Nvidia board resolution on 11/23 to reinstall Jensen Huang as CEO, followed by a 3.91% jump in the Nikkei to 58,644 points on 11/24; Advantest rose 14.1% and Tokyo Electron surged 10.5%, indicating strong buying in the AI chain.

2. 法人中長期看多：野村主情境 2026 年末 62,000、2027 年 6 月底 65,000 點；大和看 2027 年末 80,000 點、2027 年前半創新高；三菱 UFJ 投信、岩井 Cosmo 等看 2027 年 3 月底 75,000 點。

Institutional medium-term bullishness: Nomura’s main scenario projects 62,000 points by end-2026 and 65,000 points by end-June 2027; Daiwa sees 80,000 points by end-2027 and a new high in the first half of 2027; Mitsubishi UFJ Trust, Iwai Cosmo and others target 75,000 points by end-March 2027.

3. 薪資通膨良性循環：路透調查預估 2027 年春鬥加薪約 5.00%，與今年 5.01% 相近；日銀升息反映經濟正常化，加上 83% 受訪者認為股市震盪不影響升息，有利內需與金融股。

Wage-inflation virtuous cycle: Reuters poll estimates a spring 2027 wage increase of about 5.00%, close to this year’s 5.01%; BoJ rate hikes reflect economic normalization, and 83% of respondents say market volatility does not affect rate hikes, benefiting domestic demand and financial stocks.

（二）壓抑走勢的三大風險

Three risks holding the market back

1. AI 股集中風險：11/20 軟銀集團跌 14.5%、愛德萬跌 10.3%、東京威力跌 7.1%，三檔即占指數跌幅約 44%；11/25 愛德萬再跌逾 12%，日經價格加權放大高價科技股的拖累。

Concentration risk in AI stocks: On 11/20 SoftBank Group fell 14.5%, Advantest dropped 10.3% and Tokyo Electron slipped 7.1%, together accounting for roughly 44% of the index decline; on 11/25 Advantest fell another 12%, amplifying the drag from high-priced tech stocks in the Nikkei price-weighted index.

2. 美日同步緊縮：美國通膨黏著、市場預期 Fed 12 月升息，美 10 年債殖利率約 4.9%；日銀同步升息至 1.5%，日本 10 年債殖利率已達 2.87%，壓縮股市評價（野村已下修 PER 假設）。

US-Japan synchronized tightening: Sticky US inflation and market expectations of a Fed hike in December keep the 10-year Treasury yield around 4.9%; the BoJ simultaneously raises rates to 1.5%, pushing Japan’s 10-year yield to 2.87% and compressing equity valuations (Nomura has already lowered its PER assumptions).

3. 技術面空頭格局：指數 57,105 點，自 6/25 高點 72,366 點回落逾 2 成，跌破 240 日線 60,455 與 60 日線 64,192 點；野村悲觀情境 2026 年末 50,000、2027 年末 51,000 點。

Technical bearish pattern: The index at 57,105 points has fallen more than 20% from the 6/25 peak of 72,366 points, breaking below the 240-day line at 60,455 and the 60-day line at 64,192; Nomura’s pessimistic scenario projects 50,000 points by end-2026 and 51,000 points by end-2027.

三、投資觀察指標

若要追蹤12月至2027年Q1的多空風向轉換，建議密切留意以下三個訊號：

To track the shift between bull and bear from December to Q1 2027, watch these three signals:

（一）美日利率會議：Fed 12/8–9 是否升息、日銀 12/17–18 升至 1.5% 後對終端利率的指引；若指引模糊，日圓賣壓可能再起，反之若鷹派過頭則壓抑股市評價。

US-Japan rate meetings: Whether the Fed raises rates on 12/8–9 and the BoJ lifts to 1.5% on 12/17–18 will guide terminal rate expectations; ambiguous guidance could revive yen-selling pressure, while an overly hawkish stance may suppress equity valuations.

（二）日圓 160 關卡：美元兌日圓 11 月以來在 159 附近窄幅盤整，財務大臣片山 11/13 示警並表示將與美國財政部協調；有效突破 160 恐引發干預，日圓急升將衝擊出口股。

Yen 160 barrier: USD/JPY has been trading narrowly around 159 since November; Finance Minister Katayama warned on 11/13 and said coordination with the US Treasury is underway; a decisive break above 160 could trigger intervention, and a rapid yen rise would hurt export-related stocks.

（三）AI 半導體動向：觀察 Nvidia 管理層穩定度、AI 資本支出與融資疑慮，以及愛德萬、東京威力、軟銀集團能否止跌；若站回 240 日線 60,455 點，才算脫離空頭結構。

AI semiconductor outlook: Monitor Nvidia management stability, AI capex and financing concerns, and whether Advantest, Tokyo Electron and SoftBank Group can halt their declines; a rebound above the 240-day line at 60,455 points would signal exit from the bearish structure.
