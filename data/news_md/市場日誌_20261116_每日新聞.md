### 🏭 Industry / 產業動態

- **Foxconn posts record third-quarter profit on AI server boom** — Hon Hai (Foxconn) reported third-quarter EPS of NT$5.51, up from NT$4.27 in Q2 and NT$4.15 a year earlier, with revenue of about NT$3.02 trillion, up 46% year on year. Cloud and networking products rose to 54% of sales. Management guided for high double-digit sequential growth in AI rack shipments in the fourth quarter and into 2027.
  **鴻海Q3每股賺5.51元創高 AI伺服器成長引擎** — 鴻海公布第三季每股稅後盈餘5.51元，優於第二季4.27元與去年同期4.15元；營收約3.02兆元、年增46%，雲端網路產品占比升至54%，成最大成長動能。公司預期第四季AI機櫃出貨將季增高雙位數，並看好2027年延續強勁，顯示AI基建需求推升代工龍頭營運續攀高峰。
  - 來源 / Source：[壹蘋新聞網](https://news.nextapple.com/finance/20261112/D566D07E5C054836DE510FB38F6781C9)
- **Foxconn's ASIC AI servers outgrow GPU shipments** — Foxconn said third-quarter ASIC AI server shipments jumped about 1.5 times year on year, outpacing roughly 70% growth in GPU-based AI servers, as custom-silicon demand accelerates. The company cautioned that some GPU and ASIC racks ship on a consignment basis, so recognized revenue growth may lag unit shipments. The trend underscores a broadening AI hardware cycle extending beyond GPUs.
  **鴻海ASIC AI伺服器出貨增1.5倍 動能快過GPU** — 鴻海表示第三季ASIC AI伺服器出貨量年增約1.5倍，高於GPU AI伺服器約七成的增幅，反映客製化晶片需求加速。公司提醒部分GPU與ASIC機櫃採寄售模式，認列營收增幅可能低於出貨增幅。此趨勢顯示AI硬體週期正由GPU向ASIC擴散，台廠在客製化AI方案的佈局有望帶來新成長曲線。

The build-up cushions insurers against currency swings but weighs on reported earnings, keeping capital resilience in focus as regulators monitor the sector's buffers.

- **Regulators press ahead with IFRS 17 and new solvency regime** — The Financial Supervisory Commission is pushing forward the parallel adoption of IFRS 17 and the Insurance Capital Standard (ICS), the industry's long-flagged accounting overhaul, with a multi-year transition designed to let insurers shore up capital, reprice products and adjust investments. The shift is reshaping product mix away from long-dated guaranteed policies and sharpening the focus on insurers' balance-sheet strength.
  **金管會續推IFRS 17與ICS新制 壽險體質重塑** — 金管會持續推動保險業IFRS 17與ICS（保險資本標準）接軌，並規劃多年過渡期，讓業者得以補足資本、調整商品與投資配置。此一被業界稱為「會計大魔王」的新制，正促使長年期保證型保單占比下降，並讓外界更聚焦壽險業的資產負債表強度與清償能力。

### 💎 Wealth Management / 財富管理

- **High-asset wealth management AUM tops NT$2 trillion, up about 50%** — Assets under management in Taiwan's high-net-worth wealth management scheme surged past NT$2.07 trillion, a year-on-year gain of roughly 50%, as banks expand private-banking platforms, overseas stock and ETF access and structured offerings for wealthy clients. The rapid growth highlights intensifying competition among domestic banks to capture affluent families' cross-asset, cross-border allocations.
  **高資產財管規模突破2兆元 年增約50%** — 國內高資產財富管理業務的管理資產規模（AUM）衝破2兆767億元、年增約50.63%，各銀行積極擴充私人銀行平台、國外股票與ETF投資管道及結構型商品，搶攻高資產客群。此一爆發性成長凸顯本土銀行競逐高資產家族跨資產、跨境配置商機的激烈程度。
  - 來源 / Source：[經濟日報](https://money.udn.com/money/story/5613/9221671)
- **Banks revamp private-banking tiers to court the ultra-wealthy** — Domestic banks are overhauling their wealth-management membership structures, with several adding dedicated private-banking tiers for clients holding NT$100 million or more and rolling out active investment advisory services. The push toward "wealth governance" and family-office-style planning reflects a shift from single-product selling to integrated, multi-generational wealth strategies amid higher funding costs and complex global markets.
  **銀行改版私銀分級 搶攻超高資產客群** — 國內銀行紛紛改版財富管理會員制度，多家新增資產達新台幣一億元以上適用的「私人銀行會員」，並推出主動式投資諮詢服務。此波朝「財富治理」與家族辦公室式規劃的轉向，反映在資金成本上升、全球市場複雜化之下，財管業務正由單一商品銷售走向整合性、跨世代的財富傳承策略。
