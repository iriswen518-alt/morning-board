### 💎 Wealth Management / 財富管理

- **Taiwan's wealth-management pool swells to NT$36tn, led by ETFs and the ultra-rich** — Taiwan's wealth-management assets have grown from under NT$20tn to NT$36tn in six years, spanning funds, discretionary accounts, bank trusts, high-net-worth services and insurance. Onshore funds jumped from NT$4.86tn to NT$11.38tn in three years, with ETFs alone at NT$7.4tn after 220% growth, reshaping how households and private banks allocate capital.
  **台灣財富管理規模衝上36兆 ETF與高資產領軍** — 台灣財富管理規模6年內從不到20兆成長到36兆元，涵蓋基金、全權委託、銀行信託、高資產業務與保險。境內基金3年由4.86兆增至11.38兆，其中ETF一項就達7.4兆、3年成長220%，正重塑家庭與私銀的資產配置邏輯，也帶動業者競逐財管與傳承商機。
  - 來源 / Source：[財富管理趨勢分析](https://xmy.tw/articles/taiwan-wealth-management-2026)
- **High-net-worth ranks surge as NT$1bn-plus clients nearly triple** — Taiwan's high-net-worth population rose by 25,000 in two years, with the NT$1bn-plus tier nearly tripling and its assets ballooning from NT$4.5tn to NT$18.7tn. Insurance premiums reached NT$2.62tn in life and NT$286.8bn in non-life, with banks selling NT$405.3bn through their channels, underscoring insurance and trusts as core vehicles for asset allocation and wealth succession.
