### 🏭 Industry / 產業動態

- **Nanya lifts 2026 capex 34% to NT$69.7bn for 5A fab** — Nanya Technology's board on Aug 5 raised its 2026 capex ceiling to NT$69.7bn from NT$52bn, a 34% jump, to accelerate its 5A DRAM plant. The wider 5A project carries a NT$346.6bn four-year budget targeting 35,900 wafers/month by 2029 using 10nm-class EUV nodes — a decisive bet on the AI-driven memory upcycle.
  **南亞科上調資本支出 34% 至 697 億衝刺 5A 新廠** — 南亞科董事會 8/5 決議將 2026 年資本支出上限由 520 億元上調至 697 億元、增幅 34%，加速 5A 新廠建置。5A 新廠四年資本支出上限達 3466 億元，導入 10 奈米級 EUV 製程，目標 2029 年月投片 35,900 片，反映其押注 AI 帶動的記憶體上升循環。
  - 來源 / Source：[鉅亨網](https://news.cnyes.com/news/id/6561041)
- **CoWoS capacity to grow ~67% by end-2026 on AI packaging demand** — TSMC's CoWoS advanced-packaging capacity is projected to rise from about 70,000–75,000 wafers/month at end-2025 to 100,000–105,000 by end-2026, roughly a 67% gain. With CoWoS-L outsourcing running near 50%, OSAT players such as ASE stand to benefit as the industry pivots to solving power and thermal limits in AI chips.
  **CoWoS 產能 2026 年底估增 67% 先進封裝供應鏈受惠** — 台積電 CoWoS 先進封裝月產能預估由 2025 年底約 7～7.5 萬片，成長至 2026 年底 10～10.5 萬片、年增約 67%。因 CoWoS-L 等外包比重約五成，日月光投控等封測廠可望受惠；業界焦點亦轉向以高壓直流與面板級封裝解決 AI 晶片的電力與散熱瓶頸。

### 🏦 Financial Sector / 金融業

- **Financial trio earns record NT$904.2bn in H1 2026** — Taiwan's banks, insurers and securities/futures firms earned a combined NT$904.2bn in the first half of 2026, up 139% year on year and a record for the period. Banks alone posted pre-tax profit of NT$400.2bn and insurers NT$309.4bn, both first-half records, leaving the full-year result on course to challenge an all-time high.
  **金融三業上半年大賺 9042 億 創同期新高** — 銀行、保險、證券期貨三業 2026 年上半年合計獲利 9042.45 億元、年增 139%，創歷年同期新高。其中銀行業稅前盈餘 4002.2 億元、保險業 3094 億元同創同期新高。獲利動能強勁，全年有望挑戰歷史紀錄，並支撐金融股評價與股利發放預期。
  - 來源 / Source：[經濟日報](https://money.udn.com/money/story/5613/9666289)
- **FSC tightens disclosure on interest-sensitive insurance policies** — The FSC is rolling out new disclosure rules for interest-sensitive (利變型) insurance products to stop consumers treating them like time deposits and to curb mis-selling disputes. Insurers must present return and risk information more clearly at the point of sale, a move aimed at protecting policyholders and improving the quality of the fast-growing savings-type policy market.
  **金管會推利變保單資訊揭露新制 防當定存** — 金管會針對利率變動型保險商品推出資訊揭露新制，避免保戶將利變保單誤當定存、引發銷售爭議。新制要求業者於銷售時更清楚揭露報酬與風險資訊，強化保戶保護並提升儲蓄型保單市場品質，對壽險通路銷售行為將形成規範。
