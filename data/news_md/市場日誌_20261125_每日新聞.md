### 🏭 Industry / 產業動態

- **TSMC says global AI demand keeps advanced-node capacity tight** — TSMC took the unusual step of publicly addressing AI-bubble concerns, stressing that global AI demand keeps growing and that its advanced-process capacity remains in short supply. The remarks aim to reassure investors and reinforce expectations of sustained orders for leading-edge nodes and CoWoS packaging that anchor Taiwan's semiconductor supply chain into 2027.
  **台積電罕見發聲：AI需求強勁 先進製程供不應求** — 面對市場對AI泡沫的疑慮，台積電罕見公開說明，強調全球AI需求持續成長，先進製程產能仍供不應求。此番表態意在安撫投資人信心，也印證先進節點與CoWoS封裝訂單能見度延續，為台灣半導體供應鏈後續動能定調，對設備、材料與封測族群亦提供正面指引，惟仍須留意終端需求變化。
  - 來源 / Source：[聯合新聞網](https://udn.com/news/story/7251/9897512)
- **Hon Hai doubles AI server rack capacity; MediaTek lifts ASIC outlook** — Hon Hai's weekly AI server rack capacity has doubled to about 2,000 racks, while its Q3 ASIC AI-server shipments rose roughly 1.5x year-on-year, outpacing GPU-based servers; MediaTek lifted its Q4 ASIC revenue target to about US$2.5bn. The updates point to broadening AI hardware momentum across Taiwan's chain, supporting earnings visibility for assemblers and chip designers.

### 🏦 Financial Sector / 金融業

- **FSC pre-announces draft rules for stablecoin issuance** — Taiwan's Financial Supervisory Commission has pre-announced a draft regulation governing the permission and administration of stablecoin issuance, alongside revised anti-money-laundering rules for financial institutions. The framework would set licensing and oversight standards for issuers, laying groundwork for regulated digital-asset services and signaling the regulator's intent to balance financial innovation with investor protection and systemic stability.
  **金管會預告穩定幣發行許可及管理辦法草案** — 金管會預告「穩定幣發行許可及管理辦法」草案，並同步修正金融機構洗錢防制相關規定。此草案將為穩定幣發行者建立發照與監理標準，是我國推動合規數位資產服務的重要基礎，顯示主管機關在鼓勵金融創新之餘，亦著重投資人保護與金融穩定。後續子法與配套措施進度，將牽動虛擬資產業者與金融機構的布局節奏。
  - 來源 / Source：[金管會](https://www.fsc.gov.tw/ch/home.jsp?id=96&parentpath=0)
- **Virtual Asset Service Act passed; three-industry profits stay strong** — Taiwan's Virtual Asset Service Act has cleared its third reading and been promulgated, with the FSC drafting nine authorized sub-regulations to implement it. Separately, the regulator reported pre-tax profits across banking, securities/futures and insurance reached about NT$1.17tn in the first eight months. The new regime should bring virtual-asset operators under formal supervision, improving transparency and consumer safeguards.
  **《虛擬資產服務法》三讀通過 金融三業獲利強勁** — 《虛擬資產服務法》已三讀通過並經總統公布，金管會正研訂九項授權子法以利落實。另據金管會報告，今年前八月銀行、證券期貨及保險三業合計稅前盈餘達約1兆1,702億元。新法將使虛擬資產業者納入正式監理框架，有助提升市場透明度與消費者保障；金融三業獲利則反映整體金融環境穩健，惟後續仍須關注子法細節與落地時程。
