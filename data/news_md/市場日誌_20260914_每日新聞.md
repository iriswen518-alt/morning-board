### 🏦 Financial Sector / 金融業

- **FSC steps up anti-fraud defenses and senior-care trust push** — Taiwan's Banking Bureau reiterated on September 1 that the public should not invest through financial institutions lacking approved local operations, and promoted "senior-care trust" measures to protect retirees' assets. It also launched a 2026 anti-fraud coordination summit combining regulators, examiners and police. The moves tighten defenses against online scams and unlicensed cross-border solicitation as digital-finance fraud grows more sophisticated.
  **金管會強化阻詐與安養信託** — 金管會銀行局9月1日重申，民眾勿透過未經核准在台設立據點的金融機構投資，並推動「安養信託」守護樂齡族群資產；同時啟動2026金檢警聯防高峰會，結合主管機關、金檢與警政力量防詐。此舉針對線上詐騙與未經許可的跨境招攬加強防堵，在數位金融詐欺手法日益精密之際強化消費者保護，亦牽動金融機構法遵成本與信託業務布局。
  - 來源 / Source：[銀行局](https://www.banking.gov.tw/)
- **Insurance capital steered toward public infrastructure** — The FSC's Insurance Bureau said regulators are working across agencies to broaden the range of public-infrastructure targets eligible for insurers' capital, channeling long-term insurance funds into domestic construction. By expanding qualifying projects and easing investment rules, the policy aims to turn Taiwan's large insurance asset pools into a stable financing source for infrastructure while supporting insurers' search for steady, long-duration yields.
  **引導保險資金挹注公共建設** — 金管會保險局表示，正跨部會攜手擴大保險業可投資的公共建設標的範圍，引導長天期保險資金投入國內建設。透過放寬合格投資項目與相關規範，政策盼將龐大的保險資產池轉化為基礎建設的穩定資金來源，同時協助壽險業尋求穩健的長天期收益。相關鬆綁牽動保險資金運用配置與收益結構，亦與公共建設融資動能相互連動。
