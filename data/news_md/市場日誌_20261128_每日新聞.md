### 🏭 Industry / 產業動態

- **Nvidia halts AI server shipments to four Southeast Asian nations** — Nvidia has told server makers including Dell and Supermicro to suspend shipments of AI servers to Singapore, Malaysia, Thailand and Indonesia, with no restart date, as Washington tightens curbs aimed at stopping China accessing AI compute. The move could ripple through supply chains and regional customers.
  **輝達暫停對東南亞4國AI伺服器出貨，供應鏈恐受波及** — 外電報導，輝達已通知戴爾、美超微等伺服器廠，暫停對新加坡、馬來西亞、泰國、印尼出貨AI伺服器，恢復時間未定，被視為美方防堵中國取得AI算力的最新動作。此舉可能衝擊相關供應鏈出貨節奏與東南亞客戶布局。
  - 來源 / Source：[今周刊](https://businesstoday.com.tw/article/category/183025/post/202611270001)
- **Chip-equipment stocks tumble as traders book profits** — Semiconductor-equipment names fell about 3.44% intraday Friday as investors trimmed high-priced winners, pressuring the sub-sector even as the broader index recovered. Analysts said the pullback was short-term positioning, with structural demand for AI chips and CoWoS advanced packaging still firm.
  **半導體設備股盤中重挫3.44%，資金短線調節** — 週五盤中半導體設備族群一度重挫約3.44%，部分高價強勢股遭獲利了結，拖累類股表現，但大盤仍收紅。法人認為此為短線籌碼調節，AI晶片與CoWoS先進封裝的長線需求結構依舊強勁，中長期展望不變。
  - 來源 / Source：[CMoney](https://www.cmoney.tw/notes/note-detail.aspx?nid=1300484)

### 🏦 Financial Sector / 金融業

- **FSC orders banks and insurers to self-assess cyber resilience by year-end** — Taiwan's Financial Supervisory Commission on Nov. 26 unveiled measures requiring financial institutions to complete 14 cybersecurity self-assessment items and report them to their boards before the end of 2026, part of a push to harden the sector's digital defenses and lift cyber governance to board level.
  **金管會要求金融業年底前完成14項資安自評並提報董事會** — 金管會於11月26日發布「金融業資安防護強化措施」，要求各金融機構在今年底前完成14項資安自評事項，並提報最近一次董事會審議。此舉旨在強化金融體系數位防護韌性，督促業者將資安治理提升至董事會層級。
  - 來源 / Source：[金管會](https://www.fsc.gov.tw/ch/home.jsp?id=96&parentpath=0&mcustomize=news_view.jsp&dataserno=202611260005&dtable=News)
- **Regulator tightens short-selling rules to steady the market** — Amid elevated volatility, the FSC rolled out market-stabilization steps, barring short sales below the prior close and raising the minimum margin ratio for short selling on listed securities to 150% (effective Nov. 23), after earlier cutting intraday securities-lending sell quotas. Officials urged investors to stay rational.
  **金管會祭穩定市場措施，融券保證金成數調高至150%** — 因應台股波動加劇，金管會陸續推出穩定市場措施，包括「全面禁止平盤以下放空」，並自11月23日起將上市櫃有價證券最低融券保證金成數調高至150%，先前亦調降每日盤中借券賣出委託數量。金管會呼籲投資人理性因應。
