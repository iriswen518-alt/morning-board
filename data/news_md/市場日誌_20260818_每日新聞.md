### 🏦 Financial Sector / 金融業

- **FSC advances Virtual Asset Service Act, moving crypto players to a licensing regime** — Taiwan's Virtual Asset Service Act, passed by the Legislature earlier this year with the FSC as competent authority, is progressing toward implementation as related sub-regulations are drafted. The law upgrades virtual-asset providers from AML registration to a prior-approval licensing system, sets minimum capital and reserve requirements, formally regulates stablecoin issuance, and imposes heavy penalties for manipulation and fraud, reshaping the compliance landscape for exchanges and banks eyeing digital-asset services.
  **金管會推進虛擬資產服務法 加密業者邁向許可制** — 台灣虛擬資產服務法今年稍早經立法院三讀通過、以金管會為主管機關，隨相關子法陸續研擬，正朝正式施行推進。該法將虛擬資產業者由洗錢防制法的登記制升級為「事前核准許可制」，訂定最低資本與營業準備金要求，正式納管穩定幣發行，並對操縱與詐欺祭出重罰。此舉重塑交易所與有意布局數位資產的銀行的合規環境，牌照與內控門檻同步提高。
  - 來源 / Source：[鏈新聞 ABMedia](https://abmedia.io/taiwan-virtual-asset-act-crypto-regulation)
    
- **FSC lets insurers invest in strategic "AI New Ten Major Construction" industries, eases PE fund limits** — The FSC has opened the door for insurers to invest in strategic industries under the government's "AI New Ten Major Construction" programme and relaxed caps on their investment in domestic private-equity funds. The move channels the sector's large long-term capital pool toward public infrastructure and the real economy, giving life insurers new yield-seeking outlets while supporting national AI and infrastructure build-out, and marking a notable loosening of insurance-investment rules.
  **金管會開放保險業投資「AI新十大建設」策略性產業 鬆綁私募股權基金限額** — 金管會開放保險業投資政府「AI新十大建設推動方案」下的策略性產業，並放寬對國內私募股權基金的投資限額。此舉引導保險業龐大的長期資金投入公共建設與實體產業，讓壽險業在低利與資產配置壓力下取得新的收益出口，同時支持國家AI與基礎建設推動，也象徵保險資金運用規範的明顯鬆綁，後續可望帶動相關產業與私募基金募資動能。
  - 來源 / Source：[金管會](https://www.fsc.gov.tw/ch/home.jsp?id=96&parentpath=0%2C2&mcustomize=news_view.jsp&dataserno=202606160003&dtable=News)

### 💎 Wealth Management / 財富管理

- **Taiwan's asset-management hub push hits two-year target early as high-net-worth wealth keeps growing** — The government's Asian asset-management centre initiative, launched in 2024 around five programmes with cross-agency coordination and streamlined procedures, saw managed assets expand fast enough to hit its two-year policy target ahead of schedule by end-2025. Studies project Taiwan's overall high-net-worth segment to grow at roughly a 10% CAGR from 2025 to 2029, with the "billion-NT-dollar" ultra-wealthy tier growing fastest at about 11%, sustaining demand for private banking and cross-border structuring.
  **亞洲資產管理中心政策提前達標 高資產財富持續成長** — 政府2024年啟動的亞洲資產管理中心政策，以五大計畫、跨部會協作與程序簡化推動，資產管理規模快速擴大，於2025年底提前達成兩年政策目標。研究預估台灣整體高資產客群2025至2029年年複合成長率約10%，其中「十億」超高資產族群成長最快、達約11%。此一趨勢支撐私人銀行、跨境財富架構與傳承信託需求，成為銀行、投信與保險財管業務競逐的核心成長引擎。
  - 來源 / Source：[亞洲資產管理中心](https://www.aamc.tw/zh/five3-1.html)

- **Ten life insurers now cleared to run wealth-management business, deepening HNW channel** — As of May 2026, ten life insurers — including Shin Kong, Nan Shan, Fubon, Cathay, KGI, Taiwan Life, Chubb, BNP Paribas Cardif, Prudential and Yuanta — had won FSC approval to operate wealth-management business, widening the insurance sector's push into high-net-worth services. The expansion lets insurers bundle protection, savings and investment solutions for affluent clients, intensifying competition with banks and securities firms for a fast-growing wealth pool and reinforcing Taiwan's broader asset-management-hub ambitions.
  **10家壽險獲准辦理財富管理業務 深化高資產通路** — 截至2026年5月，包括新光、南山、富邦、國泰、凱基、台灣、保誠、安達、法國巴黎及元大人壽在內共10家壽險公司，已獲金管會核准辦理財富管理業務，擴大保險業切入高資產服務。此一開放讓壽險公司得為富裕客群整合保障、儲蓄與投資方案，與銀行、券商競逐快速成長的財富大餅，也呼應台灣打造亞洲資產管理中心的政策目標，帶動保險通路在財管市場的布局與競爭。
