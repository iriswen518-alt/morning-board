### 🏭 Industry / 產業動態

- **USITC opens Section 337 probe naming Foxconn, Delta and Quanta (2026-10-11)** — The U.S. International Trade Commission launched a Section 337 investigation (case 337-TA-1526) into vertical power-delivery systems and related components, following a complaint by Vicor. Twenty firms were named, including Foxconn, Delta Electronics and Quanta. Delta shares fell as much as 3.8% intraday; the companies say shipments and operations are unaffected and they will respond through legal channels, with the case expected to take 12–16 months.
  **美啟動337調查、鴻海台達電廣達入列** — 美國國際貿易委員會（USITC）對垂直供電系統及相關零組件啟動337條款調查（案號337-TA-1526），申訴人為Vicor，鴻海、台達電、廣達等20家企業入列。台達電股價一度重挫3.8%至1,890元，業者強調產品出貨與營運正常、將依程序積極因應維護權益。貿易署指出審理時程約12至16個月，若申訴方勝訴USITC可發布進口排除令。
  - 來源 / Source：[中央社](https://cna.com.tw/news/afe/202610110175.aspx)
- **Goldman lifts Hon Hai target to NT$400 on AI momentum** — Goldman Sachs reiterated a bullish stance on Hon Hai (Foxconn), raising its price target to NT$400 and projecting earnings per share could roughly double over three years. The brokerage cites strong Nvidia AI-server rack revenue, a rising share of orders from cloud service providers, and sovereign-AI projects expected to contribute to revenue from the second half of 2026.
  **高盛上修鴻海目標價至400元** — 高盛看好鴻海AI成長動能，將目標價上修至400元，預估未來三年每股盈餘（EPS）有機會翻倍。主要利基在於輝達（NVIDIA）AI伺服器機櫃營收成長強勁、雲端服務供應商（CSP）下單比重提升，以及主權AI專案可望自2026年下半年起貢獻營收，推升鴻海在AI供應鏈的戰略地位與獲利能見度。
  - 來源 / Source：[uAnalyze 優分析](https://uanalyze.com.tw/articles/2342735837)

### 🏦 Financial Sector / 金融業

- **FSC chief lays out six policy priorities ahead of legislative briefing (2026-10-16)** — A written report from FSC Chairman Peng Jin-lung, released ahead of his Oct 19 Legislative Finance Committee briefing, set out six policy directions: strengthening financial resilience and risk supervision, sound market development and corporate governance, consumer protection, building a Taiwan-style Asian asset-management hub, fintech innovation, and green/transition finance. It also flagged a financial-business large language model for 2027 Q1 and a virtual-asset custody pilot.
  **金管會六大施政面向出爐** — 金管會主委彭金隆10月19日赴立法院財委會業務報告，書面報告先行曝光，列出六大施政面向：強化金融韌性與風險監理、健全金融市場發展與公司治理、落實消費者權益保護、打造具台灣特色亞洲資產管理中心、金融科技創新，以及綠色與轉型金融。並預告2027年第1季推出金融業務知識大語言模型，及推動虛擬資產保管業務主題式試辦。
  - 來源 / Source：[工商時報](https://www.ctee.com.tw/news/20261016701613-430301)
- **Banks, brokers and insurers post NT$1.17tn pre-tax profit; 2026 exams target fraud** — The same report showed combined pre-tax profit for banks, securities/futures and insurers reached NT$1.1702tn in January–August, with domestic banks' capital adequacy ratio at 15.16%, above the 10.5% statutory minimum. The FSC continues to refine the new-generation insurance solvency regime and is pressing insurers to fix currency and duration mismatches, while anti-fraud, AML, consumer protection and cyber-resilience top its 2026 examination priorities.
  **三業獲利逾1.17兆元、金檢聚焦防詐** — 金管會報告顯示，今年1至8月銀行、證券期貨、保險三業稅前盈餘合計1兆1,702億元，本國銀行資本適足率15.16%、高於10.5%法定最低標準。金管會持續檢討新一代保險清償能力制度，要求保險業改善資產負債的幣別與期間錯配；2026年金檢重點鎖定防制詐騙、防制洗錢、金融消費者權益保護及資通安全韌性，檢查對象並納入已登記的虛擬資產業者。

### 💎 Wealth Management / 財富管理

The bank says demand is shifting away from simple product allocation toward integrated asset-liability management, asset preservation, cross-border allocation and generational wealth transfer.
