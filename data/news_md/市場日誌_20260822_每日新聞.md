### 💰 Tax & Regulations / 稅務法規

- **Estate-tax overhaul shifts "deemed estate" burden onto recipients** — The Executive Yuan approved amendments to the Estate and Gift Tax Act under which assets gifted to specified relatives within two years before death are added back to the taxable estate and taxed on the recipient, capped at the value of what they received, rather than shared among all heirs. The draft also removes the threshold for instalment payments, letting even small tax bills be paid over time.
  **遺贈稅修法將「擬制遺產」稅負移轉至受贈人** — 行政院通過《遺產及贈與稅法》修正草案，明定死亡前 2 年贈與特定親屬的財產須併入遺產總額課稅，並由受贈人按比例單獨負擔、上限以其受贈財產價值為限，不再由全體繼承人分攤。草案並取消分期繳納門檻，讓即使小額稅款也能申請分期，使資產傳承的稅負分配更公平、對繼承人現金流更友善。
  - 來源 / Source：[ETtoday 財經雲](https://finance.ettoday.net/news/3105961)
- **2026 gift/estate brackets lifted for cumulative inflation** — For 2026 the gift-tax exemption stays at NT$2.44 million per parent (NT$4.88 million combined for a couple), while cumulative CPI gains above 10% triggered an upward adjustment of the tax brackets: the 10% gift-tax band now extends to NT$28.11 million and the 15% band to NT$56.21 million. The estate-tax exemption is unchanged, so the bracket shift mainly eases "bracket creep" on mid-sized transfers.
  **2026 遺贈稅級距隨累計通膨上調** — 2026 年贈與稅免稅額維持每人 244 萬元，父母各有額度、一年合計可免稅贈與子女 488 萬元；因累計 CPI 漲幅逾 10%，課稅級距上調：贈與稅 10% 稅率級距上限升至 2,811 萬元、15% 級距上限至 5,621 萬元。遺產稅免稅額則未調整，故此次調整主要緩解通膨造成的級距擠壓，略減中等規模移轉的有效稅負。
  - 來源 / Source：[商業周刊](https://www.businessweekly.com.tw/business/blog/3018870)
