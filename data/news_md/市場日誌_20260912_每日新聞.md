### 🏦 Financial Sector / 金融業

- **FSC advances holding-company capital rules to ease TIS transition** — The FSC's banking bureau has pre-announced amendments to capital-adequacy rules for financial holding companies to smooth the industry's 2026 shift to the new-generation solvency regime (TIS). Under the transition, eight life-insurance-type holdings gain capital relief and 11 insurance-type holdings may defer their capital-adequacy filing by one month. The measures cushion near-term capital strain while phasing in tougher solvency standards.
  **金管會鬆綁金控資本　助接軌TIS** — 金管會銀行局預告「金融控股公司合併資本適足性管理辦法」修正草案，為壽險業2026年接軌新一代清償能力制度（TIS）鋪路。過渡措施下，8家壽險型金控可獲資本減壓，11家保險型金控適用資本適足率延後1個月申報。此舉在推動更嚴格清償標準的同時，緩解業者短期資本壓力與增資節奏，兼顧監理強度與產業穩定，攸關金控股評價與股利政策，市場密切關注過渡期效應。
  - 來源 / Source：[鉅亨網](https://news.cnyes.com/news/id/6472396)
- **FSC steps up anti-fraud drive with cross-agency "tech shield"** — The FSC launched a 2026 anti-fraud summit uniting financial regulators and police under a "technology shield, whole-domain protection" banner, and reminded the public not to invest through financial institutions lacking approved local operations. The push tightens defenses around online scams and unlicensed cross-border solicitation, reinforcing consumer protection as digital-finance fraud grows more sophisticated.
  **科技阻詐　金管會強化金檢警聯防** — 金管會啟動「2026金檢警聯防：科技阻詐，全域守護高峰會」，結合金融監理與警政力量強化聯防，並提醒民眾勿透過未經核准在台設立據點之金融機構進行投資，以維自身權益。此舉針對線上詐騙與未經許可的跨境招攬加強防堵，在數位金融詐欺手法日益精密之際深化消費者保護，相關措施亦牽動金融機構法遵成本與KYC作業，提醒投資人審慎查證平台合法性。

The policy aims to repatriate offshore wealth and deepen private-banking, trust and structured-product capabilities, positioning Taiwan as a regional hub for family-office and legacy planning.
