### 🏦 Financial Sector / 金融業

- **Short-selling curbs take effect Nov. 9: securities-lending cap cut to 3%, margin ratio raised to 130% (2026-11-09)** — The Financial Supervisory Commission's package of stabilizing measures took effect on Nov. 9. The daily cap on securities-lending short sales was slashed to 3% of the prior 30-session average volume, from 30%, and the minimum margin ratio for margin short selling was raised to 130% from 90%. The FSC also broadened acceptable collateral to ease forced-sale pressure. The curbs aim to raise the cost of bearish bets and steady market order.
