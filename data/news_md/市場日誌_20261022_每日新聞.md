### Taiwan Equities / 股市行情

- **Taiwan stocks rebound 595 points as TSMC firms into futures settlement** — The TAIEX closed up 594.84 points, or 1.28%, at 46,887.77 on Oct 21, reclaiming the 46,800 level on turnover of about NT$833bn. The index had surged as much as 962 points to an intraday high of 47,254.93 before a futures settlement trimmed gains late in the session. TSMC rose NT$15 (0.62%) to NT$2,420 to anchor the tail-end bid, while the OTC index jumped 1.94% to 395.42.

### 🏭 Industry / 產業動態

- **TSMC lifts full-year outlook as AI demand outstrips supply** — At its latest earnings briefing, TSMC reported third-quarter revenue of about NT$1.49tn and guided fourth-quarter US-dollar revenue to US$52.3–54.3bn, raising its full-year dollar-revenue growth forecast to roughly 44% and setting 2026 capex at US$60–64bn. Management said demand for AI-related silicon continues to run well ahead of capacity, with the A14 node preparing pilot output in 2027 and volume production targeted for 2028.
  **台積電上修全年展望 AI需求遠超供給** — 台積電最新法說會公布第三季營收約1.49兆元，第四季美元營收預估523億至543億美元，全年美元營收成長上修至約44%，2026年資本支出上看600億至640億美元。公司表示AI相關晶片需求持續遠高於自身產能；先進製程A14規劃2027年試產、2028年量產，延續技術領先布局。
  - 來源 / Source：[鉅亨網](https://news.cnyes.com/news/id/6631238)
- **AI-server demand keeps Taiwan's supply chain humming** — Analysts flagged around ten Taiwan suppliers set to benefit from a fresh wave of AI-server volume, as next-generation platforms such as Nvidia's Vera Rubin, AWS Trainium 3 and Google's TPU v8 move toward ramp in late 2026. Assemblers and component makers including Wiwynn, Wistron, Hon Hai and Auras are positioned across the build-out, which also drew focus at the recent OCP summit covering high-voltage power, cooling, optical interconnects and ASICs.
  **AI伺服器需求續旺 台廠供應鏈迎放量** — 法人點名約10檔台廠供應鏈可望受惠新一波AI伺服器放量，Vera Rubin、Trainium 3與TPU v8等次世代平台預計2026年下半年進入量產。緯穎、緯創、鴻海、奇鋐等組裝與零組件廠全面卡位；近期OCP全球高峰會亦聚焦高壓供電、散熱、光互連與ASIC等基建升級議題。
  - 來源 / Source：[經濟日報](https://udn.com/news/story/7251/9818556)

### 🏦 Financial Sector / 金融業

- **FSC lays out six policy priorities, plans financial-sector LLM** — Financial Supervisory Commission chair Peng Chin-lung set out six policy directions in a legislative report, spanning risk supervision, market and corporate governance, consumer protection, the Asian asset-management hub, fintech and sustainable transition. The regulator plans to release a first version of a finance-knowledge large language model (FinLLM) in the first quarter of 2027 and continues trials of virtual-asset custody and bank deposit-token services.
  **金管會揭六大施政方向 規劃金融業大語言模型** — 金管會主委彭金隆在立院業務報告列出六大施政方向，涵蓋風險監理、市場與公司治理、消費者保護、亞洲資產管理中心、金融科技與永續轉型。金管會規劃2027年第一季發布首版金融業務知識大語言模型（FinLLM），並持續推動虛擬資產保管及銀行存款代幣業務試辦。
  - 來源 / Source：[經濟日報](https://money.udn.com/money/story/5613/9819807)
- **Regulator to launch cross-industry credit-data exchange by end-October** — The FSC said it is pushing the Joint Credit Information Center and the stock exchange to strengthen cross-industry credit-information sharing, with the mechanism due to go live by Oct 31 so banks can gauge customers' overall credit risk. The commission also urged lenders to tighten loan risk controls; domestic banks' average capital-adequacy ratio stood at 15.16% at the end of the second quarter, comfortably above the 10.5% statutory floor.
  **金管會：跨業信用資訊交換10月底前上線** — 金管會表示將督促聯徵中心與證交所強化跨業信用資訊交換機制，預計10月31日前上線，讓銀行掌握客戶整體信用風險，並要求落實授信風險控管。金融體質方面，本國銀行今年第二季底平均資本適足率為15.16%，高於法定最低10.5%門檻，體質穩健。

### 💎 Wealth Management / 財富管理

- **Regulator weighs wider high-asset access as 21 banks win private-wealth licenses** — Under the Asian asset-management hub push, 21 banks have been approved to run high-net-worth wealth services, and the FSC is studying two further openings: letting high-asset clients invest in onshore private-equity funds and allowing policy-backed lending against insurance. Bank high-asset assets under management have topped NT$2tn, as regulators court wealthy clients to keep capital and family-succession planning onshore.
  **金管會研議擴大高資產業務 21家銀行獲准承作** — 在亞洲資產管理中心政策下，已有21家銀行獲准辦理高資產客戶業務，金管會並研議二大開放：放寬高資產客戶投資境內私募股權（PE）基金，以及開放保單融資。銀行高資產財富管理規模已突破2兆元，主管機關藉此爭取高資產客戶資金與家族傳承規劃留在境內。
  - 來源 / Source：[亞洲資產管理中心](https://www.aamc.tw/zh/five3-1.html)
- **Active ETFs keep pulling in wealth-management money** — Taiwan's active-ETF segment has expanded to about 28 funds with combined assets nearing NT$500bn, one of the fastest-growing product lines in the local fund market and an increasingly core wealth-management holding. Survey data on high-net-worth clients show equities/ETFs and bond products topping their priority lists for added exposure, underscoring how packaged, actively managed vehicles are reshaping onshore portfolio allocation.
  **主動式ETF續吸金 成財富管理核心配置** — 台灣主動式ETF已擴張至約28檔、合計規模逼近5,000億元，是本地基金市場成長最快的產品線之一，也逐漸成為財富管理核心持有。高資產客群調查顯示，股票與ETF、債券型商品是優先增持的前兩大標的，凸顯包裝化、主動管理型工具正重塑境內資產配置結構。
