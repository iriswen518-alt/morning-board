### Taiwan Equities / 股市行情

- **Taiwan stocks surge 820 points to a two-month high as chip heavyweights lead** — The TAIEX opened firm on the first trading day of September and closed at 46,948.72, up 820.25 points (+1.78%), a near two-month closing high, as TSMC, UMC and the optical-communications, IC-design and foundry groups all rallied on the eve of SEMICON. Turnover eased to NT$1.08tn. The advance, coinciding with new water-cooling plate launches from TSMC and AMD, signals renewed appetite for AI-linked hardware even as Middle East tensions unsettle global risk assets.

### 🏭 Industry / 產業動態

- **Taiwan August auto sales slip below 30,000 to a six-month low, Q4 seen recovering** — New-car registrations fell to 29,222 units in August, down 24.4% month-on-month and 0.8% year-on-year, the weakest since March, largely reflecting the Ghost Month lull.
