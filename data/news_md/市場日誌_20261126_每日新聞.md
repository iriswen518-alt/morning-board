### 💰 Tax & Regulations / 稅務法規

- **Estate- and gift-tax exemptions set to rise in 2027 as inflation crosses threshold** — Taiwan's cumulative consumer-price gains reached about 11.91% through October, exceeding the statutory adjustment threshold.
