### 🏭 Industry / 產業動態

- **TrendForce sees memory contract prices still climbing in Q4 on AI-server demand** — TrendForce projects fourth-quarter conventional DRAM contract prices rising about 10–15% sequentially—and 15–20% including HBM—while NAND Flash gains 15–20%, as AI-server and data-center demand keeps supply extremely tight. Makers keep prioritizing advanced-process capacity for server products, leaving the market undersupplied, though the pace of increases is expected to moderate from earlier quarters.
  **研調估Q4記憶體合約價續漲 AI伺服器撐盤** — TrendForce預估第四季標準型DRAM合約價季增約10~15%，計入HBM後整體上漲15~20%，NAND Flash則季增15~20%，AI伺服器與資料中心需求使市況維持極度緊缺。供應商持續將先進製程產能優先配置給伺服器產品，市場供不應求，惟漲幅可望較前幾季趨緩。報價續強撐起台廠記憶體與模組業者營收與毛利，但擴產節奏與消費端需求仍是後續觀察重點。
  - 來源 / Source：[iTiger](https://www.itiger.com/news/1136682305)
- **PC makers stockpile as memory supply seen staying tight into 2027** — Notebook brands are carrying higher component costs yet keep buying aggressively, expecting DRAM supply to remain tight into 2027 as capacity shifts toward servers; PC DRAM supply could even contract next year. The server DRAM supply-demand imbalance is likely to persist through the fourth quarter, with some pricing capped by long-term-agreement mechanisms, reinforcing a multi-quarter up-cycle for the sector.
  **記憶體供給緊俏延續至2027 PC廠加大備貨** — 研調指出，筆電品牌雖承擔較高零組件成本，仍積極拉貨，預期DRAM供給緊張將延續至2027年，因產能持續移往伺服器，明年PC用DRAM供給甚至可能萎縮。伺服器DRAM供需失衡估延續至第四季，部分報價受長約價格區間機制約束。整體顯示記憶體產業處於跨季度上行循環，台廠相關供應鏈營運動能可望延續，惟須留意消費性終端需求與價格漲勢收斂情況。

### 🏦 Financial Sector / 金融業

- **FSC rolls out a package of 2026 securities-market reforms** — Taiwan's Financial Supervisory Commission is phasing in multiple capital-market measures through 2026, including earlier uploading of shareholder-meeting handbooks and annual reports, a shift to electronic filing for treasury-share buybacks from May 1, and a central-depository "eGift" platform (from March 31) letting shareholders claim meeting souvenirs online. The steps aim to improve disclosure, efficiency and investor convenience.
  **金管會推2026證券市場多項新制** — 金管會自2026年起分階段實施多項資本市場新制，包括上市櫃公司提前上傳股東會議事手冊及年報、庫藏股買回申報自5月1日起改採電子化，以及集保公司3月31日起建置電子化紀念品（eGift）平台，讓股東線上領取股東會紀念品。相關措施旨在強化資訊揭露、提升作業效率與投資人便利性，並推動股東會數位化，為證券市場制度面帶來一波調整，業者須配合調整申報與作業流程。
  - 來源 / Source：[聯合新聞網](https://udn.com/news/story/7239/9235638)
- **FSC tightens financial-sector cybersecurity supervision for 2026** — The regulator is adding cybersecurity requirements for financial institutions in 2026, clarifying the chief information security officer's duties, making a zero-trust architecture a baseline expectation, and requiring software-supply-chain security mechanisms. The push responds to rising digital-finance and third-party risks, pressing banks, insurers and securities firms to upgrade governance and resilience as services move further online.
  **金管會強化2026金融資安監理** — 金管會2026年新增金融機構資安監理要求，明定資安長權責、將零信任架構列為必備，並要求建立軟體供應鏈安全機制。此舉回應數位金融與第三方風險升高的趨勢，要求銀行、保險與證券業者強化治理與韌性。隨著金融服務持續線上化，資安監理標準提高將牽動業者的系統投資與組織調整，也凸顯主管機關對營運持續與客戶資料保護的重視，為金融業重要的法遵與風控課題。
