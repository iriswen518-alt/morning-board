### 💰 Tax & Regulations / 稅務法規

- **Estate and gift tax exemptions set to rise for 2027 as CPI crosses threshold** — Taiwan's estate- and gift-tax exemptions look set to increase for cases arising from 2027, after cumulative CPI gains since the last adjustment passed the statutory 10% trigger (about 11.6%). The finance ministry's estimates point to the estate-tax exemption rising from NT$13.33 million toward roughly NT$14.78 million and the gift-tax exemption from NT$2.44 million toward about NT$2.72 million, with final figures to be confirmed in a year-end announcement — a meaningful shift for wealth-transfer planning.
  **CPI 跨過門檻，遺產稅與贈與稅免稅額可望 2027 年調高** — 累計 CPI 自上次調整以來漲幅已過 10% 法定門檻（約 11.6%），遺產稅與贈與稅免稅額可望自 2027 年發生的案件起調高。財政部試算指出，遺產稅免稅額可能由 1,333 萬元升至約 1,478 萬元、贈與稅免稅額由 244 萬元升至約 272 萬元，最終金額以年底正式公告為準，對財富傳承規劃是重要變化。
  - 來源 / Source：[經濟日報](https://udn.com/news/story/6839/9547804)
- **Income-tax exemption and standard deduction to climb for 2027 filing** — For the 2027 filing season (covering 2026 income), the per-person income-tax exemption is set to rise from NT$97,000 to NT$101,000, and to NT$155,000 for those aged 70 and over, while the standard deduction increases from NT$131,000 to NT$136,000. The inflation-linked adjustments lift the tax-free threshold for a single salaried worker toward roughly NT$644,000, modestly easing the burden on households when they file in May 2027.
  **2027 年報稅免稅額與標準扣除額同步調高** — 2027 年報稅（2026 年所得）適用的個人免稅額可望由每人 9.7 萬元調高至 10.1 萬元、70 歲以上提高至 15.5 萬元，標準扣除額也由 13.1 萬元升至 13.6 萬元。此一與物價連動的調整，使單身上班族免稅門檻約提高至 64.4 萬元，將於 2027 年 5 月報稅時適用，略為減輕家庭稅負。
