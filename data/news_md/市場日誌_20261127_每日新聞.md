### 🏦 Financial Sector / 金融業

- **FSC advances stablecoin issuance rules limited to banks and bank-led ventures** — Taiwan's Financial Supervisory Commission has advanced draft rules for stablecoin issuance, limiting issuance to banks or bank-led ventures and tightening related anti-money-laundering requirements, alongside draft regulations for virtual-asset service providers under the new Virtual Asset Service Act. The move aims to build a supervised framework for digital assets, channeling stablecoin activity through regulated banking institutions while the authority finalizes licensing and reserve standards.
  **金管會推進穩定幣發行草案，初期限銀行與金融業合資機構** — 金管會推進「穩定幣發行許可及管理辦法」草案，初期將發行限於銀行或金融業合資機構，並同步收緊相關洗錢防制規定；證期局亦預告「虛擬資產服務法」下虛擬資產服務商管理的多項授權命令草案。此舉意在為數位資產建立受監理框架，先將穩定幣業務導入受規範的銀行體系，同時敲定發照與準備金等標準。
  - 來源 / Source：[金管會](https://www.fsc.gov.tw/ch/home.jsp?id=96)
- **FSC widens insurer executive talent pool from 7 to 12 fields** — The FSC has relaxed professional-background rules for insurance-company executives, expanding eligible cross-disciplinary fields from seven to twelve and adding areas such as medicine, investment, marketing, real estate and sustainability. Effective in late November, the change is designed to help insurers recruit broader skill sets for product innovation, risk management and the transition to new solvency and accounting regimes, part of a wider push to modernize the sector's talent base.
  **金管會放寬保險業經理人跨域人才資格，由 7 類增至 12 類** — 金管會鬆綁保險公司經理人專業背景規定，可延攬跨領域人才由 7 類擴增至 12 類，新增醫療、投資、行銷、不動產及永續等領域，新制於 11 月下旬生效。此舉旨在協助保險業延攬商品創新、風險管理及接軌新一代清償能力與會計制度所需的更廣泛人才，為壯大產業人才基礎的一環。
  - 來源 / Source：[金管會](https://www.fsc.gov.tw/ch/home.jsp?id=96)

### 💎 Wealth Management / 財富管理

- **High-asset AUM at banks tops NT$2 trillion, up more than 50% year-on-year** — High-asset wealth management continues to expand rapidly in Taiwan. FSC data show the 20 banks running high-net-worth client programs managed about NT$2.08 trillion in assets, up more than 50% year-on-year and crossing the NT$2 trillion mark. Industry research projects the number of individuals with assets above NT$100 million rising toward 124,000 in 2026, with ETFs and alternatives such as private equity and private credit driving allocation growth.
  **銀行高資產管理規模突破 2 兆元、年增逾 5 成** — 台灣高資產財富管理持續快速擴張。金管會統計，已開辦高資產客戶業務的 20 家銀行管理規模約達 2.08 兆元、年增逾 50%，正式突破 2 兆元大關。產業研究並預估資產逾 1 億元的高資產人數 2026 年將增至約 12.4 萬人，ETF 及私募股權、私募信貸等另類資產成為推升配置成長的主要動能。
  - 來源 / Source：[KPMG Taiwan](https://kpmg.com/tw/zh/media/press-releases/2026/06/2026-taiwan-wealth-management-trends-high-net-worth-growth.html)
- **Banks race to build private banking and family-office services** — Banks are racing to build out private banking and family-office services for the wealthy. Taipei Fubon has promoted a "wealth blueprint" integrating investment, insurance, trust, financing and family-office functions, KGI Bank has launched a private-banking membership for clients with over NT$100 million in assets, and CTBC centers its model on dedicated relationship managers coordinating trust, legal, tax and cross-border expertise, reflecting intensifying competition for Taiwan's high-net-worth families.
  **銀行競相擴充私人銀行與家族辦公室服務** — 銀行競相擴充面向富裕客群的私人銀行與家族辦公室服務。台北富邦銀行倡議整合投資、保險、信託、融資與家族辦公室的「財富藍圖」；凱基銀行推出資產逾 1 億元適用的「私人銀行會員」；中信銀行則以專屬客戶經理為核心，整合信託、法律、稅務與跨境專業資源，反映各行爭奪高資產家族客群的競爭日益白熱化。
