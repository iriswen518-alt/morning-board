### 💰 Tax & Regulations / 稅務法規

- **Taiwan reshapes how pre-death gifts are taxed in estates** — Under a legislative amendment, gifts made within two years before death are folded into the taxable estate, with the resulting tax borne solely by the recipient rather than shared among all heirs; the Finance Ministry is drafting enforcement sub-regulations. The estate-tax exemption stands at NT$13.33 million and is projected to rise toward NT$14.78 million in 2027, a key input for year-end succession planning.
  **遺贈稅制調整 死前兩年贈與併入遺產** — 依修法，被繼承人死亡前兩年內的贈與財產將併入遺產總額，稅負改由受贈人單獨承擔、不再由全體繼承人分攤，財政部正研擬修正遺贈稅法施行細則等子法。目前遺產稅免稅額為1,333萬元，預估2027年可望調升至約1,478萬元，牽動高資產族群年底傳承與贈與規劃，建議及早試算稅負。
  - 來源 / Source：[中央社](https://www.cna.com.tw/news/afe/202608210289.aspx)

- **2026 filing thresholds and deductions rise** — For the 2026 tax year the basic living-expense allowance increases to NT$213,000 per person and the long-term-care special deduction to NT$180,000, while the lineal-descendant deduction is NT$560,000 per head. The Finance Ministry has also eased rules so relatives of same-sex married couples qualify under the Income Tax Act, broadening eligibility for households preparing their returns.
