### 💎 Wealth Management / 財富管理

- **High-asset wealth management scales up sharply (2026)** — FSC data put Taiwan's high-asset wealth-management scheme at roughly NT$2.08 trillion as of end-November 2025, up about 50% year-on-year, as banks expand private-banking services for clients with NT$100 million or more. Offerings now extend to Lombard lending against financial-asset portfolios and family-governance services, reflecting rising demand from ultra-high-net-worth clients for integrated, discretionary solutions.
  **高資產財富管理規模快速擴張（2026年）** — 金管會統計，截至2025年11月底高資產財富管理業務規模約2.08兆元、年增約50%，銀行積極擴充針對1億元以上客戶的私人銀行服務。服務內容延伸至以金融資產組合為擔保的Lombard Lending融資及家族治理，反映超高資產客群對整合型、全權委託式理財方案的需求升溫，帶動銀行財管手續費收入動能。
  - 來源 / Source：[經濟日報](https://money.udn.com/money/story/5613/9282175)

- **Study flags wealth-governance gap among Taiwan's affluent families (2026)** — A North Fubon survey found only about 16.9% of wealthy families have reached consensus on wealth governance, prompting the bank to promote a "wealth blueprint" combining investment, insurance, trusts, financing and family-office services. KPMG separately estimated the number of households holding NT$100 million or more rose from 93,000 in 2023 to 118,000 in 2025, with alternative assets such as private equity and private credit gaining traction.
