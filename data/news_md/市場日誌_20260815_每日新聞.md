### 🏦 Financial Sector / 金融業

- **Regulator lets insurance-linked financial holdcos push monthly revenue disclosure to the 15th** — The FSC amended rules allowing financial holding companies with insurance subsidiaries, and insurers themselves, to report monthly revenue on the 15th instead of the 10th. The change affects 11 financial holding companies, 12 life insurers, 11 non-life insurers and one reinsurer, giving them more time to compile figures under the incoming accounting regime taking effect in 2026.
  **金管會鬆綁 有保險業務金控月營收可延至15日公布** — 金管會修正規定，讓具保險子公司的金控及保險業者，得將每月營收申報由10日延後至15日公告。此調整涵蓋11家金控、12家壽險、11家產險與1家再保險業者，主要因應2026年新會計制度上路後編製作業更為複雜，給予業者更充裕的結算與揭露時間。
  - 來源 / Source：[Yahoo新聞](https://tw.news.yahoo.com/11%E5%AE%B6%E6%9C%89%E4%BF%9D%E9%9A%AA%E6%A5%AD%E5%8B%99%E9%87%91%E6%8E%A7-2026%E5%B9%B4%E5%8F%AF%E5%BB%B6%E8%87%B315%E6%97%A5%E5%85%AC%E5%B8%83%E7%8D%B2%E5%88%A9-115008337.html)

- **Life insurers brace for 2026 "dual convergence" to IFRS 17 and new solvency regime** — The FSC has flagged eight new measures taking effect from 2026, with the insurance bureau focused on life insurers' transition to IFRS 17 and the new-generation ICS solvency framework. The simultaneous adoption of both standards marks the industry's biggest accounting and capital overhaul in years, reshaping how insurers value liabilities and manage capital adequacy.
  **壽險業迎2026雙接軌 同步導入IFRS 17與ICS** — 金管會示警2026年將有八項新制上路，保險局聚焦壽險業接軌國際財務報導準則第17號（IFRS 17）與新一代清償能力制度（ICS）。兩大制度同步接軌，是壽險業近年最大規模的會計與資本改革，將重塑保單負債評價與資本適足管理方式，牽動整體壽險經營策略與商品結構調整。

The push is reinforced by the Kaohsiung Asia Asset Management Center, established with relaxed regulatory requirements, as banks broaden offerings across deposits, trusts, insurance and structured products for high-net-worth clients.
