### 🏦 Financial Sector / 金融業

- **Regulator eases capital rules as life insurers face first TIS reporting test** — Taiwan's FSC has granted supervisory flexibility ahead of the end-September deadline for the new Taiwan Insurance Capital Standard (TIS), the industry's first capital test. Eight financial holdings with life units get capital relief, while 11 insurance-focused holdings may defer their capital-adequacy filing by one month. The measures aim to smooth the transition to the new solvency regime and cut the risk of capital shortfalls.
  **壽險TIS資本首考 金管會祭兩招穩資本** — 面對9月底新一代清償能力制度（TIS）的首次資本申報大考，金管會給予監理彈性：旗下有壽險的8家金控可望獲得資本減壓，11家保險型金控則可延後一個月申報資本適足率。此舉旨在協助壽險業平順接軌新清償制度、降低資本不足衝擊，穩定市場對保險業財務體質的信心。
