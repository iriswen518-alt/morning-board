### 🏭 Industry / 產業動態

- **TSMC's CoWoS advanced-packaging capacity set to more than double as AI demand runs hot** — TSMC's monthly CoWoS capacity is projected to reach roughly 70,000-75,000 wafers by end-2026 and 100,000-105,000 by end-2027, implying annual output of about 636,000 and 1.06 million wafers — year-on-year growth of around 104% and 67%. The aggressive build-out reflects still-surging demand from AI accelerator customers, keeping advanced packaging the structural leadership theme for TSMC and Taiwan's broader semiconductor supply chain.
  **台積電 CoWoS 先進封裝產能將倍增、AI 需求續強** — 法人估台積電 CoWoS 先進封裝月產能今、明年底將分別達約 7～7.5 萬片、10～10.5 萬片，對應年產能約 63.6 萬、106.2 萬片，年增幅分別約 104%、67%。此積極擴產反映 AI 加速器客戶需求仍旺，先進封裝續為台積電與台廠半導體供應鏈的結構性主流題材，並帶動 CoWoS、散熱、ABF 載板等相關次族群的中長期成長動能。
  - 來源 / Source：[玉山證券](https://www.esunsec.com.tw/article/post/478)
- **Taiwan's machinery and equipment exports surge on the AI capacity-expansion wave** — Taiwan's machinery exports are riding the AI build-out: semiconductor and flat-panel-display equipment shipments reached about US$2.857 billion, up 44.75% year on year, as global compute demand and chip-capacity expansion accelerate. Overall machinery exports rose 18.51%, led by semiconductor tooling and AI-related robotics. The data underscore how the AI supply chain is broadening beyond chipmakers into upstream equipment and automation suppliers.
  **半導體設備領軍、台灣機械出口受 AI 擴產浪潮帶動大增** — 台灣機械產業乘 AI 建置浪潮，半導體與平面顯示器設備出口額達約 28.57 億美元、年增 44.75%，主因全球 AI 算力需求爆發與半導體擴產持續。整體機械出口年增 18.51%，由半導體設備與 AI 相關機器人領軍。數據顯示 AI 供應鏈受惠面正由晶片業者向上游設備、自動化與零組件供應商擴散，帶動台廠中上游族群同步受惠、出口動能延續。
  - 來源 / Source：[台灣機械公會 PMC](https://www.pmc.org.tw/tw/news/show.aspx?num=2601)

### 🏦 Financial Sector / 金融業

- **FSC amends financial-report preparation standards for financial holdings and banks** — The Financial Supervisory Commission amended on Aug 18 the preparation standards for the financial reports of financial holding companies and publicly issued banks, aiming to lift disclosure transparency and comparability. The revisions form part of the groundwork as the sector converges toward next-generation accounting and solvency frameworks, nudging institutions toward cleaner, more consistent balance-sheet reporting ahead of a broader run of new rules taking effect.
  **金管會修正金控與銀行財報編製準則** — 金管會 8 月 18 日修正「金融控股公司財務報告編製準則」與「公開發行銀行財務報告編製準則」，以提升資訊揭露透明度與可比較性。相關調整為金融業接軌新一代會計與清償能力制度鋪路，促使機構提升資產負債表揭露品質。此舉為後續多項新制上路預作準備，牽動金融業整體體質與監理節奏，也讓投資人更易比較各金控與行庫的財務狀況與經營成果。
  - 來源 / Source：[金管會](https://www.fsc.gov.tw/ch/home.jsp?id=96&parentpath=0,2)
- **Regulators move to channel insurance capital into Taiwan's public infrastructure** — In an Aug 20 statement, the FSC said it is working across agencies to widen the pool of eligible public-construction projects so insurers' long-term capital can be directed into domestic infrastructure. The initiative aims to give life insurers stable, long-duration investment targets while supporting national building programmes — a policy that could reshape insurers' asset allocation as they adapt to IFRS 17 and the new solvency regime.
  **金管會引導保險資金挹注公共建設** — 金管會 8 月 20 日發布新聞稿指出，正跨部會擴大公共建設可投資標的範圍，導引壽險業長期資金活水挹注國內基礎建設。此舉在提供壽險業穩定、長天期的投資去化管道，同時支持國家重大建設，兼顧保險業資產負債配置與公共政策目標。隨壽險業接軌 IFRS 17 與新清償制度，長期公建投資可望成為業者調整資產配置的重要方向。
