### 🏭 Industry / 產業動態

- **Server DRAM and NAND Flash contract prices seen rising another 10–20% in Q4 on AI demand** — TrendForce projects fourth-quarter server DRAM contract prices will climb about 10–15% and NAND Flash 15–20%, as AI server demand keeps memory supply tight. Some suppliers lag the market average because of long-term contract price bands. SK Hynix expects commodity DRAM supply to trail demand through 2028. The sustained up-cycle supports earnings momentum for Taiwan's memory makers and AI-server component suppliers.
  **AI需求帶動 Q4伺服器DRAM、NAND Flash估再漲10%至20%** — 研調機構TrendForce預估，第四季伺服器DRAM合約價季增約10%至15%、NAND Flash約15%至20%，AI伺服器需求使記憶體供給持續吃緊，部分受長約價格區間限制的供應商漲幅落後市場平均。SK海力士認為通用型DRAM供給將落後需求至2028年。漲價循環延續，有利台廠記憶體業者與AI伺服器零組件供應鏈的營收與獲利動能，惟須留意終端需求與長約價格對實際漲幅的牽動。
  - 來源 / Source：[優分析](https://uanalyze.com.tw/articles/4215256202)
- **TSMC posts record Q3 and lifts full-year dollar revenue growth outlook as 2nm ramps** — At its earnings call TSMC reported record third-quarter net profit of NT$763.1bn and EPS of NT$29.43, and raised its full-year revenue growth guidance in US-dollar terms, underscoring that AI and advanced-node demand remain the chip supply chain's main engine. The 2nm (N2) node continues to ramp with high-performance computing a dominant share of sales, keeping capacity, CoWoS advanced packaging and equipment orders central to suppliers' 2027 planning.
  **台積電Q3獲利創高、上修全年美元營收成長 2奈米放量** — 台積電法說會公布第三季稅後純益7,630.6億元、每股純益29.43元同創新高，並上修今年以美元計算的營收成長預估，顯示AI與先進製程需求仍是晶圓代工供應鏈的主要動能。2奈米（N2）製程持續放量、高效能運算（HPC）占營收比重居高，使產能、CoWoS先進封裝與設備訂單成為供應鏈2027年規劃核心，牽動台廠設備、材料與封測業者的接單與擴產節奏。

### 🏦 Financial Sector / 金融業

- **FSC opens family-office advisory beyond Kaohsiung, with 11 banks qualifying in first wave** — The Financial Supervisory Commission is extending family-office advisory services beyond the Kaohsiung asset-management pilot zone, with an initial 11 banks meeting the application criteria. Regulators view the expansion as a new growth driver for 2027, building on Taiwan's push to become an Asian asset-management hub. The move broadens the addressable market for banks' high-end advisory, trust and succession-planning services and intensifies competition for wealthy clients.
  **金管會開放家辦顧問業務走出高雄 首波11家銀行符合資格** — 金管會開放家族辦公室（家辦）顧問業務走出高雄亞資專區，首波有11家銀行符合申請資格，被視為2027年的新成長動能，延續政府打造亞洲資產管理中心的政策方向。此舉擴大銀行高端顧問、信託與傳承規劃業務的可及市場，有助業者爭取高資產與家族客群，並牽動財富管理、私人銀行與信託業務的布局與競爭態勢，後續須留意相關子法與業者實際承作進度。
  - 來源 / Source：[工商時報](https://ctee.com.tw/news/20261012701862-430301)
- **Life insurers operate under IFRS 17 and new ICS solvency regime in 2026** — Taiwan's insurance sector is running under IFRS 17 and the new-generation solvency framework based on the Insurance Capital Standard (ICS), which replaced the RBC regime from the start of 2026 with four-stage transitional measures. Monthly revenue filing deadlines for insurers and listed companies with insurance subsidiaries moved from the 10th to the 15th. The regime reshapes product design toward protection policies and influences insurers' capital planning and asset allocation.
  **壽險業2026年接軌IFRS 17與新一代清償能力(ICS)制度** — 台灣保險業2026年起接軌IFRS 17與以保險資本標準（ICS）為基礎的新一代清償能力制度，自元旦起取代原RBC制度，並採四階段調適措施引導業者健全經營；保險業與擁有保險子公司的公開發行公司月營收申報期限，也由每月10日延後至15日。制度轉換推動商品設計轉向保障型，牽動壽險業資本規劃、避險策略與資產配置，為金融業中須持續觀察的結構性議題，影響獲利表現與財報呈現。

### 💎 Wealth Management / 財富管理

- **High-net-worth wealth AUM at Taiwan's banks tops NT$3tn as deposit share falls below 40%** — FSC data cited by industry reports put assets under management in banks' high-net-worth wealth business at about NT$3.11tn as of end-August, up roughly 70% year-on-year, with client numbers near 30,600 across 26 approved banks. The deposit share of these portfolios fell below 40% to 39.4%, a low since April 2022, as money rotated into investments and insurance. The milestone underscores robust demand and a shift toward integrated asset management.
  **國銀高資產財管規模突破3兆元 存款占比降破4成** — 業界引述金管會數據指出，國銀高資產財富管理業務資產規模（AUM）至8月底約達3兆1,089億元、年增約七成，客戶數近3萬600人，已核准26家銀行辦理。這些投組的存款占比降破4成、來到39.4%，創2022年4月以來新低，資金轉向投資與保險部位。規模突破3兆元凸顯高資產客群需求強勁，並反映配置從單純存款轉向資產負債整合管理與多元商品，成為銀行財富管理爭取高端客群的主戰場。
  - 來源 / Source：[工商時報](https://ctee.com.tw/news/20261013700098-430301)
- **KGI Bank extends private banking into family governance and private-equity access** — KGI Bank is integrating group resources to broaden its private-banking offering from individual asset management into family governance, drawing on China Development Financial's private-equity capabilities to give wealthy clients access to private-market investments. The expansion reflects a broader industry pivot among Taiwan's banks toward holistic services—covering succession, trusts and alternative assets—for high-net-worth families, as competition for top-tier clients intensifies amid the asset-management-hub drive.
  **凱基銀行私人銀行服務延伸家族治理 導入私募股權** — 凱基銀行整合集團資源，將私人銀行服務從個人資產管理延伸至家族治理，並透過中華開發資本提供私募股權投資，讓高資產客戶得以布局私募市場。此布局反映台灣銀行業朝向為高資產家族提供涵蓋傳承、信託與另類資產的全方位服務轉型，在政府推動亞洲資產管理中心、業者競逐高端客群之際，私人銀行與家族辦公室服務成為差異化關鍵，牽動財富管理商品與顧問能力的升級競賽。
