### Taiwan Equities / 股市行情

- **Taiwan's TAIEX plunges a record 2,953 points in historic "717" crash** — The TAIEX collapsed 2,953.71 points, or 6.47%, on Friday to close at 42,671.27 — its largest single-day point loss on record — on turnover of about NT$1.21 trillion, breaking below the quarterly moving average. The rout followed a heavy selloff in US chip stocks, TSMC's post-earnings profit-taking, and worries over stretched AI valuations and over-leveraged Korean tech. TSMC alone tumbled NT$180, or 7.29%, to NT$2,290, its worst single-day point drop ever. The scale of the fall matters because it tests domestic and state-linked buying support ahead of a fragile weekend for global risk sentiment.
  **台股上演「717慘案」崩跌2,953點 創史上最大單日跌點** — 加權指數週五重挫2,953.71點、跌幅6.47%，終場收42,671.27點，成交量約1.21兆元，跌破季線，改寫史上單日最大跌點紀錄。此波下殺主要受美股晶片股遭大舉拋售、台積電法說會後賣壓全面出籠，以及AI類股估值偏高、韓股過度槓桿等疑慮拖累。權值王台積電單日重挫180元、跌幅7.29%收2,290元，亦創個股史上最大單日跌點。跌勢規模考驗內資與國家隊低檔承接力道，市場關注週末國際情緒與下週開盤能否止穩。

- **Foreign investors sell record NT$189 billion as three major institutions dump NT$261.7 billion** — Foreign institutional investors net sold a record NT$189.04 billion of Taiwan shares on Friday, the single largest daily foreign outflow ever, while the three major institutional groups combined offloaded NT$261.7 billion. Cumulative foreign selling in 2026 has now reached roughly NT$1.56 trillion. Heavyweights TSMC and UMC bore the brunt of the selling. The record outflow underscores how quickly global risk-off and AI-valuation jitters can drain liquidity from an export-heavy, tech-concentrated market, and it raises the stakes for whether government-linked funds and domestic buyers can stabilize the index in the sessions ahead.

### 🏭 Industry / 產業動態

- **TSMC posts record Q2 EPS of NT$27.25, lifts 2026 capex to US$60–64 billion on AI demand** — TSMC reported second-quarter revenue of NT$1.27 trillion, up 12% sequentially and 36% year-on-year, with gross margin of 67.7% and record EPS of NT$27.25, as 2-nanometer output began contributing. Management guided third-quarter revenue to US$44.6–45.8 billion and raised full-year 2026 capex to US$60–64 billion, citing strong AI, HPC and emerging agentic-AI demand, with 70–80% earmarked for advanced processes. Q3 gross margin is seen at 65–67% as fast 2nm ramp dilutes margins by 3–4 points. The upbeat outlook reaffirms AI-driven structural growth even as the stock sold off on profit-taking.
  **台積電Q2每股純益27.25元創高 資本支出上修至600–640億美元** — 台積電第二季合併營收1.27兆元、季增12%、年增36%，毛利率67.7%，每股純益27.25元，雙率及EPS再創新高，2奈米開始貢獻營收。展望第三季，公司預估營收446–458億美元；因應AI、HPC及新興Agentic AI需求，2026年資本支出上修至600–640億美元，其中約七至八成投入先進製程。第三季毛利率估65–67%，受2奈米快速量產稀釋約3–4個百分點。強勁展望重申AI驅動的結構性成長，惟股價因獲利了結賣壓重挫，市場關注資本支出放量對供應鏈的挹注。

The tie-up targets the surging demand for high-bandwidth, low-latency memory integration driven by AI accelerators, which increasingly require memory and logic to be stacked in the same package. The partnership deepens Taiwan's grip on the advanced-packaging value chain — a bottleneck for AI chips — and gives Winbond a path into the high-end memory supply chain alongside HBM incumbents, supporting longer-term earnings visibility for both firms.
  **台積電、華邦世紀大合作 攜手WoW晶圓堆疊搶攻AI記憶體** — 台積電與華邦電啟動先進3D晶圓對晶圓（Wafer-on-Wafer, WoW）堆疊技術「世紀大合作」，由華邦提供DRAM等記憶體晶圓，與台積電邏輯製程晶圓共同堆疊。此案鎖定AI加速晶片帶動的高頻寬、低延遲記憶體整合需求，因AI晶片日益需要將記憶體與邏輯堆疊於同一封裝內。合作深化台灣在AI晶片瓶頸——先進封裝價值鏈的主導地位，也讓華邦切入高階記憶體供應鏈，與HBM業者並進，為雙方中長期獲利能見度提供支撐，後續觀察量產時程與客戶導入進度。

### 🏦 Financial Sector / 金融業

- **FSC says market reflects global conditions, pledges close monitoring as margin cushion stays ample** — Following Friday's record crash, Taiwan's Financial Supervisory Commission said the plunge mainly reflected international developments and pledged to monitor volatility closely, asking the stock and OTC exchanges to track global markets. The regulator noted that as of July 16 the market-wide margin maintenance ratio for credit trading stood at 183.71%, well above the 130% forced-liquidation threshold, leaving a substantial buffer. The reassurance matters because it signals authorities are watching for disorderly deleveraging and stands ready to consider stabilization measures if selling pressure intensifies, even as it stopped short of announcing intervention.
