### 🏦 Financial Sector / 金融業

- **E.Sun Financial completes Mercuries Life takeover to become fifth-largest listed holdco** — E.Sun Financial has finalized its acquisition of Mercuries Life, folding the 33-year-old insurer in as a subsidiary.
