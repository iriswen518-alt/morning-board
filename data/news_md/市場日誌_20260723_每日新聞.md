### 🏭 Industry / 產業動態

- **Powerchip raises July DRAM foundry prices 45%; benefit to surface from November** — Powerchip (PSMC) told its earnings call it lifted DRAM wafer-foundry prices by about 40%-45% in July and raised mature-node logic foundry prices 10%-15%, framing the moves as part of a structural up-cycle. Management said the pricing benefit will progressively show in revenue and profit from November, with peak operations expected around year-end. The company sees the DRAM supply-demand gap persisting into 2027 as AI demand keeps absorbing capacity.

### 🏦 Financial Sector / 金融業

- **FSC lays out three insurance-supervision priorities: AI, green finance and all-age coverage** — At the 2026 East Asia-Pacific Insurance Forum on July 22, the FSC said its future supervision will focus on three areas: driving AI-led technological transformation, guiding green-finance investment, and building an all-age financial system that promotes micro-insurance. Deputy chairman Chen Yen-liang said the priorities respond to a super-aged society, climate change and the industry's adoption of new regimes such as IFRS 17. The forum gathered US, Japanese and Taiwanese experts on digital transformation and sustainable finance.
  **金管會揭保險監理3方向 推AI轉型與全齡金融** — 金管會7月22日於「2026年東亞太平洋保險論壇」表示，未來監理將聚焦三面向：推動AI科技轉型、引導綠色金融投資，以及建構全齡金融體系並推廣微型保險。副主委陳彥良指出，此三方向係因應超高齡社會、氣候變遷，以及保險業今年接軌IFRS 17等新制的挑戰。論壇邀集美、日、台專家探討數位轉型、總體經濟投資與永續金融，並就綠色保險商品揭露機制舉行圓桌座談，勾勒保險業未來監理與轉型路徑。
