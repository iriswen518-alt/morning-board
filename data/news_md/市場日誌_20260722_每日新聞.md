### 🏦 Financial Sector / 金融業

- **FSC tightens disclosure rules for interest-sensitive life policies to protect policyholders** — The Financial Supervisory Commission amended its guidelines governing interest-sensitive (rate-variable) life insurance products, strengthening information-disclosure requirements and reinforcing protection of policyholder rights. The revision aims to ensure consumers better understand the mechanics and risks of these popular savings-linked policies. It reflects the regulator's continued push to raise sales-conduct standards across the life-insurance sector amid a high-rate environment.
  **金管會修正利變型保單規範 強化揭露保障保戶** — 金管會修正「人身保險業辦理利率變動型保險商品業務應注意事項」，強化利率變動型保險商品的資訊揭露機制，並加強保障保戶權益。此次修正旨在確保消費者充分理解這類熱銷儲蓄型保單的運作與潛在風險，反映主管機關在高利率環境下，持續提升壽險業銷售行為標準、督促業者落實公平待客與資訊透明的政策方向。

- **FSC clears O'Pay to run small-value cross-border remittances for migrant workers** — The FSC approved electronic-payment operator O'Pay to conduct small-value foreign remittance services, including a dedicated channel for foreign migrant workers. The licence expands regulated, lower-cost remittance options beyond traditional banks and informal channels. It marks another step in Taiwan's build-out of the fintech and e-payment ecosystem, improving financial access for a large migrant-worker population that regularly sends money home.
  **金管會許可歐付寶經營國外小額匯兌 便利移工** — 金管會許可歐付寶電子支付經營國外小額匯兌業務，並兼營外籍移工國外小額匯兌。此執照擴大了傳統銀行與非正式管道之外、受監理且成本較低的匯款選擇，為台灣建構金融科技與電子支付生態系的又一進展，可改善為數眾多外籍移工的金融近用，讓其定期匯款返鄉更便利、透明且安全，並有助強化跨境資金流動的合規監理。

### 💎 Wealth Management / 財富管理

- **Land Bank wins FSC nod to offer high-asset (Wealth 2.0) products and services** — The FSC approved state-owned Land Bank to provide financial products and services to high-net-worth clients under the "Wealth Management 2.0" regime. The green light adds another major lender to the roster competing for Taiwan's fast-growing affluent segment, where high-asset client counts and AUM have expanded sharply year-on-year. It broadens the range of overseas bonds, structured products and bespoke allocation solutions available to wealthy investors.
  **土地銀行獲准辦理高資產客戶財管2.0業務** — 金管會同意臺灣土地銀行辦理高資產客戶適用之金融商品及服務，正式取得財管2.0資格。此核准為競逐台灣快速成長高資產客群的銀行陣容再添一家公股大行；近年高資產客戶數與管理資產規模年增顯著。此舉將擴大提供高資產投資人的海外債、結構型商品與客製化資產配置方案選擇，深化私人銀行與財富傳承服務量能。
  - 來源 / Source：[金管會](https://www.fsc.gov.tw/ch/home.jsp?id=96&parentpath=0,2)
- **Hua Nan Securities cleared for Wealth 2.0, a private-banking milestone for brokers** — Hua Nan Yong Chang Securities received regulatory approval to launch high-asset Wealth Management 2.0 services, a milestone as securities firms push deeper into private banking traditionally dominated by banks. The move lets brokers offer high-net-worth clients a wider suite of products and cross-border solutions. It signals intensifying competition for Taiwan's affluent investors, whose managed assets are projected to challenge NT$3 trillion this year.
  **華南永昌證獲准開辦財管2.0 券商跨入私銀里程碑** — 華南永昌證券獲准開辦高資產財管2.0業務，象徵券商深入過往由銀行主導的私人銀行領域的重要里程碑。此舉使券商能為高資產客戶提供更完整的商品線與跨境解決方案，反映市場競逐台灣高資產投資人日益白熱化。全體財管2.0管理資產今年可望挑戰3兆元大關，通路多元化將推升整體財富管理市場的競爭強度與服務升級。
