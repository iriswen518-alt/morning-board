### Taiwan Equities / 股市行情

- **Taiwan stocks close above 46,300 as turnover tops NT$1 trillion** — Taiwan's benchmark TAIEX jumped 356 points, or 0.77%, to close at 46,331 on Sept 28, with turnover swelling past NT$1 trillion (NT$1.023tn) for the first time since mid-August. Market bellwether TSMC added NT$10 to NT$2,420. The heavy volume signals renewed risk appetite led by large-cap tech, though concentration in a few heavyweights keeps the index sensitive to any wobble in AI demand.
  **台股攻上46,331點 成交量重返兆元** — 台股9月28日開高走高，加權指數終場大漲356點、漲幅0.77%，收在46,331點，成交量放大至1兆230億元，為8月18日以來再度站上兆元；權值龍頭台積電上漲10元至2,420元。量能回升顯示資金風險偏好回溫、由大型科技股領軍，但盤面高度集中於少數權值股，一旦AI需求出現雜音，指數波動恐將加劇。

- **TSMC's index weight leaves TAIEX hostage to one stock** — TSMC now accounts for well over 40% of the TAIEX by market value, so each NT$1 move in the chipmaker shifts the index by roughly 8 points. With foreign funds rotating around the AI theme, that single-stock concentration means Taiwan's headline index increasingly tracks TSMC's order outlook rather than the broader economy—amplifying both rallies and pullbacks for index and ETF investors.

### 🏭 Industry / 產業動態

- **TSMC's AI revenue seen topping US$100bn as advanced-node demand booms** — Strong pull for leading-edge processes is set to lift TSMC's AI-related revenue toward US$100 billion next year, with advanced-node capacity fully booked. Chairman C.C. Wei has flagged that AI server processors could exceed 20% of revenue by 2028. The scale of committed orders underscores TSMC's grip on the AI supply chain and supports sustained capex across Taiwan's chip ecosystem.

  - 來源 / Source：[經濟日報](https://money.udn.com/money/story/5612/9782072)
- **TSMC's 2nm ramp hits milestone as quarterly earnings climb** — TSMC's most advanced 2-nanometer node is ramping fast across five Taiwan fabs, with its Fab 20 reaching a monthly output milestone of 20,000 wafers. Analysts expect quarterly results to keep setting records as the company grows alongside its AI-application customers. The rapid 2nm scale-up cements Taiwan's technology lead but also concentrates the island's growth on a single, capital-intensive product cycle.

### 🏦 Financial Sector / 金融業

- **FSC eases capital rules for financial holdings ahead of new solvency regime** — Taiwan's FSC on Sept 28 previewed amendments to the group capital-adequacy rules for financial holding companies, granting supervisory flexibility as the insurance industry transitions to the new-generation TIS solvency regime in 2026. Eight life-insurance-led holdcos will get capital relief and 11 insurance-type holdcos may defer their CAR filing by a month, cushioning the initial hit from subsidiary revaluations.
  **金管會鬆綁金控資本規範 因應清償能力新制** — 金管會9月28日預告「金控合併資本適足性管理辦法」修正草案，配合2026年保險業接軌新一代清償能力制度（TIS），給予監理彈性；其中8家壽險型金控可獲資本減壓，11家保險型金控得延後1個月申報資本適足率。此舉旨在避免子公司帳目重估導致母金控接軌初期資本適足率驟降，穩定金控體系資本結構與市場信心。
  - 來源 / Source：[鉅亨網](https://news.cnyes.com/news/id/6472396)
- **Life insurers begin transition to IFRS 17 and new solvency framework** — As Taiwan's life insurers move onto IFRS 17 and the TIS solvency regime, the FSC has rolled out three transitional measures to smooth the switch. The phased approach aims to prevent sharp swings in reported capital and reserves during adoption. Insurers gain room to rebuild capital buffers, while the reforms lift long-term transparency and comparability, reshaping how the sector prices and reserves for policies.
  **壽險業接軌IFRS 17與清償能力新制 金管會提3暫行措施** — 隨壽險業正式接軌IFRS 17與新一代清償能力制度（TIS），金管會提出3大暫行措施協助平順轉換，避免接軌期間帳列資本與準備金劇烈波動。過渡安排讓壽險公司有餘裕逐步強化資本體質；長期而言，新制提升財報透明度與可比性，將牽動保險商品的定價與準備金提存方式，重塑產業經營樣貌與競爭格局。
  - 來源 / Source：[金管會](https://www.fsc.gov.tw/ch/home.jsp?id=96&parentpath=0,2)

### 💎 Wealth Management / 財富管理

- **Ultra-rich Taiwanese pile into offshore assets, CTBC-BCG study finds** — A CTBC–BCG report on Taiwan's ultra-high-net-worth clients shows offshore asset allocation has become standard at the billion-NT-dollar tier, with 86% of such clients holding overseas assets. The segment is projected to grow at an 11% CAGR through 2025–2029. The findings point to rising demand for cross-border wealth structures, trusts and private-banking services as Taiwan's wealthiest diversify beyond the domestic market.
  **台灣超高資產客群大舉海外布局 中信、BCG報告揭86%配置境外** — 中國信託攜手BCG發布《2026台灣超高資產客群財富洞察》指出，進入十億元超高資產層級後，境外資產配置幾乎成為標配，達86%；該客群2025至2029年預估年複合成長率達11%。報告反映高資產家族對跨境財富架構、信託與私人銀行服務需求升溫，台灣頂端財富正加速走向全球化與多元化配置，帶動財管業者積極搶進布局。
  - 來源 / Source：[老喬報](https://joelin.cc/?p=8309)
- **Ten life insurers cleared for wealth-management business as high-asset rules widen** — As of May 2026, ten life insurers have won FSC approval to offer wealth-management services, extending competition beyond banks. Under rules that ease product access for clients with NT$100mn or more in assets, insurers and banks are broadening offerings—structured products, offshore bonds, funds and insurance-based solutions. The push supports Taiwan's ambition to build a regional asset-management hub and deepens the high-net-worth product shelf.
  **10家壽險獲准辦理財管業務 高資產專法擴大商品範圍** — 截至2026年5月，已有新光、南山、富邦、國泰、凱基等10家壽險公司獲金管會核准辦理財富管理業務，使財管戰局由銀行延伸至保險業。在放寬資產達1億元以上高資產客戶商品條件的專法下，業者得提供結構型商品、海外債、基金與保險型方案等多元選擇。此舉呼應政府打造亞洲資產管理中心政策，深化高資產客群的產品供給與競爭。
