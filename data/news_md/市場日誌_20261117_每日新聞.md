### Taiwan Equities / 股市行情

- **Taiwan stocks post second-largest point drop in history, sinking 2,635 points** — The TAIEX plunged 2,635.04 points, or 6.03%, to close Monday at 41,040.08, its second-biggest single-day point loss ever, trailing only July's 2,953-point crash. The over-the-counter index fell to 319.12. The rout was driven by anxiety that TSMC's sharply higher capital spending will squeeze margins, compounded by a steep US semiconductor selloff the prior session and the shock of Nvidia's leadership change, which together triggered heavy foreign-investor selling across tech heavyweights.
  **台股崩2,635點 史上第二大收盤跌點** — 加權指數週一重挫2,635.04點、跌幅6.03%，收在41,040.08點，為史上第二大單日收盤跌點，僅次於今年7月的2,953點；櫃買指數同步下探319.12點。市場憂心台積電資本支出大幅上調將壓縮毛利率，加上前一交易日美股半導體股重挫、輝達執行長黃仁勳請辭的衝擊，引爆外資對權值股的沉重賣壓，全場逾九成個股收黑，投資氣氛急凍。
  - 來源 / Source：[ETtoday財經](https://finance.ettoday.net/news/3273039)
- **TSMC and Foxconn lead the sell-off as AI-spending jitters bite** — TSMC dropped NT$135, or 5.74%, to NT$2,215 after an 8,560-lot sell order slammed the stock at the close, while Foxconn slid NT$22.5, or 9.34%, to NT$218.5. Analysts pointed to worries that aggressive AI-driven capital expenditure will erode profitability, together with rumors of possible delays to Rubin-generation HBM4 supply. Both names are bellwethers for Taiwan's AI supply chain, so their weakness amplified the index's historic decline.
  **台積電、鴻海領跌 AI燒錢疑慮發酵** — 台積電終場遭8,560張大單灌壓，下跌135元、跌幅5.74%，收2,215元；鴻海重挫22.5元、跌幅9.34%，收218.5元。分析師指出，市場擔憂AI驅動的龐大資本支出將侵蝕獲利，加上Rubin世代HBM4供應時程恐延後的傳聞，使兩大AI供應鏈指標股承壓。權值雙雄同步走弱，放大了大盤的歷史級跌勢，短線須觀察外資動向與止跌訊號。

### 🏭 Industry / 產業動態

- **Brokers lift TSMC 2027 capex estimates toward US$85–90 billion** — Foreign brokerages are raising forecasts for TSMC's capital spending as AI demand accelerates. Bank of America sees 2027 capex potentially reaching US$80–85 billion, above its earlier US$78 billion view and a market consensus near US$75 billion, while UBS lifted its 2027 and 2028 estimates to US$90 billion and US$105 billion. TSMC officially guides 2026 capex at US$60–64 billion but expects 2026–2028 spending to rise significantly versus the prior three years.
  **券商上修台積電2027資本支出 上看850至900億美元** — 隨AI需求加速，外資券商陸續調高台積電資本支出預估：美銀估2027年可能達800至850億美元，高於先前的780億美元與市場共識約750億美元；瑞銀則將2027、2028年預估分別上修至900億與1,050億美元。台積電官方2026年指引維持600至640億美元，並預期2026至2028年資本支出規模將較過去三年顯著增加。資本支出攀升雖反映接單強勁，卻也引發毛利率是否受壓的疑慮，成為此波賣壓導火線。
  - 來源 / Source：[經濟日報](https://money.udn.com/money/amp/story/5607/9702316)
- **Morgan Stanley sees CoWoS capacity expanding sharply through 2028** — Morgan Stanley estimates TSMC's advanced CoWoS packaging capacity could climb to roughly 117,000 wafers per month in 2027 and 175,000 in 2028, with Nvidia and Broadcom absorbing the bulk of the output. The projected expansion underscores how custom AI silicon and GPU demand continue to drive leading-edge packaging investment, positioning Taiwan's packaging and testing supply chain as a key beneficiary even amid near-term market volatility.
  **大摩估CoWoS產能2028年倍增 輝達博通吃下大半** — 摩根士丹利預估台積電先進封裝CoWoS產能有望在2027年擴增至每月約11.7萬片、2028年進一步放大到17.5萬片，且多數產能將由輝達與博通包下。此一擴產規劃凸顯客製化AI晶片與GPU需求持續推升先進封裝投資，使台灣封測供應鏈成為中長線受惠要角；儘管近期大盤劇烈震盪，AI基建的結構性需求仍為產業提供支撐。
