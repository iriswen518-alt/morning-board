### 🏦 Financial Sector / 金融業

- **FSC sets three supervisory priorities for insurers (2026-07-22)** — The Financial Supervisory Commission outlined three focus areas for insurance oversight as the sector adopts IFRS 17 and related regimes this year: driving AI-led technology transformation, steering green-finance investment, and building an all-age financial framework with wider micro-insurance to meet super-aged-society and climate challenges. The direction pushes insurers toward modernization and long-horizon product design, shaping capital allocation and compliance priorities across the industry.
  **金管會揭保險監理三大方向（2026-07-22）** — 金管會指出，保險業今年接軌IFRS 17等新制，未來監理聚焦三面向：推動AI科技轉型、引導綠色金融投資，並建構全齡金融體系、擴大微型保險，以因應超高齡社會與氣候變遷。此方向促使壽險業加速現代化與長期商品設計，牽動全業資本配置與法遵重點。
  - 來源 / Source：[中央社](https://www.cna.com.tw/news/afe/202607220318.aspx)
- **FSC rolls out 22-item 'financial giveback' guideline (2026-07-29)** — The FSC published a financial-giveback guideline listing 22 reference measures across society, customers and employees. Banks are encouraged to cut fees and lift digital-account benefits, while insurers can design reward-linked products and offer small, fast claims and instant hospitalization payouts. The framework nudges the industry toward customer-friendly pricing and service, encouraging households to set long-term financial plans and potentially intensifying competition on fees and benefits.
  **金管會推金融大回饋、提22項參考項目（2026-07-29）** — 金管會發布金融大回饋指引，就社會、客戶、員工三面向列出22項參考項目。銀行可減少手續費、提高數位帳戶優惠；保險業可設計具回饋機制商品，或提供小額快速理賠、住院即時給付。此舉引導業者朝友善定價與服務發展，鼓勵民眾訂定長期理財規劃，也可能加劇費用與優惠競爭。
