### Taiwan Equities / 股市行情

- **Taiwan stocks tumble 529 points as TSMC slips toward NT$2,400** — Taiwan's benchmark TAIEX fell 529.15 points, or 1.13%, to close at 46,292.93 on Oct 20, with turnover near NT$758.7bn and an intraday swing of roughly 800 points. Chipmaking bellwether TSMC dropped NT$15 (0.62%) to NT$2,405, briefly breaking below NT$2,400 and shaving about 118 points off the index, while the OTC index fell 1.57% to 387.88. A looming index-futures settlement and a softer Taiwan dollar added to the selling pressure.
  **台股重挫529點 台積電逼近2,400元關卡** — 加權指數10月20日收46,292.93點，下跌529.15點、跌幅1.13%，成交值約7,587億元，盤中震盪約800點。權王台積電收2,405元、跌15元（0.62%），盤中一度跌破2,400元，拖累大盤約118點；櫃買指數跌1.57%收387.88點。台指期結算在即加上新台幣走貶，使賣壓加劇。
  - 來源 / Source：[中央社](https://www.cna.com.tw/news/afe/202610200145.aspx)
- **Foreign funds extend aggressive retreat from Taiwan equities** — Overseas investors sold a net NT$76.7bn of Taiwan shares on Oct 20 (one tally put it near NT$79.5bn), extending a retreat that has reached roughly NT$838bn over the past ten trading days. Cumulative foreign outflows from Taiwan equities have now topped NT$2.04trn year-to-date. A weakening Taiwan dollar and the approaching futures settlement amplified the exodus, leaving domestic investment trusts as the main buyers propping up the market.
  **外資連賣 近10日提款逾8,380億元** — 外資10月20日賣超台股約767億元（一說約795億元），連續賣超擴大，近10個交易日累計賣超約8,380億元；今年以來累計提款台股已突破2.04兆元。新台幣走貶與台指期結算在即加重外資撤離力道，盤面僅剩投信苦撐買盤。
  - 來源 / Source：[中時新聞網](https://www.chinatimes.com/realtimenews/20261020003242-260410)

### 🏭 Industry / 產業動態

- **Wistron bucks the sell-off as AI-server names stay resilient** — While Taiwan's broad market sank more than 500 points on Oct 20, AI-server assembler Wistron bucked the trend with a gain of about 5%, underscoring continued investor appetite for the artificial-intelligence hardware supply chain even on a risk-off day. Small- and mid-cap stocks weakened broadly, highlighting how money rotated toward large-cap AI beneficiaries while the rest of the market retreated.
  **緯創逆勢漲約5% AI伺服器族群相對抗跌** — 10月20日大盤重挫逾500點之際，AI伺服器組裝廠緯創逆勢上漲約5%，顯示資金在避險氛圍下仍聚焦AI硬體供應鏈；相對地，中小型股同步走弱，反映盤面資金向大型AI受惠股集中、其餘個股承壓的輪動格局。

### 🏦 Financial Sector / 金融業

- **Financial heavyweights lead Taiwan's market lower** — Financial-holding and banking shares were among the biggest drags on Taiwan's market on Oct 20, with large-cap financial names falling sharply as the index shed 529 points. The weakness in financials, alongside broad declines in small- and mid-cap stocks, reflected risk-averse positioning amid heavy foreign selling and a softer local currency that pressured sentiment across the sector.
  **金融權值股重挫 成10/20大盤重跌主因之一** — 10月20日台股大跌529點，金融保險類股為主要拖累之一，大型金控股跌勢明顯。在外資大量賣超、新台幣走貶的避險氛圍下，金融股與中小型股同步走弱，顯示市場風險意識明顯升高，類股資金動能轉弱。
