### 🏭 Industry / 產業動態

- **TSMC July revenue jumps 44.68% as AI demand powers Q3** — TSMC posted July sales of NT$467.58 billion, up 44.68% year on year and above the roughly 37% pace implied by the midpoint of its Q3 guidance. If August and September hold the momentum, third-quarter revenue could approach the upper end of the US$44.6–45.8 billion guidance. The company has lifted its full-year dollar-revenue outlook to grow more than 40%.
  **台積電 7 月營收年增 44.68%、AI 需求助攻 Q3** — 台積電 7 月營收 4,675.8 億元，年增 44.68%，高於第三季指引中間值隱含的約 37%。若 8、9 月維持強勁態勢，Q3 營收有機會朝 446 億至 458 億美元指引上緣靠攏。公司已將全年美元營收預期上調至成長逾 40%，AI 出口動能延續，多家基金公司同步上修台股展望。
  - 來源 / Source：[鉅亨網](https://news.cnyes.com/news/id/6568804)
- **TSMC approves US$29.4bn capital budget for 2nm and packaging** — TSMC's board approved a US$29.4 billion capital budget to expand advanced-process and advanced-packaging capacity, easing market concerns over the durability of AI demand. Management expects AI-related revenue to compound at close to 60% annually from 2024 to 2029, with 2nm production ramping quickly to meet a strong order backlog from customers such as Nvidia, AMD and Apple.

### 🏦 Financial Sector / 金融業

- **Regulators launch cross-agency "tech anti-scam" defense summit** — The Financial Supervisory Commission joined prosecutors and investigators on Aug 31 to launch a 2026 joint anti-fraud initiative, deploying technology-based tools to block scams across the whole financial system. The move deepens the bank-regulator-law-enforcement partnership as online fraud losses stay elevated, pushing banks to strengthen transaction monitoring and customer alerts.
  **金管會啟動金檢警聯防、科技阻詐** — 金管會 8/31 偕同檢調機構啟動「2026 金檢警聯防：科技阻詐，全域守護高峰會」，運用科技工具全域攔阻詐騙。此舉深化金融、監理與執法單位的防詐合作，在網路詐騙損失居高不下之際，推動銀行強化交易監控與客戶示警機制，防詐監理力道再升級。

- **FSC tightens rules on suspicious and abnormal account transactions** — The commission amended provisions of its regulations governing deposit accounts and suspected unlawful or abnormal transactions on Aug 31, sharpening the tools banks use to freeze and flag accounts linked to fraud or money laundering. Alongside reminders urging borrowers to use only licensed lending channels, the update reinforces a broader supervisory push on financial-crime prevention.
  **金管會修正疑似不法異常交易帳戶管理辦法** — 金管會 8/31 修正「存款帳戶及其疑似不法或顯屬異常交易管理辦法」部分條文，強化銀行凍結與示警涉詐、涉洗錢帳戶的工具。此前金管會亦提醒民眾申辦貸款應選擇合法管道，並公布外銀在台分行營運穩健。整體監理方向持續聚焦金融犯罪防制，銀行法遵成本可能墊高。
