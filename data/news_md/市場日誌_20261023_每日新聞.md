The rotation back into large-cap tech signals renewed risk appetite after an extended stretch of selling, keeping AI-driven semiconductor names in the market's leadership seat, though follow-through volume remains to be seen.

Domestic investment trusts extended their own multi-session buying of TSMC, reinforcing the rebound. The shift eases the overhang that had capped the index below its early-October record high near 49,700.

### 🏭 Industry / 產業動態

- **Analysts lift TSMC CoWoS capacity forecasts on strong AI accelerator demand** — Robust demand for AI accelerators has prompted analysts to raise projections for TSMC's CoWoS advanced-packaging capacity, now seen reaching roughly 140,000 wafers per month by end-2026 to meet first-quarter 2027 needs, and up to 170kwpm by end-2027 as new Chiayi and Tainan fabs ramp. Nvidia is expected to reserve over half of output, with Google TPU next in line, keeping advanced packaging the key bottleneck in the AI supply chain.
  **法人上修台積電CoWoS產能 AI加速器需求強勁** — AI加速器需求強勁，法人上修台積電CoWoS先進封裝產能預估，估2026年底達每月約14萬片以支應2027年第1季需求，待嘉義AP7、台南AP8全面運作後，2027年底總產能可達170kwpm。輝達預料持續預訂逾半產能、Google TPU居次。先進封裝仍是AI供應鏈最大瓶頸，擴產動能牽動載板、設備與封測等台廠外溢訂單與後市評價，為相關供應鏈注入中長期成長題材。
  - 來源 / Source：[經濟日報](https://money.udn.com/money/story/5607/9343218)
- **CoWoS expansion still falls short, spilling orders to OSAT players** — Even with TSMC's aggressive CoWoS build-out, advanced-packaging supply remains unable to keep pace with AI demand, pushing overflow orders toward outsourced assembly and test (OSAT) houses. The persistent shortage benefits packaging-and-testing specialists and substrate suppliers across Taiwan's ecosystem as customers scramble to secure capacity. It underscores how advanced packaging, rather than wafer fabrication alone, has become the gating factor for AI chip output into 2027.
  **CoWoS擴產仍供不應求 外溢訂單挹注OSAT封測廠** — 台積電積極擴充CoWoS先進封裝產能，仍不足以滿足AI需求，使外溢訂單轉向專業封測（OSAT）業者。供給持續吃緊，嘉惠台灣封測廠與載板供應鏈，客戶競相鎖定產能。此現象凸顯先進封裝、而非僅晶圓製造，已成為AI晶片產出的關鍵瓶頸，相關台廠在2026至2027年擴產循環中可望延續接單與營收成長動能，營運與股價評價同步受惠。
  - 來源 / Source：[科技新報](https://technews.tw/?p=1505683)

### 🏦 Financial Sector / 金融業

- **FSC raises bank SME-lending target, reports record industry profits** — At a meeting of domestic bank presidents, the Financial Supervisory Commission lifted the 2026 growth target for small-and-medium-enterprise lending to NT$500 billion from NT$460 billion, while reporting that banking, securities and insurance posted combined pre-tax profit of about NT$1.17 trillion in the first eight months, up sharply year on year. Banks' average capital adequacy stayed above statutory minimums at roughly 15.16%. The moves channel credit toward SMEs while affirming sector stability.
  **金管會上調銀行中小企業放款目標 三業獲利創高** — 金管會召開本國銀行總經理會議，將2026年對中小企業放款餘額成長目標由4,600億元上調至5,000億元，並公布今年1至8月銀行、證券期貨與保險三業合計稅前盈餘約1.17兆元，年增顯著；本國銀行第2季底平均資本適足率約15.16%，優於法定門檻。政策引導信用資源挹注中小企業，同時凸顯金融體系獲利與資本體質維持穩健，亦為後續業務鬆綁與商品創新奠定基礎。
  - 來源 / Source：[工商時報](https://wantrich.chinatimes.com/news/20261016900432-420501)
- **Taiwan plans a financial-industry LLM and an Asian asset-management hub** — The FSC outlined plans to publish a financial-industry large language model, dubbed FinLLM, in the first quarter of 2027, alongside a broader push to turn Taiwan into a regional asset-management centre. The initiative aims to give banks, insurers and brokers a shared, supervised AI foundation for compliance and services, while new cybersecurity rules define chief information security officer duties and software-supply-chain safeguards, reshaping how financial firms adopt AI.
  **台灣擬推金融業大語言模型FinLLM 建亞洲資產管理中心** — 金管會規劃2027年第1季發布金融業大型語言模型（FinLLM），並推動將台灣打造為亞洲資產管理中心。此舉盼為銀行、保險與證券業提供共用且受監理的AI基礎，應用於法遵與客戶服務；同時2026年新增資安監理要求，明定資安長權責與軟體供應鏈安全機制。政策方向將影響金融業AI導入節奏與資安投資，並為資產管理業務與金融科技創新開拓成長空間。

### 💎 Wealth Management / 財富管理

- **Taishin wins global wealth awards as high-asset needs shift to balance-sheet management** — Taishin Bank took twin honours at the PBI global wealth-management awards, citing AI-driven innovation and deeper high-net-worth services. The bank said on Oct 12 that wealthy clients' needs have moved beyond traditional product allocation toward integrated asset-and-liability management, with greater emphasis on asset protection, cross-border allocation, family governance and generational succession. The trend pushes banks toward advisory, trust and family-office capabilities rather than simple product sales.
  **台新奪全球財管大獎 高資產需求轉向資產負債整合** — 台新銀行以AI創新與深化高資產服務，一舉摘下PBI全球財富管理大獎雙獎。該行10月12日指出，高資產客戶需求已從傳統投資商品配置，轉向資產與負債整合管理，更重視資產保全、跨境配置、家族治理與世代傳承。此趨勢促使銀行財管業務從單純銷售商品，升級為顧問、信託與家族辦公室整合服務，牽動人才布局與數位平台投資，成為業者爭取頂端客群的關鍵戰場。
  - 來源 / Source：[台新銀行](https://www.taishinbank.com.tw/TSB/personal/common/news/TSBankNews-008407/)
- **Taiwan's high-net-worth population swells, cross-border allocation goes mainstream** — Industry reports from KPMG and CTBC estimate Taiwan's population with assets above NT$100 million has climbed past 120,000 and could reach about 124,000 in 2026, with more than 70% already holding offshore assets. Total private wealth among the ultra-rich is projected to grow roughly 10% a year through 2029. The surge is driving demand for family-office services, cross-border structuring and succession planning across private banks.
  **台灣高資產人口快速攀升 跨境配置成主流** — KPMG與中信等機構報告估計，台灣資產逾新台幣1億元的高資產人數已突破12萬人，2026年可能達約12.4萬人，且逾七成已布局海外資產；超高資產族群個人財富總額預估至2029年將以年均約10%成長。人數與財富同步擴張，帶動家族辦公室服務、跨境資產結構規劃與世代傳承需求升溫，各私人銀行競相強化信託、海外債與結構型商品等高階服務，以爭取頂端客群並提升手續費收入。
