### 🏭 Industry / 產業動態

The projects cover robotic arms and mobile robots, with customers prizing multi-year component-supply guarantees when designing long-life equipment.

- **Apple AI-glasses clues grow as an equipment supplier confirms involvement** — Chinese manufacturing-equipment maker Quick Intelligent (603203) told its Q3 briefing it is involved in Apple's AI-glasses and a new smart-earbuds project, expecting a lift to precision welding/assembly and machine-vision inspection work, though it disclosed no specs, order size or launch date. ### 🏦 Financial Sector / 金融業

- **FSC eases financial-holding capital rules to steady insurance-led groups** — Taiwan's Financial Supervisory Commission amended its financial-holding capital-adequacy rules so eight insurance-led financial holding companies can apply the same 15-year transition measure as their life-insurance subsidiaries when computing capital adequacy, and extended the filing deadline for eleven insurance-type holdings by a month. The move aims to stabilize parent-company capital and support dividend capacity ahead of new solvency standards.
  **金管會鬆綁金控資本適足，穩壽險型母金控體質** — 金管會修正金控資本適足性管理辦法，讓八家壽險型金控在計算資本適足率時，比照旗下壽險子公司適用十五年過渡措施，並將十一家保險型金控的申報期限延後一個月。此舉在新一代清償能力制度上路前，有助穩定母金控資本、支撐配息能力，兼顧審慎監理與集團穩健經營，對壽險型金控的資金調度與股利政策具正面影響。
  - 來源 / Source：[經濟日報](https://money.udn.com/money/story/5613/9199813)
- **FSC flags first-half examination shortcomings across banks and holdings** — The FSC published the deficiencies found in its first-half 2026 financial examinations, spanning ten business categories from holding companies to domestic banks. Findings centered on four areas—fraud and anti-money-laundering controls, consumer protection, internal management and information security—including cases where some banks failed to make clear that mortgage life insurance is optional rather than a lending condition.
  **金管會公布上半年金檢缺失，點名銀行與金控** — 金管會公布 2026 年上半年金融檢查缺失，涵蓋金控、國銀等十個業別，聚焦防制詐騙與洗錢、消費者保護、內部管理及資安四大領域。其中點名部分銀行辦理房貸時，未清楚說明房貸壽險可自主選擇，使客戶誤以為是核貸條件。金管會要求業者落實改善，強化消費者權益保障與內控法遵，對銀行作業流程與金融消保具提醒意義。
  - 來源 / Source：[工商時報](https://ctee.com.tw/news/20260930700532-430301)

- **High-asset wealth management keeps expanding as banks court private-banking clients** — Taiwan's high-asset wealth-management AUM reached NT$2.0767 trillion, up 50.63% year-on-year (as of end-November 2025), as banks deepen services for wealthy clients.
