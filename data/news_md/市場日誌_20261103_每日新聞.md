### 💰 Tax & Regulations / 稅務法規

- **Estate-tax reform shifts liability to gift recipients on pre-death transfers** — Under Taiwan's amended Estate and Gift Tax Act, assets gifted to a spouse, heirs or their spouses within two years before death must be folded back into the taxable estate, with the recipient—not the estate—now liable for the related tax, capped at the value of what they received. The reform also removes the prior exemption for those who disclaim inheritance and scraps the NT$300,000 threshold for paying tax in instalments, while the Finance Ministry drafts implementing sub-regulations.
  **擬制遺產修法 稅負改由受贈人負擔** — 依《遺產及贈與稅法》修正，被繼承人死亡前2年內贈與配偶、各順序繼承人及其配偶的財產須併入遺產總額課稅，並改由受贈人（而非遺產）負擔相關遺產稅，稅額以其實際領到的受贈財產價值為上限。修法並取消拋棄繼承者的免責規定，以及申請分期繳納須達30萬元的門檻。財政部正研擬施行細則等子法規，牽動高資產族群的贈與與傳承規劃布局。
