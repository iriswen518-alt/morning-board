### 🏭 Industry / 產業動態

- **TSMC builds advanced-packaging validation center in Kaohsiung** — TSMC is setting up an advanced-packaging validation center at the Kaohsiung Baipu industrial park, bringing together Taiwanese equipment and materials makers. The hub aims to lift local suppliers' technology and capacity so they can jointly deliver complete advanced-packaging solutions and capture demand from fast-iterating AI chips.
  **台積電於高雄設先進封裝驗證中心** — 台積電在高雄白埔產業園區建立先進封裝驗證中心，集結本土設備、材料業者，加速提升在地供應鏈的技術、能量，共同提供完整先進封裝解決方案，搶攻AI晶片快速迭代所帶來的封裝需求商機，強化台灣半導體產業聚落。
  - 來源 / Source：[Newtalk新聞](https://newtalk.tw/news/view/2026-09-22/1061159)
- **Taiwan earnings seen up 50% on AI demand** — Bloomberg now forecasts Taiwan corporate earnings will grow about 50% this year, nearly triple the 18% expected at end-2025, with analysts citing especially strong NVIDIA-related demand. The upgrade underscores how AI infrastructure spending is reshaping the earnings outlook for Taiwan's tech supply chain.
  **AI需求推升 台企獲利估增50%** — 彭博最新預估台灣企業今年獲利將成長約50%，幾乎是2025年底預估18%的近三倍，分析師特別點名輝達（NVIDIA）相關需求強勁。此次大幅上修凸顯AI基礎建設投資正重塑台灣科技供應鏈的獲利展望，也支撐台股評價續揚。

### 🏦 Financial Sector / 金融業

- **FSC opens bank trial for deposit-token business** — The Financial Supervisory Commission will formally allow banks to pilot deposit-token services, a step toward tokenized bank deposits. Alongside it, the regulator plans a "stock-market dashboard" consolidating margin-financing and market-leverage data, default statistics and listed-company revenue trends to improve market transparency.
  **金管會開放銀行試辦存款代幣** — 金管會將正式開放銀行試辦「存款代幣」業務，朝銀行存款代幣化邁進。同時規劃推出「股市儀表板」，彙整融資授信與市場槓桿數據、違約人數統計及上市櫃營收走勢，提升市場資訊透明度，協助投資人掌握整體風險與資金動能。
  - 來源 / Source：[華視新聞](https://news.cts.com.tw/cna/money/202609/202609223081702.html)
- **FSC pushes market excellence plan toward Asia asset hub** — FSC Chairman Peng Chin-lung outlined a "market excellence development plan" with five pillars: advancing an Asian asset-management center, building a world-class capital market, and promoting all-age finance, trust-based finance and "three-track" finance. The blueprint aims to deepen Taiwan's capital markets and cement its push to become a regional asset-management hub.
  **金管會市場卓越計畫 拚亞資中心** — 金管會主委彭金隆提出「市場卓越發展計畫」，涵蓋五大面向：持續推動亞資中心、打造國際級資本市場，以及推動全齡金融、信任金融與三軌金融。此藍圖意在深化台灣資本市場結構，鞏固邁向區域級資產管理中心的政策目標與長期競爭力。

- **Active ETFs keep gaining ground in Taiwan** — Since Taiwan opened active ETF investing in 2025, 40 active ETFs are now listed — 22 active Taiwan-equity products, 10 overseas-equity and 8 bond ETFs.
