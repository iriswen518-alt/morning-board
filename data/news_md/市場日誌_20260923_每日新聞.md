### 💰 Tax & Regulations / 稅務法規

- **President signs estate- and gift-tax amendment tightening deemed-estate rules** — President Lai has promulgated amendments to the Estate and Gift Tax Act adding "deemed estate" provisions. The Ministry of Finance flagged four key points, chiefly that property gifted to a spouse or statutory heirs within two years before death is now treated as part of the decedent's existing estate for tax purposes, closing a common transfer loophole.
  **賴總統公布遺贈稅法修正 強化擬制遺產** — 總統賴清德公布遺產及贈與稅法部分條文修正，增訂「擬制遺產」規範。財政部釋出四大重點，核心為被繼承人死亡前2年內贈與配偶或民法各順序繼承人的財產，視為現存遺產併入課稅，堵住生前移轉的常見缺口，高資產族群的傳承規劃須重新檢視時點與架構。
  - 來源 / Source：[Yahoo奇摩股市](https://tw.stock.yahoo.com/news/%E8%B3%B4%E7%B8%BD%E7%B5%B1%E5%85%AC%E5%B8%83%E9%81%BA%E8%B4%88%E7%A8%85%E6%B3%95%E4%BF%AE%E6%AD%A3-%E8%B2%A1%E9%83%A8%E9%87%8B4%E5%A4%A7%E9%87%8D%E9%BB%9E%E5%B0%87%E5%8A%A0%E5%BC%B7%E5%AE%A3%E5%B0%8E-030642565.html)
- **Gifts to spouse and heirs within two years must be added back to estate** — Tax authorities reiterated that under Article 15, property gifted within two years before death to a spouse, statutory heirs of each order and their spouses is treated as part of the estate and must be included in the estate total. An exception applies where the spousal or kinship relationship no longer exists at the time of death.
  **死亡前2年內贈與親屬須併入遺產申報** — 國稅局重申，依遺產及贈與稅法第15條，被繼承人死亡前2年內贈與配偶、民法各順序繼承人及其配偶的財產，視為遺產併入遺產總額課稅；惟若配偶或親屬關係於被繼承人死亡時已消滅者，得免併入申報，提醒民眾辦理繼承時務必留意贈與時點與親屬關係認定。
