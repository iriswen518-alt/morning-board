### 🏭 Industry / 產業動態

- **Taiwan's September exports hit record US$87.2bn, up 60.9% YoY** — The Ministry of Finance reported September exports of US$87.22 billion, an all-time monthly high and a 60.9% annual jump that extended the record winning streak to 35 straight months. ### 🏦 Financial Sector / 金融業

### 💎 Wealth Management / 財富管理

- **FSC widens insurers' "health and welfare" investment scope** — The FSC issued a ruling, effective immediately, letting insurers invest in health-and-welfare businesses without the prior requirement that the services link to their own policyholders. Long-term-care advisory, assistive-living and smart-aid ventures now qualify, and insurers may hold up to 100% of unlisted targets, channelling long-term capital into the aging-society economy.
  **金管會擴大保險業「健康福祉事業」投資範疇** — 金管會發布令釋即日生效，取消投資對象須與保單業務連結的限制，服務不再限於保戶。長照顧問、輔助服務與智慧生活輔具納入範圍，非上市櫃標的最高可100%持股，引導壽險長期資金投入高齡化經濟。
  - 來源 / Source：[優分析 uAnalyze](https://uanalyze.com.tw/articles/4063056619)
- **FSC signals looser rules and pricing flexibility for aging-society insurance** — Facing a super-aged society, the FSC said it will push regulatory easing and relax insurance-product pricing to encourage protection and long-term-care coverage. The direction aims to steer the industry from pure claims-payer toward prevention and elder-care services, opening new wealth- and retirement-planning product lines.
  **因應高齡化 金管會將推法規鬆綁與保險定價放寬** — 金管會透露，因應超高齡社會將推動法規鬆綁並放寬保險商品定價彈性，鼓勵保障型與長照型保單。政策方向盼使保險業自單純理賠角色延伸至事前預防與長照服務，為財富與退休規劃開拓新型商品線。

### 💰 Tax & Regulations / 稅務法規

- **Legislature passes "deemed estate" tax amendment; MOF drafts implementing rules** — Taiwan's legislature gave final reading to amendments on the taxation of "deemed estates," tightening how certain transfers before death are counted into taxable estates. The Ministry of Finance said it is revising the estate-and-gift tax enforcement rules and filing forms, and will step up guidance once the president promulgates the law, affecting succession and wealth-transfer planning.
  **立院三讀「擬制遺產」課稅修法 財部研擬修正子法** — 立法院三讀通過擬制遺產課稅修正，收緊死亡前特定財產移轉計入遺產稅的認定。財政部表示正研擬修正遺產及贈與稅法施行細則與申報書表，待總統公布後加強宣導，牽動高資產族群的傳承與財富移轉規劃。
  - 來源 / Source：[中央社 CNA](https://www.cna.com.tw/news/afe/202608210289.aspx)
- **Property transaction tax overtakes land value tax as top real-estate revenue** — Finance Ministry data show the consolidated housing-and-land income tax has become the largest source of real-estate transaction revenue, with its share climbing above 50% to 52.4% in 2025, while land value increment tax fell to a 16-year low. The shift reflects how the anti-speculation regime now dominates property-related tax collection.
  **房地合一稅躍居房地交易最大稅源 占比衝破5成** — 財政部統計顯示，房地合一稅已超越土增稅成為房地交易最大稅源，2025年占比突破5成達52.4%，土增稅則跌至16年新低。顯示打炒房稅制已主導不動產相關稅收結構，影響房市交易與持有成本評估。
