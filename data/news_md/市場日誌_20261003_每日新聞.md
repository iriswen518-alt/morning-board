### 🏦 Financial Sector / 金融業

- **Taiwan's FSC rolls out a packed October agenda** — Taiwan's Financial Supervisory Commission is launching a cluster of measures from October into early next year, including the Kaohsiung asset-management special zone and a virtual-asset supervisory regime. Draft rules under the Virtual Asset Service Act head to public hearings in October, with regulatory pre-announcement slated for November and December, setting the compliance roadmap for operators.
  **金管會10月政策大爆發、高雄專區與虛擬資產接力上路** — 金管會多項措施將自10月起一路排至明年首季，包括亞資中心高雄專區與虛擬資產監理制度。《虛擬資產服務法》九大子法已完成內部討論，預計10月啟動公聽程序、11至12月進入法規預告，為業者合規與市場發展定調。
  - 來源 / Source：[聯合新聞網](https://udn.com/news/story/7239/9771565)
- **Banks and brokers to share credit data from late October** — A cross-industry credit-inquiry mechanism between banks and securities firms goes live at end-October; the FSC stressed it is information-sharing, not a "four-loan" lending cap. Separately, domestic banks must file monthly reports on personal working-capital loans from October, targeting funds booked as working capital but funneled into stocks and funds, tightening credit oversight.
  **銀行、券商跨業信用查詢10月底上路** — 銀行與證券商跨業信用查詢機制將於10月底上線，金管會澄清這並非「四貸同堂」管制、僅為信用資訊交流，助掌握客戶負債與信用風險。另要求本國銀行自10月起按月申報個人理財周轉金貸款，鎖定掛名周轉金卻流入股票、基金的資金，強化授信控管。
  - 來源 / Source：[NOWnews](https://nownews.com/news/6879792)

### 💎 Wealth Management / 財富管理

- **Kaohsiung asset-management zone draws 57 firms and 21 banks** — Taiwan's Kaohsiung asset-management special zone has attracted 57 financial institutions (21 of them banks) since its July 2025 launch; its core family-office business now serves 161 clients with nearly NT$71.2 billion under mandate. The FSC recently approved Bank of Taiwan, Taiwan Business Bank and Far Eastern Bank for high-asset wealth 2.0 operations.
  **高雄亞資中心進駐57家機構、家族辦公室受託逾712億** — 亞資中心高雄專區自2025年7月掛牌以來已有57家金融機構進駐、其中21家為銀行，核心「家族辦公室」業務已服務161家客戶、受託管理近712億元。金管會近期再核准臺灣銀行、臺灣企銀與遠東銀行辦理高資產財管2.0，推動高資產傳承與跨境配置新藍海。
  - 來源 / Source：[自由時報財經週報](https://ec.ltn.com.tw/article/paper/1757000)
- **Private banks race into the high-net-worth wealth hub** — Private-sector banks are jostling for the high-net-worth "blue ocean," rolling out family-office and one-stop succession services in the Kaohsiung zone. Chang Hwa Bank opened a Kaohsiung high-asset wealth center offering leverage optimization and succession planning, while KGI Bank's zone operation marked its first anniversary with assets under management nearing NT$40 billion.
  **民營銀行搶灘高資產財管新藍海** — 民營銀行競相布局高資產「新藍海」，於高雄專區推出家族辦公室與一站式傳承服務。彰銀高雄高資產財管中心揭幕，主打槓桿優化與家族傳承一站式服務；凱基銀行亞資中心進駐屆滿週年，高資產客戶資產管理規模已逼近400億元，顯示跨境配置與傳承商機持續升溫。
