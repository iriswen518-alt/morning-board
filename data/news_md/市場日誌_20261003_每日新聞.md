### 🏦 Financial Sector / 金融業

- **Taiwan's FSC rolls out a packed October agenda** — Taiwan's Financial Supervisory Commission is launching a cluster of measures from October into early next year, including the Kaohsiung asset-management special zone and a virtual-asset supervisory regime. Draft rules under the Virtual Asset Service Act head to public hearings in October, with regulatory pre-announcement slated for November and December, setting the compliance roadmap for operators.
  **金管會10月政策大爆發、高雄專區與虛擬資產接力上路** — 金管會多項措施將自10月起一路排至明年首季，包括亞資中心高雄專區與虛擬資產監理制度。《虛擬資產服務法》九大子法已完成內部討論，預計10月啟動公聽程序、11至12月進入法規預告，為業者合規與市場發展定調。
  - 來源 / Source：[聯合新聞網](https://udn.com/news/story/7239/9771565)
- **Banks and brokers to share credit data from late October** — A cross-industry credit-inquiry mechanism between banks and securities firms goes live at end-October; the FSC stressed it is information-sharing, not a "four-loan" lending cap. Separately, domestic banks must file monthly reports on personal working-capital loans from October, targeting funds booked as working capital but funneled into stocks and funds, tightening credit oversight.
  **銀行、券商跨業信用查詢10月底上路** — 銀行與證券商跨業信用查詢機制將於10月底上線，金管會澄清這並非「四貸同堂」管制、僅為信用資訊交流，助掌握客戶負債與信用風險。另要求本國銀行自10月起按月申報個人理財周轉金貸款，鎖定掛名周轉金卻流入股票、基金的資金，強化授信控管。
  - 來源 / Source：[NOWnews](https://nownews.com/news/6879792)

### 💰 Tax & Regulations / 稅務法規

- **2026 estate- and gift-tax thresholds held unchanged** — Taiwan's 2026 estate-tax exemption stays at NT$13.33 million, with a spousal deduction of NT$5.53 million and brackets of 10% (up to NT$56.21m), 15% and 20%. The parameters were not adjusted for inflation this year, so high-net-worth families should mind bracket thresholds and timing when planning intergenerational transfers.
  **2026年遺產稅、贈與稅免稅額維持不變** — 財政部公告，2026年度遺產稅免稅額維持1,333萬元，配偶扣除額553萬元；課稅級距為10%（5,621萬元以下）、15%與20%。今年免稅額與各項扣除額未隨物價上漲調整，高資產家庭在規劃世代傳承時，應留意級距門檻與移轉時點的稅負影響。
  - 來源 / Source：[財政部賦稅署](https://www.dot.gov.tw/singlehtml/ch26?cntId=4c40feb43876418f94ac0cb8dbe4ea86)

### 📜 Macro & Policy / 總經政策

- **Taiwan's August exports hit a record US$82.4bn, up 41%** — Exports jumped to a monthly record of US$82.4 billion in August, rising 9.4% on the month and 41.0% on the year for a 34th straight annual gain. Electronic components led with a record US$32.22bn (+58% y/y) on strong advanced-chip and memory demand, while ICT shipments rose 41.8% on AI servers and networking gear.
  **台灣8月出口824億美元創歷史新高、年增41%** — 8月出口衝上824.0億美元、月增9.4%、年增41.0%，連34個月正成長。電子零組件以創紀錄的322.2億美元（年增58%）領軍，受先進晶片與記憶體需求強勁帶動；資通與視聽產品亦年增41.8%，反映AI伺服器與網通設備需求延續，外需為台灣經濟提供強勁動能。
  - 來源 / Source：[uAnalyze 進出口統計](https://uanalyze.com.tw/articles/5400254964)
- **Central bank lifts its 2026 inflation forecast** — At its September board meeting, Taiwan's central bank revised up its 2026 forecasts, putting headline CPI at about 2.03% and core CPI at 2.16%, citing resilient domestic demand and robust export-driven activity. The upward revision signals policymakers see price pressures running slightly firmer than earlier projected while growth momentum holds.
  **央行上修2026年通膨預測** — 央行9月理監事會上修今年預測，估全年消費者物價指數（CPI）年增率約2.03%、核心CPI約2.16%，反映內需具韌性與出口動能強勁。此次上修顯示政策當局認為物價壓力略高於先前預期，但整體經濟成長動能仍維持穩健。
