### 🏦 Financial Sector / 金融業

- **FSC sets 2026 exam focus on fraud, AML and cyber-resilience** — Taiwan's Financial Supervisory Commission flagged four priority areas for its 2026 financial examinations—anti-fraud, anti-money-laundering, financial consumer protection and cyber-security resilience—spanning a combined 113 inspection points. Domestic banks and foreign branches will also face tighter account-opening scrutiny on customer location and purpose, signalling heightened supervisory expectations for the year ahead.
  **金管會2026金檢聚焦防詐、防洗錢與資安韌性** — 金管會公布2026年金檢重點，將防制詐騙、防制洗錢、金融消費者權益保護及資通安全韌性列為四大特別關注領域，合計113項重點。國銀與外銀在台分行另須強化客戶開戶的地緣性與目的合理性審查，顯示主管機關對明年金融監理要求更趨嚴格，業者須提早布局因應。
  - 來源 / Source：[經濟日報](https://money.udn.com/money/story/5613/9222164)
- **New insurance accounting regime and reporting relief take effect in 2026** — From 2026 Taiwan's insurers adopt the IFRS 17 and ICS solvency frameworks, which the FSC expects will gradually phase out long-term guaranteed policies. To ease the heavier revenue-calculation workload, most financial holding companies may delay monthly revenue disclosure from the 10th to the 15th, though groups such as Waterland, E.Sun and SinoPac must still report by the 10th.
  **保險業2026導入IFRS與ICS新制 營收申報放寬至15日** — 2026年起保險業正式適用IFRS與ICS清償能力新制，金管會預期長期保證型保單將逐步退場。考量新制使保險收入計算更繁瑣，多數金控月營收申報期限自每月10日放寬至15日，惟國票金、玉山金、永豐金等仍須於10日前公告，牽動壽險業商品策略與財報揭露節奏。

### 💎 Wealth Management / 財富管理

- **Taiwan's high-net-worth pool keeps expanding into 2026, KPMG says** — A KPMG report citing trust-association data put domestic bank trust assets at NT$9.83 trillion as of Q4 2025, up 7% year-on-year. Residents with over NT$100 million in assets are estimated to have risen from about 93,000 in 2023 to 118,000 in 2025 and may reach 124,000 in 2026, with roughly 74% already holding offshore assets—underscoring demand for cross-border allocation and succession planning.
  **KPMG：台灣高資產族群2026持續擴張 信託規模達9.83兆** — KPMG引用信託公會統計指出，截至2025年第4季國內銀行信託業務規模達9.83兆元、年增7%。資產逾1億元的高資產人口估由2023年約9.3萬人增至2025年11.8萬人，2026年上看12.4萬人，其中約74%已持有境外資產，顯示跨境資產配置與世代傳承需求帶動財管業務成長。
  - 來源 / Source：[KPMG Taiwan](https://kpmg.com/tw/zh/media/press-releases/2026/06/2026-taiwan-wealth-management-trends-high-net-worth-growth.html)
- **Taishin Bank wins double PBI global wealth-management awards** — Taishin Bank secured two honours at the Private Banker International (PBI) global wealth-management awards, recognising its deepened high-net-worth services and AI-driven innovation. The bank noted affluent clients' needs have shifted from traditional product allocation toward integrated asset-and-liability management, asset preservation, cross-border allocation and generational transfer—trends reshaping Taiwan's private-banking competition.
  **台新銀行深化高資產服務 奪PBI全球財富管理雙獎** — 台新銀行以深化高資產客戶服務及AI創新，一舉拿下PBI（Private Banker International）全球財富管理大獎雙獎。該行指出，高資產客戶需求已從傳統投資商品配置，轉向資產負債整合管理，並更重視資產保全、跨境配置與世代傳承，反映國內私人銀行競爭主軸正加速轉變。
