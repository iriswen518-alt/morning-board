### 🏦 Financial Sector / 金融業

- **FSC lays out six policy priorities in legislative report** — FSC chair Peng Chin-lung's report to the legislature set six priorities: financial resilience and risk supervision, market development and corporate governance, consumer protection, the Asian asset-management hub, and fintech innovation. Officials noted domestic banks' average capital adequacy ratio stood at 15.16% in Q2, with an August NPL ratio of 0.14%, signalling a sound banking system heading into year-end.
  **金管會端出六大施政方向 銀行體質穩健** — 金管會主委彭金隆向立法院財委會提出書面報告，列出六大施政方向，涵蓋金融韌性與風險監理、市場發展與公司治理、消費者保護、亞洲資產管理中心及金融科技創新。報告指出，本國銀行第 2 季底平均資本適足率 15.16%，8 月底逾放比 0.14%，均符合法定標準，顯示整體銀行體質穩健，監理重心轉向韌性與創新並重。
  - 來源 / Source：[工商時報](https://www.ctee.com.tw/news/20261016701613-430301)
- **FSC targets finance-sector LLM and virtual-asset custody rules** — The FSC said it plans to release a first version of a financial-industry large language model in the first quarter of 2027, while the Bankers Association drafts rules for virtual-asset custody. The regulator is also weighing tax incentives to expand participation in the TISA savings scheme and tightening cybersecurity requirements, including making zero-trust architecture mandatory for financial institutions.
  **金管會推金融業大語言模型與虛擬資產保管規範** — 金管會表示，預計 2027 年第 1 季發布首版金融業務知識大語言模型，並由銀行公會規劃虛擬資產保管業務相關規範。金管會也將與相關部會研商適合本國特性的租稅鼓勵機制，擴大 TISA 參與，市場傳出最高免稅額以 2.4 萬元呼聲最高；同時強化資安監理，將零信任架構由鼓勵轉為必備，提升金融機構防護。

### 💰 Tax & Regulations / 稅務法規

- **Taiwan rolls out NT$13.9 billion in income-tax relief for 2026 filing** — Taiwan's Ministry of Finance applies a new personal income-tax framework for the 2026 filing season, lifting the basic living-expense allowance to NT$213,000 per person from NT$210,000 and expanding the long-term-care special deduction. The package delivers roughly NT$13.9 billion in relief aimed at easing household burdens, part of a broader adjustment of thresholds and deductions indexed to living costs.
  **台灣 2026 報稅上路 釋出 139 億減稅利多** — 財政部針對 2026 年報稅季適用新制，將每人基本生活費由 21 萬元調高至 21.3 萬元，並加碼長照特別扣除額。整體釋出約 139 億元減稅利多，以減輕家庭負擔；相關扣除額與課稅級距依物價連動調整，為近年綜所稅調整幅度較大的一次，納稅人申報時可留意新制適用情形與試算結果。
  - 來源 / Source：[工商時報](https://ctee.com.tw/news/tax-law/502473.html)
- **Gift-tax exemption held at NT$2.44 million as brackets widen** — Taiwan has confirmed its 2026 gift-tax settings, keeping the annual exemption at NT$2.44 million while widening tax brackets in line with inflation adjustments. The move gives high-net-worth families more room for intergenerational transfers before higher rates apply, reinforcing succession-planning strategies even as overall estate- and gift-tax rules stay broadly stable year on year.
  **贈與稅免稅額維持 244 萬 課稅級距隨物價放寬** — 台灣 2026 年贈與稅制度確立，年度免稅額延續 244 萬元不變，課稅級距則因物價指數調整而放寬。此舉讓高資產家庭在適用較高稅率前，有更大的財富移轉空間，有助於家族傳承與贈與規劃；整體遺贈稅規則大致維持穩定，傳承稅務安排仍以善用年度免稅額與級距調整為重點。
  - 來源 / Source：[康健雜誌](https://www.commonhealth.com.tw/article/93913)

### 📜 Macro & Policy / 總經政策

- **Taiwan's central bank holds rate at 2% for a tenth straight quarter** — At its September policy meeting, Taiwan's central bank kept the discount rate at 2%, unchanged for a tenth consecutive quarter, while signalling its monetary operations are tilting toward tightening. Governor Yang Chin-long said Taiwan would not follow the US Fed's move, choosing to weigh domestic growth and inflation on its own path as AI-driven exports keep momentum strong.
  **台灣央行利率連 10 季凍結於 2% 操作偏緊** — 台灣央行在 9 月理監事會維持重貼現率 2%，已連續第 10 季按兵不動，但表示貨幣操作朝緊縮方向移動。總裁楊金龍指出，台灣不跟進美國聯準會動作，將依國內經濟成長與通膨「走自己的路」。在 AI 相關出口帶動下，成長動能強勁，央行以審慎態度因應通膨與資金情勢，政策基調維持穩中偏緊。
  - 來源 / Source：[Uanalyze](https://uanalyze.com.tw/articles/9516755544)
- **Central bank lifts growth forecast as inflation nears the 2% line** — Taiwan's central bank raised its GDP growth forecast on the back of AI-related exports and investment, with first-half growth running in double digits. It also nudged its CPI forecast up to around 2.03% and core CPI to about 2.16%, both slightly above the 2% alert level, pointing to sticky service-sector prices and elevated international oil costs as the key uncertainties ahead.
  **央行上修成長預測 通膨貼近 2% 警戒線** — 台灣央行因 AI 相關出口與投資強勁而上修 GDP 成長預測，上半年成長達雙位數。央行同時將 CPI 預測上修至約 2.03%、核心 CPI 約 2.16%，均略高於 2% 警戒線。央行示警服務類通膨偏黏著、國際油價仍高為主要不確定因素，顯示在高成長環境下仍須留意物價壓力與輸入性通膨風險，貨幣操作維持穩中偏緊。
  - 來源 / Source：[Uanalyze](https://uanalyze.com.tw/articles/9497355553)
