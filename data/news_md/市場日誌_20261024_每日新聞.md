### Taiwan Equities / 股市行情

TSMC, which accounts for more than 40% of total market capitalization, eased slightly on the day even as the broader index advanced, underscoring how AI-related demand continues to anchor investor sentiment toward Taiwan's export-heavy technology sector.
  **台股再創新高 權值晶片股領軍（2026-09-22，最近可確認交易日）** — 台股加權指數攻上約 47,800 點的歷史新高，連續第五個交易日收紅，主要由大型半導體權值股帶動。占大盤市值逾四成的台積電當日小幅回檔，但指數仍收高，顯示 AI 相關需求持續支撐市場對台灣科技出口類股的信心，資金對高權值電子股偏好不變。

### 🏭 Industry / 產業動態

- **SEMICON Taiwan gathers 65 nations around AI-era chip collaboration** — At SEMICON Taiwan, SEMI said AI-driven demand keeps lifting semiconductor needs, with roughly 89 fabs under construction worldwide and 80-plus more expected between 2030 and 2035. Officials stressed Taiwan's complete industry cluster and AI supply chain keep it a trusted partner, as the island ranks among the world's top exporters on the strength of chips and AI-related hardware.
  **SEMICON Taiwan 集結 65 國 聚焦 AI 時代晶片合作** — SEMI 在 SEMICON Taiwan 指出，AI 浪潮持續推升半導體需求，全球約有 89 座晶圓廠興建中，2030 至 2035 年還將新增 80 多座。主辦方強調，台灣擁有完整產業聚落與 AI 供應鏈，仍是全球可信賴的合作夥伴；在晶片與 AI 相關硬體帶動下，台灣已躋身全球主要出口國之列，聚落優勢短期難以取代。
  - 來源 / Source：[經濟日報](https://udn.com/news/story/7240/9644383)
- **MIC sees Taiwan chip output hitting record NT$7.1 trillion** — Taiwan's Market Intelligence & Consulting Institute projects the island's semiconductor output value will reach a record NT$7.1 trillion this year, with foundry output around NT$4.6 trillion and packaging-and-test near NT$779 billion. MIC estimates Taiwan still commands about 83% of global advanced capacity, highlighting the sector's central role as AI demand sustains orders across the chip supply chain.
  **資策會 MIC 估台灣半導體產值衝 7.1 兆創高** — 資策會 MIC 預估，今年台灣半導體產值可望再創新高、達新台幣 7.1 兆元，其中晶圓代工約 4.6 兆元、封測約 7,787 億元。MIC 指出，台灣在晶圓代工與先進封裝具不可替代性，目前約占全球八成三產能。隨 AI 需求延續，相關供應鏈訂單可見度仍高，支撐產業維持成長動能與資本支出力道。
  - 來源 / Source：[經濟日報](https://udn.com/news/story/7240/9470063)

### 🏦 Financial Sector / 金融業

- **FSC lays out six policy priorities in legislative report** — FSC chair Peng Chin-lung's report to the legislature set six priorities: financial resilience and risk supervision, market development and corporate governance, consumer protection, the Asian asset-management hub, and fintech innovation. Officials noted domestic banks' average capital adequacy ratio stood at 15.16% in Q2, with an August NPL ratio of 0.14%, signalling a sound banking system heading into year-end.
  **金管會端出六大施政方向 銀行體質穩健** — 金管會主委彭金隆向立法院財委會提出書面報告，列出六大施政方向，涵蓋金融韌性與風險監理、市場發展與公司治理、消費者保護、亞洲資產管理中心及金融科技創新。報告指出，本國銀行第 2 季底平均資本適足率 15.16%，8 月底逾放比 0.14%，均符合法定標準，顯示整體銀行體質穩健，監理重心轉向韌性與創新並重。
  - 來源 / Source：[工商時報](https://www.ctee.com.tw/news/20261016701613-430301)
- **FSC targets finance-sector LLM and virtual-asset custody rules** — The FSC said it plans to release a first version of a financial-industry large language model in the first quarter of 2027, while the Bankers Association drafts rules for virtual-asset custody. The regulator is also weighing tax incentives to expand participation in the TISA savings scheme and tightening cybersecurity requirements, including making zero-trust architecture mandatory for financial institutions.
  **金管會推金融業大語言模型與虛擬資產保管規範** — 金管會表示，預計 2027 年第 1 季發布首版金融業務知識大語言模型，並由銀行公會規劃虛擬資產保管業務相關規範。金管會也將與相關部會研商適合本國特性的租稅鼓勵機制，擴大 TISA 參與，市場傳出最高免稅額以 2.4 萬元呼聲最高；同時強化資安監理，將零信任架構由鼓勵轉為必備，提升金融機構防護。
  - 來源 / Source：[NOWnews](https://www.nownews.com/news/6884087)

### 💎 Wealth Management / 財富管理

- **Taishin wins global private-banking awards as HNW demand shifts** — Taishin Bank took two honours at the PBI Global Wealth Awards, citing deeper high-net-worth services and AI-driven innovation. The bank said HNW clients are moving from traditional product allocation toward integrated asset-and-liability management, and that its private-banking client count and assets under management grew sharply in the first half of 2026, reflecting rising demand for holistic wealth and succession planning.
  **台新奪全球私銀大獎 高資產需求轉向整合管理** — 台新銀行以深化高資產服務與 AI 創新，獲 PBI 全球財富管理大獎兩項肯定。台新指出，高資產客戶需求已從傳統投資商品配置，轉向資產與負債的整合管理；其私人銀行高資產客戶數與資產管理規模（AUM）在 2026 年上半年大幅成長，反映市場對全方位財富規劃與家族傳承服務的需求升溫，帶動業者加速升級服務。
  - 來源 / Source：[台新銀行](https://www.taishinbank.com.tw/TSB/personal/common/news/TSBankNews-008407/)
- **Taiwan's high-net-worth wealth seen growing to NT$59 trillion** — Industry reports project Taiwan's high-net-worth personal wealth could expand at roughly 10% a year to NT$59 trillion by 2029, with about 74% of HNW clients already holding offshore assets. Trust-business scale reached NT$9.83 trillion by Q4 2025, up 7% year on year, as banks race to upgrade cross-border allocation, ETF platforms and family-succession services to capture the growing affluent segment.
  **台灣高資產財富估 2029 年達 59 兆** — 多份產業報告預估，台灣高資產客群個人財富規模有望以年均約一成速度成長，至 2029 年達新台幣 59 兆元，且約 74% 高資產客戶已持有境外資產。截至 2025 年第 4 季，國內信託業務規模達 9.83 兆元、年增 7%。隨市場成長，各銀行加速升級跨境配置、ETF 投資平台與家族傳承服務，爭取高資產與私人銀行客群商機。
  - 來源 / Source：[KPMG Taiwan](https://kpmg.com/tw/zh/media/press-releases/2026/06/2026-taiwan-wealth-management-trends-high-net-worth-growth.html)
