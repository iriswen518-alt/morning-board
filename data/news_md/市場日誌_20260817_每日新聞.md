### Taiwan Equities / 股市行情

- **Institutions net-buy nearly NT$51 billion even as the TAIEX slips below 46,000** — The three major institutions net bought roughly NT$50.9 billion in Friday's session, led by about NT$45.4 billion of foreign inflows, but the benchmark TAIEX still closed at 45,811 (-210 points, -0.46%) as late selling in heavyweight TSMC capped the index. Sustained institutional demand kept turnover heavy above the NT$1 trillion mark, underpinning the market despite the pullback from recent highs.

### 🏭 Industry / 產業動態

- **Morgan Stanley lifts TSMC CoWoS outlook as Nvidia and Broadcom lock in supply** — Morgan Stanley expects TSMC's CoWoS advanced-packaging capacity to reach about 125,000 wafers per month in 2026, up roughly 79% from around 70,000 at end-2025, with the 2026 slots effectively sold out. Nvidia is reported to have raised its CoWoS-L booking toward 700,000 units while Broadcom lifted total orders to about 230,000, underscoring that AI accelerator demand still runs well ahead of packaging supply.
  **大摩調升台積電CoWoS展望 輝達、博通鎖定產能** — 摩根士丹利預估台積電CoWoS先進封裝產能2026年將達每月約12.5萬片，較2025年底約7萬片大增約79%，且明年產能幾近售罄。輝達傳將CoWoS-L訂單量上調至約70萬顆，博通整體訂單則提升至約23萬顆，凸顯AI加速器需求仍遠超封裝供給，帶動台積電與台灣先進封裝設備供應鏈同步受惠。
  - 來源 / Source：[經濟日報](https://money.udn.com/money/story/11162/9176309)

- **Microsoft reported in talks for over 300,000 Maia AI chips as CoWoS runway extends to 2029** — Industry reports say Microsoft is negotiating with TSMC for more than 300,000 units of AI accelerator capacity tied to its Maia 300 chip, adding another hyperscaler to the CoWoS order book alongside Nvidia, Google, AMD and MediaTek. Analysts see TSMC's CoWoS output challenging a 14-fold expansion by 2029, reinforcing multi-year visibility for Taiwan's advanced-packaging and equipment suppliers.
  **微軟傳洽逾30萬顆Maia晶片 CoWoS產能能見度延伸至2029年** — 產業消息指出，微軟正與台積電洽談逾30萬顆與其Maia 300晶片相關的AI加速器產能，成為繼輝達、Google、超微與聯發科後再一家大舉下單的雲端巨頭。法人估台積電CoWoS產能到2029年有望挑戰14倍以上擴張，為台灣先進封裝與設備供應鏈提供多年期的訂單能見度與成長動能。

### 🏦 Financial Sector / 金融業

- **FSC eases capital and dividend pressure on insurance-linked holdcos ahead of new solvency regime** — The FSC has given breathing room to holding companies with life-insurance subsidiaries, letting eleven insurance-type holdcos defer capital-adequacy reporting from August to September and allowing eight groups a 15-year transition as Taiwan moves to its new-generation solvency system (TIS) and ICS. The move is designed to stabilise parent-company capital and preserve dividend-paying capacity through the accounting transition.
  **金管會鬆綁保險型金控資本與配息 因應新清償能力制度** — 金管會為旗下有壽險子公司的金控爭取緩衝，同意11家保險型金控將資本適足率申報自8月延後至9月，並給予8家金控最長15年的過渡措施，以因應台灣導入新一代清償能力制度（TIS）與ICS接軌。此舉旨在穩定母金控資本、維持配息能力，避免會計制度轉換衝擊金控股東權益與現金股利。
  - 來源 / Source：[經濟日報](https://money.udn.com/money/story/5613/9532077)

- **FSC tightens watch on stock-buying leverage, to require monthly reporting of "investment" personal loans** — The FSC is stepping up surveillance of "multi-loan" borrowers using bank credit to trade stocks, flagging abnormal moves in mortgages, personal and stock-pledge lending plus bad-loan trends as key monitoring metrics. From October, banks must report personal working-capital loans where the stated purpose is "investment," and the regulator says any two abnormal indicators will trigger inquiries and possible on-site checks.
  **金管會盯槓桿炒股 10月起要求按月申報「投資理財」個人貸款** — 金管會強化對以銀行信用炒股的「多重貸款」族群監理，將房貸、信貸、股票質押成長及逾放變化列為監測重點，異常即要求說明並強化控管。自10月起，銀行須按月申報用途填報為「投資理財」的個人週轉金貸款金額；金管會強調只要兩項指標同時異常，就會啟動查核甚至進一步金檢，防範資金過度流入股市。
  - 來源 / Source：[聯合新聞網](https://udn.com/news/story/7239/9656718)
