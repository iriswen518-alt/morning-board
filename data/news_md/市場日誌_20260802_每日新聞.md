The breadth of the move underlines that end demand for AI compute, rather than a single stock, is powering the current leg of the rally.
  **台積電財報前，半導體鏈全線飆漲** — AI 需求強勁，台積電財報公布前台灣半導體供應鏈全面走揚，晶圓代工、IC 設計、載板與散熱同步上攻。漲勢廣度顯示推動這波行情的是 AI 算力的終端需求，而非單一個股，族群輪動仍具續航力。

### 🏭 Industry / 產業動態

- **Global chip market seen hitting a record US$760.7bn in 2026** — Industry forecasts put the 2026 global semiconductor market at about US$760.7 billion, up 8.5% year on year and a fresh record, driven by AI across the value chain from equipment to chipmaking. The outlook reinforces Taiwan's central role in advanced packaging and foundry as AI compute demand broadens.
  **全球半導體 2026 年估達 7,607 億美元創高** — 法人預估 2026 年全球半導體市場規模約 7,607 億美元、年增 8.5%，改寫歷史新高，動能來自 AI 帶動的設備到晶片製造全鏈擴張。此一展望凸顯台灣在先進封裝與晶圓代工的關鍵地位，隨 AI 算力需求擴散而受惠。
  - 來源 / Source：[投資臺灣入口網](https://investtaiwan.nat.gov.tw/intelNewsPage202512260001cht?lang=cht&search=202512260001)
- **TSMC quadruples CoWoS capacity as June revenue jumps** — TSMC reported June consolidated revenue of about NT$442.68bn, up 6.2% on the month and 67.9% on the year, breaking a four-year seasonal decline. The company is expanding CoWoS advanced-packaging output from roughly 35,000 wafers a month in late 2024 toward 120,000-140,000 by end-2026, a fourfold rise to meet AI demand.
  **台積電 CoWoS 產能擴四倍、6 月營收大增** — 台積電 6 月合併營收約 4,426.8 億元，月增 6.2%、年增 67.9%，打破近四年 6 月營收下滑的季節性。公司正把 CoWoS 先進封裝產能自 2024 年底每月約 3.5 萬片，拉升至 2026 年底每月 12～14 萬片，成長四倍以因應 AI 需求。
  - 來源 / Source：[永豐金證券](https://www.esunsec.com.tw/article/post/478)

### 🏦 Financial Sector / 金融業

- **FSC sets three supervisory priorities for insurers** — Taiwan's Financial Supervisory Commission outlined three directions for insurance oversight as the industry adopts IFRS 17 this year: promoting AI-driven transformation, guiding green-finance investment, and building an all-ages financial system with expanded micro-insurance. The framework responds to a super-aged society and climate risk, shaping how insurers deploy capital and design products.
  **金管會揭保險監理三方向** — 金管會指出，保險業今年接軌 IFRS 17，未來監理聚焦三面向：推動 AI 科技轉型、引導綠色金融投資，以及建構全齡金融體系並擴大微型保險，以因應超高齡社會與氣候變遷。此監理框架將牽動壽險資金運用與商品設計方向。
  - 來源 / Source：[中央社 CNA](https://www.cna.com.tw/news/afe/202607220318.aspx)
- **Insurers post record first-half profit but momentum cools** — Taiwan's life and non-life insurers booked pre-tax profit of about NT$309.4bn in the first half, a record for the period, according to FSC data. However, life-insurance earnings fell for a second straight month in June and faced further pressure in July as stocks and bonds declined together, signalling the sweet spot may be passing.
  **保險業上半年獲利創高、動能降溫** — 據金管會統計，產壽險上半年稅前盈餘約 3,094 億元，寫下史上同期新高。不過壽險 6 月獲利連兩月下滑，7 月又逢股債齊跌承壓，顯示最甜蜜時刻可能已過，下半年獲利波動值得留意。
  - 來源 / Source：[聯合新聞網](https://udn.com/news/story/7239/9661676)
