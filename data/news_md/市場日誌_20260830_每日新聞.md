### 🏭 Industry / 產業動態

- **TSMC pushes CoWoS toward mass output as advanced packaging enters "system" era** — TSMC packaging chief Ho Chun said CoWoS has reached 5.5x-reticle high-volume production with several AI customers' yields above 98%, and the firm aims to reach 14x reticle size by 2029 on a yearly cadence of new nodes. With roughly ten advanced-packaging fabs now running, the business is an increasingly meaningful profit contributor.
  **台積電CoWoS邁入量產 先進封裝進入系統戰** — 台積電先進封裝副總何軍指出，CoWoS已進入5.5倍光罩尺寸高量產，多項AI客戶產品良率逾98%，並維持每年推新節奏，預計2029年推進至14倍光罩。目前已有約10座先進封裝廠，相關業務對獲利貢獻持續提升，鞏固台積電在AI算力供應鏈的關鍵地位。
  - 來源 / Source：[工商時報](https://www.ctee.com.tw/news/20260812700094-439901)
- **Packaging expansion spreads gains to equipment, substrate and OSAT vendors** — Analysts estimate TSMC's CoWoS monthly capacity could climb to 140k and 220k wafers by end-2026 and 2027, and the near-annual doubling of capacity is set to lift Taiwan's wafer- and panel-level packaging equipment makers, ABF substrate suppliers and back-end testers. The upgrade cycle broadens the beneficiary list well beyond the pure-play OSAT names.
  **封裝擴產帶旺設備、載板與封測鏈** — 法人預估台積電CoWoS月產能2026、2027年底可分別增至14萬、22萬片，產能近乎逐年倍增，將帶動台灣晶圓級與面板級封裝設備、ABF載板及封測廠受惠。此波升級不僅利多封測廠，設備與材料供應鏈亦同步進入擴產週期，受惠面明顯擴大。

### 🏦 Financial Sector / 金融業

- **FSC pre-announces holding-company capital rule to smooth TIS transition** — The Banking Bureau formally pre-announced amendments to the consolidated capital-adequacy rules for financial holding companies, part of measures easing insurers' switch to the new-generation solvency regime (TIS) taking effect in 2026. Eight insurance-heavy holdings gain capital relief and 11 groups may defer their CAR filings, cushioning the industry through a major regulatory shift.
  **金管會預告金控合併資本適足性辦法 助壽險接軌TIS** — 金管會銀行局預告修正「金融控股公司合併資本適足性管理辦法」，配合壽險業2026年接軌新一代清償能力制度(TIS)。過渡措施下，8家壽險型金控資本壓力獲紓解、11家CAR可延後申報，降低接軌對集團資本衝擊，讓金融業能較平順因應此波監理制度轉換。
  - 來源 / Source：[鉅亨網](https://news.cnyes.com/news/id/6472396)
- **Insurers brace for eight new rules and a major transformation next year** — The FSC has flagged eight new measures set to take effect, pushing life insurers into a broad transformation as IFRS 17 and the new solvency framework reshape product design, capital planning and disclosure. Regulators are pairing the tighter standards with transitional relief, signalling a multi-year adjustment for the sector's balance sheets and business mix.
  **金管會8項新制上路 壽險業迎大轉型** — 金管會公布明年將有8項新制陸續上路，隨IFRS 17與新一代清償能力制度接軌，壽險業在商品設計、資本規劃與資訊揭露面臨全面轉型。主管機關同步搭配過渡性紓緩措施，顯示此波調整將是跨年度工程，牽動業者資產負債結構與經營策略的長期重塑。

### 💎 Wealth Management / 財富管理

- **Bull market swells Taiwan's ranks of NT$100mn-plus households** — A rising stock market's wealth effect has lifted the number of households with over NT$100 million in assets to about 22,000, deepening the client pool that banks' private-banking and high-net-worth units are chasing. The expansion is reshaping demand for trusts, structured products, offshore bonds and family-office services as affluent clients diversify.
  **股市財富效應 億元級高資產戶數達2.2萬** — 股市走多帶動財富效應，全台資產逾億元的高資產家戶數增至約2.2萬戶，擴大銀行私人銀行與高資產部門競逐的客群。隨富人資產配置多元化，對信託、結構型商品、海外債及家族辦公室服務的需求同步升溫，成為財富管理業務下一階段成長動能來源。
  - 來源 / Source：[ETtoday 財經雲](https://finance.ettoday.net/news/3144737)
- **Banks mass forces in high-net-worth race as "Wealth 2.0" tops NT$2.7tn** — Bank-side high-net-worth assets under the "Wealth 2.0" regime have grown to NT$2.7 trillion across some 25,000 clients as of end-May, and lenders are concentrating in Kaohsiung's asset-management zone to win share. Private banking, family offices and offshore asset-management platforms (OAMU) have become the three core battlegrounds in the fight to retain and attract capital.
