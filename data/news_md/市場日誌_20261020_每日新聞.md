### Taiwan Equities / 股市行情

- **TSMC's Q3 beat lifts the market's heavyweight** — TSMC posted a record Q3 with consolidated revenue of about NT$1.4942tn (+50.9% YoY), EPS of NT$29.43 and a 66.4% gross margin. Management raised full-year USD revenue growth guidance to around 44% and guided Q4 revenue of US$52.3–54.3bn, reinforcing the AI-chip cycle that anchors the TAIEX's largest constituent.
  **台積電法說報喜，權值王定調大盤** — 台積電第三季合併營收創新高達1兆4,942億元、年增50.9%，EPS 29.43元、毛利率66.4%，並將全年美元營收成長上修至約44%，第四季估523至543億美元。作為台股權值王，其AI訂單能見度持續為大盤定調。
  - 來源 / Source：[優分析 uAnalyze](https://uanalyze.com.tw/articles/7013345497)
- **TSMC September revenue hits record for the month** — TSMC reported September consolidated revenue of about NT$511.9bn, up 54.6% year-on-year and a record for the month, with nine-month revenue near NT$3.9tn (+41.1%). The figures topped analyst estimates and kept the AI supply chain firmly in focus as the market's leadership group.
  **台積電9月營收創歷年同期新高** — 台積電9月合併營收約5,118.57億元，月減0.6%、年增54.6%，創歷年同期新高；前三季累計約3兆8,987億元、年增41.1%，優於法人預期，鞏固AI供應鏈作為盤面主流的地位。

### 🏭 Industry / 產業動態

- **Memory up-cycle accelerates as capacity shifts to HBM** — TrendForce says DRAM contract prices are rising sharply as leading makers divert capacity to high-margin HBM, tightening commodity DRAM supply; Jefferies projects a further 30–40% price increase in Q4 2026. Morgan Stanley upgraded Taiwan memory names Nanya, Winbond, Macronix and PSMC on the persistent shortage.
  **記憶體上行循環加速，產能轉進HBM** — 集邦科技指出，大廠將產能轉向高毛利HBM，使通用型DRAM供給吃緊、合約價漲勢擴大；Jefferies估第四季再漲30至40%。摩根士丹利看好供不應求延續，調升南亞科、華邦電、旺宏、力積電等台廠記憶體族群評級。
  - 來源 / Source：[經濟日報](https://udn.com/news/story/7240/9347586)
- **TSMC's process-technology lead stays unrivalled** — Industry coverage notes TSMC's advanced-process edge remains hard to challenge, with 2nm (N2) entering volume production this year and its revenue contribution surpassing 3nm, while A16 and CoWoS advanced-packaging capacity expand to meet AI demand. The widening lead underpins pricing power even as the 2nm ramp temporarily dilutes margins.
  **台積電先進製程領先地位難撼動** — 產業分析指出，台積電2奈米（N2）今年進入量產、營收貢獻超越3奈米，A16與CoWoS先進封裝產能同步擴充以滿足AI需求。製程優勢擴大鞏固其定價能力，儘管2奈米放量初期將對毛利率造成稀釋。
  - 來源 / Source：[科技新報](https://finance.technews.tw/2026/10/07/tsmcs-advanced-process-technology-has-no-rivals-recently/)

### 🏦 Financial Sector / 金融業

- **FSC outlines six priorities in Legislative report** — FSC Chairman Peng Jin-lung delivered a business report to the Legislature on Oct 19 setting out six policy priorities. Highlights include a financial-industry large language model slated for Q1 2027 and virtual-asset custody rules the Bankers Association plans to file by year-end. Pre-tax profit of the banking, securities and insurance sectors totalled NT$1.1702tn in Jan–Aug.
  **金管會立院報告提六大施政方向** — 金管會主委彭金隆10月19日赴立法院財委會業務報告，提出六大施政方向，包括預計2027年第一季發布金融業大型語言模型、銀行公會年底前報核虛擬資產保管業務規範。報告並揭露今年1至8月銀行、證券期貨、保險三業合計稅前盈餘達1兆1,702億元。
  - 來源 / Source：[NOWnews](https://www.nownews.com/news/6884087)
- **Far Eastern Bank cleared for Wealth Management 2.0** — Far Eastern International Bank won FSC approval to launch Wealth Management 2.0 services, rolling out its "Ten Joys" (十樂) brand to compete in private banking. The approval widens the roster of banks cleared for high-asset business, intensifying competition among domestic lenders for affluent and high-net-worth clients.
  **遠東商銀獲准開辦財管2.0** — 遠東商銀獲金管會核准開辦財富管理2.0業務，並以「十樂」品牌搶進私人銀行市場。此核准使取得高資產業務資格的銀行再添一家，國銀競逐富裕及高資產客群的態勢更趨白熱化，也反映財管2.0成為各行庫擴張手續費收入的重要戰場。
  - 來源 / Source：[自由時報](https://ec.ltn.com.tw/article/breakingnews/5487559)

### 💎 Wealth Management / 財富管理

- **Taishin Bank wins PBI Global Wealth Awards** — Taishin Bank announced on Oct 12 that it won two honours at the 2026 Private Banker International (PBI) Global Wealth Awards, recognised for deepening high-net-worth services and AI-driven innovation. The accolade underscores how Taiwanese lenders are investing in digital advisory and family-office capabilities to serve affluent clients.
  **台新銀行榮獲PBI全球財富管理大獎** — 台新銀行10月12日公告榮獲2026年「國際私人銀行家（PBI）全球財富管理大獎」兩項肯定，表彰其深化高資產服務與AI創新。此獎項凸顯台灣銀行業積極投入數位理財顧問與家族辦公室能力，以爭取富裕客群，呼應高資產財管市場競爭日趨激烈的趨勢。
  - 來源 / Source：[台新銀行](https://www.taishinbank.com.tw/TSB/personal/common/news/TSBankNews-008407/)
- **Taiwan's high-net-worth pool keeps growing** — Industry reports point to robust growth in Taiwan's high-net-worth segment. KPMG and E.Sun estimate individuals with assets over NT$100m numbered about 118,000 in 2025 holding NT$43.7tn, projected to rise to roughly 124,000 and NT$46.1tn in 2026. Cross-border allocation and wealth-succession demand are pushing banks and regulators to expand family-office services.
  **台灣高資產客群持續擴大** — 多份報告顯示台灣高資產客群快速成長。KPMG與玉山銀行估計，資產逾億元者2025年約11.8萬人、持有43.7兆元，2026年估增至約12.4萬人、46.1兆元。跨境資產配置與家族傳承需求升溫，促使銀行與監理機關加速擴大家族辦公室與一站式財管服務。
