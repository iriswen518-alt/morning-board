### 🏦 Financial Sector / 金融業

- **Life insurers adopt IFRS 17 in 2026 as FSC adds four metrics** — Taiwan's life insurance industry moves onto IFRS 17 accounting in 2026, and the Financial Supervisory Commission has added four key disclosure ratios covering insurance-service results, contractual-service-margin release, new-contract margins and onerous-contract losses. The overhaul pushes insurers toward protection-type products over savings policies, meaning premium income should "normalize" and the long run of near-trillion-dollar annual inflows is unlikely to repeat.
