### 💰 Tax & Regulations / 稅務法規

- **New estate-tax law eases the burden on heirs** — A newly implemented amendment lets "deemed estate" assets be taxed separately rather than dumped entirely on heirs, and allows even small estate-tax bills to be paid in installments. The change aims to reduce cash-flow strain during inheritance and make the system fairer where multiple heirs are involved. Tax advisers suggest families review succession and gifting plans to take advantage of the more flexible payment and assessment rules.
  **遺產稅新法上路 減輕繼承人負擔** — 新上路的修法讓「擬制遺產」可分開課稅，不再由繼承人一次全扛，且連小額遺產稅都能申請分期繳納。此舉旨在降低繼承過程的現金流壓力，並在多位繼承人情況下使制度更公平。稅務顧問建議家庭重新檢視傳承與贈與規劃，善用更具彈性的繳納與核課規定。
