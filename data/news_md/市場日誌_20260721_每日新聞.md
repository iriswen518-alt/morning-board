### 🏭 Industry / 產業動態

- **Morgan Stanley lifts DDR4 shortage view to ~20% and sharply raises Nanya, Winbond targets** — In a bullish report, Morgan Stanley raised its second-half DDR4 supply-shortfall estimate to 19-20% (from about 14%), with the gap persisting into 2027-2028, and projected third-quarter DDR4 prices could climb around 20% quarter-on-quarter. The house lifted Winbond's target to NT$222 (up to NT$264 in a bull case) and Nanya's to NT$380, with an optimistic scenario as high as NT$805. The upgrade reframes mature-node memory as a high-margin cycle and keeps Taiwan memory makers central to the AI-era supply story.
  **大摩上修DDR4缺口至約兩成 大升南亞科、華邦電目標價** — 摩根士丹利發布看多報告，將下半年DDR4供給缺口估值由約14%上修至19-20%，缺口延續至2027-2028年，並預期第3季DDR4價格季增幅上看約20%。大摩將華邦電目標價調升至222元（樂觀上看264元）、南亞科上修至380元，最樂觀情境甚至挑戰805元。此舉把成熟製程記憶體重新定位為高毛利循環，使台灣記憶體廠成為AI時代供應鏈焦點；惟須留意三星、海力士擴產與價格能否如期走揚的變數。
  - 來源 / Source：[經濟日報](https://money.udn.com/money/story/5607/9538703)
- **DDR4 seen "severely short" in H2 as makers pivot capacity to HBM and DDR5** — Follow-on analysis maps a deepening DDR4 squeeze into the second half, with the top-three makers steering capacity toward high-margin HBM and DDR5 and leaving legacy DRAM supply tight. That crowding-out dynamic underpins target-price upgrades across Nanya, Winbond, Macronix, PSMC and Etron, though analysts flag that aggressive capacity additions in 2026-2027 could eventually narrow the gap. For Taiwan's supply chain, the near-term setup favours pricing power, but investors are urged to track inventory, capex and any demand cooling rather than chase prices.
  **DDR4下半年嚴重短缺 原廠產能轉向HBM、DDR5** — 後續分析指出，DDR4供給缺口下半年持續擴大，三大原廠將產能導向高毛利HBM與DDR5，排擠標準型DRAM供給。此排擠效應支撐南亞科、華邦電、旺宏、力積電、愛普等個股評價調升；惟分析師示警2026-2027年積極擴產，恐使缺口最終收斂。對台灣供應鏈而言，短期定價力占優，但投資人宜追蹤庫存、資本支出與需求是否降溫，避免在情緒主導下追高，關注供需反轉訊號以控管風險。

### 🏦 Financial Sector / 金融業

- **FSC clears E.Sun Financial's takeover of Mercuries Life; merger benchmark set for around September 1** — The Financial Supervisory Commission approved E.Sun Financial Holding's plan to convert Mercuries Life Insurance into a wholly-owned subsidiary via share swap, ruling that E.Sun meets requirements on policyholder and employee protection, capital adequacy and long-term commitment. The merger benchmark date is tentatively September 1, with Mercuries Life to delist and the entity likely renamed E.Sun Life by year-end. The deal reshapes Taiwan's life-insurance landscape and gives E.Sun a full-service financial platform spanning banking, securities and insurance.
  **金管會核准玉山金併三商美邦人壽 合併基準日暫定9月1日** — 金管會同意玉山金控以股份轉換方式，將三商美邦人壽轉換為100%持股子公司，認定玉山金在保戶與員工權益保障、財務健全及長期經營承諾等均符合規定。合併基準日暫定9月1日，三商壽屆時下市，年底可望更名玉山人壽。此案重塑台灣壽險版圖，讓玉山金補齊銀行、證券、保險的全方位金融平台，市場關注後續整併綜效、資本配置與商品策略調整，以及對壽險同業競爭態勢的影響。

### 💎 Wealth Management / 財富管理

- **Land Bank becomes the 25th bank licensed for high-asset "Wealth 2.0" services** — The FSC approved Land Bank of Taiwan to offer financial products and services for high-asset clients (the "Wealth 2.0" regime), making it the 25th licensed bank and likely opening the business by year-end. Regulator data through end-February show Taiwan's high-net-worth pool has reached 22,199 clients with assets under management of NT$2.3044 trillion; allocation skews to deposits (42.7%), funds (17.1%), bonds (14.4%), insurance (11.9%) and offshore structured products (7.4%). The push intensifies competition for one-stop private-banking-style services among domestic banks.
  **土銀取得財管2.0執照 成第25家搶攻高資產商機** — 金管會核准台灣土地銀行辦理高資產客戶適用之金融商品及服務（財管2.0），成為第25家取得執照的銀行，原則上年底前有望開辦。金管會統計至2月底，高資產客戶達22,199人、管理資產規模2兆3,044億元；商品配置以存款42.7%最高，其次基金17.1%、債券14.4%、保險11.9%、境外結構型商品7.4%。此案加劇本國銀行搶攻一站式私銀型服務的競爭，高資產客戶可望取得更多元的財富管理與傳承規劃選擇。
  - 來源 / Source：[中央社](https://www.cna.com.tw/news/afe/202607160344.aspx)
- **2026 stock-picking favourites: 0050, 00919 and 00878 top the list as payout-frequency myths persist** — A round-up of 2026's most-held ETFs among Taiwan's buy-and-hold crowd shows market-cap and high-dividend products such as 0050, 00919 and 00878 leading, alongside active and tech-themed funds. Advisers stress that distribution frequency — monthly, quarterly or semi-annual — changes cash-flow timing, not long-run total return: monthly payers suit retirees needing steady income, while market-cap ETFs better serve long-term growth. The guidance urges investors to blend income and growth by goal rather than chase the highest headline yield.
  **2026存股族最愛ETF 0050、00919、00878領銜 破解配息迷思** — 2026年台股存股族最愛ETF盤點顯示，市值型與高股息如0050、00919、00878居前，主動式與科技型ETF熱度也升。專家強調，配息頻率（月配、季配、半年配）只改變現金流時點，不代表長期總報酬更高：月配適合需穩定現金流的退休族，市值型則更利於長期資產成長。建議投資人依理財目標搭配「收益＋成長」配置，而非單純追逐帳面最高殖利率，並留意填息與淨值波動風險，兼顧現金流與資產累積。
