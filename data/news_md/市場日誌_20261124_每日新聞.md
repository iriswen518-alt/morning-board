### 💰 Tax & Regulations / 稅務法規

- **Taiwan tightens estate-tax treatment of pre-death gifts** — Following a legislative amendment, gifts made within two years before a decedent's death are to be folded back into the taxable estate, with the recipient bearing the corresponding tax. The Ministry of Finance says it is drafting the enforcement rules and updating return forms and online filing systems ahead of full implementation.
  **擬制遺產修法：死亡前兩年內贈與併入遺產課稅** — 立法院三讀修正遺贈稅法，將被繼承人死亡前兩年內的贈與併入遺產課稅，並改由受贈人負擔相應稅額。財政部表示正研擬施行細則等子法規、修訂申報書表與線上申辦系統，待總統公布後加強宣導，提醒高資產族群提前檢視贈與與傳承規劃。
  - 來源 / Source：[中央社](https://cna.com.tw/news/afe/202608210289.aspx)
- **2027 estate- and gift-tax exemptions seen rising with inflation** — With cumulative CPI gains exceeding the statutory threshold, the estate-tax exemption (currently NT$13.33m) and gift-tax exemption (NT$2.44m) are expected to be adjusted upward for 2027, with estimates pointing to roughly NT$14.9m and NT$2.7m. The figures remain estimates pending the Ministry of Finance's year-end announcement.
  **2027年遺贈稅免稅額可望隨CPI調升** — 累計消費者物價漲幅達法定門檻，市場預估明年遺產稅免稅額可能由現行1,333萬元調升至約1,492萬元、贈與稅免稅額由244萬元調升至約273萬元。實際幅度須待財政部年底正式公告為準，相關扣除額部分品項維持不變，建議納稅人留意年底公告後再行規劃。
  - 來源 / Source：[經濟日報](https://udn.com/news/story/7243/9197837)
