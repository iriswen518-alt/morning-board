### 🏭 Industry / 產業動態

- **TSMC honors record 35 suppliers at "grand alliance" forum** — At its 2026 Supply Chain Management Forum on Nov 17, themed "Grand Alliance for a New Era," TSMC named a record 35 suppliers for excellence awards, including local firms such as Marketech, Martek and Jentech alongside global tool makers like ASML and Applied Materials. ### 🏦 Financial Sector / 金融業

- **FSC keeps six "too big to fail" banks for a seventh year** — The Financial Supervisory Commission released its 2026 list of domestic systemically important banks (D-SIBs), keeping the same six lenders for a seventh straight year. All six already meet the final capital requirements, and their Pillar 2 stress-test results comply with standards, the regulator said, signaling solid capital buffers at Taiwan's largest banks.
  **金管會公布 6 家「大到不能倒」銀行 連 7 年不變** — 金管會公布 2026 年系統性重要銀行（D-SIBs）名單，六家銀行續列、名單連續 7 年未變動。金管會表示，6 家 D-SIBs 的資本適足比率均已符合最終資本要求，第二支柱壓力測試結果也符合標準，顯示大型銀行資本體質穩健，有助強化金融體系抗風險能力。
  - 來源 / Source：[經濟日報](https://money.udn.com/money/story/5613/9885038)
- **Insurers face new regimes as FSC rolls out oversight measures** — Taiwan's life insurers are adopting IFRS 17 and the new ICS solvency regime this year, prompting the FSC to introduce multiple supervisory measures. At a Nov 16 risk-management forum the regulator pressed insurers to strengthen asset-liability and capital management across assets, liabilities and net worth to withstand the transition.
  **保險業接軌新制 金管會推多項監理措施** — 國內壽險業今年正式接軌 IFRS 17 與新一代清償能力制度（ICS），金管會因此推動多項監理措施。11 月 16 日舉辦保險業風險管理趨勢論壇，要求業者強化資產負債與資本管理；監理層並從資產、負債與淨值三面向引導壽險業強化體質，確保接軌後清償能力穩定。

### 💎 Wealth Management / 財富管理

- **CTBC upgrades personal trusts to hold domestic ETFs** — CTBC Bank has upgraded its personal trust service so clients can now allocate to onshore ETFs, effective from October 2026. The move widens the investment menu within discretionary and family trusts, reflecting surging demand for ETFs as a core wealth-management building block among affluent Taiwanese clients seeking both flexibility and succession planning.
  **中國信託個人信託升級 可配置境內 ETF** — 中國信託銀行將個人信託服務大升級，自 2026 年 10 月起把境內 ETF 納入可運用標的，擴大信託資產配置彈性。此舉反映高資產與一般財管客群對 ETF 需求快速升溫，ETF 已成為財富管理核心配置之一。透過信託架構配置 ETF，也有助客戶兼顧資產傳承與靈活投資需求。
  - 來源 / Source：[經濟日報](https://money.udn.com/money/story/5636/9884628)
- **High-net-worth wealth assets top NT$3 trillion as rich clients surge** — Six years into Taiwan's "Wealth Management 2.0" scheme, banks' high-net-worth wealth assets have broken through NT$3 trillion, with the number of high-asset clients jumping more than 80% year-on-year. Portfolios are shifting from deposits and funds toward ETFs, offshore assets and insurance; onshore funds now exceed NT$10 trillion, of which ETFs make up about NT$7.4 trillion.
