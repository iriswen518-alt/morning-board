### 💰 Tax & Regulations / 稅務法規

- **New estate-tax rule makes recipients liable for death-bed gifts** — The president promulgated amendments to the Estate and Gift Tax Act on Sep 11, under which assets gifted to certain relatives within two years before death are still counted in the taxable estate, but the burden now falls proportionally on the recipients — even if they renounce their inheritance. Effective for 2026, the change tightens succession planning for wealthy families.

  - 來源 / Source：[壹蘋新聞網](https://news.nextapple.com/finance/20260911/E59215CC30AEE7EED4FEE085A66422E8)
- **Estate- and gift-tax exemptions to rise in 2027 on inflation adjustment** — With cumulative CPI gains clearing the statutory threshold, the Ministry of Finance is set to lift the estate-tax exemption to NT$14.92 million (from NT$13.33 million) and the gift-tax exemption to NT$2.73 million (from NT$2.44 million).
