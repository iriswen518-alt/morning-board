### 🏭 Industry / 產業動態

- **TSMC takes a roughly $500 million stake in Anthropic at IPO price** — TSMC disclosed on Nov 19 that its subsidiary TSMC Partners bought 6,024,096 common shares of US AI firm Anthropic at $83 each, totaling about $500 million and matching Anthropic's IPO price. The investment deepens the foundry's ties to a leading AI model developer and signals confidence in sustained long-term demand for advanced AI compute and packaging.
  **台積電砸約 5 億美元入股 Anthropic** — 台積電 11 月 19 日公告，子公司 TSMC Partners 以每股 83 美元買進美國 AI 公司 Anthropic 普通股 602 萬 4,096 股，總金額約 5 億美元，價格對應 Anthropic 首次公開發行價。此舉深化台積電與領先 AI 模型開發商的連結，也反映晶圓代工龍頭對 AI 先進運算需求長期看好，等同為先進製程與封裝需求預作綁定。

- **US tightens AI-chip controls; TSMC said to suspend some China orders** — Washington's Bureau of Industry and Security rolled out new rules on Nov 16 targeting advanced AI chips and remote compute access across five Southeast Asian nations. Reports say TSMC has told affected Chinese chip-design clients that 16nm-and-below orders must pause absent a US export license, raising the risk of order deferrals even as analysts see limited direct impact.

### 🏦 Financial Sector / 金融業

- **Insurers get three transitional measures to ease ICS capital strain** — As Taiwan's life insurers adopt the new ICS solvency regime in its first year in 2026, the FSC has set out three transitional measures letting insurers phase in capital over an extended period, with lower-RBC firms pushed to accelerate capital top-ups within roughly five years. The framework aims to cushion the transition while safeguarding solvency.

The longer runway gives insurers more room to raise capital while adapting to tougher interest-rate and asset-risk requirements.
  **台版 ICS 過渡期拉長至 15 年 減輕股票風險係數壓力** — 金管會定案的台版 ICS 將過渡期由 10 年延長至 15 年，較南韓多出 5 年；上市櫃股票風險係數由現行 21.65% 逐步拉高至 35%，不動產則在期間內逐步達 15%。較長緩衝期給予壽險業更充裕的籌資與調整空間，以因應利率風險與資產風險計提提高，降低一次到位的資本衝擊，有助金融體系穩定。

### 💎 Wealth Management / 財富管理

- **Taiwan's wealth-management pool swells to NT$36 trillion, says KPMG** — KPMG's 2026 Taiwan Wealth Management Trends report estimates the domestic market has grown from under NT$20 trillion in 2019 to NT$36 trillion by end-2025, propelled by ETFs, high-net-worth business and the Asian asset-management hub policy. Banks' high-asset business has topped NT$2 trillion and brokerages' NT$439.2 billion, though funds and trusts remain the core.
  **KPMG：台灣財管市場規模衝上 36 兆元** — KPMG《2026 台灣財富管理趨勢報告》指出，國內財管市場規模由 2019 年不到 20 兆元，擴大至 2025 年底的 36 兆元，主要動能來自 ETF、高資產業務與亞洲資產管理中心政策。銀行高資產業務規模突破 2 兆元、證券端達 4,392 億元，惟主體仍為基金與信託。市場結構快速質變，高資產與跨境配置比重持續攀升，業者布局更趨多元。
  - 來源 / Source：[理財鴿](https://www.fintechgo.com.tw/FinDiary/Article/2642555423403751)
- **Bank trust assets hit NT$9.83 trillion, rising for a sixth year** — Industry data show Taiwanese banks' trust assets reached NT$9.83 trillion, growing for a sixth consecutive year, as trusts cement their role at the core of household wealth management. Alongside brokerages' roughly NT$280 billion wealth books, the figures underscore how funds, ETFs and trust structures dominate where Taiwanese savers park their money.
  **銀行信託規模達 9.83 兆元 連六年成長** — 業界統計顯示，台灣銀行信託業務規模達 9.83 兆元，連續第六年成長，信託持續鞏固其在家庭財富管理核心的角色。搭配券商財管規模約 2,799 億元，數據凸顯基金、ETF 與信託架構仍是台灣民眾資產停泊的主要去處。隨 ETF 與高資產需求升溫，信託可運用標的擴大也帶動資產配置彈性進一步提升。
