### 🏭 Industry / 產業動態

- **Samsung seeks to double 2027 HBM prices as AI memory stays tight** — Korean media report Samsung has proposed raising its 2027 high-bandwidth-memory supply prices to more than double current levels, with talks entering their final stage after securing sizable orders from Nvidia and Broadcom. TrendForce projects average HBM prices could jump about 121% in 2027, and Micron has locked in most of its 2027 HBM capacity at higher prices—signaling a seller-driven cycle extending well into 2027.
  **三星擬將2027年HBM價格翻倍 AI記憶體續緊俏** — 韓媒報導，三星在2027年高頻寬記憶體（HBM）供給談判中，規劃將價格調升至現行逾兩倍，並已鎖定輝達、博通等大客戶相當數量訂單，談判進入最終階段。TrendForce估2027年HBM平均價格漲幅上看121%，美光亦已以高於2026年的價格簽下大部分2027年HBM產能。顯示由賣方主導的記憶體上行循環將延續至2027年，台廠記憶體與封測供應鏈營運動能可望受惠。
  - 來源 / Source：[科技新報](https://finance.technews.tw/2026/10/07/samsung-hbm4-supply-prices-are-expected-to-double-by-2027/)
- **Q4 DRAM contract prices keep climbing as capacity shifts to HBM** — TrendForce expects fourth-quarter conventional DRAM contract prices to rise 10–15% sequentially, and 15–20% including HBM, as makers divert capacity to HBM and squeeze general DRAM supply. Taiwanese suppliers stand to benefit: Nanya Technology is pushing DDR5 and higher-end niches as prices recover, while Winbond says capacity is fully booked into 2027 and has lifted 2026 capex to NT$42.1bn.
  **Q4 DRAM合約價續漲 產能轉向HBM推升缺口** — TrendForce預估第四季標準型DRAM合約價季增10至15%，計入HBM後季增15至20%，因原廠將產能移往HBM、擠壓一般DRAM供給。台廠同步受惠：南亞科推進DDR5與高階利基產品，隨報價回升、庫存去化告一段落可望脫離谷底；華邦電受惠邊緣運算與AI需求，產能已滿載至2027年，並將2026年資本支出調升至421億元。記憶體上行循環延續，牽動台廠營收與毛利表現。

### 🏦 Financial Sector / 金融業

- **FSC unveils eight 2026 reforms spanning securities and insurance** — Taiwan's Financial Supervisory Commission announced eight measures for 2026—six from the securities bureau and two from the insurance bureau. From March 31, shareholders can claim non-physical meeting souvenirs online via the central-depository e-platform; listed firms must upload meeting handbooks and annual reports earlier; and treasury-share buyback filings move to electronic submission from May 1. The package aims to improve disclosure, efficiency and investor convenience.
  **金管會公布2026年八大新制 涵蓋證券與保險** — 金管會公布2026年八大新制，其中證期局六項、保險局兩項。自3月31日起，股東可透過集保e平台線上領取非實體股東會紀念品；上市櫃公司須提前上傳議事手冊與年報；庫藏股買回申報自5月1日起改採電子化。新制旨在強化資訊揭露、提升作業效率與投資人便利性，並推動股東會數位化，業者須配合調整申報與作業流程，為證券市場制度面帶來一波調整。
  - 來源 / Source：[聯合新聞網](https://udn.com/news/story/7239/9235638)
- **Insurers adopt IFRS 17 and new solvency regime from Jan 2026** — Taiwan's insurance sector began applying IFRS 17 and the new-generation Insurance Capital Standard (ICS) from Jan 1, 2026. Regulators expect the stricter accounting and capital rules to push insurers away from long-guarantee savings-type products toward protection-oriented offerings. The shift reshapes product mixes and capital planning across the industry, affecting how insurers manage interest-rate risk and reserve adequacy under the new framework.
  **保險業2026年元旦起施行IFRS 17與ICS新制** — 台灣保險業自2026年1月1日起接軌IFRS 17國際財務報導準則與新一代清償能力制度（ICS）。主管機關預期，更嚴格的會計與資本規範將促使業者減少「長期保證」的儲蓄型商品，轉向保障型商品。此舉牽動全業產品組合與資本規劃，影響保險公司在新制下的利率風險管理與準備金適足性，為金融業重大的法遵與經營課題，投保人亦須留意商品結構與保費調整。
