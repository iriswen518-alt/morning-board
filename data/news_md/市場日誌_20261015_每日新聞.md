### 💰 Tax & Regulations / 稅務法規

- **Estate-and-gift-tax amendment makes gift recipients the taxpayers** — Taiwan's legislature passed amendments (third reading Aug 21) changing how pre-death gifts are taxed. Property gifted within two years before death is still added to the estate, but each recipient is now the taxpayer for their share, so tax authorities issue assessments directly to them—regardless of whether they waived or lost inheritance rights or are non-statutory heirs. The change also removes the NT$300,000 threshold previously required to apply for installment payment.
  **遺贈稅修法 受贈人成納稅義務人** — 立法院8月21日三讀通過遺產及贈與稅法修正，調整死亡前贈與的課稅方式。被繼承人死亡前兩年內贈與的財產仍併入遺產總額，但改以各受贈人為其受贈部分的納稅義務人，稽徵機關將直接對受贈人發單課稅，不論其是否拋棄繼承、喪失繼承權或非屬法定繼承人。修法並刪除申請分期繳納須「應納稅額達30萬元以上」的門檻。財政部正研擬施行細則等子法規，牽動高資產族群傳承與贈與規劃。
