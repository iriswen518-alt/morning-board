### Taiwan Equities / 股市行情

- **TAIEX plunges 1,196 points as foreign investors dump NT$60.95bn** — Taiwan's benchmark index cratered 1,195.97 points (2.67%) on July 24 to close at 43,654.84, losing the 44,000 level and breaking below its quarterly moving average. Foreign investors pulled a net NT$60.95 billion and the three institutional groups net-sold NT$66.39 billion, though investment trusts bucked the trend with a 23rd straight buying session (NT$5.71bn). Decliners swamped advancers 1,240 to 566 on turnover of NT$794.2 billion, as weak US tech earnings and AI-spending doubts soured sentiment.
  **台股崩跌1,196點 外資提款609.5億元** — 台股7月24日重挫1,195.97點、跌幅2.67%，收在43,654.84點，失守44,000點整數關並跌破季線。外資單日大砍609.5億元，三大法人合計賣超663.94億元；投信逆勢連23買、加碼57.08億元。全場跌逾千家、僅566家上漲，19檔跌停，成交值7,941.75億元。導火線為美股科技財報疲弱與AI資本支出疑慮發酵，市場追高意願急凍，短線量能與外資動向為後續觀盤重點。
  - 來源 / Source：[Newtalk新聞](https://newtalk.tw/news/view/2026-07-24/1049185)
- **Foreign selling concentrated in electronics heavyweights, Powerchip hit hardest** — The July 24 sell-off was heavily concentrated in large-cap electronics, financials and ETFs. TSMC alone drew net foreign sales of about 38,450 lots worth nearly NT$100 billion, while foreigners net-sold Powerchip (6770) 43,857 lots — the single largest — extending a six-session dumping spree totaling 224,044 lots. The "out of tech, into traditionals" rotation underscores how AI-spending doubts are driving deleveraging in the priciest chip names.
  **外資賣壓集中電子權值 力積電居倒貨之冠** — 台股7月24日外資賣壓集中於大型電子權值股、金融股與ETF；台積電遭賣超約3.8萬張、金額逼近千億元，力積電（6770）單日賣超43,857張居冠，短短6個交易日累計倒貨達22.4萬張。盤面呈「棄電子、抱傳產」格局，反映AI獲利兌現疑慮升溫下，資金對高評價電子股去槓桿；傳產與內需題材則相對抗跌，族群輪動節奏將牽動後市反彈力道。

### 🏭 Industry / 產業動態

- **TSMC drops NT$55 and drags TAIEX 470 points as Asian chip stocks tumble** — TSMC fell NT$55 (2.29%) to NT$2,350 on July 24, breaking below its monthly line and single-handedly dragging the benchmark down more than 470 points. The rout spread across Asian chip and AI names after disappointing US tech earnings, with Samsung Electronics down more than 8% and SK Hynix off over 7%, making semiconductor and AI-linked stocks the day's main drag on regional markets and raising near-term doubts about AI profit delivery.
  **台積電跌55元拖累大盤470點 亞洲晶片股同崩** — 台積電7月24日下跌55元（跌2.29%）收2,350元、跌破月線，單檔即拖累加權指數逾470點。受美科技財報不佳拖累，亞洲晶片與AI族群同步重挫，三星電子跌逾8%、SK海力士跌逾7%，半導體與AI相關類股成為當日亞股主要壓盤來源。市場短線對AI獲利兌現的疑慮升高，惟先進製程供需與台積電後續展望仍是族群能否回穩的關鍵。
  - 來源 / Source：[經濟日報](https://money.udn.com/money/story/5607/9648679)
- **Analysts see "chip inflation" era as AI capex heads toward US$765bn** — Despite the sell-off, analysts argue the structural AI up-cycle remains intact, projecting global AI capital spending to surge toward US$765 billion by 2026 and usher in an era of "chip inflation," with advanced-process and AI supply-chain names staying a magnet for hot money — one related stock has jumped 77% in three months. The view frames the current pullback as sector rotation rather than a cycle top, though valuations and profit delivery remain the key watch points.
  **分析師看「晶片通膨」時代 AI資本支出上看7,650億美元** — 儘管盤面重挫，分析師認為AI結構性上升循環未變，預估全球AI資本支出2026年將飆升至7,650億美元，引爆「晶片通膨」時代，先進製程與AI供應鏈仍是熱錢首選，相關個股3個月狂飆77%。此觀點將本波回檔定調為族群輪動而非循環見頂，為長線題材提供支撐；惟高評價股的獲利兌現節奏與資本支出能否轉為報酬，仍是市場後續檢視的重點。
  - 來源 / Source：[三立新聞網](https://www.setn.com/news/1876396)

### 🏦 Financial Sector / 金融業

- **FSC sets out three priorities for insurance supervision** — The Financial Supervisory Commission on July 22 laid out three directions for insurance oversight: promoting AI-driven transformation backed by AI guidelines and data governance; optimizing mechanisms to steer green-finance investment; and building an all-age financial system while expanding micro-insurance. The framework signals regulators want insurers to modernize technology and channel long-term capital toward green and inclusive goals, shaping product design and investment strategy across the life sector.
  **金管會揭保險監理三大方向 推AI轉型與全齡金融** — 金管會7月22日揭示保險監理三大方向：推動AI轉型並落實AI指引與資料治理、優化機制引導綠色金融投資，以及建構全齡金融體系並推廣微型保險。此一架構顯示主管機關要求壽險業加速科技升級，並引導長期資金投入綠能與普惠金融目標，將牽動壽險業的商品設計與投資布局。業者須在法遵、數位轉型與資金運用間及早因應，兼顧保戶保障與監理新要求。
  - 來源 / Source：[中央社](https://www.cna.com.tw/news/afe/202607220318.aspx)
- **FSC approves E.Sun FHC's takeover of Mercuries Life; merger set for Sept 1** — The FSC on July 7 approved E.Sun Financial Holding's plan to acquire 100% of Mercuries Life Insurance via a share swap, with the merger tentatively effective September 1 and Mercuries to delist. E.Sun pledged to protect policyholders, retain all staff on unchanged terms for three years, and inject capital potentially well beyond NT$10-20 billion, while being barred from transferring control for at least a decade. The deal hands E.Sun a life-insurance arm and marks further consolidation in Taiwan's financial sector.
  **金管會核准玉山金併三商美邦人壽 預計9/1合併** — 金管會7月7日核准玉山金控以股份轉換方式取得三商美邦人壽100%股份，合併基準日暫定9月1日、三商壽將下市。玉山金承諾保障保戶權益、員工全數留用且三年內勞動條件不變，並將對三商壽增資（金額上看逾百億、甚至數百億元），且至少十年內不得移轉控制權。此案為玉山金補上壽險拼圖，象徵國內金融業整併再進一步，後續增資與資產體質改善將是市場關注焦點。
  - 來源 / Source：[工商時報](https://www.ctee.com.tw/news/20260707701801-430304)

### 💎 Wealth Management / 財富管理

- **Banks' high-asset AUM breaks NT$2.66tn, nearing the NT$2.7tn mark** — Domestic high-net-worth wealth business kept booming: as of end-May, AUM across the 21 licensed banks reached NT$2.6633 trillion, up NT$111 billion in a single month, while high-asset client numbers rose 1,090 to 25,290 — a 70.4% year-on-year jump — putting NT$2.7 trillion within reach. The rapid growth cements high-net-worth banking as a key earnings engine for domestic lenders chasing this year's NT$3 trillion target.
  **國銀高資產AUM突破2.66兆 叩關2.7兆** — 國銀高資產財管業務續熱：截至5月底，21家銀行管理資產規模（AUM）達2兆6,633億元、單月大增1,110億元，高資產客戶數增1,090人至25,290人、年增70.4%，即將叩關2.7兆元大關。快速成長使高資產業務成為國銀重要獲利引擎，也呼應業界今年挑戰3兆元的目標。私人銀行式服務與金融資產擔保融資需求持續擴張，帶動整合式財管與跨境布局深化。
  - 來源 / Source：[中時新聞網](https://www.chinatimes.com/realtimenews/20260629000953-260410)
- **High-asset clients rotate from deposits into funds, insurance and structured notes** — The FSC noted a clear allocation shift among wealthy clients: by end-May, deposits fell to 42.5% of the NT$2.66tn pool, while funds made up 16.8%, bonds 14%, insurance 12.1% and offshore structured products 7.9%. The regulator attributed rising appetite for funds, insurance and structured products to the buoyant AI-driven investment mood — a trend pushing banks to broaden alternative and cross-border wealth offerings for high-net-worth customers.
  **高資產客戶資產配置轉向 基金、保險、結構型商品升溫** — 金管會指出高資產客戶資產配置明顯轉變：截至5月底，存款占比降至42.5%，基金占16.8%、債券14%、保險12.1%、境外結構型商品7.9%。主管機關分析，AI題材帶動投資氣氛，高資產客戶對基金、保險及結構型商品的配置意願提高。此趨勢促使銀行加速擴充另類資產與跨境財管商品線，以承接高資產客群的多元配置與收益需求，也考驗業者的商品開發與風險控管能力。
  - 來源 / Source：[中央社](https://www.cna.com.tw/news/afe/202606280161.aspx)
