### 🏦 Financial Sector / 金融業

- **FSC unveils eight new 2026 rules as life insurers brace for a major overhaul** — The FSC outlined eight regulatory changes taking effect in 2026, headlined by the simultaneous adoption of IFRS 17 and the Taiwan version of the new solvency regime (TW-ICS), which the industry calls "year one." IFRS 17 measures insurance liabilities at fair value, and from January all listed companies must also upload shareholder-meeting handbooks and annual reports earlier, tightening disclosure timelines.
  **金管會公布明年8項新制 壽險業迎IFRS 17與ICS大轉型** — 金管會公布2026年八項新制，核心為壽險業同步接軌IFRS 17與台版新一代清償能力制度（TW-ICS），業界稱為「元年」。IFRS 17以公允價值衡量保險負債，可望促使業者強化資產負債管理、提高保障型商品銷售；此外明年元旦起全體上市櫃公司須提前上傳股東會議事手冊及年報，收緊資訊揭露時程，全面提升治理透明度。
  - 來源 / Source：[聯合新聞網](https://udn.com/news/story/7239/9235703)
- **Transition measures ease capital strain on eight financial-holding groups** — To smooth the shift to the new solvency standard (TIS), the FSC's banking bureau previewed amendments to consolidated capital-adequacy rules for financial-holding companies. The changes are expected to relieve capital pressure on eight life-insurance-led financial holdings and let 11 insurance-type holdings defer their capital-adequacy filings by one month, cushioning the transition ahead of the 2026 rollout.
  **接軌TIS過渡措施出爐 8家壽險型金控資本大減壓** — 為協助平順接軌新一代清償能力制度（TIS），金管會銀行局預告「金融控股公司合併資本適足性管理辦法」修正草案，可望讓8家壽險型金控資本壓力大減，並使11家保險型金控適用延後一個月申報資本適足率。過渡措施降低接軌初期衝擊，替2026年制度正式上路預留緩衝，減輕金控增資壓力。
