### 💰 Tax & Regulations / 稅務法規

- **Finance Ministry clarifies how pre-death spousal gifts are folded back into estate tax** — Taiwan's Ministry of Finance issued operating principles stating that assets gifted to a spouse or heirs within two years before death are added back into the total estate for estate-tax purposes, with the liability shared by recipients in proportion to the value each received. The move tightens anti-avoidance rules around late-stage wealth transfers.
  **財政部訂原則：死亡前二年內贈與配偶財產併計遺產課稅** — 財政部發布作業原則，被繼承人死亡前二年內贈與配偶及各順序繼承人（含其配偶）之財產，應併入遺產總額課徵遺產稅，且遺產稅由各受贈人依受贈財產占遺產總額比例負擔。此舉強化對臨終前財產移轉的反避稅規範，牽動高資產族群的傳承安排與贈與時點規劃，宜提前檢視資產移轉架構。
  - 來源 / Source：[自由時報地產天下](https://estate.ltn.com.tw/article/26992)

- **2026 tax tweaks: estate and gift allowances unchanged, brackets and deductions indexed up** — For 2026, estate- and gift-tax exemptions stay put because cumulative CPI since the last change fell short of the 10% trigger, but tax brackets widen after prices rose more than 10% since 2017. Personal income-tax exemptions, basic-living allowances and deductions are also lifted in line with inflation, delivering modest relief to households at filing time.
  **2026稅制調整：遺贈稅免稅額不變 綜所稅級距與扣除額調高** — 2026年遺產與贈與稅免稅額維持不變，因上次調整以來CPI累計漲幅未達10%門檻；惟課稅級距因自2017年以來物價累計上漲逾10%而調升。綜合所得稅免稅額、基本生活費及各項扣除額亦依物價指數同步調高，形成民生減稅，減輕家庭稅負，也影響年度報稅與整體資產配置規劃的節奏。
  - 來源 / Source：[商業周刊](https://www.businessweekly.com.tw/business/blog/3018870)
