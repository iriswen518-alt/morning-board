The firm open signals resilient appetite for AI-leveraged Taiwan equities heading into an earnings-heavy "golden October."

### 🏭 Industry / 產業動態

- **TSMC's 2026 growth seen broadening beyond AI** — Analysts expect TSMC to remain the prime beneficiary of the semiconductor upcycle in 2026, with momentum no longer confined to AI accelerators. Non-AI applications on the 2-nanometer (N2) node are projected to add meaningfully, as N2 capacity roughly doubles toward 80,000–100,000 wafers per month by year-end. Broader demand would stabilize utilization and pricing, reinforcing TSMC's leadership and its pull-through effect on Taiwan's advanced-packaging and equipment suppliers.
  **台積電2026成長動能不只AI** — 法人預期台積電2026年仍是半導體上行週期最大受惠者，且成長動能不再侷限於AI加速器。2奈米（N2）製程的非AI應用可望顯著挹注，N2月產能估於年底倍增至8~10萬片。需求基礎擴大有助穩定產能利用率與報價，鞏固台積電的領導地位，也將帶動台灣先進封裝與設備供應鏈的連動成長。
  - 來源 / Source：[經濟日報](https://money.udn.com/money/story/5607/9233028)

- **IC-substrate and glass-fabric shortages tighten on AI packaging** — AI packaging demand is straining the IC-substrate supply chain, with ABF substrates and low-Dk glass fabric in short supply and BT-substrate T-glass also tight. The squeeze is handing structural pricing power to substrate and material makers, and SEMICON Taiwan has spotlighted AI custom chips, advanced packaging, HBM and ABF substrates as core themes. Persistent tightness supports earnings visibility across the mid-stream packaging-materials group into 2026.
  **AI封裝需求拉動、IC載板與玻纖布缺貨趨緊** — AI封裝需求使IC載板供應鏈吃緊，ABF載板與低介電（Low-Dk）玻纖布供不應求，BT載板用的T-glass材料亦見短缺。供需缺口讓載板與上游材料廠取得結構性的報價優勢；SEMICON Taiwan亦將AI客製晶片、先進封裝、HBM與ABF載板列為核心主題。缺貨態勢延續，有利中游封裝材料族群2026年的獲利能見度。
  - 來源 / Source：[uAnalyze](https://uanalyze.com.tw/articles/3280553281)

### 🏦 Financial Sector / 金融業

- **FSC lays out seven policy priorities for 2026** — Taiwan's Financial Supervisory Commission has outlined seven major initiatives for 2026, spanning stronger anti-fraud legislation, an Asian innovative-financing platform, virtual-asset (VASP) legislation, life-insurer asset-liability management under IFRS 17, a financial cybersecurity-resilience blueprint, the regulatory-sandbox adjustment platform, and measures to build an Asian asset-management hub. The agenda signals a regulatory tilt toward digital-asset oversight, insurer solvency and operational resilience across banks and insurers.
  **金管會揭2026七大施政重點** — 金管會公布2026年七大施政主軸，涵蓋強化打詐法制、推動亞洲創新融資平台、虛擬資產（VASP）專法、壽險業接軌IFRS 17的資產負債管理、金融資安韌性藍圖、金融創新監理調適平台，以及打造亞洲資產管理中心的各項措施。整體方向顯示監理重心偏向數位資產監管、保險業清償能力與銀行、保險的營運韌性，牽動金融業未來的合規與布局。
  - 來源 / Source：[金管會](https://www.fsc.gov.tw/ch/home.jsp?id=96)

- **IFRS 17 transition eases insurers' monthly reporting deadline** — As Taiwan's life insurers move onto IFRS 17 in 2026, the FSC has relaxed the monthly revenue-filing deadline for insurers and insurance-holding groups from the 10th to the 15th of each month, giving them more time to compile figures under the new accounting regime. The change acknowledges the heavier measurement burden of IFRS 17 and aims for smoother, more accurate disclosure as the industry shifts toward protection-type products.
  **IFRS 17過渡、金管會放寬保險業月營收申報期限** — 配合壽險業2026年接軌IFRS 17，金管會放寬保險業及保險控股集團的每月營收申報期限，由每月10日延至15日，讓業者有更充裕時間依新會計制度彙編數字。此舉反映IFRS 17衡量作業負擔加重，目的在於讓揭露更順暢、精確；隨產業轉向保障型商品，保費認列與獲利呈現方式也將更貼近實質。
  - 來源 / Source：[金管會](https://www.fsc.gov.tw/ch/home.jsp?id=128)

### 💎 Wealth Management / 財富管理

- **Kaohsiung wealth hub draws NT$273bn as asset-management law looms** — The Kaohsiung pilot zone of Taiwan's Asian asset-management center has attracted 18 banks, 2,269 high-net-worth clients and NT$273.2 billion in assets under management since its mid-2025 launch, while bank-run high-asset AUM nationwide has topped NT$2.1 trillion (up 53% year-on-year). With the FSC drafting a dedicated asset-management law, private-banking and cross-border services for wealthy clients are set to deepen, intensifying competition among banks for family-succession and discretionary-mandate business.
  **高雄亞資專區吸金2,732億、資產管理專法將上路** — 亞洲資產管理中心高雄專區自2025年年中開辦以來，已吸引18家銀行進駐、高資產客戶2,269位，管理資產規模衝上2,732億元；全國銀行承作高資產業務的管理規模則突破2.1兆元、年增達53%。隨金管會研擬「資產管理專法」，針對高資產客群的私人銀行與跨境服務可望深化，銀行在家族財富傳承與全權委託代操業務的競爭也將更激烈。

- **High-yield ETF payouts seen normalizing toward 5–6%** — Taiwan's fund industry has swelled past NT$22 trillion, with equity ETFs above NT$4.5 trillion, but strategists expect high-dividend ETF yields to normalize toward a healthier 5–6% as prices run ahead of earnings. The message to investors is a shift from chasing yield alone toward a total-return, asset-allocation approach that blends capital growth with stable income—especially as sector rotation quickens and pure high-dividend strategies face falling distribution rates.
