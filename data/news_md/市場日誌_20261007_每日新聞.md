### 🏭 Industry / 產業動態

- **ITRI sees 2026 chip output above NT$7tn, AI servers at 4.5 million units** — Taiwan's Industrial Technology Research Institute and the MIC forecast the island's 2026 semiconductor output value will exceed NT$7.13tn, up about 10% on the year, led by IC manufacturing as 3nm runs full and 2nm ramps. Global AI-server shipments are projected to reach 4.5 million units with penetration near 30%, sustaining four straight years of 40%-plus growth in advanced-process capacity.
  **工研院估2026半導體產值破7兆 AI伺服器衝450萬台** — 工研院與資策會MIC預估，台灣2026年半導體產值將突破7.13兆元、年增約10%，其中IC製造受惠3奈米滿載與2奈米放量，動能最強。全球AI伺服器出貨預估達450萬台、滲透率近三成，帶動先進製程產能連續四年成長逾四成，確立台灣在AI供應鏈的核心地位。
  - 來源 / Source：[聯合新聞網](https://udn.com/news/story/7240/9336826)
- **Taiwan's major IT makers extend record revenue streak on AI demand** — Taiwan's leading IT manufacturers, including TSMC and Hon Hai, logged a 29th straight month of revenue growth, with July combined revenue jumping more than 50% year on year and topping NT$2tn, among the largest monthly gains on record. The surge reflects relentless AI-server and advanced-chip demand, reinforcing expectations that the supply chain will stay busy through year-end.
  **台廠AI動能不墜 主要IT廠營收連29個月增長** — 台灣主要IT廠（含台積電、鴻海等）營收連續第29個月成長，7月合計營收年增逾五成、突破2兆元，為史上數一數二的單月增幅。強勁表現反映AI伺服器與先進晶片需求持續暢旺，市場預期供應鏈動能可望延續至年底，產業基本面維持穩健。

### 🏦 Financial Sector / 金融業

- **FSC adds three pilot schemes, plans a "financial giveback" guideline** — Financial Supervisory Commission chair Peng Jin-lung announced new pilots to deepen the Asian Asset Management Centre, including cross-border services, relaxing the 99-investor cap and prepaid-premium accounts, alongside a forthcoming "financial giveback" guideline. By end-April the policy had completed 52 regulatory adjustments, with industry assets under management up NT$10.62tn since mid-2024, signalling a steady widening of Taiwan's wealth-management framework.
  **金管會新增三大試辦 將發布「金融大回饋指引」** — 金管會主委彭金隆宣布為深化亞洲資產管理中心，新增「跨境服務」、「99人限制鬆綁」及「預繳保費帳戶」三大試辦項目，並將發布「金融大回饋指引」。截至4月底，相關政策已累計完成52項法規調適，金融業資產管理規模較2024年中增加10.62兆元，顯示資產管理制度逐步鬆綁與擴大。
  - 來源 / Source：[自由時報](https://ec.ltn.com.tw/article/breakingnews/5474360)
- **Taiwan's financial trio booked NT$988bn pre-tax profit in 2025, second-highest ever** — The banking, insurance and securities sectors posted combined pre-tax earnings of about NT$988bn in 2025, the second-highest on record. Banks led with NT$627.9bn, up about 11% year on year, while insurers' NT$193.7bn fell roughly 43% as firms set aside foreign-exchange volatility reserves. Regulators reiterated a balance of safety and development as the framework evolves.
  **金融三業2025稅前賺9880億 史上次高** — 金管會統計，2025年銀行、保險、證券三業稅前盈餘合計約9,880億元，為史上次高。銀行業以6,279億元居冠、年增約11%；保險業1,937億元、年減約43%，主因提列外匯價格變動準備金。主管機關重申「安全與發展並進」，在推動制度革新的同時兼顧金融穩定與風險控管。

### 💎 Wealth Management / 財富管理

- **Taiwan's ultra-high-net-worth ranks hit a record, tilting to offshore assets** — CTBC's 2026 wealth report says the number of people with net assets above NT$100mn reached about 123,000 in 2025, up 17,000 from 2021. Some 74% of ultra-high-net-worth clients hold offshore assets, rising to 86% for those above NT$1bn. Core allocations centre on equity funds, especially Taiwan and tech names, with bonds favouring emerging-market and monthly-distribution tranches.
  **台灣超高資產人數創高 配置偏向境外與月配息** — 依中國信託《2026台灣超高資產客群報告》，2025年淨資產逾新台幣1億元者約12.3萬人，較2021年增加1.7萬人。超高資產客戶中74%持有境外資產，資產逾10億元者更高達86%。核心配置以股票型基金為主，偏好台股與科技類股；債券則鍾情新興市場債與月配息級別，呈現分散與收益並重的趨勢。
  - 來源 / Source：[商周財富網](https://smart.businessweekly.com.tw/Reading/IndepArticle.aspx?id=6021609)
- **Taishin builds a high-net-worth platform for the Kaohsiung asset hub** — Taishin Bank said it is answering the Asian Asset Management Centre policy by teaming up across the group — Taishin Bank, Taishin Securities, Taishin Investment Trust and Shin Kong Life — to enter the Kaohsiung special zone and build an integrated high-net-worth wealth-management platform. The move leverages cross-subsidiary collaboration to serve affluent clients, part of a broader push by banks and insurers into trust, succession and offshore-focused services.
