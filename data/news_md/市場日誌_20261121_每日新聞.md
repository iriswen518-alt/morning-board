### Taiwan Equities / 股市行情

- **Taiex plunges 6.23% to cap its worst week since 1991** — Taiwan's benchmark Taiex sank 2,393.60 points, or 6.23%, to close at 36,033.29 on Friday, Nov 20, its fifth-largest intraday point loss on record, as a deepening global selloff in AI and chip stocks battered the tech-heavy market. Turnover swelled to about NT$877.35 billion. For the week the index shed roughly 17.5%, its steepest weekly drop since 1991, underscoring how fast sentiment has turned on stretched AI valuations.
  **台股崩跌 6.23% 單週創 1991 年來最慘** — 台股 11 月 20 日（週五）重挫 2,393.60 點、跌幅 6.23%，收 36,033.29 點，為史上第五大盤中跌點，全球 AI 與晶片股賣壓擴大，重創科技權重偏高的台股。成交量放大至約 8,773.5 億元。本週累計下跌約 17.5%，為 1991 年以來最慘單週表現，凸顯市場對 AI 高評價的信心快速轉向，資金從高本益比科技股撤離，短線觀望氣氛濃厚。

Chipmakers and suppliers led losses even as some report order books remain steady, highlighting a widening gap between resilient fundamentals and collapsing share prices. For the week, the Kospi lost 7.9% and the Nikkei 7.7%.

### 🏭 Industry / 產業動態

- **Asian tech and chip stocks plunge despite Anthropic's strong Wall Street debut** — Asian technology shares, led by chip names, tumbled on Friday as worries over the scale and payback of AI spending persisted, even after AI developer Anthropic — in which TSMC holds a stake — made a strong US market debut. The disconnect shows investors rewarding individual AI winners while broadly derating the hardware supply chain that builds out data-center capacity.
  **亞洲科技與晶片股重挫 Anthropic 美股強勢掛牌仍難止血** — 儘管台積電持股的 AI 開發商 Anthropic 於美股強勢掛牌，亞洲科技股（尤以晶片股為首）11 月 20 日仍全面下挫，市場對 AI 投資規模與回收年限的疑慮未解。個別 AI 贏家獲追捧、整體硬體供應鏈評價卻遭調降，凸顯資金對「建置資料中心算力」題材由全面擁抱轉為選擇性押注，台廠 AI 供應鏈評價短線承壓。
  - 來源 / Source：[Anadolu Agency](https://www.aa.com.tr/en/economy/asian-tech-stocks-plunge-despite-anthropics-strong-us-market-debut/4131708)
- **TSMC ADR and AMD slide further as chip selloff jumps from Asia to US premarket** — US-listed Taiwan Semiconductor fell about 4.2% and AMD dropped another 7% in Friday premarket trade, with Nasdaq futures down 1.6%, as Asia's chip rout carried into Wall Street. The moves underline TSMC's position at the center of the AI supply chain — nearly every leading chip designer relies on it to manufacture and package advanced processors, so a cooling in AI capex expectations hits the whole chain first.
  **台積電 ADR 與超微續跌 晶片賣壓由亞洲蔓延至美股盤前** — 11 月 20 日美股盤前，在美掛牌的台積電 ADR 下跌約 4.2%、超微（AMD）再跌約 7%，那斯達克期貨下滑 1.6%，亞洲晶片賣壓延燒至華爾街。此波走勢凸顯台積電居 AI 供應鏈核心地位——幾乎所有領先晶片設計商都仰賴其製造與封裝先進處理器，一旦 AI 資本支出預期降溫，台積電與整條供應鏈評價首當其衝，牽動台廠出貨與接單能見度。

### 🏦 Financial Sector / 金融業

- **FSC previews stablecoin and virtual-asset sub-laws under new VASP Act** — Taiwan's Financial Supervisory Commission has opened a 40-day comment period on nine draft sub-regulations implementing the Virtual Asset Service Providers Act, including rules governing stablecoin issuance. Non-bank issuers would need NT$300 million in capital, and stablecoins could be pegged 1:1 to the NT dollar or a single foreign currency (excluding the yuan), with reserves fully backing coins in circulation and audited periodically.
  **金管會預告穩定幣與虛擬資產子法 完善 VASP 監理架構** — 金管會預告《虛擬資產服務法》9 項授權子法草案，預告期 40 天，其中包含穩定幣發行許可及管理辦法。草案規定非銀行發行人資本額門檻為 3 億元，穩定幣可與新台幣或單一外幣 1 比 1 連結（不含人民幣等），準備資產總值不得低於流通在外餘額並須定期由會計師查核。法規最快 2027 年上路，為台灣穩定幣與虛擬資產市場建立發行與贖回的合規框架。
  - 來源 / Source：[uAnalyze](https://uanalyze.com.tw/articles/7237258297)
- **FSC rolls out fifth-round sustainable-finance evaluation metrics** — The FSC announced the indicators for its fifth (2027-cycle) sustainable-finance evaluation, continuing a program that scores banks, insurers and securities firms on their green-finance and ESG practices to steer capital toward sustainability goals. The move reinforces regulators' push to channel financing into green investment as part of Taiwan's broader climate-transition and financial-stability agenda.
  **金管會公布第五屆永續金融評鑑指標** — 金管會公布第五屆（116 年）永續金融評鑑指標，延續以評分引導銀行、保險與證券業強化綠色金融與 ESG 作為的政策，促使資金投向永續目標。此舉呼應主管機關推動綠色投資的方向，將融資導入淨零轉型，為台灣氣候轉型與金融穩定議程的一環，業者後續在永續揭露與投融資配置的落實程度，將成為市場與主管機關關注重點。
  - 來源 / Source：[金管會](https://www.fsc.gov.tw/ch/home.jsp?id=96&parentpath=0)

### 💎 Wealth Management / 財富管理

- **High-net-worth population seen rising to 124,000 holding NT$46 trillion** — A KPMG–E.Sun study estimates Taiwan's population with NT$100 million-plus in assets will grow from 93,000 in 2023 to an estimated 124,000 in 2026, holding about NT$46.1 trillion, with ETFs and high-net-worth services the main engines of expansion. The data point to a fast-shifting wealth landscape as affluent clients move from pure asset growth toward cross-generational succession planning.
  **高資產人口估增至 12.4 萬人 持有資產 46 兆元** — KPMG 與玉山銀行報告估計，台灣資產逾新台幣 1 億元的高資產人口將由 2023 年的 9.3 萬人，增至 2026 年約 12.4 萬人，持有資產約 46.1 兆元，ETF 與高資產業務為主要成長動能。數據顯示財富版圖快速質變，高資產客群關注焦點由單純追求資產增值，逐步轉向完善跨世代傳承規劃，帶動信託、保險與海外資產配置等傳承型商品需求同步升溫。
  - 來源 / Source：[KPMG Taiwan](https://kpmg.com/tw/zh/media/press-releases/2026/06/2026-taiwan-wealth-management-trends-high-net-worth-growth.html)
- **CTBC–BCG study sees HNW wealth reaching NT$59 trillion by 2029** — A study by CTBC Bank and BCG projects that the total personal wealth of Taiwan's high-net-worth clients will climb to about NT$59 trillion by 2029, with the number of such clients rising to roughly 155,000. The forecast underscores sustained momentum in the affluent segment and intensifying competition among banks to capture succession, trust and cross-border asset-management mandates.
  **中信與 BCG 報告：2029 年高資產財富上看 59 兆元** — 中國信託銀行與 BCG 報告預估，台灣高資產客群的個人財富總額將於 2029 年達約 59 兆元，客群人數增至約 15.5 萬人。此預測凸顯高資產族群動能延續，也反映銀行為爭取傳承、信託與跨境資產管理業務的競爭加劇。隨高資產需求由財富積累轉向財富保全與移轉，私人銀行與家族辦公室服務將成為各大金融機構布局財管版圖的關鍵戰場。
