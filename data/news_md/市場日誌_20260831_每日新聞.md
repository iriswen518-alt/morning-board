### 🏭 Industry / 產業動態

- **TSMC's CoWoS capacity sold out for 2026 as AI demand outstrips supply** — TSMC's advanced CoWoS packaging is fully booked through 2026, with monthly capacity set to climb from roughly 70,000 wafers at end-2025 toward 115,000 in 2026. The bottleneck underscores that demand for high-end AI packaging is running well ahead of supply, cementing advanced packaging as a structural profit driver and reinforcing the pricing power of the AI-chip value chain.
  **台積電CoWoS產能2026全數售罄 AI需求遠超供給** — 台積電先進封裝CoWoS產能已售罄至2026年，月產能將自2025年底約7萬片擴增至2026年的11.5萬片。此一瓶頸凸顯高階AI封裝需求遠超供給，使先進封裝成為結構性獲利動能，並強化AI晶片供應鏈的議價能力，帶動設備、載板與封測族群後市想像空間。
  - 來源 / Source：[CMoney 投資網誌](https://cmnews.com.tw/article/newsyoudeservetoknow-2ccb3d7c-4b6c-11f1-b0f1-4b070a256d8e)
- **Packaging roadmap accelerates as CoWoS-L and 3DIC timelines compress** — TSMC packaging executive Ho Chun said the 3DIC roadmap is being pulled in from a two-year to a roughly one-year cadence, while the focus shifts from CoWoS-S toward CoWoS-L; he also flagged emerging supply-chain material risks in the substrate layer. The faster upgrade cycle broadens the beneficiary list across equipment, ABF substrate and OSAT vendors such as ASE, which gains from outsourced lower-margin steps.
  **封裝藍圖加速 CoWoS-L與3DIC時程壓縮** — 台積電封裝要角何軍表示，3DIC推進節奏由兩年縮短至約一年，重心並由CoWoS-S轉向CoWoS-L，同時示警載板端浮現供應鏈材料風險。升級週期加快擴大受惠面，涵蓋設備、ABF載板與封測廠；日月光投控可望因台積電外包毛利率較低製程而持續受惠，供應鏈題材延燒。
  - 來源 / Source：[DIGITIMES](https://www.digitimes.com.tw/tech/dt/n/shwnws.asp?id=0000764808_TRG7811Y1WID1P79X9QL3)

### 🏦 Financial Sector / 金融業

- **FSC's eight major financial measures for 2026 roll out in phases** — The FSC's slate of eight new rules for 2026 is progressing, with six taking effect from Jan 1, including a requirement for all listed companies to upload shareholder-meeting handbooks and annual reports earlier, and a three-stage adoption of IFRS sustainability-disclosure standards. The package tightens governance and disclosure while pushing listed firms toward more timely, standardised sustainability reporting.
  **金管會2026年八大金融新制分階段上路** — 金管會2026年八項新制持續推進，其中六項自元旦起施行，包括全體上市櫃公司須提前上傳股東會議事手冊及年報，並分三階段接軌IFRS永續揭露準則。此套措施強化公司治理與資訊揭露，同時推動上市櫃公司邁向更即時、標準化的永續資訊揭露，牽動企業合規與投資人資訊透明度。
  - 來源 / Source：[旺得富理財網](https://wantrich.chinatimes.com/news/20251231900246-420501)
- **FSC moves to let brokerages offer foreign-currency financing for overseas securities** — The regulator is advancing a plan to allow securities firms handling clients' trades in foreign securities to provide foreign-currency financing, expanding the scope of brokerage business. The opening would give investors more leverage options for offshore holdings and let securities houses broaden fee-and-interest income, part of a broader push to deepen Taiwan's capital-market intermediation and competitiveness.
  **金管會擬開放券商辦理外幣融資** — 金管會推進開放證券商受託買賣外國有價證券得辦理外幣融資業務，擴大證券商業務範疇。此舉將提供投資人配置海外標的的更多槓桿選擇，並使券商拓展利息與手續費收入來源，屬深化台灣資本市場中介功能、提升競爭力的一環，後續實施細則與風控規範為市場關注焦點。
