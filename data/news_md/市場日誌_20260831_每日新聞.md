### 🏭 Industry / 產業動態

- **TSMC's CoWoS capacity sold out for 2026 as AI demand outstrips supply** — TSMC's advanced CoWoS packaging is fully booked through 2026, with monthly capacity set to climb from roughly 70,000 wafers at end-2025 toward 115,000 in 2026. The bottleneck underscores that demand for high-end AI packaging is running well ahead of supply, cementing advanced packaging as a structural profit driver and reinforcing the pricing power of the AI-chip value chain.
  **台積電CoWoS產能2026全數售罄 AI需求遠超供給** — 台積電先進封裝CoWoS產能已售罄至2026年，月產能將自2025年底約7萬片擴增至2026年的11.5萬片。此一瓶頸凸顯高階AI封裝需求遠超供給，使先進封裝成為結構性獲利動能，並強化AI晶片供應鏈的議價能力，帶動設備、載板與封測族群後市想像空間。
  - 來源 / Source：[CMoney 投資網誌](https://cmnews.com.tw/article/newsyoudeservetoknow-2ccb3d7c-4b6c-11f1-b0f1-4b070a256d8e)
- **Packaging roadmap accelerates as CoWoS-L and 3DIC timelines compress** — TSMC packaging executive Ho Chun said the 3DIC roadmap is being pulled in from a two-year to a roughly one-year cadence, while the focus shifts from CoWoS-S toward CoWoS-L; he also flagged emerging supply-chain material risks in the substrate layer. The faster upgrade cycle broadens the beneficiary list across equipment, ABF substrate and OSAT vendors such as ASE, which gains from outsourced lower-margin steps.
  **封裝藍圖加速 CoWoS-L與3DIC時程壓縮** — 台積電封裝要角何軍表示，3DIC推進節奏由兩年縮短至約一年，重心並由CoWoS-S轉向CoWoS-L，同時示警載板端浮現供應鏈材料風險。升級週期加快擴大受惠面，涵蓋設備、ABF載板與封測廠；日月光投控可望因台積電外包毛利率較低製程而持續受惠，供應鏈題材延燒。
  - 來源 / Source：[DIGITIMES](https://www.digitimes.com.tw/tech/dt/n/shwnws.asp?id=0000764808_TRG7811Y1WID1P79X9QL3)

### 🏦 Financial Sector / 金融業

- **FSC's eight major financial measures for 2026 roll out in phases** — The FSC's slate of eight new rules for 2026 is progressing, with six taking effect from Jan 1, including a requirement for all listed companies to upload shareholder-meeting handbooks and annual reports earlier, and a three-stage adoption of IFRS sustainability-disclosure standards. The package tightens governance and disclosure while pushing listed firms toward more timely, standardised sustainability reporting.

The opening would give investors more leverage options for offshore holdings and let securities houses broaden fee-and-interest income, part of a broader push to deepen Taiwan's capital-market intermediation and competitiveness.

### 💰 Tax & Regulations / 稅務法規

- **2026 estate- and gift-tax brackets nudge higher as CPI passes the reset trigger** — Taiwan's estate- and gift-tax exemption amounts stay unchanged for 2026, but a cumulative consumer-price rise of 12.42% has cleared the statutory threshold that triggers a bracket adjustment, lifting the monetary boundaries of each tax band. The change modestly eases the effective burden on middle- and higher-asset estates and matters for succession and gifting timing, a recurring planning point for high-net-worth families.
  **CPI累積漲12.42% 2026遺贈稅級距上調** — 財政部公告2026年遺產稅與贈與稅免稅額維持不變，但因消費者物價指數累積上漲12.42%、達到法定調整標準，各課稅級距金額同步調升。此調整使中高資產遺產的實質稅負略為減輕，對資產傳承與贈與時點規劃具實務影響，為高資產家庭年度稅務安排的關注重點之一。
  - 來源 / Source：[財政部](https://www.mof.gov.tw/)
- **Self-use home sales keep the NT$4mn income exemption under the house-and-land tax** — Under Taiwan's consolidated house-and-land income tax, individuals selling a qualifying owner-occupied property may exempt up to NT$4 million of taxable gain, with any excess taxed at a preferential 10% minimum rate. The relief remains a key consideration for households weighing property disposals, shaping after-tax proceeds and the timing of self-use home transactions in a firm housing market.
  **自住房地交易維持400萬免稅額** — 依房地合一所得稅制，個人出售符合條件之自住房地，課稅所得400萬元以內免納所得稅，超過部分按10%最低稅率課徵。此優惠仍是家庭評估房產處分的重要考量，影響稅後實得金額與自住房地交易時點，在房市維持穩健之際，牽動民眾售屋決策與資產配置節奏。
  - 來源 / Source：[財政部房地合一稅專區](https://www.mof.gov.tw/houseandland/multiplehtml/de144e74630c4ac59f2d84a068c889c9)

### 📜 Macro & Policy / 總經政策

- **DGBAS lifts Taiwan's 2026 GDP growth forecast to a 39-year high of 11.05%** — The statistics agency raised its 2026 economic-growth projection to 11.05%, up 1.41 points from its May estimate and the strongest pace in 39 years, after first-half growth reached 14.15% and Q2 real GDP rose 12.93%. - **July export orders jump 61.9% as US demand surges nearly 89%** — Taiwan's July export orders rose 61.9% from a year earlier, beating expectations, with orders from the United States soaring 88.9% to become the strongest driver amid booming AI and high-performance-computing demand. The record momentum reinforces the AI supply chain's grip on Taiwan's growth story, even as the concentration heightens sensitivity to any shift in US tech-capex and inventory cycles.
