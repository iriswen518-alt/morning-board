### 🏦 Financial Sector / 金融業

- **Life insurers on track to clear first TIS solvency test** — Under the new Taiwan Insurance Solvency (TIS) regime, semi-annual reports must be filed by end-September for 2026-2028, with the first half-year filing due this month. The FSC's calculation method suggests all life insurers should clear the 100% statutory qualifying line for first-half TIS, easing market concern about capital adequacy after the transition from the previous RBC framework.
  **壽險 TIS 資本首考 可望全數過關** — 依新版台灣保險業清償能力制度（TIS），2026 至 2028 年半年報須於 9 月底前申報，首份半年報本月亮牌。金管會公布的計算方式顯示，各壽險公司上半年 TIS 可望全數站上 100% 法定合格線，緩解市場對制度由 RBC 過渡後資本適足性的疑慮。

### 💎 Wealth Management / 財富管理

- **FSC raises single-stock cap for equity funds and active ETFs to 25%** — The regulator eased the rule barring domestic equity funds and active ETFs from holding more than 10% of net asset value in a single listed company, lifting the ceiling to 25%. The change gives fund managers more room to reflect index-heavy names such as TSMC in their portfolios, potentially channelling fresh flows into large-cap leaders while raising concentration-risk considerations for investors.
  **金管會放寬股票型基金、主動式 ETF 單一持股上限至 25%** — 金管會發布令放寬國內股票型基金及主動式 ETF 投資單一上市公司股票不得超過基金淨值 10% 的限制，上限提高至 25%。此舉讓基金經理人更能反映台積電等權值龍頭比重，可望為大型權值股帶來活水，同時提高投資人須留意的集中度風險。

### 💰 Tax & Regulations / 稅務法規

- **Taiwan tax take tops NT$3tn in first eight months, a record** — The Ministry of Finance reported August net tax revenue of NT$198.2bn, a same-month record and up 28.9% year on year, lifting the January-August cumulative haul to NT$3.075tn, also a record and up 25.2%. Securities transaction tax logged a 13th straight month of annual gains, reflecting buoyant equity turnover, while corporate income tax rose 24.9%, signalling strong corporate profitability feeding the fiscal position.
  **台灣前八月稅收破 3 兆創新高** — 財政部公布 8 月全國賦稅實徵淨額 1,982 億元、創同月新高、年增 28.9%，累計前八月達 3 兆 749 億元、同創新高、年增 25.2%。證交稅連 13 個月正成長，反映股市交投熱絡；營所稅年增 24.9%，顯示企業獲利強勁挹注國庫，為財政穩健打下基礎。
  - 來源 / Source：[經濟日報](https://money.udn.com/money/story/6710/9748953)
- **Robust revenue revives hopes of a local "over-collection dividend"** — With central-government allocated tax revenue reaching NT$646.3bn by end-August — 73.1% of the full-year estimate of NT$884.1bn — officials suggest a year-end surplus that could flow to local governments is realistically in prospect. The strong run rate keeps alive debate over how any over-collection should be returned or spent, a recurring fiscal-policy and tax-fairness issue in Taiwan.
  **稅收亮眼 地方「超徵紅包」有望** — 中央統籌分配稅款截至 8 月底累計實收 6,463 億元，已達全年原估 8,841 億元的 73.1%，官員表示依達成率「可樂觀期待」年底超徵、分配予地方。強勁的稅收進度延續了超徵款應如何返還或運用的討論，是台灣反覆出現的財政政策與租稅公平議題。
