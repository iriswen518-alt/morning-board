### 🏦 Financial Sector / 金融業

- **Life insurers on track to clear first TIS solvency test** — Under the new Taiwan Insurance Solvency (TIS) regime, semi-annual reports must be filed by end-September for 2026-2028, with the first half-year filing due this month. The FSC's calculation method suggests all life insurers should clear the 100% statutory qualifying line for first-half TIS, easing market concern about capital adequacy after the transition from the previous RBC framework.
  **壽險 TIS 資本首考 可望全數過關** — 依新版台灣保險業清償能力制度（TIS），2026 至 2028 年半年報須於 9 月底前申報，首份半年報本月亮牌。金管會公布的計算方式顯示，各壽險公司上半年 TIS 可望全數站上 100% 法定合格線，緩解市場對制度由 RBC 過渡後資本適足性的疑慮。
  - 來源 / Source：[聯合新聞網](https://udn.com/news/story/7239/9742463)
- **FSC lines up October policy push on Kaohsiung hub and virtual assets** — The regulator is set to roll out a cluster of measures in October, including a Kaohsiung financial special zone and new virtual-asset rules, signaling a more active agenda to deepen Taiwan's capital markets and position the island as a regional asset-management centre. The pipeline points to further product and cross-border openings that could broaden fee income for banks and securities firms.
  **金管會 10 月政策接力上路 高雄專區、虛擬資產在列** — 金管會規劃 10 月推出一系列措施，包括高雄金融專區與虛擬資產新規，釋出深化資本市場、打造亞洲資產管理中心的積極訊號。相關進程指向更多商品與跨境開放，可望擴大銀行與券商的手續費收入來源。

### 💎 Wealth Management / 財富管理

- **FSC raises single-stock cap for equity funds and active ETFs to 25%** — The regulator eased the rule barring domestic equity funds and active ETFs from holding more than 10% of net asset value in a single listed company, lifting the ceiling to 25%. The change gives fund managers more room to reflect index-heavy names such as TSMC in their portfolios, potentially channelling fresh flows into large-cap leaders while raising concentration-risk considerations for investors.
  **金管會放寬股票型基金、主動式 ETF 單一持股上限至 25%** — 金管會發布令放寬國內股票型基金及主動式 ETF 投資單一上市公司股票不得超過基金淨值 10% 的限制，上限提高至 25%。此舉讓基金經理人更能反映台積電等權值龍頭比重，可望為大型權值股帶來活水，同時提高投資人須留意的集中度風險。
  - 來源 / Source：[金管會新聞稿](https://www.fsc.gov.tw/ch/home.jsp?id=96&parentpath=0%2C2&mcustomize=news_view.jsp&dataserno=202604230001&dtable=News)
- **Ten life insurers cleared to run wealth-management business** — As of May 2026, ten insurers — including Shin Kong, Nan Shan, Fubon, Cathay, KGI, Taiwan Life, Chubb, BNP Paribas Cardif and Yuanta — have won FSC approval to offer wealth-management services, expanding high-net-worth product channels beyond banks. Separately, Nan Shan's one-year hospitalisation-and-home-acute-care health policy was approved this month, illustrating continued product innovation aimed at affluent and protection-focused clients.
  **10 家壽險獲准辦理財富管理業務** — 截至 2026 年 5 月，包括新光、南山、富邦、國泰、凱基、台灣、保誠、安達、法國巴黎、元大等 10 家壽險公司獲金管會核准辦理財富管理業務，將高資產商品通路自銀行向保險端延伸。另南山人壽一年期「住院暨在宅急症照護」健康險本月獲准，反映針對高資產與保障型客群的商品持續創新。
