- **FSC's 2026 reform package tightens disclosure and digitalises shareholder meetings** — The Financial Supervisory Commission's eight measures for 2026 span securities and insurance. Listed companies must upload meeting handbooks and annual reports earlier (30 and 14 days ahead), an e-platform lets shareholders claim non-physical meeting gifts online from end-March, and insurers' monthly revenue filing deadline moves from the 10th to the 15th. The package aims to improve transparency, efficiency and investor convenience across capital markets.
