### 🏭 Industry / 產業動態

- **CPU Shortage Looms as AWS Faces Cloud Computing Bottleneck** — Amazon Web Services reported a growing shortage of central processing units as AI-driven workloads surge, prompting engineers to cut CPU usage and extending EC2 server provisioning times. The strain signals a shift in the AI compute crunch from GPUs to CPUs, prompting chipmakers Intel, AMD and Arm to anticipate sustained demand growth, which could constrain cloud capacity and increase costs for enterprises.
  **CPU恐成雲端業下一個瓶頸！亞馬遜AWS出現「CPU短缺」** — AI 代理快速普及推升雲端運算需求，AWS 內部要求削減 CPU 資源占用，導致 EC2 伺服器供應時間拉長。此現象顯示 AI 計算瓶頸正從 GPU 轉移至 CPU，英特爾、AMD 與 Arm 均看好 CPU 需求持續成長，可能限制雲端容量並推升企業成本。
  - 來源 / Source：[鉅亨網](https://news.cnyes.com/news/id/6566508)
