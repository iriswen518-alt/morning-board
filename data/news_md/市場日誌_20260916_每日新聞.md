### 🏭 Industry / 產業動態

- **TSMC: AI demand strongest in 30 years, budget raised to 1.9x** — At SEMICON Taiwan 2026, deputy co-COO Y.L. Hou said AI demand is growing faster than at any point in three decades. TSMC lifted its 2026 equipment budget from 1.5x to 1.9x its late-2025 estimate in a single quarter and is now building close to 20 fabs worldwide (13 in Taiwan). The scramble underscores structurally tight AI capacity, a long-term tailwind for Taiwan's equipment and materials suppliers.
  **台積電：AI需求30年最強 設備預算上修至1.9倍** — 台積電副共同營運長侯永清在SEMICON Taiwan 2026指出，AI需求成長速度為30年僅見；台積電2026年設備採購預算單季由原估1.5倍再上修至1.9倍，全球同步興建近20座晶圓廠（台灣13座）。擴產規模創高仍難滿足需求，顯示AI產能結構性吃緊，台廠設備與材料供應鏈可望長線受惠。
  - 來源 / Source：[科技新報 TechNews](https://finance.technews.tw/2026/09/02/tsmc-reveals-global-factory-construction-boom-and-semiconductor-supply-chain-challenges)

- **Taiwan targets NT$8tn chip output as AI theme broadens** — The Industrial Development Administration is aiming for semiconductor output above NT$8 trillion in 2026, with domestic chip-equipment output potentially topping NT$270 billion. Meanwhile the AI theme is broadening beyond GPUs and AI servers into rack-scale systems, liquid cooling, PCB/CCL, power supply, HBM/CoWoS and silicon photonics—widening the pool of Taiwanese beneficiaries and diversifying supply-chain growth drivers.
  **台灣拚半導體產值破8兆 AI題材向下游擴散** — 經濟部產業發展署力拚2026年半導體產值突破8兆元，國產半導體設備產值有望站上2,700億元。同時AI投資題材由GPU、AI伺服器向整櫃式伺服器、液冷散熱、PCB/CCL、電源供應、HBM/CoWoS與矽光子等擴散，受惠台廠範圍明顯放大，供應鏈成長動能更趨多元，降低對單一環節的依賴。
  - 來源 / Source：[永豐金證券豐雲學堂](https://www.sinotrade.com.tw/richclub/hotstock/SEMICON-Taiwan-2026-%E5%8D%8A%E5%B0%8E%E9%AB%94%E5%B1%95%E5%BF%85%E7%9C%8B%E4%BA%AE%E9%BB%9E-6a7c31f0dea2060b9179a8ad)

### 🏦 Financial Sector / 金融業

- **FSC promotes elderly-care trusts to safeguard retirees** — The FSC in early September pushed its "elderly-care trust" initiative, encouraging banks to expand trust products that protect retirees' assets and daily living needs. As Taiwan ages, the policy channels bank trust expertise toward long-term financial security for seniors, addressing a widening social-protection gap while opening a stable fee-income line for banks.
  **金管會推安養信託 守護樂齡生活** — 金管會於9月初推動「安養信託助力，守護樂齡生活」，鼓勵銀行擴大以信託守護退休族群資產與生活所需。面對高齡化社會，政策引導銀行運用信託專業，強化長者長期財務安全，既回應社會保障缺口，也為銀行開拓穩定的手續費收入業務，帶動樂齡金融市場成長。
  - 來源 / Source：[金管會銀行局](https://www.banking.gov.tw/)

- **FSC launches anti-fraud joint-defense drive** — The FSC, with financial examiners and police, launched a "2026 anti-fraud joint-defense" summit stressing technology-driven fraud prevention across the whole financial system, and warned the public against investing via institutions not licensed to operate in Taiwan. The coordinated campaign tightens front-line controls at banks and pressures firms to upgrade transaction monitoring and customer protection.
  **金管會啟動金檢警聯防 科技阻詐** — 金管會偕金檢與警政啟動「2026金檢警聯防」高峰會，主打「科技阻詐、全域守護」，強化全金融體系反詐防線，並提醒民眾勿透過未經核准在台設立據點的金融機構投資。跨機關聯防收緊銀行第一線控管，也促使業者升級交易監控與客戶保護機制，防詐合規投入預料同步升高。
  - 來源 / Source：[金管會新聞稿](https://www.fsc.gov.tw/ch/home.jsp?id=96&parentpath=0,2)

### 💎 Wealth Management / 財富管理

- **September fund wave: five launches including two active ETFs** — Five new funds launch in September, among them two active ETFs—Capital Core 50 (00415A) and Allianz Asia Semiconductor (00412A)—plus KGI's select high-yield non-investment-grade bond ETF (00992B) and multi-asset funds from Fubon and First. The pipeline reflects strong retail appetite for active ETFs and income products, deepening Taiwan's fund market and widening yield and thematic options for wealth clients.
  **9月新基金募集潮 主動式ETF添生力軍** — 9月新基金募集潮湧現，5檔新品接力登場，含2檔主動式ETF——主動群益核心50(00415A)與主動安聯亞半導體(00412A)，以及街口特選優息非投資等級債ETF(00992B)，另有富邦、第一金兩檔多重資產基金。反映投資人對主動ETF與收益型商品需求強勁，擴大台灣基金市場，也為財管客戶提供更多元的收益與題材配置選擇。
  - 來源 / Source：[Newtalk新聞](https://newtalk.tw/news/view/2026-09-07/1058205)

- **Wealth Management 2.0: HNW assets hit NT$2.4tn, clients up 229%** — Six years into the framework, banks' high-net-worth business has grown from NT$857.1 billion to NT$2.4 trillion, with client numbers up 229%; onshore fund assets jumped from NT$4.86 trillion to NT$11.38 trillion, ETFs alone reaching NT$7.4 trillion. Yuanta SITE also introduced overseas securities lending via its 20-year US Treasury ETF (00679B) to generate extra yield—signs the industry is scaling toward Taiwan's Asian asset-hub ambitions.
  **財管2.0成果 高資產規模達2.4兆、客戶增229%** — 財管2.0推動六年，銀行高資產業務規模由8,571億元增至2.4兆元、客戶數成長229%；境內基金三年由4.86兆元衝上11.38兆元，其中ETF一項即達7.4兆元。元大投信並與道富銀行合作，由元大美債20年ETF(00679B)率先導入海外證券出借以創造額外收益，顯示業者規模與商品持續升級，呼應打造亞洲資產管理中心的政策目標。
