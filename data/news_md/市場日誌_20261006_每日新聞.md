### 🏭 Industry / 產業動態

- **ITRI Sees Taiwan Chip Output Topping NT$7 Trillion in 2026** — Taiwan's Industrial Technology Research Institute projects the island's semiconductor output value will exceed NT$7 trillion (about NT$7.13tn) in 2026, up roughly 10% year-on-year, led by IC manufacturing as 3nm runs at full capacity and 2nm ramps. The outlook shows AI-driven demand keeping Taiwan central to global chip supply.
  **工研院：2026 台灣半導體產值上看 7 兆元** — 工研院預估，2026 年台灣半導體產值可突破 7 兆元、約 7.13 兆元，年增約一成，其中 IC 製造受惠 3 奈米滿載與 2 奈米放量，動能最強。顯示 AI 需求持續帶動先進製程，鞏固台灣在全球晶片供應鏈的關鍵地位，相關供應鏈營運可望同步受惠。
  - 來源 / Source：[經濟日報](https://udn.com/news/story/7240/9336826)
- **AI-Server Boom Lifts Taiwan Hardware Makers to Record Revenue** — Surging AI-server demand has driven major Taiwan IT makers to months of consecutive revenue growth, with assemblers plus advanced-PCB, substrate, cooling and power-supply suppliers all benefiting. The broad-based strength shows Taiwan's chain—spanning design, fabrication, packaging and components—remains the hub of the AI hardware build-out.
  **AI 伺服器熱潮 台廠營收續創高** — AI 伺服器需求強勁，帶動台灣主要 IT 廠營收連月成長，伺服器組裝、載板、高階 PCB、散熱與電源供應等供應鏈同步受惠。顯示台灣供應鏈橫跨設計、製造、封測與零組件，持續扮演全球 AI 硬體建置樞紐，惟須留意終端需求與庫存變化對後續動能的影響。
  - 來源 / Source：[科技新報](https://finance.technews.tw/2026/08/26/taiwan-major-it-firms-revenue-surges-over-50-exceeds-2-trillion-sets-new-record-high/)

### 🏦 Financial Sector / 金融業

- **FSC Keeps "Safety and Development in Parallel," Puts Anti-Fraud and Cyber-Resilience First** — Taiwan's Financial Supervisory Commission says its agenda continues to balance stability with growth—strengthening banks' capital resilience and risk management while elevating fraud prevention. New cyber rules define responsibilities for financial CISOs, make zero-trust architecture mandatory rather than encouraged, and require software-supply-chain security (SBOM) mechanisms.
  **金管會：安全與發展並進 防詐、資安韌性列重點** — 金管會施政延續「安全與發展並進」，強化金融機構資本韌性與風險管理，並將防詐列為重點工作。資安方面新增監理要求，包括明定金融資安長權責、零信任架構由鼓勵改為必備，以及建立軟體供應鏈安全（SBOM）機制，推升金融業合規與資安投入。
  - 來源 / Source：[金管會](https://www.fsc.gov.tw/ch/home.jsp?id=128)
- **Record Sector Profits Set Backdrop as FSC Advances Fintech Reform** — Taiwan's three major financial sectors posted among their highest-ever pre-tax earnings, giving regulators room to push fintech priorities: amending the fintech innovation-and-experimentation act, developing digital insurers, and a new regulatory-adaptation platform linking banking, insurance, securities and tech working groups to modernise supervision while safeguarding stability.
  **金融三業獲利居高 金管會推金融科技創新** — 金融三業稅前獲利居歷史高檔，為監理單位推動金融科技創新提供空間。金管會規劃修訂金融科技發展與創新實驗條例、發展數位保險公司，並設「金融行動創新法規調適平台」，由銀行、保險、證券期貨與科技創新工作小組建立產官學溝通管道，兼顧創新與穩定。
  - 來源 / Source：[uanalyze 玩股網](https://uanalyze.com.tw/articles/6397346877)

### 💎 Wealth Management / 財富管理

- **Taiwan's Ultra-Rich to Top 124,000, Commanding NT$46 Trillion** — A bank-and-accountancy study cited in local media projects Taiwan will have more than 124,000 individuals with assets over NT$100 million in 2026, together holding some NT$46.1 trillion, with the high-net-worth population growing about 10% a year toward 155,000 by 2029. The expanding pool is reshaping demand for private banking, trusts and cross-border allocation.
  **台灣億級富豪逾 12.4 萬人 掌握 46.1 兆元財富** — 據金融業者與會計師事務所調查，2026 年台灣資產逾 1 億元的高資產客戶將成長至逾 12.4 萬人，合計掌握約 46.1 兆元財富，且人數以年均約一成成長，2029 年上看 15.5 萬人。龐大高資產族群帶動私人銀行、信託與跨境配置需求，成為財管業兵家必爭之地。
  - 來源 / Source：[uanalyze 玩股網](https://uanalyze.com.tw/articles/4310248242)
- **Succession Planning and Offshore Assets Dominate HNW Portfolios** — Wealth reports show Taiwan's high-net-worth clients increasingly treat cross-border allocation as core to family and business continuity, with about 74% already holding offshore assets and roughly 85% engaged in or preparing succession plans. Equity funds—especially Taiwan and tech names—anchor portfolios, while bond demand favours emerging-market and monthly-dividend share classes.
  **高資產族群重傳承 跨境與月配息為配置核心** — 財富調查顯示，台灣高資產客戶將跨境配置視為支撐家族與企業傳承的基礎，約 74% 已持有境外資產，約 85% 已啟動或準備傳承規劃。核心配置以股票型基金為主，偏好台股與科技類股；債券則青睞新興市場債與月配息級別，顯示財管需求從單純投資轉向資產結構與世代傳承。
  - 來源 / Source：[Smart 智富](https://smart.businessweekly.com.tw/Reading/IndepArticle.aspx?id=6021609)
