- **FSC pre-announces stablecoin issuance rules; tech firms barred from going solo (2026-11-03)** — Taiwan's Financial Supervisory Commission on Nov. 3 unveiled draft rules for licensing and managing stablecoin issuers, alongside eight VASP-related orders and anti-money-laundering amendments. Issuers are limited to banks, financial holding companies and approved institutions; non-bank issuers need at least NT$300 million in paid-in capital. Joint ventures must keep financial firms as majority owners, so technology companies cannot issue alone. Only stablecoins pegged 1:1 to the NT dollar or a single foreign currency are allowed, with a 40-day comment period.

### 💰 Tax & Regulations / 稅務法規

- **FSC unveils VASP licensing and anti-money-laundering draft rules (2026-11-03)** — Alongside its stablecoin framework, the Financial Supervisory Commission pre-announced eight draft orders authorizing virtual-asset service providers (VASPs) and amendments tightening anti-money-laundering controls for financial institutions, implementing the Virtual Asset Service Act passed in July. The package sets governance, custody and compliance standards; operators are urged to accelerate legal, financial and governance readiness before the law takes effect, expected as early as Q1 2027.
  **金管會預告VASP授權命令及洗錢防制修正草案** — 配合穩定幣規範，金管會同步預告8項虛擬資產服務商（VASP）授權命令草案，以及金融機構洗錢防制辦法修正，落實7月公布的《虛擬資產服務法》。草案訂定治理、保管與法遵標準，業者被要求加速法遵、財務與治理轉軌；該法最快2027年第1季上路。
  - 來源 / Source：[中央社](https://www.cna.com.tw/business/chinese/448938)
- **Estate-and-gift tax reform would fold pre-death gifts into the estate** — The Ministry of Finance has pre-announced a draft amendment to the Estate and Gift Tax Act responding to a Constitutional Court ruling. Under the proposal, assets gifted to relatives within two years before death — including property and shares — would be added to the taxable estate, with the recipient bearing the corresponding tax rather than the burden being split among all heirs, tightening a common succession-planning loophole.
  **遺贈稅修法擬將死前2年贈與併入遺產課稅** — 財政部已預告《遺產及贈與稅法》修正草案，回應憲法法庭判決。草案將被繼承人死亡前兩年內贈與親屬的財產（含房屋與股份）併入遺產總額課稅，並改由受贈人負擔相應稅負，而非由全體繼承人分攤，收緊常見的傳承節稅安排。
  - 來源 / Source：[ETtoday](https://finance.ettoday.net/news/3105961)
