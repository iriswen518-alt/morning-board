### 🏭 Industry / 產業動態

- **TSMC posts record Q3 results on AI demand at its Oct 15 earnings call (2026-10-15)** — TSMC reported third-quarter consolidated revenue of about NT$1.49 trillion, up 17.6% sequentially and 50.9% year-on-year, with net profit of NT$763.1 billion and EPS of NT$29.43 — all quarterly records. In US-dollar terms revenue reached US$46.7 billion, above guidance, at a 66.4% gross margin. The foundry lifted 2026 capex to US$60–64 billion and guided full-year dollar revenue up about 44%, reaffirming resilient AI accelerator demand.
