### 💰 Tax & Regulations / 稅務法規

- **Taiwan finalises deemed-estate tax reform; exemption seen rising in 2027 (2026-08)** — Taiwan's legislature passed amendments changing how "deemed estate" gifts are taxed: property gifted to a spouse or specified relatives within two years before death will now be taxed to the recipient, and heirs who renounce an inheritance no longer escape the related tax on gifts received. The Ministry of Finance is drafting enforcement rules, and officials estimate the estate-tax exemption could rise from NT$13.33 million to about NT$14.78 million in 2027.
  **台灣擬制遺產課稅修法三讀，免稅額2027擬調升（2026-08）** — 立法院三讀通過擬制遺產課稅方式修正：死亡前兩年內贈與配偶及特定親屬的財產，遺產稅改由受贈人負擔；拋棄繼承者亦不再免除受贈財產的相關稅負。財政部表示正研擬遺贈稅法施行細則等子法規，俟總統公布後加強宣導。官員估遺產稅免稅額可望自1,333萬元調升至2027年約1,478萬元，牽動高資產族群的傳承與贈與規劃，信託與保單安排須及早因應。
