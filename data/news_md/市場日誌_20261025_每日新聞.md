### 🏭 Industry / 產業動態

- **TSMC's September revenue hits a fresh same-period record on AI demand** — TSMC reported September revenue of about NT$511.9 billion, down 0.6% month-on-month but up 54.6% from a year earlier, a record for the month. Third-quarter revenue reached roughly NT$1.49 trillion, with the first nine months up about 41% year-on-year, underscoring how AI accelerator and advanced-node demand continues to drive the foundry's top line.
  **台積電9月營收創同期新高，AI需求撐盤** — 台積電9月合併營收約新台幣5,118.57億元，月減0.6%、年增54.6%，創歷年同期新高；第三季營收約1.49兆元，前九月累計年增約41%。數據顯示AI加速器與先進製程需求續強，持續推升晶圓代工龍頭營收動能，也為台股AI族群後市提供基本面支撐，市場聚焦第四季展望。
  - 來源 / Source：[科技新報 TechNews](https://finance.technews.tw/2026/10/08/tsmcs-september-revenue-declined-month-on-month-but-still-hit-a-new-high-for-the-same-period/)

- **TSMC seen raising full-year growth and capex guidance on AI boom** — Reports indicate TSMC expects 2026 revenue to grow more than 40%, above an earlier 30% estimate, while lifting capital spending guidance to roughly US$60–64 billion from US$52–56 billion. High-performance computing now accounts for around 60%+ of revenue, reflecting a decisive shift toward AI and data-centre workloads and tight leading-edge and CoWoS packaging capacity.
  **台積電上修全年營收與資本支出展望** — 外媒報導，台積電預期2026年營收成長上看40%以上，高於先前約30%預估，資本支出亦自520–560億美元上調至約600–640億美元。高效能運算（HPC）營收占比升至約六成以上，凸顯業務重心加速轉向AI與資料中心；先進製程與CoWoS封裝產能持續供不應求，鞏固其在AI供應鏈的關鍵地位。
  - 來源 / Source：[自由時報財經](https://ec.ltn.com.tw/article/breakingnews/5413307)

### 🏦 Financial Sector / 金融業

- **FSC chair sets out six policy priorities, including a finance-sector LLM** — In a business report to lawmakers, FSC Chairman Peng Jin-lung outlined six priorities spanning stronger financial resilience and risk supervision, a new-generation solvency regime for insurers, and a plan to release a finance-industry large language model targeted for the first quarter of 2027. The agenda signals continued emphasis on stability, supervision and responsible adoption of AI across the sector.
  **金管會主委提六大施政，含金融業專用LLM** — 金管會主委彭金隆向立法院提出業務報告，列出六大施政面向，包括強化金融韌性與風險監理、保險業新一代清償能力制度，以及預計於2027年第一季發布金融業務專用大型語言模型。整體方向延續對金融穩定與監理的重視，並推動業界審慎導入AI，牽動後續銀行、保險與證券業的法遵與數位轉型步調。
  - 來源 / Source：[NOWnews 今日新聞](https://www.nownews.com/news/6884087)

- **Taiwan's three financial sectors post NT$1.17 trillion in pre-tax profit** — The same FSC report noted that banking, securities/futures and insurance together earned about NT$1.17 trillion in pre-tax profit in January–August, up NT$566.2 billion year-on-year. Insurance premium income reached roughly NT$2.41 trillion through August, up NT$492.2 billion, pointing to a broadly healthy financial industry even as insurers transition to tougher capital standards.
  **金融三業前8月稅前盈餘1.17兆元** — 金管會業務報告指出，今年1至8月銀行業、證券期貨業、保險業三業合計稅前盈餘約新台幣1兆1,702億元，年增5,662億元；保險業截至8月底總保費收入約2兆4,069億元，年增4,922億元。數據顯示金融業整體獲利體質穩健，惟壽險業正接軌更嚴格的資本標準，過渡期提存準備金將影響短期獲利與配息空間。
  - 來源 / Source：[NOWnews 今日新聞](https://www.nownews.com/news/6884087)
