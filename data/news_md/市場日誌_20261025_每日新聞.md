### 🏭 Industry / 產業動態

- **TSMC's September revenue hits a fresh same-period record on AI demand** — TSMC reported September revenue of about NT$511.9 billion, down 0.6% month-on-month but up 54.6% from a year earlier, a record for the month. Third-quarter revenue reached roughly NT$1.49 trillion, with the first nine months up about 41% year-on-year, underscoring how AI accelerator and advanced-node demand continues to drive the foundry's top line.
  **台積電9月營收創同期新高，AI需求撐盤** — 台積電9月合併營收約新台幣5,118.57億元，月減0.6%、年增54.6%，創歷年同期新高；第三季營收約1.49兆元，前九月累計年增約41%。數據顯示AI加速器與先進製程需求續強，持續推升晶圓代工龍頭營收動能，也為台股AI族群後市提供基本面支撐，市場聚焦第四季展望。
  - 來源 / Source：[科技新報 TechNews](https://finance.technews.tw/2026/10/08/tsmcs-september-revenue-declined-month-on-month-but-still-hit-a-new-high-for-the-same-period/)

- **TSMC seen raising full-year growth and capex guidance on AI boom** — Reports indicate TSMC expects 2026 revenue to grow more than 40%, above an earlier 30% estimate, while lifting capital spending guidance to roughly US$60–64 billion from US$52–56 billion. High-performance computing now accounts for around 60%+ of revenue, reflecting a decisive shift toward AI and data-centre workloads and tight leading-edge and CoWoS packaging capacity.
  **台積電上修全年營收與資本支出展望** — 外媒報導，台積電預期2026年營收成長上看40%以上，高於先前約30%預估，資本支出亦自520–560億美元上調至約600–640億美元。高效能運算（HPC）營收占比升至約六成以上，凸顯業務重心加速轉向AI與資料中心；先進製程與CoWoS封裝產能持續供不應求，鞏固其在AI供應鏈的關鍵地位。

The agenda signals continued emphasis on stability, supervision and responsible adoption of AI across the sector.

### 📜 Macro & Policy / 總經政策

- **Taiwan's central bank holds its policy rate at 2% for a 10th straight quarter** — Taiwan's central bank kept its benchmark discount rate unchanged at 2% at its latest board meeting, extending a hold that now spans ten consecutive quarters, and declined to follow moves by the U.S. Federal Reserve. Governor Yang Chin-long signalled that monetary operations remain tilted toward the tighter side, aimed at anchoring inflation expectations and maintaining price stability.
  **央行政策利率連10季維持2%、操作偏緊** — 台灣央行在最近一次理監事會維持政策利率（重貼現率）2%不變，已連續10季按兵不動，並未跟進美國聯準會的動作。總裁楊金龍釋出貨幣操作仍朝偏緊方向的訊號，目的在錨定通膨預期、穩定物價。市場解讀央行短期不急於轉向，匯率與資金面維持穩定，對高評價的台股與房市降溫具一定約束力。
  - 來源 / Source：[u 分析 uAnalyze](https://uanalyze.com.tw/articles/9516755544)

- **Central bank lifts its growth forecast as inflation runs just above target** — At the same meeting, the central bank again raised its full-year economic growth forecast, citing robust AI-driven export demand, while projecting consumer inflation slightly above the 2% alert level. The combination of strong growth and mild price pressure supports the bank's cautious stance, keeping policy steady rather than easing in step with global peers.
  **央行上修今年經濟成長率、通膨略高於2%警戒** — 央行在同次會議再度上修全年經濟成長率預測，主因AI帶動出口動能強勁；同時預估消費者物價年增率略高於2%的通膨警戒區。高成長搭配溫和通膨的組合，支撐央行維持穩健、不急於跟隨全球主要央行降息的立場，顯示政策重心仍放在兼顧成長與物價，為後續匯率與資金環境定調。
