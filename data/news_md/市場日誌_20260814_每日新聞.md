### Taiwan Equities / 股市行情

- **Taiwan stocks surge 503 points to record close above 46,000 on cooling US CPI** — The TAIEX jumped 503.13 points (+1.11%) to close at a record-high 46,021 on Aug 13, with turnover swelling to about NT$1.05 trillion. A softer US July CPI reading revived Fed rate-cut bets, lifting semiconductor and passive-component names; the index briefly touched 46,216 intraday before easing near the close.

  - 來源 / Source：[鉅亨網](https://news.cnyes.com/news/id/6575570)
- **TSMC leads gains as MSCI adds Taiwan memory names** — TSMC rose NT$20 to NT$2,435 (+0.83%), remaining the market's chief engine, while MSCI's August quarterly review lifted Taiwan's weighting across its main indexes and added memory-related constituents such as Nanya, Winbond and Phison. The reshuffle, effective at month-end, is expected to draw passive inflows into memory and HBM-linked stocks.
  **台積電領漲 MSCI調整納入記憶體股** — 台積電上漲20元至2,435元、漲幅0.83%，續居盤面最大推手；MSCI 8月季度調整同步調升台股於三大主要指數權重，並新增南亞科、華邦電、群聯等記憶體相關成分股。調整於月底生效，市場預期將引導被動式資金流入記憶體與HBM供應鏈，帶動相關個股成交活絡、題材延燒。

### 🏭 Industry / 產業動態

- **TSMC's AI revenue nears NT$1 trillion with order visibility into 2028** — TSMC's 2026 AI-related sales are projected to top US$40 billion (about NT$1.24 trillion), with management citing order visibility stretching to 2028. Advanced-node capacity is slated to expand roughly fourfold, from ~35,000 wafers/month at end-2024 toward 120,000-140,000 by end-2026, underscoring sustained AI-driven demand across the foundry supply chain.
  **台積電AI營收衝兆元 訂單能見度達2028年** — 台積電2026年AI相關營收上看400億美元、約新台幣1.24兆元，管理層指訂單能見度已延伸至2028年。先進製程產能規劃自2024年底每月約3.5萬片，擴增至2026年底每月12萬至14萬片、約四倍成長，反映AI需求強勁。擴產同時牽動設備、材料與封測供應鏈景氣，成為台股基本面的主要支撐。
  - 來源 / Source：[經濟日報](https://money.udn.com/money/story/5612/9143496)
- **Memory sector rides HBM boom as Taiwan output multiplies** — Taiwan's memory industry saw June revenue jump nearly fourfold year-on-year, driven by surging demand for high-bandwidth memory (HBM) packaging. The strength has spilled into equities: Nanya, Winbond and Phison rallied and were tapped for MSCI inclusion, positioning memory as one of the market's hottest AI-adjacent themes into the second half.
  **記憶體搭HBM熱潮 台廠營收翻倍跳增** — 台灣記憶體產業6月營收年增近四倍，主因高頻寬記憶體（HBM）封裝需求激增。強勁動能延伸至股市，南亞科、華邦電、群聯股價走揚並獲納入MSCI成分股，使記憶體成為市場最熱門的AI衍生題材之一。展望下半年，HBM與利基型記憶體報價續強，可望支撐相關廠商營收與獲利動能。
  - 來源 / Source：[Grenade 手榴彈](https://grenade.tw/blog/taiwan-stock-market-highlights-260813)

### 🏦 Financial Sector / 金融業

- **FSC clears Taishin–Shin Kong bank merger** — Taiwan's Financial Supervisory Commission has approved the merger of Taishin International Bank with Taiwan Shin Kong Commercial Bank, a key step in consolidating the Taishin–Shin Kong financial group. The tie-up expands the combined bank's branch network and asset base, and is seen advancing the sector's push toward scale amid intensifying competition in lending and wealth management.
  **金管會核准台新銀行合併新光銀行** — 金管會同意台新國際商業銀行合併臺灣新光商業銀行，為台新新光金控整併的關鍵一步。合併後銀行分行據點與資產規模擴大，有助發揮綜效並強化在放款與財富管理市場的競爭力。此案象徵國內金融整併持續推進，後續系統與人員整合進度將牽動存放款市占與客群經營成效。
  - 來源 / Source：[金融監督管理委員會](https://www.fsc.gov.tw/ch/home.jsp?id=2&parentpath=0)
- **FSC tightens internal-control and cross-selling rules** — The FSC has amended regulations governing internal control and audit systems for financial holding companies and banks, alongside revised rules on joint marketing and cross-selling of products among subsidiaries. The updates aim to strengthen governance and consumer protection as banks expand fee income and bancassurance, raising compliance requirements across financial groups.
  **金管會修訂內控稽核與共同行銷規範** — 金管會近期修正金融控股公司及銀行業內部控制及稽核制度實施辦法，並同步調整子公司間共同行銷及跨售商品相關規範。修法旨在強化公司治理與消費者保護，因應銀行擴大手續費收入與銀保業務。新規將提高金融集團的法遵成本與作業要求，促使業者檢視內部流程與交叉銷售控管機制。

### 💎 Wealth Management / 財富管理

- **Global banks vie for Taiwan's US$8 trillion wealth market** — International players including UBS, HSBC and BNP Paribas are ramping up hiring to capture Taiwan's fast-growing wealth-management market, estimated at around US$8 trillion. The push reflects rising demand from high-net-worth clients for offshore bonds, trusts and structured products, intensifying competition with domestic private banks for affluent Taiwanese assets.
  **瑞銀滙豐搶進台灣8兆美元財管市場** — 瑞銀、滙豐、法國巴黎銀行等國際大型銀行積極擴編招募，搶攻台灣快速成長、規模約8兆美元的財富管理市場。此舉反映高資產客戶對海外債、信託及結構型商品需求升溫，外銀與本土私人銀行競逐富裕客群資產。市場競爭加劇可望帶動產品線與服務深化，並推升相關人才與顧問資源的爭奪。
  - 來源 / Source：[經濟日報](https://money.udn.com/money/story/5613/9076340)
- **High-net-worth AUM at Taiwan banks tops NT$1 trillion** — Assets under management in Taiwan banks' high-net-worth wealth business have surpassed NT$1.0387 trillion, a fivefold jump from NT$207.7 billion at end-2020, according to FSC data. Growth has been aided by a Kaohsiung financial demonstration zone offering Lombard lending and portfolio-backed products, signalling deepening demand for private-banking services among wealthy clients.
  **銀行高資產財管AUM突破1兆元** — 依金管會統計，國內銀行高資產客戶財富管理業務管理資產規模已達新台幣1兆387億元，較2020年底的2,077億元成長逾5倍。高雄金融示範區放寬監管、提供以投資組合質借（倫巴底貸款）等商品，帶動業務快速擴張。此反映高淨值客群對私人銀行服務需求深化，也促使業者強化產品與跨境資產配置能力。
  - 來源 / Source：[金融監督管理委員會](https://www.fsc.gov.tw/ch/home.jsp?id=96&parentpath=0&mcustomize=news_view.jsp&dataserno=202405140001&dtable=News)
