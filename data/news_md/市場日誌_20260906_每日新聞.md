### 🏦 Financial Sector / 金融業

- **Dormant-account curbs take effect Sept 1, capping daily withdrawals and transfers** — From September 1, banks began imposing transaction limits on long-inactive, low-balance "dormant" accounts, sharply cutting daily ATM withdrawal and transfer ceilings. The measure is aimed at reducing the misuse of idle accounts in fraud and money-laundering, but caught some holders off guard who found funds harder to access. Customers must reactivate accounts at branches to restore full functionality, adding compliance friction across the retail-banking system.
  **久未往來帳戶新制9/1上路 提款轉帳額度大縮水** — 自9月1日起，銀行對長期無交易、低餘額的「久未往來帳戶」祭出交易限制，大幅調降每日提款與轉帳上限。此舉旨在降低閒置帳戶淪為詐騙與洗錢工具的風險，但部分民眾未留意、發現戶頭有錢卻領不出。客戶須臨櫃辦理恢復帳戶功能，全國多家銀行同步實施，短期增添零售金融的作業與法遵摩擦。

- **FSC unveils 8 new rules for 2026 as life insurers brace for IFRS 17/ICS shift** — The FSC announced eight new measures taking effect in early 2026, with insurance-bureau items centered on adopting IFRS 17 and the new-generation solvency regime (ICS). Fair-value liability measurement will structurally reshape insurers' balance sheets, pushing firms to strengthen asset-liability management and pivot from long-term guaranteed products toward protection-oriented offerings. Securities-bureau rules also advance shareholder-meeting disclosure timelines and an e-gift platform launching by end-March.
  **金管會公布明年8項新制 壽險業迎IFRS 17/ICS大轉型** — 金管會公布2026年將上路的8項新制，保險局2項聚焦壽險業接軌IFRS 17保險合約會計與新一代清償能力制度（ICS）。以公允價值衡量保險負債將對業者財報結構產生深遠影響，促使強化資產負債管理（ALM），並由長期保證型商品轉向保障型商品。證期局另有股東會議事手冊揭露時程提前、電子紀念品平台預計3月底上線等新制。
