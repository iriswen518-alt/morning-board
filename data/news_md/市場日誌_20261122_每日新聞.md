### 💰 Tax & Regulations / 稅務法規

- **Estate-tax exemption may rise with inflation in 2027** — Based on cumulative CPI gains, reports estimate Taiwan's estate-tax exemption could be lifted from the current NT$13.33 million toward roughly NT$14.92 million for 2027, though the exact figure awaits the Ministry of Finance's year-end announcement. The gift-tax exemption stands at NT$2.44 million, and the lineal-descendant deduction remains NT$560,000 per person for inheritances arising in 2026.
  **遺產稅免稅額2027年可能隨通膨調升** — 依累計CPI漲幅估算，報導推估台灣遺產稅免稅額可能自現行1,333萬元調升至約1,492萬元，但確切數字須待財政部年底正式公告。贈與稅免稅額現為244萬元；2026年發生的繼承案件，直系血親卑親屬扣除額維持每人56萬元，實際金額仍以財政部公告為準。
  - 來源 / Source：[經濟日報](https://udn.com/news/story/7243/9197837)

- **New estate-tax rule brings two-year gifts into the net** — Under amendments to the Estate and Gift Tax Act, assets gifted to specified relatives within two years before death are now treated as deemed estate and taxed in proportion to the total estate, with the tax burden shifting to the recipient. The Ministry of Finance is drafting sub-regulations and updating filing forms and online systems to implement the collection rules.
  **遺贈稅新制 身故前2年贈與納入課稅** — 依遺產及贈與稅法修正，被繼承人死亡前2年內贈與特定親屬的財產，將以擬制遺產依遺產總額比例計算稅額，且稅負改由受贈人承擔。財政部表示正研擬施行細則等子法規，並修訂相關申報書表與線上申辦系統，督導各地國稅局規劃後續稽徵作業。
  - 來源 / Source：[ETtoday財經](https://finance.ettoday.net/news/3105961)

### 📜 Macro & Policy / 總經政策

- **Central bank holds rates for a tenth straight meeting** — At its most recent quarterly board meeting the CBC kept the policy (rediscount) rate unchanged at 2%, marking ten consecutive holds. Governor Yang Chin-long cited a "K-shaped" economy with uneven sectoral strength and the mortgage burden on first-home buyers as reasons for caution, signalling steady policy ahead of the next scheduled review in December.
  **央行利率連十次按兵不動** — 央行在最近一季理監事會維持政策利率（重貼現率）2%不變，為連續第十次凍結。總裁楊金龍指出，考量產業表現分化的「K型經濟」與首購族房貸負擔，政策傾向審慎；市場預期下次12月理監事會前，貨幣政策將維持穩定基調，留意房市與資金狀況。
  - 來源 / Source：[中央社](https://www.cna.com.tw/news/afe/202609170308.aspx)

- **CBC lifts 2026 inflation and growth forecasts** — The central bank raised its 2026 CPI forecast to 2.03% and core CPI to 2.16%, citing sticky service prices and pricier consumer electronics, while trimming its 2027 CPI view to 1.83%. It also upgraded 2026 full-year GDP growth toward double digits, driven by AI-related exports and investment, underscoring an economy running hot even as price pressures persist.
  **央行上修2026通膨與成長預測** — 央行將2026年CPI年增率預測上修至2.03%、核心CPI至2.16%，理由為服務類價格僵固與消費性電子漲價；2027年CPI預測則降至1.83%。央行並上修2026全年經濟成長預測至雙位數，主要受惠AI相關出口與投資，顯示景氣仍熱、物價壓力並存，為後續政策增添觀察變數。
