### 💰 Tax & Regulations / 稅務法規

- **2026 estate and gift tax thresholds held, gift brackets widened** — For 2026, Taiwan's estate-tax exemption and deductions are unchanged, not adjusted for inflation, while the gift-tax annual exemption stays at NT$2.44mn. The taxable brackets for gift tax have been widened, so more of a gift falls into lower rate bands. Planners note timing of lifetime transfers versus inheritance remains a key variable for high-asset families managing succession.
  **2026 遺贈稅免稅額維持 贈與稅級距拉寬** — 財政部公告，2026 年遺產稅免稅額與各項扣除額維持不變，未隨物價調整；贈與稅每年免稅額仍為 244 萬元，但課稅級距金額拉寬，使更多贈與適用較低稅率級距。會計師提醒，生前移轉與身後繼承的時點安排，仍是高資產家庭規劃財富傳承的關鍵變數。
  - 來源 / Source：[財政部稅務入口網](https://www.etax.nat.gov.tw/etwmain/tax-info/understanding/tax-saving-manual/national/estate-and-gift-tax/ZQmGGWr)
- **Property tax clawback risk on repurchased owner-occupied homes** — Under the consolidated housing-and-land tax rules, individuals who claimed a tax credit or refund for repurchasing an owner-occupied home must keep that use. If within five years of the repurchase the home is switched to another use or resold, the National Taxation Bureau will claw back the original credited or refunded tax, a point owners should weigh before changing plans.
  **房地合一稅 重購自住 5 年內變更用途將被追繳** — 財政部提醒，個人房地合一稅經核准適用重購自住房地的稅額扣抵或退還後，須維持自住用途；若於重購後 5 年內將該房地改作其他用途或再行移轉，國稅局將追繳原扣抵或退還的稅額。民眾在變更房屋用途或出售前，應留意此一追繳規定，避免額外稅負。
  - 來源 / Source：[財政部中區國稅局](https://www.ntbca.gov.tw/multiplehtml/9d087e8054ea4ea1a7dae372c99ce934)

### 📜 Macro & Policy / 總經政策

- **TIER cuts 2026 GDP forecast on US tariff drag** — The Taiwan Institute of Economic Research has trimmed its 2026 GDP growth forecast to about 2.91%, warning of a "high-first, low-later" profile through the year. It cites the drag from US tariff measures on global trade volumes, with the US now Taiwan's largest export destination, alongside risks from China's industrial adjustment, the AI outlook and geopolitics.
  **台經院下修今年 GDP 至 2.91% 示警「前高後低」** — 台灣經濟研究院下修 2026 年台灣經濟成長率至約 2.91%，並示警全年呈「前高後低」走勢。主因美國關稅措施持續制約全球貿易量成長，而美國已成為台灣第一大出口對象，其貿易政策變化牽動台灣出口；此外，中國產業調整、AI 發展前景與地緣政治亦為主要風險。

- **Inflation eases but stays above 2% in mid-year data** — Taiwan's consumer price index rose 2.54% year-on-year in July, with the January–July average up 1.81%. The central bank forecasts full-year 2026 CPI and core CPI growth of around 1.66% and 1.64% respectively, expecting prices to drift lower as global inflation pressure eases, though energy costs remain a swing factor for the outlook.
