### Taiwan Equities / 股市行情

- **TAIEX tumbles 2.31% as AI jitters and Nvidia-earnings nerves weigh** — The benchmark fell 947.97 points to close at 40,092.11 on Nov 17, extending Monday's record-scale slide. TSMC, more than 40% of the index, slid 2.03% to NT$2,170 after an intraday low of NT$2,155. Traders cited AI-valuation worries, US-China chip curbs and caution before Nvidia's results. Two-day losses reached about 8.2% amid heavy swings between 39,786 and 41,533.
  **台股再跌2.31%、收40,092點 AI疑慮與輝達財報壓力續燒** — 加權指數11月17日終場下跌947.97點、以40,092.11點作收，延續周一歷史級重挫。權值逾四成的台積電下跌2.03%至2,170元，盤中低見2,155元。市場憂AI估值過高、美中晶片管制及輝達財報前觀望，兩日累計下挫約8.2%，盤中在39,786至41,533點間劇烈震盪，投資人風險意識明顯升高。
  - 來源 / Source：[Focus Taiwan](https://focustaiwan.tw/business/202611170018)
- **Monday's plunge was the second-largest point drop on record** — The TAIEX collapsed 6.03% on Nov 16 to 41,040.08, a 2,635-point fall trailing only July's historic drop. TSMC shed NT$135 (5.74%) to NT$2,215. The rout was driven by a global reassessment of AI spending and chip-sector valuations, spilling over from weakness on Wall Street and dragging the island's export-heavy, tech-concentrated market sharply lower.
  **周一崩跌創史上第二大點數跌幅** — 加權指數11月16日重挫6.03%至41,040.08點，單日下跌2,635點，僅次於7月的歷史紀錄。台積電下跌135元（5.74%）至2,215元。此波賣壓源於全球重新評價AI資本支出與晶片估值，並承接華爾街弱勢，使台灣這個以科技與出口為主的市場明顯受創。

### 🏭 Industry / 產業動態

- **TSMC sheds about US$142bn in value in two days ahead of Nvidia report** — Taiwan's chip bellwether lost roughly US$142 billion in market capitalization over two sessions as a global AI selloff hit semiconductors, closing at NT$2,170 on Nov 17. The slide dragged the chip-heavy supply chain lower, underscoring how tightly the island's tech sector is tethered to AI-spending sentiment and Nvidia's guidance.
  **輝達財報前 台積電兩日市值蒸發約1,420億美元** — 全球AI類股賣壓襲向半導體，台積電兩個交易日市值估蒸發約1,420億美元，11月17日收2,170元。龍頭重挫拖累整條晶片供應鏈走弱，凸顯台灣科技產業與AI資本支出情緒、輝達財測高度連動，短線波動恐加大，市場聚焦AI需求能見度是否鬆動。
  - 來源 / Source：[Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/why-is-taiwan-semiconductor-manufacturing-stock-tumbling-today-93CH-5028421)
- **TSMC reiterates 'insatiable' AI demand after 59.7% October sales jump** — Even amid the selloff, TSMC has described leading-node AI demand as "insatiable," with October revenue up about 59.7% year on year and January-October sales up 43.3%. Management expects AI demand to outrun supply into 2027 and beyond, keeping capex focused on advanced nodes and packaging — a structural order book that tempers near-term share weakness.
  **台積電重申AI需求「無法滿足」 10月營收年增59.7%** — 即使在賣壓中，台積電仍形容先進製程AI需求「無法被滿足」，10月營收年增約59.7%、前十月累增43.3%。公司預期AI相關需求將延續至2027年甚至更久、仍大於供給，資本支出續聚焦先進製程與封裝。基本面訂單動能強勁，一定程度緩和近期股價波動帶來的疑慮。
  - 來源 / Source：[Focus Taiwan](https://focustaiwan.tw/business/202611100014)

### 🏦 Financial Sector / 金融業

- **Regulators cut short-selling to steady volatile market** — Taiwan's authorities tightened rules to curb the AI-driven selloff, capping intraday borrowed-share sell orders and sharply raising the minimum short-sale margin, while encouraging share buybacks and investor conferences. Reports say short-selling activity was slashed around 90% under the package, which aims to arrest downside momentum and protect retail confidence during the rout.
  **主管機關收緊放空 穩定劇烈震盪市場** — 面對AI引爆的賣壓，台灣主管機關祭出市場穩定措施，限縮盤中借券賣出並大幅提高融券最低保證金成數，同時鼓勵庫藏股與法人說明會。據報放空交易在新制下縮減約九成，意在抑制下行動能、維護散戶信心，屬政策面維穩工具，後續視市況滾動調整。
  - 來源 / Source：[FourWeekMBA](https://fourweekmba.com/ai-after-tsmc-rout-taiwan-cuts-short-sales-90-taps-15b-fund/)
- **National Stabilization Fund steps in to defend 40,000** — Dealers suspected the NT$500 billion (US$15.3bn) National Financial Stabilization Fund bought shares to steady the market near the 40,000 threshold after days of heavy losses and continued foreign selling. The fund signaled it would keep supporting equities, reinforcing the state's backstop role as volatility spikes across the tech-heavy bourse.
  **國安基金傳進場 力守40,000點** — 台股連日重挫、外資持續調節，交易員研判規模達5,000億元（約153億美元）的國家金融安定基金進場買盤，力守40,000點關卡。國安基金釋出續挺股市訊號，強化政府在市場劇烈波動時的護盤後盾角色，盼穩定以科技股為主的大盤信心。
