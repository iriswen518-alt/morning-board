### Taiwan Equities / 股市行情

- **TAIEX plunges 1,374 points to 41,574.72, breaking below 42,000 in a sixth straight loss (2026-11-05)** — Taiwan's benchmark index dived 1,374.19 points, or 3.20%, to close at 41,574.72 on Nov. 5, its sixth consecutive decline and a break below the 42,000 mark. The intraday range ran from a high of 42,873.81 down to 41,513.57, with turnover around NT$960 billion. It ranked as the 13th-largest single-day point drop in the index's history, as heavyweight tech names led the retreat amid relentless foreign selling.
  **台股重挫1,374點收41,574.72點，連6跌失守42,000點** — 加權指數11月5日重挫1,374.19點、跌幅3.20%，收41,574.72點，為連續第6個交易日下跌並失守42,000點大關，單日跌點居歷史第13大。盤中最高42,873.81點、最低41,513.57點，成交值約9,600億元。權值科技股領跌，外資持續大舉調節，市場信心明顯承壓。
  - 來源 / Source：[鏡新聞](https://www.mnews.tw/story/20261105nm001)
- **Stock exchange issues reassurance as index falls for a sixth session (2026-11-05)** — The Taiwan Stock Exchange issued a confidence statement as the TAIEX kept sliding, stressing the market's resilience. It noted the index was still up 48.29% year-to-date through Nov. 4, outperforming most major Asian markets and the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index, and said economic and corporate fundamentals continue to underpin investor confidence, urging investors not to overreact to short-term volatility.
  **台股連6跌，證交所信心喊話** — 面對加權指數連六跌不休，證交所信心喊話，強調台股展現穩健韌性。證交所指出，截至11月4日加權指數今年以來累計上漲48.29%，表現優於多數亞股及費城半導體指數，並表示經濟與企業基本面持續強化市場信心，呼籲投資人勿因短線波動過度恐慌。

### 🏭 Industry / 產業動態

- **AI and tech heavyweights lead the sell-off dragging the TAIEX down nearly 1,400 points (2026-11-05)** — AI-related and heavyweight tech stocks spearheaded the plunge on Nov. 5. MediaTek dropped 6.86% and Delta Electronics fell 7.38%, tracking weakness in US chip names and the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index. Analysts said the AI supply chain, after months of outsized gains, had become crowded and momentum-driven, so once foreign investors turned sellers the pullback turned violent, underscoring correction pressure after a strong run.
  **AI與權值科技股領跌，拖累台股重挫近1,400點** — AI相關與權值科技股領跌，拖累台股11月5日重挫近1,400點。聯發科下跌6.86%、台達電跌7.38%，與美股晶片股及費城半導體指數同步走弱。市場人士指出，AI供應鏈歷經數月強漲後籌碼過度集中、動能交易濃厚，一旦外資轉賣便出現劇烈回檔，凸顯漲多拉回的修正壓力。
  - 來源 / Source：[經濟日報](https://money.udn.com/money/story/5607/9657585)
- **Delta Electronics posts record Q3 revenue but profit dips on inventory write-downs (2026-11-05)** — Delta Electronics reported third-quarter revenue of NT$197.06 billion, up 7.53% quarter-on-quarter and 31.09% from a year earlier, with gross margin at 34.08%. Net profit slipped 6.53% from the prior quarter to NT$23.50 billion, hit by inventory write-downs at its Thai subsidiary. Chairman Ping Cheng told the earnings call that fourth-quarter revenue will exceed Q3 and margins will not deteriorate, citing resilient data-center and AI power demand.
  **台達電第三季營收創高，獲利因存貨跌價損失季減** — 台達電第三季營收1,970.59億元，季增7.53%、年增31.09%，毛利率34.08%。稅後純益234.95億元，季減6.53%，主因子公司泰達電提列存貨跌價損失侵蝕獲利。董事長鄭平於法說會表示，第四季營收一定高於第三季，毛利率也不會比第三季差，看好資料中心與AI電源需求延續，支撐後續成長動能。
  - 來源 / Source：[Anue鉅亨](https://uanalyze.com.tw/articles/4466957915)

### 🏦 Financial Sector / 金融業

- **Taiwan dollar slides to 32.876, the weakest Asian currency, on foreign outflows (2026-11-05)** — The Taiwan dollar closed at 32.876 per US dollar on Nov. 5, down 5.5 cents, after sliding more than 0.16 intraday to a low of 32.985, within a whisker of the 33 level, on FX turnover of US$3.66 billion. A single-day net foreign sell of NT$149.9 billion in equities — the fifth-largest on record — spilled into the currency market. The NT dollar has lost 1.21% so far in November, the weakest in Asia, with exporters and the central bank trimming the slide late in the session.
  **新台幣貶至32.876元，淪為最弱亞幣** — 新台幣兌美元11月5日收32.876元、貶5.5分，盤中一度挫逾1.6角、最低觸及32.985元，距33元整數關卡僅半步之遙，成交金額36.56億美元。外資單日賣超台股達1,498.99億元、為史上第5大，資金撤離壓力蔓延至匯市。11月以來新台幣累計重貶1.21%、為最弱亞幣，出口商逢低進場與央行調節使尾盤貶幅收斂。
  - 來源 / Source：[中央社](https://www.cna.com.tw/news/afe/202611050195.aspx)
- **Foreigners pull over NT$510 billion in five sessions, driving a stock-and-currency 'double kill' (2026-11-05)** — Foreign investors have withdrawn more than NT$510 billion over five trading sessions, pressuring both equities and the currency in a renewed "double kill." Reports said the central bank, led by governor Yang Chin-long, is smoothing the decline rather than defending a fixed level as capital flight accelerates, "trading time for space." Adjustments to insurers' FX hedging positions under the new valuation regime are adding to currency-market volatility.
  **外資5日提款逾5,100億元，股匯雙殺再起** — 外資近期大舉提款，5個交易日累計匯出逾5,100億元，引發台股與匯市「股匯雙殺」。報導指出，面對資金外流加速，央行總裁楊金龍採全場調節、不死守特定價位的策略因應，以時間換取空間；壽險業在外匯評價新制下調整避險部位，也加劇匯市波動。市場關注外資賣壓何時止穩，為後市資金動向關鍵。

### 💎 Wealth Management / 財富管理

- **KPMG–E.Sun report sees Taiwan's high-asset population reaching 124,000 with NT$46.1 trillion** — A KPMG–E.Sun wealth report projects the number of Taiwanese holding more than NT$100 million in assets will reach about 124,000 this year, controlling roughly NT$46.1 trillion in wealth. It flags cross-border value creation and proactive succession as leading trends, with more than 70% of these clients already allocating offshore — chiefly to Singapore, Hong Kong and the US — and rising interest in integrated succession tools such as family offices.
  **KPMG與玉山報告：高資產人數上看12.4萬、財富46.1兆元** — KPMG安侯建業與玉山銀行發布的《2026臺灣高資產顧客財富管理與傳承調研報告》預估，今年台灣資產逾1億元的高資產客群將增至約12.4萬人，合計持有約46.1兆元財富。報告歸納「跨境增值」與「主動傳承」為主要趨勢，逾七成高資產客戶已布局境外資產，主要往來新加坡、香港與美國，並日益重視家族辦公室等整合性傳承工具。
  - 來源 / Source：[KPMG](https://kpmg.com/tw/zh/media/press-releases/2026/06/2026-taiwan-wealth-management-trends-high-net-worth-growth.html)
- **CTBC–BCG study: ultra-high-net-worth wealth to grow 10% a year through 2029** — A CTBC–BCG report on Taiwan's ultra-high-net-worth segment forecasts personal wealth will grow about 10% annually from 2025 to 2029, lifting the high-asset population from roughly 123,000 in 2025 to 155,000 by 2029, and the NT$1 billion-plus tier from 8,000 to 11,000 people. More than 70% allocate cross-border, over 40% are adding US, Singapore and Japan exposure, and 74% already hold offshore assets, favoring emerging-market and monthly-dividend bond products alongside core equity funds.
  **中信與BCG報告：超高資產財富2029年前年均成長10%** — 中信銀行與BCG發布《2026台灣超高資產客群財富洞察報告》，推估台灣高資產客群個人財富2025至2029年將以年均約10%成長，人數由2025年約12.3萬人增至2029年15.5萬人，10億元級超高資產客群由8,000人增至1.1萬人。逾7成布局跨境資產、逾4成增持美星日，74%已持有境外資產，債券配置偏好新興市場債與月配息級別，核心配置仍以股票型基金為主。
