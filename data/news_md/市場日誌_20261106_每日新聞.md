### 💰 Tax & Regulations / 稅務法規

- **Estate-and-gift tax reform folds pre-death gifts into the taxable estate** — Following a Constitutional Court ruling, Taiwan has amended the Estate and Gift Tax Act so that assets gifted to relatives within two years before death are added to the taxable estate, with the tax apportioned by each recipient's share of the inherited assets and collected from the recipient rather than split among all heirs. The Ministry of Finance says it is drafting enforcement rules and sub-regulations and will step up guidance after the amended provisions take effect.
  **遺贈稅修法：死亡前2年贈與併入遺產課稅** — 回應憲法法庭判決，台灣修正《遺產及贈與稅法》，將被繼承人死亡前兩年內贈與親屬的財產併入遺產總額課稅，國稅局按各受贈人受贈財產占遺產比例計算應增稅額，並以受贈人為納稅義務人發單課徵，而非由全體繼承人分攤。財政部表示正研擬施行細則等子法規，俟修正條文公布後加強宣導，收緊常見的傳承節稅安排。

- **2026 income-tax thresholds raise long-term-care and basic-living deductions** — Under adjustments applying to 2026 income (filed in 2027), Taiwan's long-term-care special deduction rises to NT$180,000 per person from NT$120,000, and the tax-exempt basic living allowance increases to NT$213,000 from NT$210,000.
