### 🏭 Industry / 產業動態

- **TSMC keeps ramping CoWoS as AI packaging demand runs into 2028** — Institutional estimates have TSMC's CoWoS advanced-packaging capacity climbing toward roughly 125K wafers per month by end-2026 and about 170K by end-2027, with high-end AI accelerators almost wholly dependent on its lines. CoWoS-L/S capacity is described as sold out, N2 orders are booked into late-2027 and 2028, and HBM suppliers are locked out through 2027, underscoring how tight the AI compute supply chain remains.
  **台積電CoWoS持續擴產 AI封裝需求排單至2028** — 法人估台積電CoWoS先進封裝產能2026年底上看約12.5萬片／月、2027年底約17萬片，高階AI加速器幾乎全數仰賴其產線。CoWoS-L／S產能被指全數售罄，N2訂單已排至2027下半年至2028年，HBM三強2026年產能亦鎖定至2027年，凸顯AI算力供應鏈持續緊俏，支撐台廠中長期成長動能。
  - 來源 / Source：[TechNews 科技新報](https://technews.tw/2026/01/05/advanced-semiconductor-packaging-will-become-an-investment-focus-in-2026/)

- **AI server and advanced-packaging plays anchor 2026 supply-chain focus** — Analysts continue to flag AI-server assembly, advanced packaging, HBM and silicon-wafer suppliers as the core beneficiaries of 2026's capital-spending cycle, with SoIC adoption widening beyond AMD toward Nvidia and Broadcom from 2027. The read-through is that the AI build-out is pulling a broader slice of Taiwan's hardware chain — from boards and cooling to power management — into the upcycle rather than concentrating gains in the foundry leader alone.
  **AI伺服器與先進封裝族群 續居2026供應鏈焦點** — 法人持續點名AI伺服器組裝、先進封裝、HBM與矽晶圓為2026資本支出循環核心受惠者，SoIC應用自AMD向NVIDIA、Broadcom延伸、預計2027年後放量。此意味AI建置潮正把台灣硬體供應鏈更廣泛族群——從板類、散熱到電源管理——一併拉進上升軌道，而非僅集中於晶圓代工龍頭，帶動題材輪動。

### 🏦 Financial Sector / 金融業

- **FSC clears Taishin's merger with Shin Kong Bank, advancing sector consolidation** — Taiwan's Financial Supervisory Commission has approved Taishin International Bank's merger with Shin Kong Commercial Bank, a further step in the tie-up under Taishin Shin Kong Financial Holding and a marker of continued consolidation among mid-sized banks. The approval clears a key regulatory hurdle for integrating branch networks and balance sheets, and signals the FSC's willingness to let scale-building deals proceed within the domestic banking sector.
  **金管會核准台新銀行合併新光銀行 金融整併再進一步** — 金管會已同意台新國際商業銀行合併臺灣新光商業銀行，為台新新光金控旗下整併再邁一步，也是中型銀行持續整併的指標。此核准掃除整合分行通路與資產負債表的重要法規障礙，顯示金管會對具規模效益的整併案持開放態度，可望帶動國內銀行業版圖重整與競爭態勢調整。
  - 來源 / Source：[金融監督管理委員會](https://www.fsc.gov.tw/ch/home.jsp?id=2&parentpath=0)

- **FSC eases holding-company cross-marketing and capital-reduction rules** — The regulator has amended rules governing cross-marketing between financial-holding subsidiaries and the capital-reduction procedures for such subsidiaries, part of a push to give conglomerates more operating flexibility. Alongside a recent sanction on Cooperative Bank over credit-card lapses, the moves show the FSC balancing looser business rules with continued conduct enforcement across the banking system.
  **金管會鬆綁金控共同行銷與子公司減資規範** — 金管會修正金融控股公司子公司間共同行銷管理辦法及子公司減資辦法相關條文，旨在給予金控更大營運彈性。搭配近期對合作金庫信用卡缺失的行政處分，顯示金管會一方面鬆綁業務規範、一方面維持行為面監理，於銀行體系間取得平衡，兼顧金控綜效與消費者權益保障。
