### 💰 Tax & Regulations / 稅務法規

- **Cabinet-passed estate-tax amendment shifts "deemed estate" liability to recipients** — Taiwan's Executive Yuan approved amendments to the Estate and Gift Tax Act to align with the Constitutional Court's 2024 ruling, changing how gifts to spouses and specified relatives within two years before death are taxed. Estate tax on deemed estates shifts to the recipient, waiving renounced-inheritance exemptions, pending further legislative review.
  **行政院通過遺贈稅法修正草案，擬制遺產改由受贈人負擔** — 行政院通過《遺產及贈與稅法》部分條文修正草案，配合憲法法庭113年憲判字第11號判決，調整死亡前2年內贈與配偶及特定親屬財產的課稅方式：擬制遺產稅改由受贈人負擔、拋棄繼承不再免稅，草案後續仍待立法院審議。
  - 來源 / Source：[中央社 CNA](https://www.cna.com.tw/business/chinese/440345)
- **MOF updates low-tax-jurisdiction list for CFC rules** — The Ministry of Finance refreshed its reference list of low-tax countries and territories for controlled-foreign-company (CFC) reporting, now covering 31 jurisdictions with statutory rates at or below 14% plus 48 territorial-tax countries. The update shapes how Taiwanese individuals and firms with offshore entities calculate CFC income, tightening cross-border tax compliance.
  **財政部更新CFC低稅負國家名單** — 財政部更新受控外國企業（CFC）制度適用之低稅負國家或地區參考名單，涵蓋31個法定稅率未逾14%的地區，及48個僅就境內所得課稅的國家。此名單牽動個人與企業海外實體的CFC所得計算，強化跨境稅務遵循。
