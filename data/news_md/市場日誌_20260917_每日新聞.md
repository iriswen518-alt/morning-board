### Taiwan Equities / 股市行情

- **TAIEX climbs 337 points to 45,848 as TSMC fills its dividend gap** — Taiwan's benchmark index closed up 337.41 points (0.74%) at 45,848.9 on Sept 16, with turnover of about NT$643.8bn. ### 🏭 Industry / 產業動態

- **TSMC pushes 2nm and 3DFabric packaging as AI orders stay hot** — TSMC's most advanced 2nm capacity is fully booked, with its Fab 20 in Taiwan reaching a 20,000-wafer-per-month milestone. The foundry is scaling its 3DFabric platform — CoWoS, SoIC, SoW and silicon-photonics COUPE — as AI competition shifts from single-chip performance to system-level integration in ASIC, HBM and advanced packaging. Analysts expect earnings to keep hitting record highs each quarter.
  **台積電衝2奈米、先進封裝 AI訂單續熱** — 台積電最先進2奈米產能滿載，台灣晶圓20廠2奈米月產能達2萬片里程碑；同步擴大3DFabric整合平台（CoWoS、SoIC、SoW與矽光子COUPE）。SEMICON Taiwan 2026釋出訊號顯示，AI半導體競爭已從單一晶片效能走向ASIC、HBM、先進封裝的系統級整合。法人看好台積電攜AI客戶群共同成長、業績季季創高。

- **Morgan Stanley: 2026 CoWoS demand to jump 70% to 1.15m wafers** — Morgan Stanley projects total global CoWoS demand will reach 1.15m wafers in 2026, up 70% year-on-year, with Nvidia alone consuming an estimated 683,000 wafers, or 59% of the total. The forecast underscores how AI compute needs — growing four-to-five times annually for frontier model training — are keeping advanced-packaging, memory and thermal-solution supply chains tight and lifting related Taiwan suppliers.
  **大摩估2026 CoWoS需求增70%達115萬片** — 摩根士丹利預估，2026年全球CoWoS總需求將達115萬片、年增70%，其中輝達預計消耗68.3萬片、占總需求59%。前沿AI模型訓練算力每年以4~5倍速度成長，帶動先進封裝、記憶體與散熱供應鏈持續吃緊，大摩並點名台積電、散熱與記憶體等多檔受惠標的，看好相關台廠營運動能。

### 🏦 Financial Sector / 金融業

- **FSC grants transition relief as insurers adopt new TIS solvency regime** — Taiwan's Financial Supervisory Commission is rolling out its new-generation insurance solvency system (TIS) in tandem with IFRS 17, and has offered three interim supervisory measures to smooth the shift. Eight life-insurance-focused financial holding groups will see capital pressure eased, and 11 insurance-type groups may defer their capital-adequacy-ratio filing by one month, giving the sector more breathing room during the changeover.
  **金管會壽險接軌TIS 提供過渡措施紓壓** — 金管會推動保險業接軌新一代清償能力制度（TIS）並同步接軌IFRS 17，提出三大暫行監理措施以平順新舊制交替。其中8家壽險型金控資本壓力可望減壓，11家保險型金控的資本適足率（CAR）申報得延後一個月。此舉降低接軌初期的資本衝擊，讓壽險業有更充裕時間調整資產配置與強化清償能力。
  - 來源 / Source：[鉅亨網](https://news.cnyes.com/news/id/6472396)

- **FSC unveils eight new rules for 2027, ushering in a life-insurance overhaul** — The FSC has announced eight new measures set to take effect next year, signalling a major transformation for the life-insurance industry as it moves onto the new solvency and accounting frameworks. The package spans product design, capital and disclosure requirements, pressing insurers to reshape their business mix, strengthen balance sheets and adapt to a stricter, more transparent regulatory environment.
  **金管會公布明年8項新制 壽險業迎大轉型** — 金管會公布明年將上路的8項新制，涵蓋商品設計、資本與資訊揭露等面向，象徵壽險業在接軌新清償能力與會計制度後迎來結構性轉型。新制要求業者調整商品組合、強化財務體質，並因應更嚴格、更透明的監理環境。業界須加速資產負債管理與資本規劃，以順利銜接新制、維持長期經營穩健。

### 💎 Wealth Management / 財富管理

- **Taiwan wealth-management assets swell to NT$36tn as ETFs lead the surge** — Taiwan's wealth-management pool has grown from under NT$20tn to about NT$36tn in six years, driven by distinct engines across funds, discretionary mandates, bank trusts, high-net-worth services and insurance. Domestic funds jumped from NT$4.86tn to NT$11.38tn in three years, with ETFs alone at NT$7.4tn — up 220% — as regular-savings ETF investing broadens beyond high-dividend products.
  **台灣財管規模衝36兆 ETF領軍成長** — 台灣財富管理規模六年內從不到20兆成長至約36兆元，基金、全權委託、銀行信託、高資產與保險各有成長邏輯。境內基金三年由4.86兆增至11.38兆，其中ETF一項就占7.4兆、三年成長220%。2026年台股ETF定期定額熱度續升，熱門標的不再僅限高股息，投資人配置更趨多元，帶動財管業務結構持續擴張。
  - 來源 / Source：[財經M平方觀點](https://xmy.tw/articles/taiwan-wealth-management-2026)

- **High-net-worth push widens as more insurers win wealth-management licences** — Policy support for building Taiwan into an Asian asset-management hub, launched in 2024, has accelerated growth in managed assets, hitting its two-year target ahead of schedule by end-2025. As of May 2026, ten insurers had secured FSC approval to run wealth-management businesses, while private banks such as DBS keep premium thresholds high — its top private-client tier requires average monthly assets of NT$100m.
  **高資產布局擴大 保險業搶進財管** — 政府2024年啟動亞洲資產管理中心政策，資產管理規模快速擴大，並於2025年底提前達成兩年政策目標。截至2026年5月，已有10家保險公司獲金管會核准辦理財富管理業務，投入高資產客群經營。私人銀行門檻仍高，如星展頂級私人客戶須月均總資產達1億元。信託、傳承與跨境資產配置需求升溫，成為業者競逐重點。
