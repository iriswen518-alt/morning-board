### Taiwan Equities / 股市行情

- **TAIEX rebounds 407 points on Tuesday, reclaiming 45,000 and its quarterly line** — Taiwan's benchmark index bounced back on Tuesday (Aug 25), climbing about 407 points, or roughly 0.9%, with an intraday high near 45,169 as it recaptured both the 45,000 threshold and its quarterly moving average after Monday's slide. Turnover ran near NT$697 billion. The recovery was led by financials and select tech names, with sentiment stabilising as investors positioned ahead of a heavy run of catalysts, most notably Nvidia's quarterly results due after the US close on Wednesday.
  **台股週二大漲 407 點、重返 45,000 與季線** — 加權指數週二（8/25）強勢反彈，上漲約 407 點、漲幅約 0.9%，盤中高點近 45,169 點，收復 45,000 關卡與季線，一舉扭轉週一失守季線的頹勢，成交量約 6,971 億元。反彈由金融與部分電子權值領軍，市場情緒回穩，投資人在輝達（Nvidia）週三美股盤後財報等重大事件前提前布局，帶動指數量增價漲、季線再度轉為支撐。
  - 來源 / Source：[經濟日報](https://money.udn.com/money/story/5607/9712954)
- **Institutions stay cautious net sellers as foreign funds rotate into financials and panels** — Despite the index rebound, the three major institutional investors were net sellers of about NT$5.3 billion. Foreign investors took profits on ETFs and index heavyweights ahead of Nvidia's earnings while rotating into financial holdings and flat-panel names, adding to Kaishin Financial, Fubon Financial, AUO and First Financial, and trimming Powerchip and several Taiwan-50 ETFs. The rotation underscored a defensive, event-driven stance even as the broad market recovered its footing.
  **法人保守賣超、外資轉進金控與面板** — 儘管大盤反彈，三大法人合計仍賣超約 53.36 億元。外資在輝達財報前夕結帳 ETF 與權值股，同時調節力積電與多檔台灣 50 系列 ETF，資金回補金融與面板族群，買超凱基金、富邦金、友達與第一金。此一輪動顯示法人籌碼相對保守、偏防禦與事件導向，即使指數收復季線，資金仍在權值與傳產金融間審慎換手，靜待重大財報與政策事件提供明確方向。

### 🏭 Industry / 產業動態

- **TSMC lifts capex toward US$60-64 billion as AI demand powers the whole chain** — TSMC has raised its 2026 capital-expenditure plan from roughly US$52-56 billion to about US$60-64 billion and nudged its full-year US-dollar revenue growth guidance above 40%, reflecting still-surging demand from AI accelerator customers. The aggressive build-out keeps advanced process and packaging the structural leadership theme, cascading capex through equipment, substrate, cooling and server-assembly suppliers across Taiwan's AI hardware ecosystem.
  **台積電資本支出上修至約 600–640 億美元、AI 需求貫穿全鏈** — 台積電將 2026 年資本支出由原估約 520–560 億美元上調至約 600–640 億美元，並同步將全年美元營收成長率上修至 40% 以上，反映 AI 加速器客戶需求續旺。積極擴產使先進製程與封裝續為結構性主流題材，資本支出向設備、ABF 載板、散熱與伺服器組裝等次族群外溢，帶動台廠 AI 硬體生態系中上游同步受惠，成為支撐台股電子權值中長期動能的核心引擎。
  - 來源 / Source：[中時新聞網](https://www.chinatimes.com/realtimenews/20260728000003-260410)
- **Memory and HBM lead the surge as Taiwan climbs to Korea's No.2 chip export market** — AI-driven demand has reshuffled the regional semiconductor map: Taiwan has risen to become South Korea's second-largest chip export destination, underscoring the island's role in linking AI-chip manufacturing with system integration. Memory has been the fastest-growing sub-sector, with monthly revenue in some segments up sharply year on year on booming high-bandwidth-memory (HBM) packaging demand, keeping upstream memory and advanced-packaging suppliers in focus.
  **記憶體與 HBM 領軍、台灣躍升韓國第二大半導體出口市場** — AI 需求牽動亞洲半導體版圖重整，台灣已躍升為南韓第二大晶片出口市場，凸顯台灣串接 AI 晶片製造與系統整合的關鍵地位。記憶體為成長最快次產業，部分區塊月營收年增顯著，主要受惠高頻寬記憶體（HBM）封裝需求激增，帶動上游記憶體與先進封裝供應商受市場高度關注。此結構性趨勢使台廠在全球 AI 供應鏈中的話語權提升，也支撐相關族群評價與接單能見度延續。
  - 來源 / Source：[ETtoday 財經雲](https://finance.ettoday.net/news/3208399)

### 🏦 Financial Sector / 金融業

- **FSC refines insurance financial-report and non-performing-asset rules** — The Financial Supervisory Commission continued a run of rule-making in August, amending the preparation standards for insurers' financial reports to optimise presentation and disclosure for greater transparency and comparability, and revising the regulations governing insurers' sale of non-performing assets. The moves form part of the sector's groundwork as it converges toward IFRS 17 and the new solvency regime, nudging insurers toward cleaner reporting and more disciplined balance-sheet management.
  **金管會修正保險業財報與不良資產處分規定** — 金管會八月持續修法，修正「保險業財務報告編製準則」，優化財報表達與揭露，提升透明度與可比較性，並調整保險業出售不良資產的相關規範。相關調整為保險業接軌 IFRS 17 與新一代清償能力制度鋪路，促使業者提升財報品質、強化資產負債管理紀律。此舉牽動壽險與產險業體質與監理節奏，也讓投資人更易比較各保險公司的財務狀況與經營成果，為後續多項新制上路預作準備。
  - 來源 / Source：[金管會](https://www.fsc.gov.tw/ch/home.jsp?id=96&parentpath=0,2)
- **FSC flags eight major new systems steering life insurers into a full transformation** — Regulators have signalled eight major new systems taking effect over the coming year that together push life insurers toward a sweeping transformation, from earlier disclosure timelines to capital and accounting changes tied to the IFRS 17 and TW-ICS convergence. Several holding companies gain transitional relief on capital-adequacy reporting, giving the sector breathing room to adapt asset-liability structures and product mixes to the new long-term framework.
  **金管會預告八項新制、壽險業迎全面轉型** — 金管會預告未來一年將有八項新制陸續上路，合力推動壽險業全面轉型，涵蓋更早的資訊揭露時程，以及與 IFRS 17、新清償制度（TW-ICS）接軌相關的資本與會計調整。部分金控在資本適足申報上獲得過渡性緩衝，讓業者有餘裕調整資產負債結構與商品組合，以因應新的長期制度框架。此波制度變革牽動壽險型金控的資本規劃、投資策略與商品設計，為金融業中長期經營格局帶來結構性影響。
  - 來源 / Source：[聯合新聞網](https://udn.com/news/story/7239/9235703)

### 💎 Wealth Management / 財富管理

- **August ETF payout wave rolls on as 00878 tops NT$1 per unit for the first time** — More than 60 Taiwan-listed ETFs declared distributions in August, drawing income-seeking investors as the market swung on earnings and macro headlines. Cathay Sustainability High Dividend (00878) lifted its latest per-unit payout to NT$1.01, up about 53% from the prior NT$0.66 and a record high since listing. The rush reflects a broader rotation by retail and wealth clients into dividend ETFs as a defensive, cash-flow-oriented allocation during choppy conditions.
  **八月 ETF 配息潮延續、00878 首破每單位 1 元** — 八月逾 60 檔台股 ETF 公告配息，在財報與總經消息牽動、盤勢震盪之際，吸引偏好息收的投資人進場。國泰永續高股息（00878）最新一期每受益權單位配息升至 1.01 元，較前一季 0.66 元增加約 53%，首度突破 1 元並創掛牌新高。此波配息潮反映散戶與財管客戶在震盪環境下，將資金轉進高股息 ETF 作為偏防禦、重現金流的資產配置選擇，也帶動相關 ETF 規模與受益人數延續成長動能。
  - 來源 / Source：[數位時代](https://www.bnext.com.tw/article/91708/2026-august-etf-dividend-list)
- **High-yield "four kings" push annualised yields past 17% as investors weigh total return** — In this month's payout rankings, high-dividend products led the pack, with 00891, 00961, 00881 and 00894 among names showing annualised yields breaking 17%, and 00881 topping single-payout yields near 8.6%. Advisers cautioned that yield alone is not enough: wealth clients should compare a fund's ability to fill the dividend gap, total return including dividends, payout sources, fees and holdings to avoid high income without real asset growth.
  **高息四天王年化衝破 17%、投資人須看總報酬** — 本月台股 ETF 配息排行中，高股息產品領先，00891、00961、00881、00894 等多檔年化配息率突破 17%，00881 單次配息率近 8.6% 居冠。理財專家提醒，殖利率並非唯一指標，財管客戶應一併比較填息能力、含息總報酬、配息來源、費用與成分股配置，避免出現「領到高配息、資產卻沒增加」的情況。此波高息熱潮牽動存股族與高資產客戶的資產配置與商品選擇，慎選標的與布局節奏更形關鍵。
