### 🏭 Industry / 產業動態

- **TSMC calls AI demand the strongest in 30 years, races to build ~20 fabs** — TSMC executives say AI-driven demand is unlike anything seen in three decades, nearly doubling within six months. Its projected 2026 equipment purchases have climbed to 1.9x the December estimate, and the company is building 13 fabs in Taiwan plus five to six abroad—about 20 worldwide—yet still cannot fully meet orders, pointing to a sustained multi-year advanced-node and packaging cycle.

### 🏦 Financial Sector / 金融業

- **FSC eases TIS transition, cutting capital strain for eight insurance-type holdings** — Ahead of Taiwan's shift to the new-generation solvency regime (TIS) in 2026, the FSC has rolled out transition measures that materially relieve capital pressure for eight insurance-type financial holding companies and let 11 groups defer their capital-adequacy (CAR) filings by up to a month. The relief smooths the industry's first solvency review while tougher standards phase in.
  **金管會鬆綁TIS過渡　8家壽險型金控資本大減壓** — 因應2026年接軌新一代清償能力制度（TIS），金管會推出過渡措施，明顯減輕8家壽險型金控的資本壓力，並允許11家金控的資本適足（CAR）申報最多延後一個月。此舉在更嚴格清償標準逐步上路之際，協助業界順利通過首次清償能力考核，緩解短期增資與資本缺口壓力，攸關金控與壽險股評價及股利政策，市場密切關注過渡期效應。
  - 來源 / Source：[鉅亨網](https://news.cnyes.com/news/id/6472396)
- **FSC readies eight new rules for 2026 as insurers pivot to protection products** — The FSC has flagged eight major regulations taking effect in 2026, centred on the insurance industry's adaptation to IFRS 17 and the new solvency system (ICS). The changes push product design toward protection-type and "de-guaranteed" offerings, reshaping life insurers' business mix and capital planning as Taiwan aligns with international reporting and solvency standards.
  **金管會明年8項新制上路　壽險轉型保障型商品** — 金管會預告2026年將有8項重大新制上路，核心為壽險業接軌IFRS 17與新一代清償能力制度（ICS）。新制引導商品設計轉向保障型與「去保證」方向，重塑壽險業務結構與資本規劃。台灣同步接軌國際財報與清償標準，短期牽動保單策略與資本配置，長期有助體質強化。投資人關注各金控接軌進度與資本缺口，對壽險股評價具指標意義。
