### 🏭 Industry / 產業動態

- **TSMC posts record October revenue on AI demand** — TSMC reported October consolidated revenue of NT$586.89 billion, up 14.66% from September and 59.71% from a year earlier, a fresh monthly record. Ten-month revenue reached about NT$4.48 trillion, up 43.3% year-on-year. The surge reinforces expectations that AI accelerator demand will keep the foundry on track for its fourth-quarter guidance of US$52.3–54.3 billion.
  **台積電10月營收創新高 AI需求續旺** — 台積電公布10月合併營收5,868.9億元，月增14.66%、年增59.71%，改寫單月歷史新高；前10月累計營收約4.48兆元、年增43.3%。強勁數據顯示AI加速器需求延續，市場對其第四季財測（523~543億美元）達標的信心升高。

### 🏦 Financial Sector / 金融業

- **Financial sector profits hit record on market boom** — Driven by the AI rally and buoyant equities, Taiwan's banking, insurance and securities sectors earned a combined NT$1.17 trillion in pre-tax profit in the first eight months, up 93.7% year-on-year and already surpassing full-year 2024, setting an all-time high for the period. Market strength has been a major driver of the windfall.
  **金融三業前8月賺1.17兆 創同期新高** — AI狂潮加上股市熱絡，銀行、保險與證期投信前8月稅前合計獲利1兆1,702億元、年增93.7%，已超越2024年全年水準，提前刷新史上同期新高。顯示資本市場活絡大幅挹注金融機構獲利，惟後續須留意市場波動對保險與證券業的影響。
  - 來源 / Source：[鉅亨 uanalyze](https://uanalyze.com.tw/articles/4555856453)
- **Banks on track for record year; regulator urges pay rises** — FSC chair Peng Chin-lung said the banking sector's profits are likely to hit a record this year and publicly urged banks to raise employee pay, reflecting strong earnings momentum. The remarks signal regulatory expectations that banks share their windfall gains with front-line staff.
  **銀行業獲利今年拚創高 主委喊話加薪** — 金管會主委彭金隆表示，銀行業今年獲利可望創新高，並公開喊話銀行替員工加薪，反映產業獲利動能強勁。此番談話釋出監理層期望銀行將豐厚獲利回饋基層員工的訊號，也為金融業薪資調整增添想像空間。

- **FSC pushes Asian asset-management hub as high-net-worth banking expands** — The FSC lists expanding wealth management and building a Taiwan-style Asian asset-management center among its key priorities. Twenty-one banks have been approved for high-asset-client business, serving about 23,206 clients, while dozens of institutions have entered the Kaohsiung asset-management zone as regulators draft a dedicated "Asian asset" law.
  **金管會推亞資中心 高資產業務擴容** — 金管會將擴大財富管理、打造具台灣特色的亞洲資產管理中心列為施政重點；目前已核准21家銀行辦理高資產客戶業務，客戶數約2萬3,206人，高雄資產管理專區進駐機構持續增加，並研擬「亞資專法」，為高資產與財管市場注入政策動能。
