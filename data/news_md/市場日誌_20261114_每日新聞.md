### 🏭 Industry / 產業動態

- **TSMC posts record October revenue on AI demand** — TSMC reported October consolidated revenue of NT$586.89 billion, up 14.66% from September and 59.71% from a year earlier, a fresh monthly record. The surge reinforces expectations that AI accelerator demand will keep the foundry on track for its fourth-quarter guidance of US$52.3–54.3 billion.

### 🏦 Financial Sector / 金融業

- **Financial sector profits hit record on market boom** — Driven by the AI rally and buoyant equities, Taiwan's banking, insurance and securities sectors earned a combined NT$1.17 trillion in pre-tax profit in the first eight months, up 93.7% year-on-year and already surpassing full-year 2024, setting an all-time high for the period. Market strength has been a major driver of the windfall.
  **金融三業前8月賺1.17兆 創同期新高** — AI狂潮加上股市熱絡，銀行、保險與證期投信前8月稅前合計獲利1兆1,702億元、年增93.7%，已超越2024年全年水準，提前刷新史上同期新高。顯示資本市場活絡大幅挹注金融機構獲利，惟後續須留意市場波動對保險與證券業的影響。

- **Banks on track for record year; regulator urges pay rises** — FSC chair Peng Chin-lung said the banking sector's profits are likely to hit a record this year and publicly urged banks to raise employee pay, reflecting strong earnings momentum. The remarks signal regulatory expectations that banks share their windfall gains with front-line staff.
  **銀行業獲利今年拚創高 主委喊話加薪** — 金管會主委彭金隆表示，銀行業今年獲利可望創新高，並公開喊話銀行替員工加薪，反映產業獲利動能強勁。此番談話釋出監理層期望銀行將豐厚獲利回饋基層員工的訊號，也為金融業薪資調整增添想像空間。

### 💰 Tax & Regulations / 稅務法規

- **Property-transaction tax rules eased for share deals** — Taiwan's Ministry of Finance revised the consolidated housing-and-land income tax reporting guidelines, resolving a long-running dispute over how "qualifying" share transactions involving real estate are taxed. The change can apply to cases not yet finally assessed, and in certain situations lets gains on long-held shares be partly exempted under the old regime, easing the burden on some property-linked equity deals.
  **房地合一稅鬆綁 股權交易課稅爭議突破** — 財政部修正房地合一課徵所得稅申報作業要點，就「符合一定條件股權交易」的課稅方式作出重大調整，爭議獲得突破。新規可適用於尚未核課確定的案件，並在特定情形下，讓早年取得股份的出售獲利依舊制房地占比部分豁免，減輕部分不動產相關股權交易的稅負。
  - 來源 / Source：[聯合新聞網](https://udn.com/news/story/7243/9457680)
- **Estate-tax amendments clear the legislature** — Lawmakers passed amendments to the Estate and Gift Tax Act, delivering six key changes. These include scrapping the NT$300,000 threshold for applying to pay estate tax in instalments and adjusting how gifts made to certain relatives within two years before death are counted. The Ministry of Finance is drafting sub-regulations and will step up public guidance once the law is promulgated.
  **遺贈稅法三讀修正 取消分期門檻** — 立法院三讀通過遺產及贈與稅法部分條文修正，共六大重點，包括取消稅額滿30萬元才能申請分期的門檻，並調整被繼承人死亡前兩年內贈與特定親屬財產的計算方式。財政部表示正研擬施行細則等子法規，俟總統公布後將加強宣導，影響高資產族群的傳承與稅務規劃。
  - 來源 / Source：[中央社](https://cna.com.tw/news/afe/202608210289.aspx)

The upgrade underscores how AI-fueled demand is powering one of Taiwan's strongest expansions in decades.
  **台經院上修今年GDP至12.34%** — 台灣經濟研究院公布最新預測，將2026年經濟成長率上修至12.34%，主因AI相關出口暢旺；在高比較基期下，明年GDP仍可達6.45%，2027年CPI年增率預估為1.94%、通膨溫和。上修幅度凸顯AI需求帶動台灣近數十年來最強勁的成長動能之一。

The bank said it still has time to observe, keeping options open amid robust growth and lingering inflation pressure.
  **央行續偏緊 通膨逼近警戒線** — 中央銀行維持政策利率不變，總裁楊金龍釋出貨幣操作仍偏緊、以穩定物價的訊號；此前CPI年增率預測一度略高於2%的警戒水準。央行表示「還有時間觀察」，在經濟強勁與通膨壓力並存下保留政策彈性，後續升降息動向仍視數據而定。
