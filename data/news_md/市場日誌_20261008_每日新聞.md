### 🏭 Industry / 產業動態

- **Foxconn becomes first Taiwan firm to clear NT$1 trillion in monthly revenue** — Foxconn (Hon Hai) posted September revenue of NT$1.159 trillion, the first time any Taiwanese company has topped NT$1 trillion in a single month, up 25.7% from August and 38.4% year-on-year. Third-quarter revenue reached NT$3.03 trillion, up 47% year-on-year and beating estimates, driven by AI server shipments and the new iPhone launch. Management expects AI-led growth to continue into Q4.
  **鴻海9月營收首度單月破兆，寫台灣企業新猷** — 鴻海公布9月營收1兆1,586億元，月增25.7%、年增38.42%，成為台灣史上首家單月營收破兆的企業；第三季營收3兆269億元、年增47.12%，優於市場預期。主要動能來自AI伺服器拉貨與iPhone新機上市雙引擎。鴻海看好AI營運與ICT旺季延續至第四季，完整財報將於11月12日公布，市場聚焦營收能否轉化為獲利。
  - 來源 / Source：[中央社](https://www.cna.com.tw/news/afe/202610050180.aspx)
- **Gigabyte September revenue jumps 125% to record on AI-server demand** — Gigabyte reported September revenue of NT$63.1 billion, surging 125% year-on-year to a record monthly and quarterly high, as demand for AI servers and GPU platforms stayed robust. Coming right after Foxconn's trillion-dollar month, the figure adds to evidence that Taiwan's AI hardware supply chain remained in full swing through the third quarter, reinforcing its role as the main driver of the island's export and equity-market strength.
  **技嘉9月營收年增125%創高，AI伺服器需求強勁** — 技嘉9月合併營收630.99億元，年增125.45%，單月與第三季營收同創歷史新高，反映AI伺服器與GPU平台需求持續強勁。繼鴻海單月營收破兆後，技嘉等供應鏈業者同步交出亮眼成績，顯示台灣AI硬體供應鏈第三季維持高速運轉，持續扮演支撐出口與台股表現的核心引擎，也強化市場對下半年AI行情的信心。

### 🏦 Financial Sector / 金融業

- **Insurers move to new TIS solvency regime with three FSC disclosure rules** — Taiwan's insurers switched to the new Taiwan Insurance Solvency (TIS) regime in 2026, replacing the old RBC framework with a more market-based measure of assets, liabilities and risk. The FSC set three disclosure rules—capping reported ratios at a target value, banning peer rankings, and requiring explanatory notes for shortfalls. The first TIS scorecard, based on end-September figures, is expected to see all life insurers pass.
  **壽險接軌TIS清償新制，金管會訂三項揭露規範** — 壽險業2026年起接軌新一代清償能力制度（TIS），取代舊有RBC制，以更貼近市場方式衡量資產、負債與風險。金管會對資本適足率揭露訂三原則：以目標值為上限、禁止排名比較、未達標須加註說明。首張以9月底為基準的TIS成績單可望全員過關。新制有助提升壽險財務透明度，但也考驗業者資本韌性，牽動後續配息與增資規劃。
  - 來源 / Source：[經濟日報](https://udn.com/news/story/7239/9742463)
- **FX accounting rule to save insurers ~NT$90bn a year in hedging costs** — A new foreign-exchange accounting rule effective in 2026 lets life insurers amortize the FX impact of long-term overseas bonds over their remaining maturity, rather than booking it fully each month. The change is estimated to save the industry about NT$90 billion a year in hedging costs, though gains must first be provisioned. At end-March, insurers' FX reserve stood at NT$911.1 billion with a 45.15% hedge ratio.
  **匯率會計新制上路，壽險年省約900億避險成本** — 金管會匯率會計新制2026年上路，壽險業長期持有的海外債券匯率影響，改依剩餘年限逐年攤銷，不再每月全額認列，業界估計每年可省約900億元避險成本，但須先提存至一定水位才能按比例計入資本。截至3月底，壽險業外匯價格變動準備金達9,111億元、國外投資避險比率45.15%。新制可望緩解匯率波動對壽險獲利與資本的衝擊。
  - 來源 / Source：[經濟日報](https://money.udn.com/money/story/5613/9327550)

### 💎 Wealth Management / 財富管理

- **Banks race to build high-net-worth services under Asian Asset Management Center push** — Taiwan's major banks are expanding high-net-worth services under the government's Asian Asset Management Center initiative. Cathay, CTBC and Yushan have launched pilot programs in the Kaohsiung zone covering cross-border asset allocation, financing and family-office advisory, with entry thresholds around NT$100 million. The push aims to keep wealthy clients' assets onshore and deepen Taiwan's private-banking market.
  **銀行搶攻高資產，亞洲資產管理中心加速財管升級** — 在政府推動的「亞洲資產管理中心」政策下，國銀積極搶攻高資產財富管理。國泰、中信、玉山等已於高雄專區啟動試辦業務，涵蓋跨境資產配置、財務融資與家族辦公室諮詢，服務門檻約新台幣1億元。此布局意在把高資產客戶資金留在境內、深化私人銀行市場，預期將加速財管服務升級與跨業整合，並帶動信託、保險等傳承工具需求。
  - 來源 / Source：[遠見](https://www.gvm.com.tw/article/122450)
- **High-net-worth population to reach 124,000 holding NT$46tn in 2026, report says** — A KPMG–Yushan report estimates Taiwan's high-net-worth population—those with over NT$100 million in assets—rose from 93,000 in 2023 to 118,000 in 2025, holding NT$43.7 trillion combined, and projects 124,000 people with NT$46.1 trillion in 2026. ETFs and HNW wealth management are cited as key growth engines; over 70% of such clients already hold offshore assets and 85% are planning wealth succession.
  **報告：2026年高資產族群估達12.4萬人、持有46兆元** — 據KPMG與玉山報告，台灣淨資產逾1億元的高資產族群由2023年的9.3萬人增至2025年的11.8萬人，合計持有43.7兆元，並預估2026年達12.4萬人、資產約46.1兆元。ETF與高資產財管是推升市場規模的主要引擎；逾七成高資產客戶已布局境外資產、85%已進行或準備傳承規劃，顯示財富管理需求由單純投資轉向跨境配置與家族傳承整合。
