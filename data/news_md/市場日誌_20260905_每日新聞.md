### 💰 Tax & Regulations / 稅務法規

- **MOF advances estate-and-gift tax amendment on pre-death spousal transfers** — Taiwan's Ministry of Finance has moved forward a draft amendment to the Estate and Gift Tax Act, under which assets gifted to a spouse and heirs within two years before death are combined into the total estate for taxation, with the resulting tax apportioned among recipients by their share of gifted assets. The change tightens a common inheritance-planning route and affects high-net-worth succession structuring.
  **財政部推動遺贈稅法修正 死亡前2年贈與配偶財產併計** — 財政部推進「遺產及贈與稅法」部分條文修正草案，被繼承人死亡前二年內贈與配偶及各順序繼承人的財產須併入遺產總額課徵遺產稅，應納稅額由各受贈人按受贈財產占比分擔。影響上，此舉收緊常見的生前贈與節稅路徑，牽動高資產族群的傳承與贈與時點規劃，相關家族財富布局須重新檢視配置節奏與稅負分攤方式。

- **2026 income-tax brackets and allowances adjusted for inflation** — With cumulative price gains exceeding the 10% threshold since the last revision in 2017, Taiwan's 2026 tax parameters — personal income-tax exemption, basic living expense, standard and special deductions, tax brackets and retirement-income limits — are being adjusted upward in line with the consumer price index.
