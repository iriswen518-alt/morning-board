### 🏦 Financial Sector / 金融業

- **FSC refines bank and financial-holding reporting standards** — The Financial Supervisory Commission amended the preparation standards for financial reports of banks and financial holding companies and updated credit-cooperative regulations in August, aiming to improve disclosure transparency and comparability. The changes form part of the groundwork as the sector moves toward new accounting and solvency frameworks, nudging institutions toward cleaner, more consistent balance-sheet reporting.
  **金管會優化銀行與金控財報編製準則** — 金管會於 8 月修正銀行及金控公司財務報告編製準則，並更新信用合作社相關規範，以提升資訊揭露的透明度與可比較性。相關調整為金融業接軌新一代會計與清償能力制度鋪路，促使機構提升資產負債表揭露品質。此舉牽動整體金融業體質與後續監理節奏，也讓投資人更易比較各行庫的財務體質。
  - 來源 / Source：[金管會](https://www.fsc.gov.tw/ch/home.jsp?id=2&parentpath=0) ｜ [銀行局新聞稿](https://www.banking.gov.tw/ch/home.jsp?id=540&parentpath=0%2C524%2C539)
- **Regulators move to channel insurance funds into public construction** — The FSC said it is working across departments to expand insurers' participation in public-construction investment, providing long-duration outlets for insurance capital while supporting infrastructure financing. The initiative aims to match life insurers' long-term liabilities with stable, cash-flow-generating assets, easing reinvestment pressure as the industry transitions to the new solvency regime.
  **金管會推動保險資金投入公共建設** — 金管會表示正跨部會研議擴大保險業參與公共建設投資，為壽險資金提供長天期去化管道，同時支持基礎建設融資。此舉意在使壽險業長期負債與穩定現金流資產相互匹配，紓解低利與再投資壓力。在接軌新清償能力制度之際，導引保險資金投入公建，兼顧產業穩健與國家建設資金需求，具政策雙重效益。

Domestic players including E.Sun are rolling out asset-pool collateralised lending and non-specified-beneficiary trusts to serve high-net-worth families with cross-border tax and succession needs.
