### Taiwan Equities / 股市行情

- **TAIEX drops 307 points to 45,857.66, losing the 46,000 level** — Taiwan's benchmark index fell 307.06 points (-0.67%) on Sept 3 to close at 45,857.66, slipping below 46,000 after an intraday swing of nearly 680 points, with turnover around NT$946.3bn. Electronics dragged the tape (-0.85%) while the financial index jumped 2.19%, signalling a rotation out of high-flying chip names into defensive, higher-yield sectors as global rate jitters persisted.

The split — a firm bellwether against a soft tape — highlights investors' preference for structural AI winners even as they trim broader tech exposure amid rising bond yields.
  **台積電逆勢收2,390元、權王撐盤** — 權值王台積電9月3日逆勢上漲5元（+0.21%）收2,390元，在大盤走弱之際相對抗跌，凸顯AI需求持續為晶圓代工龍頭提供支撐。權王走穩、大盤走弱的分歧，顯示在美債殖利率走高、投資人縮減整體科技部位之際，資金仍偏好具結構性成長題材的AI受惠核心標的。

### 🏭 Industry / 產業動態

- **TSMC and MediaTek flag Taiwan's AI-era bottlenecks at SEMICON** — At SEMICON Taiwan 2026, a headline dialogue among TSMC, MediaTek and industry leaders warned that the constraints of the AI build-out now extend well beyond wafer fabrication to land, power, construction labor, equipment and materials. As fab counts rise sharply, the wider ecosystem was never pre-provisioned for such violent demand growth, pointing to sustained investment — and potential shortages — across Taiwan's chip supply chain.
  **SEMICON大師對談：台積、聯發科示警台灣AI瓶頸** — 在SEMICON Taiwan 2026半導體展，台積電、聯發科與產業領袖同台對談，示警AI建置的瓶頸已不僅止於晶圓製造，更擴及土地、電力、營建人力、設備與材料。隨晶圓廠數量快速增加，整個生態系並未為如此劇烈的需求成長預作準備，意味台灣半導體供應鏈後續投資將延續，部分環節也可能出現供給吃緊。
  - 來源 / Source：[三立新聞網](https://www.setn.com/news/1900552)
- **TSMC's 2026 equipment-buying estimate climbs to 1.9x on relentless AI demand** — TSMC executive Y.P. Chyn said AI demand growth is the fiercest in 30 years, nearly doubling in six months, with almost 20 fabs advancing in parallel still unable to keep pace. The company's 2026 equipment-purchase estimate has risen from 1x at end-2025 to 1.5x a quarter later and 1.9x by July, signalling a prolonged capex super-cycle that benefits upstream equipment and materials suppliers.
  **台積電2026設備採購估攀升至1.9倍、AI需求30年最猛** — 台積電副共同營運長侯永清指出，AI需求成長為30年僅見，短短半年幾近翻倍，全球近20座晶圓廠同步推進仍供不應求。台積電2026年設備採購預估已從2025年底的1倍，一季後升至1.5倍、7月更攀至1.9倍，顯示資本支出超級循環延續，利多上游半導體設備與材料供應鏈，也支撐相關台廠營運展望。
