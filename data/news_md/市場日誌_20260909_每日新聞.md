### 🏭 Industry / 產業動態

- **TSMC and ASML push 12-inch High-NA EUV photomasks** — On Sept 8 TSMC and ASML announced a joint drive to shift the industry from today's 6-inch masks to 12-inch masks to unlock High-NA EUV lithography. The larger format removes the current two-mask stitching limit, lifting fab productivity and lowering cost; TSMC aims to adopt it in advanced nodes from 2030 and reach volume around 2033.
  **台積電攜手 ASML 推 12 吋 High-NA EUV 光罩** — 台積電與ASML於9月8日宣布，共同推動半導體由現行6吋光罩轉向12吋大尺寸光罩，以發揮High-NA EUV微影價值。大尺寸可消除兩張6吋光罩拼接限制、提升晶圓廠產能並降低成本；台積電規劃2030年於先進製程導入，力拚2033年量產。
  - 來源 / Source：[聯合新聞網](https://udn.com/news/story/6811/9742606)
- **Quanta sees AI-server orders visible into 2027** — Quanta says AI-server order visibility now extends into 2027 with capacity planning already reaching 2028, projecting continued revenue growth. Beyond ongoing GB300 shipments, its next-generation Vera Rubin AI servers are set to ramp this quarter and contribute revenue by year-end, underscoring resilient demand across Taiwan's ODM supply chain.
  **廣達 AI 伺服器訂單能見度延伸至 2027 年** — 廣達表示AI伺服器訂單能見度已延伸至2027年，並提前布局2028年產能，預估營收持續成長。除GB300持續出貨外，新一代Vera Rubin AI伺服器本季逐步進入量產、年底前開始貢獻營收，凸顯台廠ODM供應鏈需求續強。
  - 來源 / Source：[經濟日報](https://money.udn.com/money/story/5607/9631367)

### 🏦 Financial Sector / 金融業

- **Life insurers adopt IFRS 17 and ICS 2.0 in 2026** — Taiwan's insurers open their books under IFRS 17 in 2025 and formally adopt the standard, alongside ICS 2.0, in 2026. The FSC has revised the industry's financial-reporting rules—adding metrics such as the insurance-service-result-to-pretax-profit ratio—so that 2026 annual reports better reflect underlying profitability and reduce distortions from interest-rate swings on net worth.
  **壽險業 2026 年接軌 IFRS 17 與 ICS 2.0** — 台灣壽險業2025年開帳、2026年正式接軌IFRS 17，並同步導入ICS 2.0。金管會修正保險業財務報告編製準則，增訂「保險服務結果對稅前純益比率」等指標，使2026年年報更能反映實質獲利，並降低利率變動對淨值的衝擊。
  - 來源 / Source：[經濟日報](https://money.udn.com/money/story/5613/9138153)

The move signals continued state backstopping of financial institutions and has implications for regional lenders and insurers.
