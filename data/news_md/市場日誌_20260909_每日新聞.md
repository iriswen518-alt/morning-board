### 🏭 Industry / 產業動態

- **TSMC and ASML push 12-inch High-NA EUV photomasks** — On Sept 8 TSMC and ASML announced a joint drive to shift the industry from today's 6-inch masks to 12-inch masks to unlock High-NA EUV lithography. ### 💰 Tax & Regulations / 稅務法規

- **2026 income-tax filing brings tax cuts** — Under the approved 2026 central-government budget, individual income-tax relief takes effect: a higher basic-living allowance and long-term-care and preschool special deductions, rental spending reclassified as a special deduction, and upward-adjusted tax brackets. The changes lower effective burdens for households and reshape year-end tax planning ahead of the 2027 filing season.
  **2026 綜所稅報稅將減稅** — 依已通過的115年度中央政府總預算，綜所稅減稅措施上路：調高基本生活費、長照與幼兒特別扣除額，租屋支出改列特別扣除額，並上調課稅級距金額。相關調整可降低家庭實質稅負，也牽動年底前的節稅規劃布局。

- **House-and-Land Tax 2.0 gains calculation clarified** — For enterprises computing land-transaction income under House-and-Land Tax 2.0, taxable income is revenue less related costs, expenses or losses, then less the total land-value increment. The framework keeps property gains inside the consolidated regime, sustaining the government's effort to curb short-term speculation and align taxation with realized profit.
  **房地合一稅 2.0 所得計算原則明確** — 營利事業計算適用房地合一稅2.0之土地交易所得時，應以收入減除相關成本、費用或損失後之餘額，再減除土地漲價總數額後之餘額為課稅所得額。此架構使不動產交易所得續納入整體制度，延續政府抑制短線炒作、使課稅貼近實際獲利的方向。
