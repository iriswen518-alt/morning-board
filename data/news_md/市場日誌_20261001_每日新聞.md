### 🏭 Industry / 產業動態

### 🏦 Financial Sector / 金融業

- **Eight new financial rules take effect from Sept 30** — Taiwan's FSC rolled out eight new measures, six covering securities and two covering insurance accounting. Listed firms must upload shareholder-meeting handbooks and annual reports earlier — 30 and 14 days before meetings — financial-report filings move fully paperless, and an e-gift platform lets shareholders claim vouchers after e-voting, tightening disclosure and cutting paperwork.
  **金管會8大新制9月30日上路** — 金管會公布8項新制與新措施上路，其中6項與證券有關、2項為保險會計新制。上市櫃公司股東會議事手冊及年報須提前上傳（會前30天及14天），公開發行公司財報改採電子化無紙申報，集保並建置電子化紀念品平台供股東電子投票後線上領券，強化資訊揭露與便民。
  - 來源 / Source：[NOWnews](https://www.nownews.com/news/6770618)
- **FSC pushes insurers to strengthen balance sheets, channel funds to public works** — Regulators outlined a three-pronged effort to improve life insurers' financial quality as IFRS 17 and the ICS solvency regime take effect in 2026, expecting high-rate long-term guaranteed policies to gradually fade. The FSC is also steering insurers' large capital pools toward domestic public-infrastructure investment while balancing solvency.
  **金管會三面向強化壽險體質 引導資金投入國內公建** — 隨IFRS 17與ICS新制2026年上路，金管會提出三面向強化壽險業財務體質，預期高利率「長期保證」保單將逐步退場；同時引導保險業龐大資金投入國內公共建設，兼顧清償能力與資金運用效率，推動保險資本支持實體經濟。

### 💎 Wealth Management / 財富管理

- **Banks build dual-track wealth platforms for HNW clients and offshore ETFs** — Taiwanese banks are expanding wealth management around high-net-worth (HNW) clients and offshore investing. Bank of Kaohsiung is upgrading its franchise on two axes — an overseas equity and ETF platform launched in early 2026 and a new HNW wealth-management business — linking southern Taiwan's industrial clusters with tailored advisory to deepen asset allocation.
  **銀行雙軸布局 搶攻高資產與海外ETF平台** — 台灣銀行業以高資產客群與海外投資為主軸擴張財富管理。高雄銀行以「國外股票及ETF投資平台」與「高資產業務」雙軸全面升級，平台於2026年初上線、高資產業務同步開辦，結合南台灣產業聚落與在地資源，導入專業顧問與客製化服務，提升資產配置效率與財富管理深度。
  - 來源 / Source：[經濟日報](https://money.udn.com/money/story/5613/9221671)
- **Over 70% of Taiwan's wealthy now hold offshore assets** — Wealth reports show Taiwan's HNW population heading toward 140,000 people and NT$46 trillion in assets by 2027, near a fifth of national wealth. Amid rising geopolitical and macro uncertainty, more than 70% of HNW clients have allocated to offshore assets, prioritizing wealth preservation, tax planning and global diversification over Taiwan-only exposure.
  **逾七成高資產客戶已布局境外資產** — 財富報告指出，台灣高資產客群至2027年上看14萬人、資產規模達46兆元，近全台財富兩成。在全球政經不確定性升高下，逾七成高資產客戶已布局境外資產配置，核心目標聚焦財富保值、稅務規劃與資產增長，投資放眼全球而非侷限台灣，反映風險規避與傳承需求升溫。
