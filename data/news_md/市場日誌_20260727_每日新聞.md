### 🏭 Industry / 產業動態

- **Fire halts GlobalWafers' 8-inch line at Italy's Novara plant** — GlobalWafers said a fire in a cleanroom tool knocked out the 8-inch wafer line at its Novara facility in Italy, with no casualties but production suspended pending damage assessment. Supply-chain estimates put the affected output at roughly 5% of the group's global 8-inch capacity; the 12-inch line was unaffected. Shares fell more than 8% and dragged peers Sino-American Silicon and Tainergy lower.
  **環球晶義大利 Novara 廠 8 吋產線遭火吻停產** — 環球晶（6488）公告義大利 Novara 廠無塵室機台起火，8 吋產線停止生產，無人員傷亡，損失與復工時程待評估。供應鏈估受影響產能約占集團全球 8 吋產能 5%，12 吋產線不受影響。公司將透過全球據點調度因應；消息帶動股價一度重挫逾 8%，中美晶、台勝科等同步走弱。
  - 來源 / Source：[工商時報](https://www.ctee.com.tw/news/20260723700190-439901)

- **AI-server demand keeps powering Foxconn's record run** — Foxconn's cloud-and-networking segment continues to ride surging AI-server orders, after the group posted June revenue of NT$821.8bn (up 52%) and second-quarter revenue of NT$2.51tn (up 39.8%), both same-period highs. Management expects AI chassis demand to keep growing into the third quarter as ICT products enter their seasonal peak, underscoring resilient hardware momentum despite equity-market volatility.
  **AI 伺服器需求續強，撐起鴻海創高動能** — 鴻海雲端網路產品受惠 AI 伺服器訂單暢旺，6 月營收 8,218 億元、年增 52%，第二季營收 2.51 兆元、年增 39.8%，同創同期新高。公司預期第三季 AI 機櫃需求延續成長、ICT 產品進入傳統旺季，營運可望季年雙增，顯示硬體供應鏈動能未因股市震盪而降溫。
  - 來源 / Source：[鉅亨網](https://news.cnyes.com/news/id/6522712)

### 🏦 Financial Sector / 金融業

- **FSC approves E.Sun Financial's acquisition of Sanshang Life** — The Financial Supervisory Commission cleared E.Sun Financial Holding's plan to acquire 100% of Sanshang American Bankers Life Insurance via a share swap, setting a September 1 merger base date. Regulators judged the deal met financial-holding and insurance-law requirements on policyholder protection, financial soundness and management capability. The share-swap ratio is 0.2486 E.Sun shares per Sanshang share.
  **金管會核准玉山金併三商美邦人壽** — 金管會同意玉山金控以換股方式併購三商美邦人壽 100% 股權，合併基準日訂為 9 月 1 日。金管會認定本案符合金控法與保險法規定，兼顧保戶與員工權益、財務健全及經營能力等條件；換股比例為每 1 股三商壽換 0.2486 股玉山金，成為國內壽險整併再添一樁指標案例。
  - 來源 / Source：[工商時報](https://www.ctee.com.tw/news/20260707701801-430304)

- **Taiwan's life-insurance consolidation wave reshapes the sector** — Beyond the E.Sun deal, the industry is entering 2026 amid a broader restructuring, with Taishin Life set to disappear as it merges into the Shin Kong group and other holding companies pursuing insurance tie-ups. The trend reflects pressure from IFRS 17 and ICS capital rules, pushing smaller insurers to seek stronger parents and reshaping competition across bancassurance and wealth channels.
  **壽險整併潮改寫產業版圖** — 除玉山金案外，壽險業 2026 年進入結構重整期，台新人壽將併入新光體系走入歷史，多家金控同步推進保險整併。此趨勢反映 IFRS 17 與新一代清償能力（ICS）資本規範壓力，促使體質較弱的壽險公司尋求強力母體，並牽動銀保與財富管理通路的競爭格局。
