### Taiwan Equities / 股市行情

- **TSMC posts record Q3 revenue ahead of its Oct 15 earnings call** — TSMC reported September sales of NT$511.86bn, down 0.6% month-on-month but up 54.6% from a year earlier, a record for the month and its second-highest ever. Third-quarter revenue reached NT$1.4942tn, up 17.62% sequentially and a single-quarter high that beat its own guidance, with nine-month sales near NT$3.90tn (+41.1% y/y). Its Oct 15 results call—covering AI demand, 2nm ramp and 2027 capex—should steer the weighting-heavy index.
  **台積電Q3營收創高 10/15法說登場成風向球** — 台積電9月合併營收5,118.57億元，月減0.6%、年增54.6%，創歷年同期新高、單月次高；第三季營收1兆4,942.44億元，季增17.62%，改寫單季新高並超越財測高標，前九月累計約3.90兆元、年增41.1%。台積電占大盤權重逾四成，10月15日（週四）法說會將說明AI需求、2奈米進度與明年資本支出，牽動加權指數與電子股後市多空方向，為本週市場最大焦點。
  - 來源 / Source：[經濟日報](https://money.udn.com/money/story/5607/9802747)
- **Memory-chip names stay in focus as the up-cycle broadens** — Taiwan's memory sector remains a market theme as DRAM and NAND demand recovers. Earlier results showed Nanya Technology swinging to a sharp profit while Macronix and Adata hit operating highs, reflecting tight supply and firmer pricing. With research houses projecting further contract-price gains, investors continue rotating into memory-related stocks as a complement to AI-server and foundry leaders, keeping the group among the market's more active themes into the fourth quarter.

### 🏭 Industry / 產業動態

- **Memory contract prices seen rising further this quarter on tight supply** — Research firms expect the memory up-cycle to extend. TrendForce projects fourth-quarter DRAM contract prices still climbing sequentially while NAND Flash holds firm, as AI-server and data-center demand keeps the market extremely tight; some banks model even steeper DDR gains. The sustained pricing strength supports revenue and margins across Taiwan's memory makers and module houses, though the pace of increases is expected to moderate from the earlier, more aggressive quarterly jumps.
  **研調估本季記憶體合約價續揚 供給緊俏** — 研調機構預期記憶體漲勢延續。TrendForce估本季DRAM合約價仍將季增、NAND Flash維持堅挺，AI伺服器與資料中心需求使市況維持極度緊缺，部分外資對DDR漲幅估得更高。報價持續走強，撐起台廠記憶體與模組業者的營收與毛利，惟漲幅可望較前幾季的大幅跳升趨於收斂。供給面擴產節奏與需求端拉貨力道，將是影響後續合約價走勢與相關個股評價的關鍵變數。
  - 來源 / Source：[ETtoday財經](https://finance.ettoday.net/news/3095852)
- **Foxconn keeps AI-server shipment lead over Quanta and Wistron** — Foreign-broker channel checks show Foxconn holding the top share in Nvidia GB-series rack shipments, above 44%, ahead of Quanta and Wistron, as hyperscaler build-outs drive demand for GB200 and GB300 systems. Analysts expect profits across Taiwan's AI-server supply chain to be released more fully through 2026 as rack volumes scale and next-generation platforms ramp, keeping the big assemblers among the key beneficiaries of the ongoing AI capital-expenditure wave.
  **AI伺服器出貨 鴻海市占逾四成領先廣達緯創** — 外資通路訪查顯示，鴻海在輝達GB系列機櫃出貨市占逾44%，居台系ODM之首，領先廣達與緯創，反映雲端業者加大建置、帶動GB200與GB300系統需求。法人預期，隨機櫃出貨量放大、新世代平台陸續放量，AI伺服器產業鏈獲利可望於2026年更全面釋放，大型組裝廠續居AI資本支出浪潮的主要受惠者，惟須留意世代交替期的出貨節奏與零組件供貨調整。

### 🏦 Financial Sector / 金融業

- **Regulator broadens insurers' scope to invest in "health and wellbeing" businesses** — Taiwan's FSC issued an order on Oct 6 removing the requirement that insurers' investments in health-and-wellbeing businesses be tied to policy underwriting, claims or payouts, and dropping the limit confining services to policyholders; it took effect immediately. Insurers may hold up to 100% of unlisted health-business targets. The agency groups the field into five areas—including long-term care and all-ages friendly services—to channel life-insurance capital into the care economy.
  **金管會擴大壽險投資「健康福祉事業」範疇** — 金管會保險局10月6日發布令釋，取消保險業投資健康福祉事業須與保單核保、理賠、給付連結的限制，服務對象也不再僅限保戶，並即日生效；對非上市櫃健康事業的持股上限可達100%。金管會將健康福祉事業分為五大領域，涵蓋長期照顧與全齡友善服務等，引導壽險資金投入高齡化下的照顧經濟，兼顧保戶權益與監理底線，為壽險業開拓新投資與策略布局空間，業者對健康管理顧問、藥局通路與長照輔助等領域興趣濃厚。
  - 來源 / Source：[Uanalyze優分析](https://uanalyze.com.tw/articles/4063056619)
- **Insurers' "trillion-dollar" infrastructure channel widens to AI and logistics** — Revised rules lift the cap on insurers' capital for project, public-construction and social-welfare investments from 10% to 15% of usable funds, and extend eligible targets to new areas such as AI computing centers, smart warehousing and submarine cables. The regulator has also folded the "new ten major AI construction" projects into strategic industries that insurers may invest in or lend to, while raising private-equity-fund infrastructure limits, aiming to steer long-term insurance money into real assets.
  **壽險兆元活水入基建 擴及AI算力、冷鏈倉儲** — 修正後規定將保險業辦理專案運用、公共及社會福利事業投資的資金總額上限，由可運用資金10%提高至15%，適用範圍並擴及AI算力中心、智慧倉儲、海底電纜等新型態案源。金管會另將「AI新十大建設」納入策略性產業，保險業可投資或放款，並放寬透過私募股權基金投資公建的限額。政策方向在引導長天期壽險資金投入實體資產與公共建設，支應國內投資並兼顧資產負債管理，為壽險資金去化與收益率提供新出口。
  - 來源 / Source：[Uanalyze優分析](https://uanalyze.com.tw/articles/1792336887)

### 💎 Wealth Management / 財富管理

- **High-dividend ETF 00878 pushes assets to records as payouts climb** — Cathay Sustainability High Dividend (00878) has grown from about NT$376.3bn in assets at end-2024 to over NT$460.2bn by end-2025, topping NT$470bn in January for a record. Its latest NT$1.01 distribution was paid Sept 11, and a second-quarter payout of NT$0.66 set a record; the ETF distributes quarterly in February, May, August and November. Total Taiwan equity-ETF assets have reached a record NT$7.859tn, underlining retail demand for income products.
  **高股息ETF 00878規模創高 配息走揚** — 國泰永續高股息00878基金規模由2024年底約3,763億元，成長至2025年底逾4,602億元，1月一度突破4,700億元創成立新高。最新一期配息1.01元於9月11日發放，第二季曾配0.66元創歷史新高；該ETF為季配，除息月落在2、5、8、11月。同期整體台股ETF規模達7.859兆元、同創新高，凸顯存股族對收益型商品的強勁需求，惟殖利率係依過去配息估算，實際報酬仍隨股價波動，投資人仍須留意成分股評價與填息狀況。

- **Regular-savings ETF investors keep rotating into income and market-cap funds** — Taiwan's regular-savings (dollar-cost-averaging) ETF base keeps expanding, with Yuanta Taiwan Top 50 (0050) surpassing 1.28 million dollar-cost accounts, the most among market-cap ETFs, while high-dividend products such as 0056 and 00878 retain large followings. The trend reflects households favoring diversified, lower-volatility vehicles amid choppy index action, with both market-cap and dividend themes drawing steady monthly inflows as a core allocation tool for long-term savers.
  **定期定額族持續布局市值型與高息ETF** — 台灣ETF定期定額戶數持續擴張，元大台灣50（0050）定期定額交易戶突破128萬戶，居市值型ETF之首，高股息如0056、00878亦維持龐大投資族群。此趨勢反映盤面震盪下，國人偏好分散、相對低波動的配置工具，市值型與高股息兩大主題持續吸引每月扣款資金，成為長期存股族的核心配置。對財富管理而言，透過定期定額平滑進場成本、搭配資產配置，有助於在高檔震盪行情中分散風險、累積部位。
