### 🏭 Industry / 產業動態

- **TSMC raises 2026 capex to US$60–64bn on AI demand** — At its Q2 earnings call, TSMC lifted its full-year capital-spending budget to US$60–64bn and pledged an additional US$100bn for Arizona, citing insatiable demand for AI and high-performance computing. About 70–80% of spending targets advanced nodes, with 2nm ramping as a key growth driver and CoWoS advanced-packaging capacity continuing to expand.
  **台積電上調 2026 資本支出至 600–640 億美元** — 台積電法說會將全年資本支出上修至 600–640 億美元，並加碼 1,000 億美元投資亞利桑那，反映 AI 與高效能運算需求強勁。約 70–80% 資金投入先進製程，2 奈米放量成為成長亮點，CoWoS 先進封裝產能也持續擴充。
  - 來源 / Source：[鉅亨網](https://news.cnyes.com/news/id/6536791)

- **TSMC reportedly to raise foundry prices up to 10% from 2027** — According to Nikkei Asia, TSMC plans to lift wafer-foundry pricing across both advanced and mature nodes from 2027, with increases of 5–10% depending on customer and product. The hike reflects rising costs for materials, equipment and overseas fabs; customers adding HPC orders beyond forecast may face a further 10–15% premium.
  **台積電傳 2027 年起代工漲價最高 10%** — 據《日經亞洲》報導，台積電計畫自 2027 年起調漲晶圓代工價格，涵蓋先進與成熟製程，依客戶與產品調幅 5–10%。漲價主因反映材料、設備及海外建廠成本攀升；若客戶追加超出預估的 HPC 訂單，恐再加收 10–15% 溢價。
  - 來源 / Source：[TechNews 科技新報](https://technews.tw/2026/07/21/tsmc-raise-chipmaking-prices/)

### 🏦 Financial Sector / 金融業

- **FSC to publish "financial give-back" guideline in July** — FSC Chairman Peng Jin-long said the regulator would release a "financial give-back" guideline in July, aiming to steer banks and insurers toward balancing profit growth with social responsibility and social investment. The move signals rising supervisory expectations that record-earning financial institutions channel gains into broader public benefit.
  **金管會 7 月將公布「金融大回饋」指引** — 金管會主委彭金隆表示，將於 7 月發布「金融大回饋」指引，引導金融業在追求獲利成長之餘兼顧社會責任與社會投資。此舉顯示監理端對獲利創高的金融機構回饋社會的期待升高，期望盈餘能轉化為更廣泛的公共效益。
  - 來源 / Source：[聯合新聞網](https://udn.com/news/story/7239/9570659)

- **Land Bank wins high-asset wealth licence, 25th bank approved** — The FSC approved Land Bank to offer financial products and services for high-net-worth clients under the "Wealth Management 2.0" framework, bringing the total number of licensed banks to 25. Land Bank is expected to launch the business by year-end, intensifying competition among domestic banks for affluent clients.
  **土銀取得財管 2.0 執照，累計 25 家銀行獲准** — 金管會同意台灣土地銀行辦理高資產客戶適用之金融商品與服務，成為第 25 家取得「財管 2.0」執照的銀行。土銀原則上有望於年底前開辦，國銀爭搶高資產客群的競爭態勢進一步升溫。

### 💰 Tax & Regulations / 稅務法規

- **Ministry of Finance clarifies estate-tax treatment of inherited dividends** — The Ministry of Finance issued guidance on how "receivable dividends" attached to shares left by a deceased person should be reported for estate-tax purposes, helping heirs correctly declare undistributed dividend rights. The clarification aims to reduce filing disputes as more affluent estates pass down concentrated equity holdings.
  **財政部釐清遺留股票「應收股利」遺產稅申報** — 財政部發布指引，說明被繼承人遺留股票所附「應收股利」於遺產稅之申報方式，協助繼承人正確申報未分配的股利權益。此舉旨在減少申報爭議，因應高資產遺產多以集中持股形式傳承的趨勢。
  - 來源 / Source：[財政部](https://www.mof.gov.tw/)

- **2026 estate and gift-tax allowances held steady** — For 2026, the estate-tax and gift-tax exemption thresholds remain unchanged, with the annual gift-tax allowance staying at NT$2.44m while tax brackets see minor inflation adjustments. Meanwhile Taiwan continues automatic exchange of financial-account information under the CRS framework, keeping cross-border reporting obligations in focus for high-net-worth families.
  **2026 遺產稅、贈與稅免稅額維持** — 2026 年遺產稅與贈與稅免稅額維持不變，贈與稅每年免稅額仍為 244 萬元，課稅級距則隨物價微幅調整。另外，台灣持續依 CRS 進行金融帳戶資訊自動交換，跨境申報義務仍是高資產家庭關注重點。
