### 🏦 Financial Sector / 金融業

- **FSC clears Taishin's merger with Shin Kong Bank** — Regulators approved Taishin Bank's share-swap absorption of Shin Kong Bank, with Taishin as the surviving entity and a target merger base date of Jan 1, 2027.
