### 🏦 Financial Sector / 金融業

- **FSC clears Taishin's merger with Shin Kong Bank** — Regulators approved Taishin Bank's share-swap absorption of Shin Kong Bank, with Taishin as the surviving entity and a target merger base date of Jan 1, 2027. The combined bank's assets top NT$4.7tn—seventh largest among domestic banks—and its branch network expands to 204 outlets, the island's second largest, reshaping Taiwan's banking landscape.
  **金管會核准台新併新光銀** — 金管會核准台新銀行以換股方式吸收合併新光銀行，由台新為存續公司，暫定2027年1月1日為合併基準日。合併後新銀行資產突破4.7兆元、躍居國銀資產第7大，實體據點擴增至204家、分行網絡全台第二。此案為台灣金融業重大重組，牽動存戶權益與同業競爭版圖，後續整併綜效備受關注。
  - 來源 / Source：[東森新聞](https://news.ebc.net.tw/news/business/568178)
- **Regulator eases capital rules as insurers adopt TIS** — As life insurers move to Taiwan's new-generation solvency regime (TIS) in 2026, the FSC granted supervisory flexibility: eight life-insurance-type financial holdings get capital relief, and 11 insurance-type holdings may defer capital-adequacy filing by one month.
