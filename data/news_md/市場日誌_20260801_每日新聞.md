### Taiwan Equities / 股市行情

- **TAIEX rockets a record 3,186 points as TSMC locks limit-up** — Taiwan's benchmark staged a historic reversal on Friday, surging 3,186.45 points, or 7.98%, to close at 43,119.75 — the largest single-day point gain on record — after a brutal mid-week slide. TSMC hit limit-up at NT$2,425, up NT$220, lifting its market cap to NT$62.88tn and contributing about 1,746 index points. Breadth was overwhelming: 1,752 gainers against 228 decliners, with 221 stocks limit-up and just two limit-down, as the electronics sub-index jumped 9.19%.
  **台股飆史上最大漲點 3,186 點，台積電亮燈漲停** — 加權指數週五上演史詩級反攻，終場大漲 3,186.45 點、漲幅 7.98%，收 43,119.75 點，創史上最大單日漲點，一舉扭轉本週稍早的重挫。台積電攻上漲停 2,425 元、漲 220 元，市值攀升至 62.88 兆元，單檔貢獻大盤約 1,746 點。盤面全面轉強，上漲 1,752 家、下跌僅 228 家，漲停多達 221 檔、跌停僅 2 檔，電子類指數大漲 9.19%，資金回流權值股態勢明確。

- **Three institutions swing to NT$84.7bn net buy, foreigners snap up TSMC** — After three straight sessions of selling, the three major institutional investors flipped to a combined net buy of NT$84.68bn on Friday. ### 🏦 Financial Sector / 金融業

- **FSC releases first-batch results for banks' internal-ratings-based capital approach** — The Financial Supervisory Commission announced the first round of approval results for domestic banks applying the internal-ratings-based (IRB) approach to credit-risk capital calculation. The IRB framework lets qualifying banks use their own risk models rather than standardised weights, typically improving reported capital-adequacy ratios and freeing up room for lending and dividends. The move marks a further step in aligning Taiwan's banking sector with advanced Basel capital standards and rewarding stronger in-house risk management.
  **金管會公布銀行內部評等法首批核准結果** — 金管會公布國銀採用內部評等法（IRB）計提信用風險資本的第一批核准結果。IRB 允許符合資格的銀行以自建風險模型取代標準化權數，通常可優化資本適足率、釋出放款與配息空間。此舉是台灣銀行業對接進階巴塞爾資本規範的又一進展，亦鼓勵銀行強化自身風險管理能力。獲准銀行資本運用彈性提升，有助支撐後續授信成長與股東回饋，對整體金融業體質為正向訊號，惟仍須符合主管機關持續監理要求。
  - 來源 / Source：[金管會](https://www.fsc.gov.tw/ch/home.jsp?id=2&parentpath=0)

- **Insurers post over NT$300bn first-half profit, but July momentum may cool** — Taiwan's life and non-life insurers booked more than NT$300bn in combined net profit for the first half of 2026, a bumper run powered by investment gains and a firmer rate backdrop. Analysts caution the "sweetest" stretch may be passing, warning that July results could soften as market volatility and hedging costs weigh. The sector's earnings trajectory has become a key gauge of financial-holding profitability given insurers' heavy weighting within Taiwan's major financial groups.
  **保險業上半年獲利逾 3,000 億，7 月動能恐降溫** — 台灣壽險與產險業 2026 上半年合計稅後淨利突破 3,000 億元，受惠投資收益與利率環境轉穩，繳出亮眼成績。然而分析師提醒，最甜蜜時刻恐已過去，7 月獲利可能因市場波動與避險成本上升而降溫。由於保險子公司在多家金控集團中占比甚高，保險業獲利走勢已成為評估金控整體獲利能力的關鍵指標，後續投資部位評價變動與新台幣匯率走勢，將是牽動下半年獲利表現的重要變數。

### 💎 Wealth Management / 財富管理

- **E.SUN–KPMG report: high-net-worth ranks to compound ~10% a year** — A 2026 wealth-and-succession survey by E.SUN Bank and KPMG Taiwan finds Taiwan's high-net-worth population (net worth above NT$100mn) growing at roughly a 10% compound annual rate through 2029, up from about 7% in 2021–2025. Offshore allocation is now near-standard: about 69% of NT$100mn-plus clients already hold overseas assets, rising to 86% among the ultra-wealthy above NT$1bn. The findings point to swelling demand for cross-border planning, trusts and family-succession services.
  **玉山 × KPMG 報告：高資產客群年複合成長約 10%** — 玉山銀行與 KPMG Taiwan 發布 2026 台灣高資產客戶財富管理與傳承調研報告指出，淨資產逾 1 億元的高資產族群，2025 至 2029 年年複合成長率上修至約 10%，高於 2021 至 2025 年的約 7%。境外資產配置幾成標配：億元級客群約 69% 已持有境外資產，十億等級超高資產族更達 86%。調研顯示跨境資產配置、信託與家族傳承服務需求持續攀升，銀行財管與私銀業務成長動能可期，惟須留意國際稅務與法遵合規要求。
  - 來源 / Source：[玉山銀行](https://wealth.esunbank.com/zh-tw/-/media/New-ESUNBANK/Wealth/file/report/2026ESUNWealthReport.pdf)

- **Regulatory easing keeps widening the high-net-worth product menu** — Under the FSC's high-net-worth customer rules, banks serving clients with at least NT$100mn in assets can offer a broader, more sophisticated product and advisory line-up, and the roster of approved institutions continues to expand. The regime — a deliberate loosening designed to keep wealthy Taiwanese assets onshore and support the island's asset-management-centre push — lets qualified banks distribute structured products, offshore bonds and other complex instruments to affluent clients, intensifying competition among private-banking arms for succession and cross-border mandates.
  **法規鬆綁持續擴大高資產商品版圖** — 依金管會高資產客戶管理辦法，銀行對資產達 1 億元以上客戶可提供更多元、更複雜的金融商品與諮詢服務，且獲准辦理的銀行名單持續擴增。此制度為刻意鬆綁，旨在留住高資產族群資金於境內、支持台灣打造亞洲資產管理中心，讓合格銀行得對富裕客戶銷售結構型商品、海外債等複雜工具，並加劇私人銀行業務在傳承規劃與跨境理財的競爭。財管手續費收入可望成為銀行獲利成長引擎，惟商品適配與投資人保護仍為監理重點。
