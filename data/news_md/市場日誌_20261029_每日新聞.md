The upgraded earnings trajectory supports valuations for the index's largest constituent and underpins sentiment across the broader chip-making complex.
  **法人上修台積電 2027 獲利展望** — 投顧將台積電 2027 年 EPS 估值上看 150 元，看好先進製程與 CoWoS 需求延續，加上美國先進封裝布局瞄準 2028 年。獲利上修撐起台股第一大權值股評價，也連帶支撐整體晶圓代工與封測族群的市場信心。

### 🏭 Industry / 產業動態

- **CoWoS capacity accelerates on AI orders** — Supply-chain checks point to TSMC's CoWoS advanced-packaging capacity climbing toward 140k wafers per month by end-2026 and about 170k by end-2027, with Nvidia reserving more than half of total capacity and Google's TPU close behind. The ramp underscores how tight advanced packaging remains the key bottleneck for AI accelerator shipments.
  **CoWoS 產能受 AI 訂單推動加速** — 供應鏈調查指出，台積電 CoWoS 先進封裝產能可望於 2026 年底達每月 14 萬片、2027 年底約 17 萬片，輝達預訂逾五成產能，Google TPU 緊追在後。顯示先進封裝仍是 AI 加速器出貨的關鍵瓶頸，產能擴充步調牽動整個 AI 伺服器供應鏈的供給節奏。
  - 來源 / Source：[經濟日報](https://money.udn.com/money/story/5607/9343218)
- **Heavy capex sustains advanced-node build-out** — TSMC is keeping 2026 capital spending in the US$60–64 billion range to expand leading-edge and packaging capacity, while US advanced-packaging plans extend toward 2028. The sustained investment signals confidence in multi-year AI demand and keeps equipment and materials suppliers in focus across the island's tech cluster.
  **高資本支出支撐先進製程擴產** — 台積電 2026 年資本支出維持在 600 至 640 億美元區間，用於擴充先進製程與封裝產能，美國先進封裝布局並延伸至 2028 年。持續的高投資顯示對多年期 AI 需求的信心，也讓設備與材料供應商成為市場焦點，帶動整體科技聚落的資本支出動能。
  - 來源 / Source：[StatementDog](https://statementdog.com/news/17880)

### 🏦 Financial Sector / 金融業

- **FSC opens insurers to health-and-wellness investments** — The Financial Supervisory Commission removed the requirement that such holdings link to insurance operations and now lets insurers take stakes of up to 100%, adding five categories covering 13 eligible asset types, effective immediately. The move broadens how life insurers deploy capital and channels long-term funds into domestic wellness and care infrastructure.
  **金管會開放壽險投資健康福祉事業** — 金管會取消投資須與保險本業結合的限制，開放保險公司持股至 100%，新增 5 大類、合計 13 種可投資標的，即日起實施。此舉放寬壽險資金運用範疇，引導長期資金投入國內健康照護與福祉產業，有助壽險業分散資產配置並對接高齡化社會的照護需求。
  - 來源 / Source：[NOWnews](https://www.nownews.com/news/6884087)
- **New family-office advisory rules for banks** — The FSC issued guidelines governing banks' family-office consulting services, part of a push to build an Asian asset-management hub and attract high-net-worth families and family offices. Regulators also flagged a rolling review of the new-generation solvency regime and urged insurers to adjust product mixes and tighten asset-liability currency and duration matching.
  **金管會訂定銀行家族辦公室顧問業務規範** — 金管會發布銀行辦理家族辦公室顧問業務規範，呼應打造亞洲資產管理中心、吸引高資產家族與家族辦公室進駐的政策。主管機關並表示將持續滾動檢討新一代清償能力制度，促請壽險業調整商品結構、強化資產與負債的幣別及期間匹配，以降低利率與匯率風險。
  - 來源 / Source：[金管會](https://www.fsc.gov.tw/ch/home.jsp?id=96&parentpath=0,2)

### 💎 Wealth Management / 財富管理

- **FSC relaxes rules for wealth-management insurance products** — The Insurance Bureau opened wealth-management insurance products to an exemption from the rule linking death-benefit payouts to policy reserves, giving insurers more flexibility to design products aimed at affluent clients. The change dovetails with regulators' broader effort to develop high-end wealth and estate-planning solutions onshore.
  **金管會鬆綁財富管理保險商品規定** — 保險局開放財富管理保險商品不適用「人壽保險商品死亡給付對保單價值準備金」相關規定，讓業者設計鎖定高資產客群的商品更具彈性。此調整配合主管機關發展境內高端財富與傳承規劃的方向，可望帶動結構型與高保額保單商品的創新與市場需求。
  - 來源 / Source：[金管會](https://www.fsc.gov.tw/ch/home.jsp?id=96&parentpath=0,2)
- **Private-banking momentum stays strong** — Taishin Bank said it won dual honours at the PBI global wealth-management awards, with high-net-worth client numbers and AUM up about 186% and 176% respectively in the first half of 2026. Industry reports project Taiwan's HNW population rising from roughly 123,000 in 2025 toward 155,000 by 2029, with over 70% already holding offshore assets.
  **私人銀行動能強勁** — 台新銀行表示獲 PBI 全球財富管理大獎雙獎肯定，2026 年上半年高資產客戶數與 AUM 分別成長約 186% 與 176%。業界報告預估台灣高資產人口將由 2025 年約 12.3 萬人增至 2029 年約 15.5 萬人，逾七成已布局境外資產，跨境配置與傳承需求成為私人銀行成長主軸。
  - 來源 / Source：[經濟日報](https://money.udn.com/money/story/5636/9499607)
