### 🏭 Industry / 產業動態

- **CoWoS capacity accelerates on AI orders** — Supply-chain checks point to TSMC's CoWoS advanced-packaging capacity climbing toward 140k wafers per month by end-2026 and about 170k by end-2027, with Nvidia reserving more than half of total capacity and Google's TPU close behind. The ramp underscores how tight advanced packaging remains the key bottleneck for AI accelerator shipments.
  **CoWoS 產能受 AI 訂單推動加速** — 供應鏈調查指出，台積電 CoWoS 先進封裝產能可望於 2026 年底達每月 14 萬片、2027 年底約 17 萬片，輝達預訂逾五成產能，Google TPU 緊追在後。顯示先進封裝仍是 AI 加速器出貨的關鍵瓶頸，產能擴充步調牽動整個 AI 伺服器供應鏈的供給節奏。

The sustained investment signals confidence in multi-year AI demand and keeps equipment and materials suppliers in focus across the island's tech cluster.
  **高資本支出支撐先進製程擴產** — 台積電 2026 年資本支出維持在 600 至 640 億美元區間，用於擴充先進製程與封裝產能，美國先進封裝布局並延伸至 2028 年。持續的高投資顯示對多年期 AI 需求的信心，也讓設備與材料供應商成為市場焦點，帶動整體科技聚落的資本支出動能。

### 🏦 Financial Sector / 金融業

- **FSC opens insurers to health-and-wellness investments** — The Financial Supervisory Commission removed the requirement that such holdings link to insurance operations and now lets insurers take stakes of up to 100%, adding five categories covering 13 eligible asset types, effective immediately. The move broadens how life insurers deploy capital and channels long-term funds into domestic wellness and care infrastructure.
  **金管會開放壽險投資健康福祉事業** — 金管會取消投資須與保險本業結合的限制，開放保險公司持股至 100%，新增 5 大類、合計 13 種可投資標的，即日起實施。此舉放寬壽險資金運用範疇，引導長期資金投入國內健康照護與福祉產業，有助壽險業分散資產配置並對接高齡化社會的照護需求。
  - 來源 / Source：[NOWnews](https://www.nownews.com/news/6884087)
- **New family-office advisory rules for banks** — The FSC issued guidelines governing banks' family-office consulting services, part of a push to build an Asian asset-management hub and attract high-net-worth families and family offices. Regulators also flagged a rolling review of the new-generation solvency regime and urged insurers to adjust product mixes and tighten asset-liability currency and duration matching.
  **金管會訂定銀行家族辦公室顧問業務規範** — 金管會發布銀行辦理家族辦公室顧問業務規範，呼應打造亞洲資產管理中心、吸引高資產家族與家族辦公室進駐的政策。主管機關並表示將持續滾動檢討新一代清償能力制度，促請壽險業調整商品結構、強化資產與負債的幣別及期間匹配，以降低利率與匯率風險。

### 💎 Wealth Management / 財富管理

- **FSC relaxes rules for wealth-management insurance products** — The Insurance Bureau opened wealth-management insurance products to an exemption from the rule linking death-benefit payouts to policy reserves, giving insurers more flexibility to design products aimed at affluent clients. The change dovetails with regulators' broader effort to develop high-end wealth and estate-planning solutions onshore.
  **金管會鬆綁財富管理保險商品規定** — 保險局開放財富管理保險商品不適用「人壽保險商品死亡給付對保單價值準備金」相關規定，讓業者設計鎖定高資產客群的商品更具彈性。此調整配合主管機關發展境內高端財富與傳承規劃的方向，可望帶動結構型與高保額保單商品的創新與市場需求。
  - 來源 / Source：[金管會](https://www.fsc.gov.tw/ch/home.jsp?id=96&parentpath=0,2)

Industry reports project Taiwan's HNW population rising from roughly 123,000 in 2025 toward 155,000 by 2029, with over 70% already holding offshore assets.

### 💰 Tax & Regulations / 稅務法規

- **Legislature finalises deemed-estate tax change** — Taiwan's legislature passed amendments folding gifts made within two years before death into the taxable estate, with recipients bearing the corresponding tax. The Ministry of Finance is drafting enforcement rules and filing forms ahead of promulgation. Estate-tax revenue hit a record NT$44.33 billion in 2025, and the exemption threshold stands at NT$13.33 million, with a possible increase under review for 2027.
  **立院三讀通過擬制遺產課稅修法** — 立法院三讀通過修法，將死亡前兩年內的贈與併入遺產總額，由受贈人承擔相應稅額；財政部正研擬修正施行細則與申報書表，待總統公布後加強宣導。2025 年遺產稅收達 443.3 億元創單年新高，目前免稅額 1,333 萬元，2027 年是否調升仍在評估，高資產族群傳承規劃須留意申報變動。
  - 來源 / Source：[中央社](https://www.cna.com.tw/news/afe/202608210289.aspx)
- **CRS exchange scope stays narrow as CARF looms** — Taiwan's automatic CRS information exchange still covers only Japan, Australia and the UK, since it must sign bilateral deals outside the OECD's multilateral framework. The Ministry of Finance stresses CRS data is used for risk assessment and case selection, not direct taxation. Globally, the OECD's crypto-asset reporting framework (CARF) heads toward a first exchange in 2027.
  **CRS 交換範圍仍窄、CARF 將上路** — 台灣 CRS 自動資訊交換對象目前仍僅日本、澳洲、英國三國，因無法加入 OECD 多邊架構，只能逐一簽署雙邊協定。財政部強調 CRS 取得的資訊不會直接據以課稅，而是供國稅局評估逃漏風險與選案。國際間 OECD 已推出加密資產申報框架 CARF，首次正式交換預計落在 2027 年，海外資產配置者宜及早檢視申報。
  - 來源 / Source：[Stockfeel](https://www.stockfeel.com.tw/%E4%BD%95%E8%AC%82crs%EF%BC%9F%E5%8F%B0%E7%81%A3%E5%8A%A0%E5%85%A5crs%E5%85%B1%E5%90%8C%E7%94%B3%E5%A0%B1%E6%BA%96%E5%89%87%E7%9A%84%E5%BD%B1%E9%9F%BF/)

### 📜 Macro & Policy / 總經政策

- **DGBAS lifts 2026 growth forecast to a near-40-year high** — The budget and statistics agency raised its 2026 GDP growth forecast to about 11.05%, the highest since 1988, driven by an AI-led export surge, with goods exports projected at US$903.6 billion (up 41.19%). It expects 2026 CPI of 2.07%, slightly above the central bank's 2% alert line, and growth moderating to about 6.04% in 2027.
  **主計總處上修 2026 GDP 至近 40 年新高** — 主計總處將 2026 年經濟成長率預測上修至約 11.05%，為 1988 年以來最高，主因 AI 供應鏈帶動出口大增，全年商品出口上看 9,036 億美元、年增 41.19%。CPI 年增率預估 2.07%，略高於央行 2% 通膨警戒線；2027 年成長率估回落至約 6.04%，顯示高成長動能集中於科技出口，內需與物價仍是政策關注焦點。

- **Fed holds rates steady in split vote** — The US Federal Reserve voted 10-2 to keep its policy rate in the 3.75%–4.00% range after its two-day meeting, following September's first hike since 2023. Officials signalled a hawkish bias, with some participants still seeing a further increase as possible before year-end, keeping global monetary policy and Taiwan's own rate path in focus.
  **聯準會以分歧票數維持利率不變** — 美國聯準會會後以 10 比 2 票決議維持政策利率於 3.75%～4.00% 區間，此前 9 月才完成 2023 年以來首度升息。官員釋出偏鷹訊號，部分與會者仍認為年底前可能再升息一次。此一全球貨幣政策基調牽動資金流向與匯率，也將影響台灣央行後續利率決策與市場資金情緒。
